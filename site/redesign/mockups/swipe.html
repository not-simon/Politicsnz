Power & fuel Party hidden

Split power companies so the same company can't both generate electricity and sell it to households.

What it would do: generation and retail would be run by different companies, so the most expensive generator would no longer set the price for the rest of the market.

The reasoning given

This party says four large companies generate about 90% of New Zealand's power and also sell it to customers, which it says keeps prices high for households and industry.
