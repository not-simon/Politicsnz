Farmers and industry should pay for the greenhouse gases they emit, even if that raises food and fuel prices.

What does this mean?

Most businesses already pay for some of their emissions through the Emissions Trading Scheme. Emissions from farm animals, mainly methane, are not priced at the moment. Supporters say paying for emissions is fairer and cuts pollution; opponents say it would raise costs for farmers and shoppers when New Zealand farming is already relatively efficient.
