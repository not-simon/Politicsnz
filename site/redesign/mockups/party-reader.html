In a nutshell

Labour is the biggest opposition party and last governed from 2017 to 2023. It wants three free GP visits a year, paid for by a new tax on profits from selling property (not the family home). It also promises to restore pay equity and to give the Commerce Commission new powers over large companies' prices.

Labour's slogan is "Better Starts Now". It says "every promise Labour has made is fully paid for" and that it "will balance the books without shrinking the economy".

What critics say
National's Nicola Willis called Labour's fiscal plan fraudulent, saying Treasury estimates restoring the old pay-equity scheme would cost $11 billion, leaving Labour about $8.5 billion short; Labour says it has enough unallocated money and won't name a figure during negotiations.

Affordable Healthcare - NZ Labour's Medicard Plan

Labour · labour.org.nz · retrieved 23 Oct 2026

The party's own words, shown as published. Not edited or fact-checked by Ballot Book.

Labour will make healthcare affordable again with three free doctor's visits, free prescriptions, free maternity scans and free cervical screening - all through one Medicard.

Labour's plan for affordable healthcare

  • Free Doctor's Visits - three free GP visits a year for every New Zealander
  • Free Prescriptions - the $5 fee removed from 1 July 2027
  • Free Maternity Scans - everywhere in New Zealand from July 2028
  • Free Cervical Screening - for ages 25 to 69, from 1 October 2027
  • Family Doctor Loan Scheme - backing more local GPs to open and stay open

Why it matters

Under Christopher Luxon, it's become harder and more expensive to see a doctor when you're sick.

1 in 6New Zealanders can't afford to visit their doctor

~$100Some GP visit fees are heading towards this

9 in 10New Zealanders won't pay Labour's targeted property tax

No one should have to choose between seeing a doctor and putting food on the table. Labour will build a health system that doesn't depend on what’s in your wallet.

Labour's plan in detail

Free Doctor's Visits (Medicard)

Every New Zealander will get a new Medicard giving access to three free doctor's visits a year at their enrolled practice, funded by a simple, targeted tax on investment property profits.

Free Prescriptions

From 1 July 2027, the $5 fee on prescriptions will be removed for every New Zealander entitled to public healthcare - no forms, no claiming money back.

Free Maternity Scans

From July 2028, Labour will fund every scan a doctor, midwife or specialist refers a pregnant woman for, everywhere in New Zealand - no forms, no invoices.

Free Cervical Screening

From 1 October 2027, cervical screening will be free for everyone aged 25 to 69, loaded automatically onto the Medicard and available at your local doctor or a community screening event.

Family Doctor Loan Scheme

Labour will offer doctors interest-free loans for the first two years, of up to 90% of the cost of buying into a general practice (capped at $500,000), to encourage more owner-operated local clinics.

A Labour government will also guarantee a job for every eligible graduate nurse, so our hospitals and clinics have the staff to deliver this care. See our full plan for good jobs.
