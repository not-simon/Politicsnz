What this can't know

  • Turnout under the new rules: there's no enrolling after 25 October this year.
  • Special votes, counted after election night, which have shifted seats before.
  • Undecided voters, and pollsters all missing the same way, as in 2020.
  • Late swings in the last fortnight, as in 2017 and 2020.
  • Māori seats: only a few small electorate polls exist, so treat those odds as rough.
