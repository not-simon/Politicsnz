AgreeNope Energy · Policy 4 of 20

Break up the big electricity "gentailers" so companies that generate power can't also sell it.

What it would do

Force Meridian, Mercury, Genesis and Contact to separate their generation and retail arms into different companies.

The reasoning

Supporters say the combined companies have little reason to compete on price. Critics say splitting them raises costs and slows new generation.
