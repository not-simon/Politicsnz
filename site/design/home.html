How MMP works

  1. You get two ticks. One for a party, one for a local electorate candidate. They can be for different sides.
  2. The party vote decides almost everything. A party's share of the party vote sets its share of the 120 seats.
  3. To get in, a party needs 5% or an electorate seat. Below that, its party votes are set aside.
  4. 61 seats make a government. No party has won that alone under MMP, so parties agree deals to govern together.
