# Freedoms New Zealand (Freedoms NZ)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status (Oct 2026)** | **Deregistered** at its own request, 1 May 2025; disbanded as an umbrella |
| **Founded** | Announced 22 August 2022 by Brian Tamaki at a Parliament protest; registered 16 February 2023 |
| **Leaders** | Brian Tamaki; co-leader Sue Grey (2023) |
| **Components** | Vision NZ, NZ Outdoors & Freedom (from May 2023), New Nation (left by Sept 2023), unregistered Yes Aotearoa and Rock the Vote NZ, plus Tamaki's Freedom & Rights Coalition |
| **Ideology** | Anti-COVID-mandate "freedom movement" umbrella; anti-establishment |
| **Seats** | None |

## History

Tamaki forecast 20% of the vote. National, Labour, the Greens and ACT all ruled out working with it (National after
initially telling reporters to "read between the lines"). The High Court rejected its challenge to the Electoral
Commission's decision to give the group a single broadcasting allocation. During the 2023 campaign Freedoms NZ supporters,
notably Vision candidate Karl Mokaraka, disrupted Labour, National and ACT events, including Labour's campaign launch.
It won **0.34%** (9,586). [1]
