# Socialist Equality Group (SEG) — 2026 election policies

Every policy of **Socialist Equality Group (SEG)** for the 7 November 2026 general election that this archive could find: **10 policies** listed, backed by **1 full-text source document**.

- Party website: <https://www.wsws.org/>
- Registration: Not a registered party; one electorate candidate (Tom Petterson, Hutt South)

## About this list

The Trotskyist Socialist Equality Group is standing Tom Petterson in Hutt South. Its programme is its election statement published on the World Socialist Web Site on 13 October 2026, which says the campaign 'is not a bid for office' but an intervention for socialist leadership of the working class. The statement's explicit demands are listed below.

## Defence & foreign affairs

### End all military spending and redirect it to schools, hospitals and ending poverty (SEG-01)

**Why** (*archive*): The SEG says a third world war is developing and that both Labour and National back US war plans and higher military spending.

**How** (*archive*): Part of its 'No money for war!' demands.

## Incomes & employment

### Raise all wages by 20% immediately, raise benefits, and index wages and benefits monthly to the cost of living (SEG-04)

**Why** (*archive*): The SEG says real wages have fallen 6.4% since 2021 while billionaire wealth has grown.

**How** (*archive*): An immediate 20 percent pay rise for all workers plus automatic monthly cost-of-living adjustments.

Full text: [Fight against war and for international socialism! Reject all capitalist parties and support the Socialist Equality Group in New Zealand's election!](documents/election-statement-2026-10-13.md) ([original](https://www.wsws.org/en/articles/2026/10/14/cjbj-o14.html))

### Guarantee a permanent full-time job for all who want one and a living income for those who cannot work (SEG-05)

### Build rank-and-file workers' committees independent of the unions (SEG-08)

**Why** (*archive*): The SEG says the trade union bureaucracy acts as 'an industrial police force for the state and big business'.
