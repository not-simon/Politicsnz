## History

A founding component of Freedoms NZ, it left in July 2023 to stand on its own and won 1,530 votes (0.05%) — the
lowest of any party in 2023. Its candidate in the 2022 Tauranga by-election, former councillor Andrew Hollis, won 260
votes. [1][2]
