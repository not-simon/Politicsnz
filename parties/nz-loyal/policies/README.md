# New Zealand Loyal (NZ Loyal) — 2026 election policies

Every policy of **New Zealand Loyal (NZ Loyal)** for the 7 November 2026 general election that this archive could find: **10 policies** listed, backed by **1 full-text source document** and 1 documented-as-unretrievable source.

- Party website: <https://nzloyal.com/>
- Registration: 5 August 2026 (re-registered; Electoral Commission register)

## About this list

NZ Loyal (leader Kelvyn Alp) was re-registered in August 2026. **Its own policy pages could not be captured**: nzloyal.com is behind a Cloudflare bot challenge that blocked both curl and a headless browser. The only verbatim party wording available to this archive is a ten-point list RNZ quoted from the party's website on 12 October 2026; each point is listed below with that RNZ article as the source.

**Method.** Pages and PDFs were downloaded on 2026-10-23 by [`tools/policies_minor_sites_fetch.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_sites_fetch.py) / [`tools/policies_minor_opportunity_fetch.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_opportunity_fetch.py) (raw copies in `raw/party-docs/nz-loyal/`) and reduced to their main text in [`documents/`](documents/) (markup and whitespace normalised only). The list and summaries are built by [`tools/policies_minor_build.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_build.py) from [`tools/policies_minor_catalog.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_catalog.py).

policy.nz publishes no policy statements for this party, so every summary here is marked *archive*: written for this archive from the linked full text in one to three neutral sentences ("Why" = the party's own stated reason, attributed; "How" = what it says it would do). Party claims (costs, statistics) are reported as the party's claims, not verified.

**Gaps and caveats.** Full policy text NOT retrievable (nzloyal.com HTTP 403 Cloudflare; web.archive.org unreachable from this environment; newzealandloyal.com is a parked domain). Detail behind each headline (mechanisms, costs) is unknown. A separate group calling itself the 'Real NZ Loyal' disputes Alp's leadership (reported by The Press and Wikipedia); this archive lists the registered party's stated policies only.
