## Current status

Leader of New Zealand Loyal and its **#1** list candidate; standing in **Māngere** in 2026. Never an MP. Founder Liz Gunn disputes his leadership, calling his party a "fake NZ Loyal".

## Biography

Born 27 March 1971. Set up the "New Zealand Armed Intervention Force" in 1996 as a mercenary/paramilitary group (defunct). With partner Hannah Spierer co-founded Counterspin Media, a far-right streaming outlet carried on Steve Bannon-linked GTV.

## Political career

- Led the Direct Democracy Party (2005); stood for Manukau mayor (2007); co-led OurNZ and came last in the 2011 Te Tai Tokerau by-election (72 votes).
- Wrote policy for NZ Loyal in 2023; after Gunn deregistered the party in 2024 he took control at a November 2024 AGM; last (23 votes) in the 2025 Tāmaki Makaurau by-election. The party was re-registered in August 2026.

## Controversies

- An agitating force at the 2022 Parliament occupation, calling for protesters to storm Parliament and arrest MPs; Counterspin has been described as a source of misinformation, and a Counterspin contributor disrupted a 2021 Ardern press conference.
