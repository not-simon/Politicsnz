# Animal Justice Party Aotearoa New Zealand (AJP) — 2026 election policies

Every policy of **Animal Justice Party Aotearoa New Zealand (AJP)** for the 7 November 2026 general election that this archive could find: **38 policies** listed, backed by **51 full-text source documents**.

- Party website: <https://animaljustice.org.nz/>
- Registration: 16 August 2023 (Electoral Commission register)

## About this list

The Animal Justice Party publishes an Election 2026 Manifesto, a plain-language Policy Summary (ten campaign themes), a 2026 full policy platform PDF (~23,000 words), four policy packs, and 40+ individual policy pages, most with their own PDF. All were captured (web text via the WordPress API plus each linked PDF). The list below gives the ten summary themes (marked as headline policies) followed by each detailed policy page.

**Method.** Pages and PDFs were downloaded on 2026-10-23 by [`tools/policies_minor_sites_fetch.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_sites_fetch.py) / [`tools/policies_minor_opportunity_fetch.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_opportunity_fetch.py) (raw copies in `raw/party-docs/animal-justice/`) and reduced to their main text in [`documents/`](documents/) (markup and whitespace normalised only). The list and summaries are built by [`tools/policies_minor_build.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_build.py) from [`tools/policies_minor_catalog.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_catalog.py).

policy.nz publishes no policy statements for this party, so every summary here is marked *archive*: written for this archive from the linked full text in one to three neutral sentences ("Why" = the party's own stated reason, attributed; "How" = what it says it would do). Party claims (costs, statistics) are reported as the party's claims, not verified.

**Gaps and caveats.** Some individual policy PDFs date from 2024–2025 (publication dates are in each document's front matter); the party presents them as current. PDF text extraction splits decorative drop-capitals onto their own line.

## Community & inclusion

### Recognise animals in law as sentient beings rather than property (AJP-02)

**Why** (*archive*): The AJP says the property classification makes meaningful protection very hard.

**How** (*archive*): Write sentience into legislation with enforceable protections and end codes of practice that let industries sidestep the Animal Welfare Act.

Full text: [Policy Summary](documents/policy-policy-summary.md) ([original](https://animaljustice.org.nz/policies/policy-summary/)); [Election 2026 Manifesto](documents/election-2026-manifesto.md) ([original](https://animaljustice.org.nz/election-2026/election-manifesto/)); [AJP 2026 Policy Platform — Election 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-ajp-2026-policy-platform-election-2026.md) ([original](https://animaljustice.org.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/AJP-2026-Policy-platform-election-2026.pdf)); [Legislative Foundations](documents/policy-legislative-foundations.md) ([original](https://animaljustice.org.nz/policies/legislative-foundations/))

### Create an independent Commissioner for Animals with investigative and enforcement powers (AJP-04)

**Why** (*archive*): The AJP says welfare is overseen by MPI, the same ministry that promotes farm exports — a conflict of interest.

**How** (*archive*): Modelled on the Children's Commissioner, with funding for welfare research and accountability for enforcement agencies.

Full text: [Policy Summary](documents/policy-policy-summary.md) ([original](https://animaljustice.org.nz/policies/policy-summary/)); [Election 2026 Manifesto](documents/election-2026-manifesto.md) ([original](https://animaljustice.org.nz/election-2026/election-manifesto/)); [AJP 2026 Policy Platform — Election 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-ajp-2026-policy-platform-election-2026.md) ([original](https://animaljustice.org.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/AJP-2026-Policy-platform-election-2026.pdf)); [Commissioner for Animals](documents/policy-commissioner-for-animals.md) ([original](https://animaljustice.org.nz/policies/commissioner-for-animals/)); [Commissioner for Animals](documents/policy-animal-rights-commissioner.md) ([original](https://animaljustice.org.nz/policies/animal-rights-commissioner/)); [Regulatory Oversight and Enforcement](documents/policy-regulatory-oversight-and-enforcement.md) ([original](https://animaljustice.org.nz/policies/regulatory-oversight-and-enforcement/))

### Make rehoming the default in council pounds, with euthanasia only as a last resort (AJP-05)

**Why** (*archive*): The AJP says 60% of dogs impounded in Auckland last year were killed rather than rehomed.

**How** (*archive*): Public reporting of pound outcomes by every council, fairer reclaim processes and fees, funded foster networks and wide access to desexing.

Full text: [Policy Summary](documents/policy-policy-summary.md) ([original](https://animaljustice.org.nz/policies/policy-summary/)); [Election 2026 Manifesto](documents/election-2026-manifesto.md) ([original](https://animaljustice.org.nz/election-2026/election-manifesto/)); [AJP 2026 Policy Platform — Election 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-ajp-2026-policy-platform-election-2026.md) ([original](https://animaljustice.org.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/AJP-2026-Policy-platform-election-2026.pdf))

### Remove GST from veterinary services and fund public and mobile vet clinics (AJP-07)

**Why** (*archive*): The AJP says vet bills force impossible choices on families.

**How** (*archive*): A national network of publicly funded and mobile clinics plus guaranteed funding for injured wildlife care and desexing.

Full text: [Policy Summary](documents/policy-policy-summary.md) ([original](https://animaljustice.org.nz/policies/policy-summary/)); [Election 2026 Manifesto](documents/election-2026-manifesto.md) ([original](https://animaljustice.org.nz/election-2026/election-manifesto/)); [AJP 2026 Policy Platform — Election 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-ajp-2026-policy-platform-election-2026.md) ([original](https://animaljustice.org.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/AJP-2026-Policy-platform-election-2026.pdf))

### Ban the cruellest farming and fishing practices first (intensive winter grazing, fast-growing chicken breeds, live export, mutilations, bottom trawling), with a funded farmer transition (AJP-01)
