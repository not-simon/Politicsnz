# Ensuring adequate living conditions and environmental standards

### Web page text

The Animal Justice Party Aotearoa NZ (AJP) is working towards a future without animal agriculture. In the meantime, we are committed to addressing the widespread neglect of shelter, housing and environmental conditions that cause immense suffering to farmed animals. Despite existing legal protections, animals across New Zealand are routinely exposed to harsh weather, overcrowding and barren environments that deny them the ability to express natural behaviours. AJP is calling for urgent reforms to ensure farmed animals are afforded basic protections, dignity and care.
