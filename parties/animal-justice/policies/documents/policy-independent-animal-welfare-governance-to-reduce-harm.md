# Independent Animal Welfare Governance to Reduce Harm

> Archive note: Web text is the policy post body from animaljustice.org.nz/wp-json/wp/v2/policies; the PDF version linked from the page (if any) follows, extracted with pdftotext -layout.

## Full text (verbatim)

### Web page text

The Animal Justice Party (AJP) envisions a New Zealand where compassion, sustainability and ethical practices guide our treatment of animals. While we advocate for a complete transition to a plant-based farming system, we recognise that meaningful societal changes require time and a ‘Just Transition’. In the interim, we are committed to reducing the most severe harm and mistreatment of animals by improving oversight of farming practices and strengthening legislation to provide stronger protections and ensure higher welfare standards for all animals in Aotearoa.
