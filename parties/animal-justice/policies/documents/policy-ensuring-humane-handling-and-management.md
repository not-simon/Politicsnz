# Ensuring Humane Handling and Management

> Archive note: Web text is the policy post body from animaljustice.org.nz/wp-json/wp/v2/policies; the PDF version linked from the page (if any) follows, extracted with pdftotext -layout.

## Full text (verbatim)

### Web page text

The Animal Justice Party Aotearoa New Zealand (AJP) is committed to ending harmful animal handling practices and advancing a system of care that upholds the dignity,rights and welfare of all animals used in agriculture. While our ultimate goal is to abolish all animal farming, we call for urgent reforms to eliminate cruel procedures, ensure prompt access to veterinary care, and establish humane handling standards rooted in compassion and respect for sentient beings.

Industrial farming often prioritises profit over animal welfare, leading to practices that cause unnecessary pain, distress and harm. AJP advocates for the elimination of harmful handling practices, ensuring access to veterinary care and legislative reform that prevents cruelty.
