# The environmental impacts of animal-based research

> Archive note: Web text is the policy post body from animaljustice.org.nz/wp-json/wp/v2/policies; the PDF version linked from the page (if any) follows, extracted with pdftotext -layout.

## Full text (verbatim)

### Web page text

### The Animal Justice Party of Aotearoa New Zealand (AJP) aims to ban all harmful forms of animal experimentation. AJP commits to promoting the use of compassionate research.

Animal-based research isn’t just an ethical issue. It also has significant environmental impacts that demand attention. The environmental cost of maintaining these practices contradicts commitments to sustainability and climate action.

### Key Objectives:

#### Interim measures, while we work towards the AJP Policy Ending Animal testing, include:

1. AJP will encourage the minimisation of the environmental impacts of animal-based research by requiring all research facilities, laboratories, and institutions engaged in animal research and testing, including academic, governmental, and private-sector organisations, to implement sustainable practices, reduce waste, and ensure compliance with relevant environmental regulations.\
 \
2. AJP will support the adoption of the following measures by all research facilities and institutions:\

- *Reduce, Refine, Replace (3Rs):* Prioritise alternatives in order to replace animal testing where possible, refine methods to minimise environmental impact and reduce the number of animals used. Strengthen the adherence to the 3Rs.\
- *Waste Management:* Reduce reuse and recycle materials wherever possible, properly dispose of hazardous and biological waste in compliance with regulations and implement sustainable alternatives for animal bedding and feed where feasible.\
- *Energy and Resource Efficiency:* Utilise energy-efficient equipment and lighting in animal research facilities and optimise water usage in cleaning, animal care and laboratory operations.\
- *Sustainable Procurement:* Source laboratory supplies, animal feed and equipment from environmentally responsible suppliers and prioritise biodegradable or recyclable materials in research processes.\
- *Reduction of Greenhouse Gas Emissions:* Reduce transportation-related emissions by consolidating supply shipments and using local vendors when possible, implementing practices that minimise the release of harmful substances into the air and water.
