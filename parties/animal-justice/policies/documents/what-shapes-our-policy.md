# What Shapes Our Policy

### Our Policy Documents

Our policies aren’t just words - they’re blueprints for change. Backed by experts and evidence, our Policy Framework sets out the values behind everything we do, while our Policy Directory brings those principles to life with specific, practical policies across every area we work on. These comprehensive documents lay out our path to a world where animals and nature thrive alongside humans.

[Policy Framework Document](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1hgQ3Zo3HU9XhjS1_RJ9v66_uD78HDIV9/view)[Policy Directory Document](https://drive.google.com/file/d/15IUFRZw04B6DanyzXQUXLdCjylN9lJ53/view?usp=drive_link)

### Policy Documents

Each policy supports actionable campaigns that bring our vision to life: a healthier planet where all beings are protected and respected. They have been developed by our Policy Committee, often via engagement with experts and members, and all include clear short- and long-term objectives.

### Positions

Animal advocacy is our passion, but we don’t live in a single-issue world. At times we may need to take a stand on a wider range of topics. Our position statements give you a clear snapshot of where we stand on these diverse issues. Always guided by our core values of compassion, non-violence, equality, sustainability and a just transition, these statements show how our animal-friendly philosophy shapes our approach to every aspect of society.
