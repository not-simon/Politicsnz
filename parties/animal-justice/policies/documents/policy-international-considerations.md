### Web page text

The shift in the international animal welfare and protection policy environment highlights the growing recognition of animal welfare as a critical issue on the global stage.

It is no longer viewed as peripheral but has become integral to discussions in regional and international policy forums.

This change is underscored by the emergence of an expanding framework of science-based standards, conventions, treaties, regulations, directives, and agreements.
