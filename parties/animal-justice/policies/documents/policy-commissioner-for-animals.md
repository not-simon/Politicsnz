### Web page text

The Animal Justice Party Aotearoa NZ (AJP) believes that all animals in Aotearoa deserve adequate representation in Parliament, to ensure they are protected from exploitation and mistreatment.

To strengthen the voice of animals, we are advocating for the independent position of Commissioner for Animals to be established. Animal welfare is currently regulated by the Ministry for Primary Industries (MPI). This is the same Government department responsible for seizing export opportunities and overseeing agriculture productivity. Decisions on animal welfare policies are being driven by industry stakeholders who are invested in profiting from the use of animals.

Key Objectives.

1. Appoint an independent Commissioner for Animals who will have the authority to
undertake a comprehensive review of the current system, and provide national
leadership on animal protection.
2. Ensure adequate funding towards research and policy making that will improve
animal well-being as backed by science.
3. Propose that the Commissioner for Animals has the power to hold MPI and other
stakeholders accountable for the effective administration of the Animal Welfare
Act 1999.
4. Ensure the Commissioner for Animals maintains independence from the
executive Government to prevent conflicts of interest.
5. Ensure the Commissioner for Animals is equipped to make recommendations to
improve enforcement of the Animal Welfare Act 1999 and to increase or add
penalties where these are considered necessary.
