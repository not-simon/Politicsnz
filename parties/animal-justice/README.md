## History and policy

The party's 2023 platform included banning recreational fishing and hunting competitions, rodeo, horse racing, fur and
leather production and animal experimentation; supporting the greyhound-racing ban; ending intensive winter grazing
("mud farming") and factory farming; opposing 1080; a commissioner for animals; and a phased shift from animal farming
to plant-based agriculture with support for farmers. It also campaigns against the coalition government's plan to
reverse the 2023 live-export ban. [1]

policy.nz says co-leader Robert McNeil cited a lack of Green candidates in East Auckland seats and a perception that the
Greens compromise as reasons for founding it. [2]

The party ran 17 candidates in 2023 and won **0.18%** (5,018 votes); it came fourth in the 2023 Port Waikato
by-election. An August 2025 poll put it at 1% for the first time. In the 2025 local elections it stood unsuccessfully
for the Auckland and Hamilton mayoralties and other seats. [1]
