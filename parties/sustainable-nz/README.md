| **Ideology** | Environmentalist but willing to work with National or Labour ("blue-green"/"teal" in media; Tava preferred "green-green") |
| **Seats** | None |

## History

Former party secretary Helen Cartwright alleged in February 2020 that she had been asked to falsify membership records
to reach the 500 needed for registration; Tava said the party complied fully with the Electoral Act. Cartwright left and
founded the Integrity Party. Sustainable NZ received a $53,840 broadcasting allocation and won 1,880 votes (0.07%) in
2020. [1][2]
