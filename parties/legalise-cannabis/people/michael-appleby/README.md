## Current status

ALCP candidate for **Wellington North** and **#2** on its list in 2026. Never an MP.

## Biography

A Wellington human rights lawyer (BA in politics and English; MA covering administrative, tax/trusts and constitutional law) who has taught law in New Zealand and Australia, and a long-time cannabis law reform activist.

## Political career

- Led the Aotearoa Legalise Cannabis Party from its founding in 1996 until 2013; elected to the Terawhiti Licensing Trust in 1998.
- Contested Wellington Central at elections from 1996 (best: third in 1999) through 2023 (418 votes); 2013 Ikaroa-Rāwhiti by-election; Wellington City Council bids in 2004 and 2013.
