### Why I'm standing

I am standing up for human rights and promoting policy for a better economy. The ALCP want law reform for recreational cannabis. 1.4 million people voted YES in the 2020 cannabis referendum they should not  be criminalised, limiting job and travel prospects. Abolishing the expensive, failed Misuse of Drugs Act 1975, will save millions of dollars per annum, better spent on housing, education, school lunches, health and infrastructure. Regulating cannabis will bring jobs, tax revenue.
