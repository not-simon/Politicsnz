# Aotearoa Legalise Cannabis Party (ALCP) — 2026 election policies

Every policy of **Aotearoa Legalise Cannabis Party (ALCP)** for the 7 November 2026 general election that this archive could find: **11 policies** listed, backed by **1 full-text source document**.

- Party website: <https://www.alcp.org.nz/>
- Registration: 30 May 1996 (Electoral Commission register)

## About this list

The ALCP's policy page ("Full Spectrum Policy") is a single document, credited as developed in 2005 by Irinka Britnell, Paula Lambert and Kevin O'Connell, with a 'Policy Minimum Programme', eleven numbered portfolio sections and a 2020 addendum of ideas 'under discussion' prepared for the cannabis referendum bill. The party presents this page as its current policy; each distinct commitment is listed below.

**Method.** Pages and PDFs were downloaded on 2026-10-23 by [`tools/policies_minor_sites_fetch.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_sites_fetch.py) / [`tools/policies_minor_opportunity_fetch.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_opportunity_fetch.py) (raw copies in `raw/party-docs/legalise-cannabis/`) and reduced to their main text in [`documents/`](documents/) (markup and whitespace normalised only). The list and summaries are built by [`tools/policies_minor_build.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_build.py) from [`tools/policies_minor_catalog.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_catalog.py).

policy.nz publishes no policy statements for this party, so every summary here is marked *archive*: written for this archive from the linked full text in one to three neutral sentences ("Why" = the party's own stated reason, attributed; "How" = what it says it would do). Party claims (costs, statistics) are reported as the party's claims, not verified.

**Gaps and caveats.** The policy text is largely from 2005 with a 2020 addendum; no 2026-specific manifesto, costings or press releases were captured (the site's News page was not mined, and web search capacity ran out during this research). The 2020 items are described by the party as 'under discussion and in progress'.

## Defence & foreign affairs

### Reallocate drug-war resources to border security and civil defence (ALCP-10)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says the international war on drugs aids organised crime and terrorism.

**How** (*archive*): Resources freed by ending the cannabis 'drug war' would strengthen border security and civil defence.

Full text: [Full Spectrum Policy (ALCP policy page)](documents/full-spectrum-policy.md) ([original](https://www.alcp.org.nz/policy))

## Economy

### Enable full-scale hemp production, prioritising local low-cost biofuel, fibre and hurd (ALCP-04)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says hemp would create jobs (especially in depressed regions), cut emissions and pollution, save forests and improve soils.

**How** (*archive*): Allow full-scale cannabis-hemp farming and use, with priority for localised biofuel production and fibre and hurd for building materials, paper, plastics and textiles; hemp seed as food.

Full text: [Full Spectrum Policy (ALCP policy page)](documents/full-spectrum-policy.md) ([original](https://www.alcp.org.nz/policy))

### Allow at least five cannabis plants per household and affordable micro-licences for small growers (ALCP-11)

**Why** (*archive*): Part of the party's 2020 'cottage industry' ideas, which it says are still under discussion.

**How** (*archive*): A 'bare minimum' five-plant home-grow allowance, affordable micro-licences for existing growers, Kiwi seed strains, safe edibles, quality-controlled retail products and protection against racially loaded enforcement; an age limit more consistent with alcohol and tobacco.

Full text: [Full Spectrum Policy (ALCP policy page)](documents/full-spectrum-policy.md) ([original](https://www.alcp.org.nz/policy))

## Health

### Create regulated R18 cannabis commerce like alcohol and tobacco, but with no promotion (ALCP-02)

**Why** (*archive*): The ALCP says cannabis prohibition is unworkable and discriminatory, wastes public money and fuels the black market.

**How** (*archive*): Legal adult-only sales with no advertising or promotional aspects, with details fine-tuned by a policy council.

Full text: [Full Spectrum Policy (ALCP policy page)](documents/full-spectrum-policy.md) ([original](https://www.alcp.org.nz/policy))

### Provide for medicinal cannabis, including home-grown medicine and prescribed preparations (ALCP-03)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says legal access would promote safer modes of use such as vaporisation and mouth sprays.

**How** (*archive*): Establish therapeutic applications of cannabis, allowing both home-grown medicinal cannabis and prescribed preparations.

Full text: [Full Spectrum Policy (ALCP policy page)](documents/full-spectrum-policy.md) ([original](https://www.alcp.org.nz/policy))

### Set up a tobacco, alcohol and cannabis authority (Drugs Policy Council) to regulate recreational substances (ALCP-07)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says credible, harm-reduction drug education is impossible under current 'double standards'.

**How** (*archive*): A single regulator to develop the regulatory framework for recreational substances, with 'adults only' enforcement and honest drug education in schools funded from prohibition savings.

Full text: [Full Spectrum Policy (ALCP policy page)](documents/full-spectrum-policy.md) ([original](https://www.alcp.org.nz/policy))

## Law, justice & government

### Make possession, cultivation and use of cannabis free from prosecution (ALCP-01)

**Why** (*archive*): The ALCP says cannabis prohibition is unworkable and discriminatory, wastes public money and fuels the black market.

**How** (*archive*): Part of the party's 'Policy Minimum Programme': no prosecution for possessing, growing or using cannabis and cannabis/hemp products.

Full text: [Full Spectrum Policy (ALCP policy page)](documents/full-spectrum-policy.md) ([original](https://www.alcp.org.nz/policy))

### Expunge all cannabis convictions and apologise for prohibition (ALCP-05)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says up to 20,000 people a year are convicted of cannabis offences and that Māori are four to five times more likely to be convicted.

**How** (*archive*): Wipe all cannabis convictions; the 2020 addendum adds that apologies must be made for the injustices of prohibition.

Full text: [Full Spectrum Policy (ALCP policy page)](documents/full-spectrum-policy.md) ([original](https://www.alcp.org.nz/policy))

### Hold a prohibition 'truth and reconciliation' commission (ALCP-06)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says Parliament has a duty to challenge injustice and break down prejudice.

**How** (*archive*): A full commission on prohibition 'in the interests of accountability'.

Full text: [Full Spectrum Policy (ALCP policy page)](documents/full-spectrum-policy.md) ([original](https://www.alcp.org.nz/policy))

### Redirect prohibition enforcement money to health, education, community and serious crime (ALCP-08)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says police would be freed up for 'real crime' and public trust in the law would improve.

**How** (*archive*): Shift funds currently spent enforcing cannabis laws into health, education, community and employment, and more police and court resources for serious crime.

Full text: [Full Spectrum Policy (ALCP policy page)](documents/full-spectrum-policy.md) ([original](https://www.alcp.org.nz/policy))

## Migration

### Stop overseas cannabis offences being a barrier to immigrating to New Zealand (ALCP-09)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says it has no other immigration position beyond inclusiveness and tolerance.

**How** (*archive*): Cannabis offences in other jurisdictions should not bar migrants.

Full text: [Full Spectrum Policy (ALCP policy page)](documents/full-spectrum-policy.md) ([original](https://www.alcp.org.nz/policy))
