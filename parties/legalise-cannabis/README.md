## History

The ALCP has contested every general election since 1996 and many by-elections without winning a seat. Its best
results were 1.66% (1996) and 1.10% (1999); since then it has stayed below 1%. Former ALCP candidates Nándor Tánczos and
Metiria Turei later became Green MPs (Turei was Green co-leader), and Tim Shadbolt became Invercargill's mayor. Former
deputy leader Abe Gray later joined TOP. [1]

Its policies cover legal possession, growing and use for over-18s, a "medpot" card, taxing cannabis companies,
licence-free hemp growing and funded drug education and research. [1] Cannabis policy changed around it in this
period: medicinal-cannabis access was widened by a December 2018 amendment to the Misuse of Drugs Act, and the 2020
non-binding referendum on recreational legalisation was narrowly defeated. [1][2]
