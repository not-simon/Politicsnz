Every New Zealander should be able to get the care they need, close to home, without facing high costs or long wait times.

Christopher Luxon’s Government is making it harder and more expensive to see a doctor or nurse. GP fees are heading toward $100, forcing too many families to choose between getting care and paying the bills.

When people can’t afford to see a doctor, they stay sicker for longer - putting even more pressure on our already overstretched hospitals.

Labour will make healthcare cheaper and easier to access, close to home. That way, problems are caught early, families stay healthier, and hospitals are there when they matter most.

We will invest in a health system that delivers affordable, quality care - for everyone, when they need it.
