## Disability

Labour will strengthen disability rights, end annual benefit renewals for people with verified permanent disabilities, and require at least 25% of new Kāinga Ora builds to be accessible.

Key facts
- Annual benefit renewals ended for people with verified permanent disabilities
- At least 25% of new Kāinga Ora builds required to be accessible
- Enabling Good Lives ny requiring government agencies to produce impact assessments, targets and actions
- Human Rights Act amended to define reasonable accommodation and recognise that denying it is discrimination
- UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities: Labour will progress incorporating it into law
- Disability Support Services Act repealed, with a rights-based framework developed with disabled people, carers and disabled people's organisations

### Rights and accountability

Labour will:

- Require government agencies to produce Enabling Good Lives impact assessments, targets and actions
- Amend the Human Rights Act to define reasonable accommodation, and recognise that denying it is discrimination
- Progress incorporating the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities into law
- Repeal the Disability Support Services Act and work with disabled people, carers and disabled people's organisations on a rights-based Disability Support Services framework that includes a focus on safeguarding

### Work and income

Labour will work towards systemic change so disabled people can transition into open employment.

Labour will end annual benefit renewals for people with verified permanent disabilities.

### Housing and leadership

Labour will require at least 25% of new Kāinga Ora builds to be accessible.

Labour will work with disability communities to build disabled leadership capacity.
