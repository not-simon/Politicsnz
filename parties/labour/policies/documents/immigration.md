## Immigration

Labour will plan immigration around New Zealand's capacity, protect migrants from exploitation and make it easier for Pacific whānau to reconnect.

Key facts
- Immigration planned around capacity through a Government Policy Statement that reflects workforce demographic, housing and infrastructure needs
- Permanent residence after four years in New Zealand instead of two, supported by orientation and civic education delivered by community education providers
- Protection from exploitation: migrants can move between accredited employers after three months, and the Accredited Employer Work Visa scheme is strengthened
- Humanitarian appeals against deportation protected, and a citizenship pathway for children born in New Zealand
- Reviews of parent visa, health and English-language rules
- Pacific visa-waiver access expanded to citizens from the Pacific Islands Forum nations

### Plan immigration around New Zealand's capacity

Labour will plan immigration around New Zealand's capacity by setting goals through a Government Policy Statement on immigration that reflects our workforce demographic, housing and infrastructure needs.

### Encourage migrants to put down roots

Labour will encourage migrants to put down roots by extending the period migrants must live here before becoming eligible for permanent residence from two years to four.

This will be supported by a programme of New Zealand orientation and civic education delivered by community education providers to strengthen social cohesion.

### Protect migrants from exploitation

Labour will protect migrants from exploitation by:

- Enabling migrants to move between accredited employers after three months
- Strengthening the Accredited Employer Work Visa scheme
- Improving access to support in their communities

See also Labour's Fairer Workplaces policy.

### Treat people with decency

Labour will treat people with decency by:

- Protecting humanitarian appeals against deportation
- Setting up a citizenship pathway for children born in New Zealand
- Reviewing parent visa, health and English-language rules

### Pacific visa-waiver

Labour will expand visa-waiver access to citizens from the Pacific Islands Forum nations, making it easier and more affordable for Pacific whānau to reconnect.

We know that too often, it will fall on families here in New Zealand to sponsor loved ones from the islands for a visit.

Families shouldn't have to choose between meeting their everyday costs and being able to welcome loved ones from the islands to New Zealand.

We recognise New Zealand's special relationship and deep family ties with Pacific countries, and we hope this will bring relief to Pacific families welcoming their loved ones from the islands to our beautiful country.
