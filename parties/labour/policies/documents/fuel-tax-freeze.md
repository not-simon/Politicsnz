## Freeze Fuel Tax

Labour will freeze fuel excise tax for three years, saving an average family $520 a year by 2030.

Key facts
- Fuel excise tax frozen for three years
- Saves an average family $520 a year by 2030
- Stops a planned increase: the current Government intends to increase fuel taxes by 22 cents a litre over the next term, starting soon after the next election
- Helps beyond the pump: higher fuel costs flow through to farmers, truck drivers, tradies, businesses and the price of goods

### What Labour will do

Labour will freeze fuel excise tax for three years, saving an average family $520 a year by 2030.

When families are already paying more at the pump, the last thing they need is Christopher Luxon and National reaching into their pockets for more.

The current Government intends to increase fuel taxes by 22 cents a litre over the next term, starting soon after the next election.

### Why fuel is not a luxury

Fuel is not a luxury. People need it to get to work, get their kids to school, run their businesses and get around their communities.

Higher fuel costs also flow through the entire economy. They mean higher costs for farmers, truck drivers, tradies and businesses, and ultimately higher prices for the goods New Zealanders buy.

Labour will not make that worse with another fuel tax increase that New Zealanders can't afford.
