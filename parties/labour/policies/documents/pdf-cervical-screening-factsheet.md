FREE CERVICAL
SCREENING
With Labour’s Medicard, cervical screening will be free,
and every New Zealander gets three free doctor’s visits
every year.
Cost should never be a barrier to care. Under
Christopher Luxon, it’s become harder and more
expensive to see a doctor. He promised to fix the
cost of living, but it’s getting worse - and there’s no
plan to fix our health system.

Why it matters
Cervical cancer is among the most preventable
cancers - yet 85 percent of those diagnosed have
never been screened or missed screenings.

It doesn’t have to be this way.
Labour will build a health system that keeps
people healthy, not just treating them when
they’re sick.
Affordable, reliable healthcare is the foundation
on which New Zealanders build their lives. Just as
every New Zealander wants the best healthcare
for their family, a Labour Government wants the
best healthcare for every New Zealander.
