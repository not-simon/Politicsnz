## A Government You Can Trust

Labour will make it easier to vote, harder to buy influence, and harder for any government to rush laws through without scrutiny.

Key facts
- Election-day enrolment restored
- 16 and 17 year olds can vote in local elections
- Lobbying: a public register and code of conduct for lobbyists, and former ministers prevented from working as lobbyists immediately after leaving
- Official Information Act (OIA) regime modernised
- Urgency limited to genuine emergencies, with rushed laws sent back to select committee

The reason costs keep going up and jobs keep disappearing is because this Government doesn't think they're accountable to everyday New Zealanders.

When government isn't accountable, ordinary people pay the price. Labour will restore trust in government, so that leaders always put your needs first.

### Easier to vote

Labour will restore election-day enrolment and allow 16 and 17 year olds to vote in local elections, along with other changes.

### Harder to buy influence

Labour will:

- Create a public register and code of conduct for lobbyists
- Prevent former ministers from working as lobbyists immediately after leaving
- Modernise the Official Information Act (OIA) regime

### Scrutiny of new laws

Labour will limit urgency to genuine emergencies and send rushed laws back to select committee.
