A Labour Government is committed to free doctor’s visits,
shorter wait times, and care closer to home. For everyone.
Everyone should be able to see a local doctor when they need to - and

Labour will set up the Family Doctor Loan Scheme to support our local
doctors to set up new practices, or buy into existing ones, by offering lowinterest loans, to secure the future of our family GPs. This is a practical,
targeted way to boost the number of clinics across New Zealand and to
strengthen the ones we already have.

CARE CLOSE TO HOME
Labour’s Medicard will provide three
free doctor’s visits a year to every New
Zealander - a simple commitment that
you and your family can see your local
doctor when you need to. We will also
make cervical cancer screening free for
everyone who needs it, to save lives and
prevent cancers before they develop.
Family doctors are the backbone of our
health system. They’re the first port of call
when people are unwell, taking pressure
off our busy hospitals. But doctors tell us
that owning a practice is not always a
sustainable option - especially for younger
GPs with student loans, starting families,
or trying to buy their first home.
Practices are expensive to establish,
difficult to run, and high interest rates
make taking on that risk even harder.
On top of student loan repayments and a
mortgage, many younger doctors simply
can’t afford to own a clinic - even when
that’s what they’d like to do.
We know we need more clinics, closer
to where people live, run by those
people who know and understand their
communities. Yet the number of owneroperated practices is declining. Without
support for owner-operators, it will get
even harder to see a local doctor. That
means fewer opportunities for doctors to
build businesses in their communities and
fewer options for patients - especially in
smaller towns and rural areas where it is
already difficult to access care.

Providing low-interest loans will back

their careers caring for their local
communities. Delivered through the
existing Small Business Cashflow Loan

EXAMPLE:
Katherine wants to move back to her hometown of Cambridge to buy a house and
be closer to her family. A local doctor in Cambridge is retiring and wants to sell his
practice in 2029. He has had interest from a corporate provider but would like the
practice to remain owner-operated. Katherine would need to borrow $400,000
to buy into the practice and could be paying back upwards of $5,000 a month if
borrowing through a bank. She is reluctant to take on this loan, as she plans to buy
her first home in Cambridge when she moves back, with a mortgage of $500,000
with monthly repayments of $2,500.
With Labour’s Family Doctor Fund, she would be able to borrow the $400,000 to
buy into the practice. If the loan is approved, she would have no repayments and
interest until 2031, and then until 2039 to pay off the loan. This reduces the risk
for Katherine and she’s able to spend the first two years focusing on patients and
ensuring the clinic runs smoothly, rather than worrying about monthly payments.

Helping doctors see more

Labour has worked with health experts
on a practical plan to free up more than
4.5 million GP appointments every year.
These are simple, affordable changes
that will free up doctors’ time so they can
focus on patients, not paperwork.
Here’s how we’ll make sure doctors can
see more patients:

Getting more doctors into local
communities
Targeted funding to expand clinic
space in high-demand areas could
free up 180,000 appointments a year.
Alongside the Family Doctor Fund,
Labour will provide targeted support so
clinics in high-need and fast-growing
areas have enough rooms, facilities, and
staff spaces to see more patients safely
and quickly. This investment in extra
consulting rooms and better-equipped
clinics will help GPs keep up with
demand. It could free up around 180,000
extra doctor’s appointments every year.

Smarter systems that save time
Fund and train general practices
to introduce clinical phone triage
so patients are directed to the right
care - freeing up around 1.9 million
appointments a year.
Support doctors to provide advice for
long-term conditions, saving
1 million appointments.
Right now, many appointments are
allocated on a first-come, first-served
basis, not necessarily by need.
Supporting practices to introduce a
phone triage system will ensure people
seeking same-day care are directed to
the right option.
Where it’s already in place, this approach
has reduced unplanned appointments
and provided timely advice,
prescriptions, and tests. Implemented
more widely, it could free up 1.9 million
appointments a year and streamline
a further 2.9 million visits. If a patient’s
issue is resolved through telehealth
triage, it won’t count towards their three
free doctor’s visits under Medicard.
Labour will also support doctors to give
patients high-quality information for
long-term conditions, reducing lowcomplexity visits. This could free up a
further one million appointments a year.

FIXING GP FUNDING
For years, family doctors have been
funded under a broken model that
underpays practices and forces them
to either raise fees, cut services, or close
their doors.
Labour will introduce independent
pricing - a fair, transparent funding
system where an Independent Pricing
Authority sets a national GP funding rate
based on real costs and patient need.
When that rate goes up, Health NZ will
fund the increase, instead of leaving
practices or patients to carry the cost.

Money that currently comes from
patient co-payments will go into general
practice funding, so clinics aren’t worse
off. This reform will keep care affordable,
back local doctors, and shift our health
system towards prevention - keeping
people well, not just treating them when
they’re sick.
