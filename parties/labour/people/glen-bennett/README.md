## Current status

Bennett has been Labour's senior (chief) whip since 11 March 2025 and is tourism and hospitality spokesperson (No. 21 in the shadow cabinet). He is Labour's Coromandel candidate, No. 25 on the list ([policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/party-vote/parties/labour-party)).

## Biography

Born in Dunedin to Salvation Army officers, Bennett grew up around the country and worked in television production (*Fair Go*, *McPhail and Gadsby*) before more than 20 years in the community sector, including fostering troubled teenage boys ([Taranaki Daily News](https://www.pressreader.com/new-zealand/taranaki-daily-news/20080806/281809984678786)). He married Jon O'Neill at Parliament in February 2021.

### Career summary

- **New Plymouth MP 2020–2023:** unexpectedly unseated National's Jonathan Young by 2,555 votes; lost to National's David MacLeod by 6,991 in 2023 ([2020](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2020/electorate-details-29.html); [2023](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-29.html)).
- Re-entered Parliament on 25 March 2024 as the next on Labour's list when Grant Robertson resigned ([NZ Gazette](https://gazette.govt.nz/notice/id/2024-au1289)); economic development and associate energy spokesperson until March 2025.

## Controversies
