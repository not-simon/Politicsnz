## Current status

Brooking is Labour's environment and RMA reform spokesperson (No. 17 in the shadow cabinet) and is defending Dunedin, ranked 11 on the 2026 list ([policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/party-vote/parties/labour-party)).

## Biography

Born 18 October 1975 and raised in Dunedin (Otago Girls' High School), Brooking holds degrees in ecology and law from the University of Otago and was OUSA president in 1997 after leading student-fees protests ([ODT](https://www.odt.co.nz/news/decision-2020/student-activist-labour-list-mp)). An environmental, resource-management and local-government lawyer, she worked for the Parliamentary Commissioner for the Environment and at Anderson Lloyd, chaired the Otago/Southland branch of the Resource Management Law Association, and in 2019 was appointed to the government's RMA review panel. She is married to oncologist Chris Jackson; they have three children.

### Career summary

- List MP 2020–2023 (ranked 46); deputy chair of the Environment and Regulations Review committees.
- **Minister of Oceans and Fisheries** (outside Cabinet) from 11 April 2023, with associate environment and immigration; added Food Safety after Meka Whaitiri's defection ([ODT](https://www.odt.co.nz/news/dunedin/rise-brooking-cabinet-reshuffle); [RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/489528/hipkins-reallocates-meka-whaitiri-s-portfolios)).
- **Dunedin MP since 2023:** succeeded David Clark, beating National's Michael Woodhouse by 7,980 ([2023 results](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-08.html)).

## Record & notable actions

- With Conservation Minister Willow-Jean Prime announced six new marine reserves between Timaru and the Catlins (October 2023); in 2024 the Department of Conservation delayed their creation ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/election-2023/499457/government-announces-six-new-south-island-marine-reserves); [ODT](https://www.odt.co.nz/news/dunedin/marine-reserve-creation-delayed)).
