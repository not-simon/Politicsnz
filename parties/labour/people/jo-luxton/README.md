## Current status

Luxton is Labour's agriculture, biosecurity and customs spokesperson and its candidate for East Cape (list No. 15 in the shadow cabinet; No. 17 on the 2026 list), having relocated to Gisborne after losing Rangitata in 2023 ([Gisborne Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/gisborne-herald/news/jo-luxton-to-contest-east-cape-for-labour-with-cost-of-living-focus/2GQE6VO46FANTGM7JFTYTQUB5I/); [policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/party-vote/parties/labour-party)).

## Biography

Born in 1973 in Rotorua and raised mainly in Gisborne, Luxton is a great-niece of Prime Minister Norman Kirk. She worked on a dairy farm near Ashburton, then spent 20 years in early childhood education, founding Headstart Early Learning Centre and owning the Hinds Early Learning Centre, a living-wage employer ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/timaru-herald/news/95977455/coffee-with-the-candidate-labour-rangitata-candidate-jo-luxton)). She has spoken about raising children on a single-parent benefit and is guardian of her younger sister, who has Down syndrome ([Newshub](https://www.newshub.co.nz/home/shows/2023/07/newshub-nation-backstory-labour-mp-jo-luxton-describes-struggles-on-single-parent-benefit-guardianship-of-sister.html)).

### Career summary

- List MP 2017–2020 after contesting Rangitata; won Rangitata in 2020 by 4,408 over Megan Hands — a surprise gain in a seat National had held since 2005; lost it to National's James Meager by 10,846 in 2023 and returned on the list ([2020](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2020/electorate-details-40.html); [2023](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-40.html)).
- Chaired the Primary Production Committee (2020–2023); Parliamentary Under-Secretary (Jan 2023); **Minister of Customs and Associate Minister of Agriculture and Education (ECE)** from 8 May 2023 after Meka Whaitiri's defection ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/131980845/mp-jo-luxton-promoted-in-wake-of-meka-whaitiris-labour-exit)).

## Record & notable actions

- Oversaw legislation enabling digital arrival cards (2023) and announced ECE pay-parity increases in August 2023; RNZ later reported officials had identified a $253 million ECE funding shortfall before the election that was not made public until afterwards ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/502957/pay-parity-for-ece-teachers-further-out-of-reach-after-cost-blowout)).
- **Conscience votes:** voted for the Abortion Legislation Bill (2019–2020); said she would vote yes in the euthanasia referendum and no in the cannabis referendum (2020) ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/timaru-herald/news/122785414/labour-mp-jo-luxton-against-cannabis-legalisation)).
