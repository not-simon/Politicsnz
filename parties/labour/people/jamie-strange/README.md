## Current status

Strange was elected to Hamilton City Council for the East ward in the 2025 local elections ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/568837/two-former-labour-mps-vie-for-seats-on-hamilton-city-council)).

## Biography

Born in Nelson in 1976 and raised in Hamilton, Strange was a music teacher, former church minister and rock musician (album *Thanks for Faking It Sometimes*, 2007).

### Career summary

- **List MP 2017–2020; MP for Hamilton East 2020–2023**, defeating National's David Bennett by 2,973 votes ([Electoral Commission](https://electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2020/electorate-details-12.html)).
- Chaired the Economic Development, Science and Innovation Committee (2020–2023).
- Announced in December 2022 that he would not stand again, citing family strain ([1News](https://www.1news.co.nz/2022/12/13/six-labour-mps-including-david-clark-to-retire-at-election/)).

## Record & notable actions

- Advocated for the Hamilton–Auckland Te Huia rail service (opened 2021).
- Socially conservative voting record: against the End of Life Choice Act 2019, the Abortion Legislation Act 2020 and abortion safe areas (2022) ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/roe-v-wade-abortion-decision-nzs-labour-mps-explain-their-votes-against-reform/OJMNBNKCSHCXC7SQSDRT7UYP6A/)).
