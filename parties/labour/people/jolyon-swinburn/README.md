## Current status

Labour candidate for Kaipara ki Mahurangi (electorate only, not on the party list) at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography

Swinburn, who campaigns as Jo Swinburn, is a principal policy advisor at the Ministry for Cities, Environment, Regions and Transport. His Labour profile says he worked previously at MBIE and as a senior Treasury analyst on the Sovereign Green Bonds programme and Public Finance Act reform, and co-founded a coffee business. ([Labour candidate profile](https://www.labour.org.nz/2026-campaign/our-candidates/jo-swinburn/), self-description; occupation also from the policy.nz questionnaire)
