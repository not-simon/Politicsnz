## Current status

Utikere is No. 11 in the shadow cabinet; in March 2026 he became Deputy Shadow Leader of the House and added SOEs to transport, local government and racing ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/politics/360949687/hipkins-reshuffles-labour-portfolios-henare-departs-and-plan-hatched-maori-seats-race)). He is defending Palmerston North at No. 14 on the list ([policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/party-vote/parties/labour-party)).

## Biography

Born and educated in Palmerston North, of Cook Islands descent, Utikere has a BA in politics and social policy and a secondary teaching diploma from Massey University and taught history at Freyberg High School; he is a JP and marriage celebrant. He was appointed to the Criminal Cases Review Commission in 2020 ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/manawatu-standard/news/64563229/mayoral-hopeful-tangi-utikere-pitches-values-over-promises)).

### Career summary

- Palmerston North city councillor from 2010 and deputy mayor 2016–2020 (the first non-Pākehā in the role); ran second for mayor in 2015.
- **Palmerston North MP since 2020:** majorities of 12,508 (2020) and 3,087 (2023) ([2020](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2020/electorate-details-36.html); [2023](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-36.html)).
- Chair of the Health Select Committee (2022); Chief Government Whip from January 2023 and Labour's chief whip in opposition until March 2025.

## Record & notable actions

- His member's bill requiring local-council elected members to publicly register their pecuniary interests became law in May 2022 ([Parliament](https://www.parliament.nz/en/pb/bills-and-laws/bills-proposed-laws/document/BILL_111983/local-government-pecuniary-interests-register-amendment)).
