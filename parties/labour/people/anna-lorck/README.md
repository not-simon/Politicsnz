## Current status

Lorck lost Tukituki to National's Catherine Wedd by 10,118 votes in 2023 and came last in the 2024 Hawke's Bay Power Consumers Trust election ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/hawkes-bay-today/news/hawkes-bay-power-consumers-trust-election-four-out-of-five-trustees-retain-their-seat/4US7GZYFLFBCRCCSPU65YK6SOM/)).

## Biography

Raised in Waipukurau, Lorck trained as a journalist and ran a public relations company in Hastings. She was elected to the Hawke's Bay DHB in 2019.

### Career summary

- Contested Tukituki in 2014, 2017 and 2020; **MP for Tukituki 2020–2023**, unseating National's Lawrence Yule by 1,590 votes as an electorate-only candidate ([Electoral Commission](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2020/electorate-details-54.html)).

## Controversies

- In 2022 two former staff members alleged bullying; Lorck said she had not received complaints from them while they were employed and that she was working with a leadership coach ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/474810/labour-mp-anna-lorck-in-leadership-training-after-bullying-accusations)).
- A 2017 complaint to the Advertising Standards Authority over billboards reading "Your Local MP" when she was not the MP ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/95099736/complaints-made-over-billboard-that-appears-to-say-candidate-is-local-mp-when-she-is-not)).
