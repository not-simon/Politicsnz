## Current status

Omer is a list-only Labour candidate for 2026, ranked 31. He lost Wellington Central to the Green Party's Tamatha Paul by 6,066 votes in 2023 ([Electoral Commission](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-60.html)).

## Biography

Born in Eritrea around 1979–1980, Omer fled as a teenager to Sudan, where he worked as an interpreter in UN refugee camps, and arrived in New Zealand under the refugee quota in 2008. He worked as a cleaner at Victoria University of Wellington while studying for a BA in political science (2016), then as an E tū union organiser and chair of the ChangeMakers Resettlement Forum ([Victoria University](https://www.wgtn.ac.nz/news/2019/08/ibrahim-omer-alumnus)).

### Career summary

- **List MP 2020–2023** (ranked 42 in 2020); New Zealand's first MP from Africa ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/123129026/incoming-labour-list-mp-ibrahim-omer-says-its-a-privilege-to-be-first-the-african-mp)).
- Won the 2023 Wellington Central nomination after Grant Robertson went list-only.

## Record & notable actions

- His Crimes (Theft by Employer) Amendment Bill (introduced 2023), making an employer's deliberate withholding of wages a crime, passed its third reading in March 2025 under Camilla Belich with Labour, Green, Te Pāti Māori and NZ First support ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/544621/theft-is-theft-bill-making-employers-theft-of-workers-wages-a-crime-passes)).

## Controversies

- During the 2023 campaign he suggested Labour had not abandoned a capital gains tax; Chris Hipkins said he was wrong ([Newshub](https://www.newshub.co.nz/home/politics/2023/09/election-2023-chris-hipkins-says-ibrahim-omer-was-wrong-to-suggest-labour-hasn-t-given-up-on-wealth-cgt.html)).
