## Current status

Sosene is No. 31 in the shadow cabinet (internal affairs, statistics) and is defending Māngere at No. 42 on the list ([policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/party-vote/parties/labour-party)).

## Biography

Born in South Auckland on 14 April 1965 to Samoan parents (her father was a founding minister of the EFKS church in Ōtara), Sosene lives in Favona with her husband Afoataga ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/auckland/local-news/manukau-courier/96769239/getting-candid-with--lemauga-lydia-sosene)). A Labour member since 2000, she was elected to the Māngere-Ōtāhuhu Local Board in 2010 and chaired it from 2013 to 2022.
