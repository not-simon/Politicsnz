## Current status

Williams retired at the 2023 election, saying Christchurch East needed "fresh eyes and fresh energy" ([1News](https://www.1news.co.nz/2022/12/13/six-labour-mps-including-david-clark-to-retire-at-election/)). Reuben Davidson succeeded her in Christchurch East. No reliable source was found for her activities since 2023.

## Biography

Born 7 January 1962 in Wellington to Cook Islands parents and raised in Auckland (Auckland Girls' Grammar), Williams holds an MBA from Southern Cross University. She worked on family and sexual violence prevention, for the Ministry of Education, BirthRight, HealthCare NZ and disability agencies, and moved to Christchurch in 2013 as regional manager of St John of God Hauora Trust ([Stuff](http://www.stuff.co.nz/the-press/news/the-east/9407247/Chch-East-candidate-Poto-Williams)).

### Career summary

- **Christchurch East MP 2013–2023:** won the November 2013 by-election (after Lianne Dalziel became mayor) with 61% of the vote; majorities of 4,073 (2014), 7,480 (2017) and 17,336 (2020) ([2020](https://electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2020/electorate-details-06.html)).
- Assistant Speaker (2017–2019); minister outside Cabinet from June 2019 (Community and Voluntary Sector).
- **Cabinet 2020–2023:** Minister of Police and Building and Construction (Nov 2020 – Jun 2022); Conservation and Disability Issues (from 13 June 2022) ([DPMC 2020](https://dpmc.govt.nz/sites/default/files/2020-11/ministerial-list-2-nov-2020.pdf)).

## Record & notable actions

- **Conscience votes:** for the Abortion Legislation Act (2020); against the End of Life Choice Act (2019).

## Controversies

- **Police portfolio (2021–2022):** came under sustained pressure amid gang violence in Auckland; Christopher Luxon called for her removal, and Ardern moved her out of Police in June 2022, acknowledging "focus" had been lost ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/468706/christopher-luxon-urges-poto-williams-to-be-removed-as-police-minister); [NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/cabinet-reshuffle-jacinda-ardern-announces-shake-up-trevor-mallard-kris-faafoi-depart-poto-williams-replaced-as-police-minister/XZLPPSICJ6E5SOKXERROGRSCM4)).
- In 2017 she suggested removing the presumption of innocence for those accused of sexual assault (Stuff](http://www.stuff.co.nz/national/crime/91913865/police-are-telling-rape-victims-their-hands-are-tied-if-the-accused-denies-it)).
