## Current status

Labour candidate for Hauraki-Waikato, ranked 22 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography

Kiriona is a former TVNZ journalist, a tutor of the kapa haka group Te Iti Kahurangi and an advocate of the Kīngitanga. His Labour profile says he was appointed to the Waitangi Tribunal in 2025, was deputy chair of Te Māngai Pāho and a director of Whakaata Māori, co-founded the reo Māori provider TupuOra, and was deputy chief executive (Mātauranga Māori) of Te Aka Whai Ora. ([Labour candidate profile](https://www.labour.org.nz/kingikiriona), self-description; occupation also from the policy.nz questionnaire)
