## Biography

Perinpanayagam was born in Sri Lanka; his parents fled the civil war and settled in New Zealand in the 1980s. His Labour profile says he served overseas as a UN peacekeeper and for four years as an adviser to UN Secretary-General António Guterres, holds a Harvard master's, passed the New York Bar, and works as an immigration lawyer in South Auckland. ([Labour candidate profile](https://www.labour.org.nz/nareshperinpanayagam), self-description; occupation also from the policy.nz questionnaire)
