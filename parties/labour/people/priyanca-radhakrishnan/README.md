## Current status

Radhakrishnan holds conservation, disability issues and (since March 2026) ACC in Labour's shadow cabinet, and is contesting Maungakiekie at No. 18 on the list ([policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/party-vote/parties/labour-party)). In April 2026 her member's bill to criminalise virginity testing and hymenoplasty was drawn from the ballot ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/indonz/593877/virginity-testing-ban-bill-drawn-from-ballot)).

## Biography

Born in 1979 in Chennai, India, to a Malayali family and raised in Singapore, Radhakrishnan studied at Victoria University of Wellington (master's in development studies, with a thesis on forced marriage in New Zealand) and worked as a social worker in Auckland's Indian community ([Indian Weekender](https://www.indianweekender.co.nz/Pages/ArticleDetails/15/4694/Profile/Politics-runs-in-my-blood-Priyanca-Radhakrishnan/)). She joined Labour in 2006. She received India's Pravasi Bharatiya Samman award in 2021 ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/suriname-president-curacao-pm-nz-minister-among-30-pravasi-bharatiya-samman-awardees/article33537728.ece)).

### Career summary

- Narrowly missed election from list No. 23 in 2014; list MP from 2017 (ranked 12).
- **Maungakiekie MP 2020–2023:** won by 635 on special votes after trailing on election night; lost to National's Greg Fleming by 4,617 in 2023 and returned on the list ([2020](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2020/electorate-details-23.html); [2023](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-23.html)).
- **Minister 2020–2023:** Community and Voluntary Sector; Diversity, Inclusion and Ethnic Communities; Youth; associate social development and employment — New Zealand's first minister of Indian origin; promoted into Cabinet in June 2022 ([Beehive list 2020](https://www.beehive.govt.nz/sites/default/files/2020-11/Ministerial%20List%20announcement%202%20November%202020.pdf)).
