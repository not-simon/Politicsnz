## Current status

Sepuloni has been Labour's deputy leader since 7 November 2023 ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/501872/labour-votes-to-keep-chris-hipkins-as-leader-carmel-sepuloni-takes-over-as-deputy)) and is No. 2 on Labour's 2026 list, contesting the Glendene electorate ([policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/party-vote/parties/labour-party)). Since the March 2025 reshuffle her portfolios have been Pacific Peoples, Auckland Issues and Women; she gave up social development and child poverty reduction ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/544048/chris-hipkins-announces-new-labour-economic-team-tangi-utikere-promoted)).

## Biography

Born in 1977 and raised in Waitara, Taranaki, Sepuloni is of Samoan, Tongan and Pākehā descent — her father was a migrant freezing worker and unionist — and she was New Zealand's first MP of Tongan descent ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/131030608/who-is-the-new-deputy-prime-minister-carmel-sepuloni); [RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/national/programmes/morningreport/audio/2018874642/carmel-sepuloni-makes-history-as-first-pasifika-deputy-pm)). She trained as a primary teacher (Auckland College of Education; BEd, University of Auckland; PGDipEd), taught in Samoa and in alternative education in Auckland, and worked in Pacific health research at the University of Auckland. Between her two stints in Parliament she was chief executive of Vaka Tautua, a Pacific disability, mental-health and social-services provider ([Beehive biography](https://www.beehive.govt.nz/minister/biography/carmel-sepuloni)). She has two sons and married writer and musician Daren Kamali in 2018.

### Career summary

- **List MP, 2008–2011.** Entered at list position 35; civil defence spokesperson. Her Employment Relations (Probationary Period Repeal) Amendment Bill, to repeal the 90-day trial period, was defeated 64–57 at first reading in 2010 ([Scoop](http://business.scoop.co.nz/2010/08/05/member%E2%80%99s-bill-to-remove-90-days-voted-down/)).
- **2011 Waitakere loss.** On official results she beat Paula Bennett by 11 votes, but a judicial recount gave Bennett a 9-vote majority and Sepuloni left Parliament ([NZ Herald](http://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/news/article.cfm?c_id=1&objectid=10773368)).
- **Kelston, 2014–present.** Majorities of 15,091 (2014), 16,789 (2017), 15,660 (2020) and 4,396 (2023) ([2014](http://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2014/electorate-21.html), [2020](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2020/electorate-details-19.html), [2023](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-19.html)).
- **Minister, 2017–2023.** Minister for Social Development throughout the Sixth Labour Government; also Disability Issues (2017–2022), ACC (2020–2023), Employment, Arts, Culture and Heritage (2020–2023), and, under Hipkins, Associate Foreign Affairs (Pacific), Workplace Relations and Safety and Minister for Auckland (from June 2023) ([DPMC 2020](https://dpmc.govt.nz/sites/default/files/2020-11/ministerial-list-2-nov-2020.pdf); [NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/prime-minister-chris-hipkins-reveals-michael-woods-future/EFRLIOY63FE3BCFXFTXFX3OXGQ/)).
- **Deputy Prime Minister, 25 Jan – 27 Nov 2023** — the first Pasifika person and third woman in the role ([1News](https://www.1news.co.nz/2023/01/22/the-girl-from-waitara-carmel-sepuloni-makes-history/)).

## Record & notable actions

- As social development minister she led the government's welfare overhaul, which she likened to "trying to turn a jumbo jet in mid-air" ([Newshub](https://www.newshub.co.nz/home/shows/2018/04/welfare-overhaul-to-get-underway-in-next-three-years-carmel-sepuloni.html)); main benefits were increased during her tenure (see [Newshub](https://www.newshub.co.nz/home/politics/2020/04/25-benefit-increase-making-a-difference-for-beneficiaries-during-lockdown-carmel-sepuloni.html)).
- Represented New Zealand alongside National's Gerry Brownlee at the November 2023 Pacific Islands Forum during the caretaker period ([Newshub](https://www.newshub.co.nz/home/politics/2023/11/pacific-islands-forum-carmel-sepuloni-gerry-brownlee-arrive-in-rarotonga.html)).

## Controversies

- In 2015 she was temporarily stood down as social development spokesperson after her mother was charged with benefit fraud; her mother was later sentenced to home detention for receiving about $34,000 in benefits illegally. No wrongdoing by Sepuloni was alleged ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/national/270065/sepuloni%27s-mother-sentenced-for-benefit-fraud)).
