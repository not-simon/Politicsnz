## Current status

Roberts is Labour's 2026 candidate for Whanganui, ranked 46 (policy.nz candidate data).

## Biography

A teacher of economics and drama for 20 years, including at Stratford High School, Roberts was president of the PPTA (2013–2017), clashing with Education Minister Hekia Parata over Novopay and charter schools ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/417455/former-ppta-president-angela-roberts-to-run-for-labour)).
