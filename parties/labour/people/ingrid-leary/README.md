## Current status

Leary is No. 30 in the shadow cabinet (seniors, mental health) and is defending Taieri at No. 39 on the list ([policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/party-vote/parties/labour-party)).

## Biography

Leary attended Macleans College and studied law at Otago. She worked as a lawyer, press secretary to National MP Maurice Williamson, journalism lecturer (helping found the University of the South Pacific's journalism school in 1997), TV producer — receiving the NZ Special Service Medal for broadcasting after the 2004 tsunami in Aceh — and director of the British Council New Zealand (2008–2020) ([ODT](https://www.odt.co.nz/news/dunedin/labour-picks-dunedin-south-seat-candidate)). She lives in Dunedin and has three children.

### Career summary

- **Taieri MP since 2020:** majorities of 12,398 (2020) and 1,443 (2023) ([2020](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2020/electorate-details-47.html); [2023](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-47.html)).
- Joined the Inter-Parliamentary Alliance on China and co-founded its Indo-Pacific chapter (2022); inherited Louisa Wall's journalists' sources bill but withdrew it in 2022 over drafting problems.

## Controversies

- **Mongrel Mob meeting (July 2023):** attended a Dunedin meeting organised by Mongrel Mob figure Harry Tam on Māori roll-switching; she said she believed it was an Electoral Commission event; Hipkins called it miscommunication, National's Mark Mitchell disputed her account ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/132472301/mps-mongrel-mob-meeting-attendance-a-case-of-miscommunication--pm)).
- **Tanya Unkovich remark (2024):** apologised in the House after calling NZ First MP Tanya Unkovich a "known anti-trans activist" at a select committee ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/politics/labour-mp-ingrid-leary-apologises-to-nz-first-mp-tanya-unkovich/3SA35DXUMNFVXGAXM6AV2D3VBM/)).
- **I Am Hope/Gumboot Friday (2024–25):** called for a pause to government funding of Mike King's charity; the Auditor-General's review in December 2025 found the contract properly managed and King accused her of targeting the charity; she defended her scrutiny ([ODT](https://www.odt.co.nz/news/national/leary-defends-i-am-hope-criticism-read-letter)).
- **ChatGPT email (Dec 2025):** admitted using ChatGPT to draft questions to Seniors Minister Casey Costello after initially suggesting a staffer had sent the email ([ODT](https://www.odt.co.nz/news/dunedin/leary-admits-chatgpt-email-blunder)).
