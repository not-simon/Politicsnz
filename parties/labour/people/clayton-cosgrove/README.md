## Current status

Cosgrove retired at the 2017 election and has worked as a consultant and lobbyist, including for the proposed Foulden Maar mine and on the Greymouth Hospital project ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/business/industries/112694120/former-labour-mp-a-lobbyist-for-firm-planning-to-mine-fossilrich-nz-land)).

## Biography

Born 31 October 1969 in Nelson, Cosgrove holds a BA and an MBA from the University of Canterbury and worked in corporate affairs in the minerals and telecommunications industries. A Labour member from age 14, he was a supporter of Mike Moore.

### Career summary

- **Waimakariri MP 1999–2011**; lost the seat to National's Kate Wilkinson in 2011 and to Matt Doocey in 2014, remaining a list MP until 2017 ([2014](http://electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2014/electorate-57.html)).
- **Minister 2005–2008:** Building and Construction, Statistics, then Immigration, Small Business, Sport and Recreation and the Rugby World Cup; associate finance and justice.
- Opposition spokesperson including Canterbury earthquake recovery (2013); regarded as on Labour's right ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/labour-mp-cosgrove-wont-stand-again/UIV2JYFFULDEBNBDKC3DX7C2VA/)).

## Record & notable actions

- Campaigned against boy-racer behaviour in Canterbury (2007) and was targeted by boy racers in response ([Stuff](http://www.stuff.co.nz/national/16528)).
- Sponsored the Keep Kiwibank member's bill, which David Parker took over for its 2016 second reading, when it was defeated ([Parliament Hansard](https://www.parliament.nz/en/pb/hansard-debates/rhr/combined/HansDeb_20160629_20160629_24)).
