## Current status

Labour candidate for Napier, ranked 50 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography

Hedley is a doctor training as a public health medicine specialist in Hawke's Bay. His Labour profile says he went to Napier Boys' High School, studied biomedical sciences and medicine, worked in general and ENT surgery as a resident medical officer, and spent six years on the national executive of the Resident Doctors' Association, three as vice-president. ([Labour candidate profile](https://www.labour.org.nz/alexhedley), self-description; occupation also from the policy.nz questionnaire)
