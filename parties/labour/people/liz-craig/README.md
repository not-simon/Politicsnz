## Current status

Craig lost Invercargill to National's Penny Simmonds in 2023 and her list place was too low for re-election ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/southland-times/300989132/labours-liz-craig-to-leave-parliament)). No reliable source was found for her activities in 2026.

## Biography

Born in 1967 and educated in New Plymouth, Craig is a public health physician who directed the NZ Child and Youth Epidemiology Service at the University of Otago and helped compile the annual Child Poverty Monitor.

### Career summary

- **List MP 2017–2023**; contested Clutha-Southland (2014) and Invercargill (2017, 2020 — lost by 224 votes — and 2023).
- Chaired the Health Committee (2020–2022), then parliamentary private secretary for Health ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/300579385/labour-mp-liz-craig-appointed-new-parliamentary-private-secretary-for-health-new-role-supporting-health-ministers)).
