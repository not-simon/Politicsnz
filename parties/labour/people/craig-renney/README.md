## Current status

Renney was selected in November 2025 as Labour's candidate for Wellington Bays ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/579750/labour-selects-ctu-economist-craig-renney-as-wellington-bays-candidate)) and is ranked 51. Hipkins ruled out him replacing Barbara Edmonds as finance spokesperson ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/politics/360897437/will-craig-renney-push-out-barbara-edmonds-labours-finance-role)).

## Biography

Born in 1979 in Cramlington, Northumberland, England, Renney studied at Stirling and Northumbria universities and worked for the North East Assembly and the Audit Commission. He moved to Wellington in 2012 and worked at Treasury, MBIE and the Reserve Bank ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/130362206/craig-renney-the-union-leader-with-grant-robertsons-ear)).

## Political career

### Career summary

- Researcher for Grant Robertson from 2015, then his senior economic adviser as Finance Minister (2017–2020), working on the 2018–2020 Budgets and the COVID-19 response.
- Economist and policy director at the NZ Council of Trade Unions from 2020; elected to Labour's policy council after 2023, campaigning for tax changes.
- Author of *The Good Economy* (Bridget Williams Books, December 2025), which proposes new taxes on electricity generators and high-profit supermarkets ([The Post](https://www.thepost.co.nz/business/360930259/commentator-labour-candidate-craig-renney-spells-out-big-state-vision)).

## Record & notable actions

- In 2024 Treasury excluded him from a HYEFU lock-up. A later restriction on the CTU and non-profits for the 2025 Budget lock-up was reversed after feedback ([The Post](https://www.thepost.co.nz/politics/360689535/treasury-reverses-restriction-budget-lock-accepts-ctu-economist)).
