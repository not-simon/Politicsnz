## Current status

Lewis lost Whanganui to National's Carl Bates by 5,512 votes in 2023 ([Electoral Commission](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-62.html)). No reliable source was found for her activities in 2026.

## Biography

Raised in Whanganui and Waverley, Lewis studied law at Victoria University and worked at ACC (as a union delegate) and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.
