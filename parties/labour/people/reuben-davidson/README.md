## Current status

Davidson is No. 13 in the shadow cabinet and a member of Labour's economic team; he added economic development in March 2026 ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/politics/360949687/hipkins-reshuffles-labour-portfolios-henare-departs-and-plan-hatched-maori-seats-race)). In January 2026 he announced Labour's proposed levy on overseas streaming services to fund local screen production ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/584114/election-2026-labour-proposes-game-changing-streaming-levy-to-fund-local-productions)), and he has drafted a member's bill making social-media platforms responsible for restricting harmful content ([The Spinoff](https://thespinoff.co.nz/society/16-12-2025/what-nz-can-learn-from-australias-under-16s-social-media-ban)). He is defending Christchurch East at No. 21 ([policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/party-vote/parties/labour-party)).

## Biography

Born in Christchurch and a graduate of the New Zealand Broadcasting School, Davidson spent over 15 years as a television producer at Whitebait Media, producing *What Now* and creating *Fanimals* ([ODT](https://www.odt.co.nz/star-news/star-districts/star-selwyn/lyttelton-resident-vying-selwyn-seat-september-election)). He was elected to the Banks Peninsula Community Board in 2019 and chaired it from 2022. He lives in North New Brighton.
