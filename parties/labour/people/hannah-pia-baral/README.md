## Current status

Labour candidate for Upper Harbour, ranked 45 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography

Baral arrived in Auckland from the Philippines in 1989 and holds a BA and a master's in politics from the University of Auckland and an MBA. Her Labour profile says she held senior leadership and CEO roles in Australia, returned in 2022, and is chief executive of Adult and Community Education (ACE) Aotearoa. ([Labour candidate profile](https://www.labour.org.nz/2026-campaign/our-candidates/hannah-pia-baral/), self-description; occupation also from the policy.nz questionnaire)
