## Biography

Boynton (Ngāi Tūhoe, Tamakaimoana, Ngāti Awa, Te Rarawa, Ngāti Kahu) is a Māori ward councillor on the Whakatāne District Council. Her Labour profile says she has worked in social services, housing and community development.
