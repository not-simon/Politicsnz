## Current status

O'Connor announced on 26 January 2026 that he would not contest West Coast-Tasman again and sought the Waitaki nomination instead ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/585014/damien-o-connor-not-standing-in-west-coast-tasman-at-next-election); [ODT](https://www.odt.co.nz/regions/north-otago/o%E2%80%99connor-keen-seek-seat)); he is Labour's Waitaki candidate at No. 16 on the list ([policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/party-vote/parties/labour-party)). He gained the defence portfolio in March 2026 ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/politics/360949687/hipkins-reshuffles-labour-portfolios-henare-departs-and-plan-hatched-maori-seats-race)).

## Biography

Born 16 January 1958 in Westport, O'Connor attended St Bede's College, Christchurch and Lincoln University, worked in farming and tourism (including five years in Australia) and founded Buller Adventure Tours. He was West Coast Young Farmer of the Year ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/business/farming/98215758/coaster-and-new-minister-damien-oconnor-has-farming-in-his-blood)). He has four children from his marriage to Vicky (separated 2004) and a daughter with partner Sharon Flood; former Labour MP Greg O'Connor is his cousin.

### Career summary

- **MP since 1993** (West Coast, then West Coast-Tasman 1996–2008); lost the seat in 2008 by 971 votes, returned as a list MP in 2009 when Michael Cullen retired, regained the seat in 2011 and held it until 2023 (majority 6,208 over Maureen Pugh in 2020), when Pugh beat him by 1,017; returned on the list ([2020](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2020/electorate-details-61.html); [2023](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-61.html)).
- **Fifth Labour Government:** Minister for Racing (2003), Corrections and Tourism (2005); lost Corrections in 2007.
- **Sixth Labour Government:** Minister of Agriculture, Biosecurity, Food Safety and Rural Communities (2017–2023); Trade and Export Growth and Land Information (2020–2023).
- **Opposition:** trade spokesperson; represented New Zealand at the 2023 APEC summit during coalition talks ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/new-zealand-prime-minister-elect-will-not-attend-apec-government-says-2023-11-14/)).

## Record & notable actions

- **Mycoplasma bovis:** chose to attempt nationwide eradication of the cattle disease from 2018 ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/programmes/the-detail/story/2018843959/the-fight-against-mycoplasma-bovis)).
- **Trade:** concluded the NZ–EU free trade agreement (2022, signed July 2023) and facilitated the WTO fisheries-subsidies agreement at MC12 ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/politics/new-zealand-wins-18b-trade-deal-with-eu-at-eleventh-hour/JMOMOJUUSR3RT2T27YWACDOFY4/); [Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/129021436/new-zealand-claims-pleasing-result-at-world-trade-organisation-meeting)); walked out of the 2022 APEC trade ministers' meeting when Russia's minister spoke.
- Received a 60,000-signature petition in 2024 opposing the reversal of Labour's ban on live-animal exports by sea.
- **Conscience votes:** one of four Labour MPs to vote against marriage equality (2012–13); voted against euthanasia bills (1995, 2003, 2019) and against the Abortion Legislation Bill at later readings (2020) ([NZ Herald](http://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/news/article.cfm?c_id=1&objectid=10830383)). Crossed the floor in 2014 to support the West Coast Windblown Timber Bill.

## Controversies

- **"Gaggle of gays" (2011):** described Labour's list selection as run by "self-serving unionists and a gaggle of gays"; he apologised ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/4870142/Labour-MP-leaves-list-to-gaggle-of-gays)).
- **Corrections (2006–2007):** faced resignation calls after the murder of Liam Ashley in a prison van and for taking a suspended prison officer on a parliamentary rugby tour; lost the portfolio in 2007 ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/news/article.cfm?c_id=1&objectid=10462535)).
- **China comments (2021):** told CNBC that Australia "should follow us and show respect" to China, drawing criticism in Australia ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/124083238/trade-minister-damien-oconnor-says-australia-should-follow-us-and-show-respect-to-china-causing-transtasman-tension)).
- **Gaza (2023–24):** called Israel's actions in Gaza a genocide in December 2023, which Hipkins said was not party policy at the time; later faced calls to apologise for sharing a video containing a claim that Palestinians had a right to do what they did on 7 October; the post was taken down ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/504190/labour-s-damien-o-connor-goes-against-party-policy-in-calling-israel-s-actions-in-gaza-genocide); [RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/politics/529450/call-for-apology-from-labour-mp-after-social-media-post-taken-down)).
