## Current status

Former MP for Botany (2011–2020). Not a 2026 candidate. Filed under Advance New Zealand, his most recent party (deregistered 2021).

## Biography

Born 1985 and raised by his grandmother in Manukau (Ngāti Porou descent through a father he never met); attended Dilworth School and Pakuranga College and holds a commercial pilot's licence. Elected to Manukau City Council at 18 (2004) and to the new Auckland Council in 2010 (Citizens & Ratepayers co-leader). Formerly married to Lucy Schwaner; two children.

## Political career

- Won the March 2011 Botany by-election for National (becoming the youngest MP) and held the seat in 2011, 2014 and 2017; a leading party fundraiser; junior whip from 2014 and Chief Government Whip in 2017.
- Ranked 8th in Simon Bridges' shadow cabinet (transport, infrastructure) in 2018.
- In October 2018, after an inquiry named him as the leaker of Bridges' travel expenses, he quit National (and was expelled), alleged Bridges had broken electoral donation law, and released a secretly recorded conversation with Bridges. He remained as an independent MP.
- Founded **Advance New Zealand** in 2020 and allied with Billy Te Kahika's conspiracy-theory NZ Public Party; it won no seats and was deregistered in 2021.

## Record & notable actions

- Called in 2019 for a ban on donations from non-citizens; voted for the End of Life Choice Act (2019), the Abortion Legislation Act (2020) and marriage equality (2013).
- Member's bill against roadside windscreen washers (2017), later folded into a government bill.

## Controversies

- **Harassment and bullying:** in October 2018 four women told Newsroom of bullying, rages and harassment; a February 2020 Parliamentary Service investigation substantiated complaints of a "toxic environment" and sexualised comments. Ross denied wrongdoing and called it politically motivated. He was hospitalised after a mental-health crisis in October 2018.
- **Donations trial (2020–2022):** charged by the Serious Fraud Office over the splitting of a $100,000+ donation to National from Zhang Yikun; in 2022 Zhang and two others were convicted but Ross was acquitted because the judge could not exclude that he had lied to the SFO to damage Bridges. Bridges was never charged.
- **COVID-19 misinformation (2020):** Parliament's Privileges Committee unanimously found Advance NZ's doctored parliamentary video falsely suggesting compulsory vaccination broke the rules; Ross called the committee a "kangaroo court".
- Later business ventures included selling a product claimed to protect against electromagnetic radiation and running an escort agency.
