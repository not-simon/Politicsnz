## Current status

Vision NZ candidate for **Ōtāhuhu** and **#4** on its list in 2026. Never an MP.

## Biography

Of Samoan and Māori (Ngāpuhi, Ngāti Raukawa) descent, from Ōtara, with four children. He survived abuse in state care and gave evidence to the Royal Commission of Inquiry into Abuse in Care.

## Political career

- Stood for Vision NZ in Panmure-Ōtāhuhu in 2023 and came fifth of six (1.3%).
- Stood unsuccessfully as an independent in the 2026 Ōtara-Papatoetoe Local Board by-election.

## Controversies

- Widely reported for heckling senior politicians, including Chris Hipkins, Christopher Luxon, David Seymour and Chris Bishop. He rejects the "serial heckler" label and says he speaks for whānau without a voice.
- Interjected from the public gallery during the November 2024 state-care apology and was removed by security. He was also removed from a 2025 Tāmaki Makaurau by-election debate.
