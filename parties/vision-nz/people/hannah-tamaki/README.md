## Current status

Leader of Vision NZ, **#1** on its 2026 list and candidate for **Papakura**. Never an MP. (policy.nz questionnaire: age 65, lives in Papakura.)

## Biography

Born Hannah Lee in Tokoroa in 1960, of European and Māori descent. Married Brian Tamaki in 1980; together they founded the Pentecostal Destiny Church (from 1998 in Auckland), where she leads women's ministries. Appointed a Justice of the Peace in 2019.

## Political career

- Supported the founding of Destiny New Zealand (2003; 0.62% in 2005).
- Launched "Coalition New Zealand" in May 2019 as leader; the Electoral Commission declined the name, and it was registered as Vision NZ in October 2019.
- 2020: Vision NZ won 0.1%; she came third in Waiariki.
- 2023: Vision NZ ran within Brian Tamaki's Freedoms NZ umbrella (0.33%); she came fifth in Tāmaki Makaurau.
- Contested the 2025 Tāmaki Makaurau by-election (146 votes, preliminary).

## Record & notable actions

- Vision NZ ran on socially conservative positions (abortion, homosexuality, immigration) alongside Māori economic-autonomy policies. She opposed building new mosques and later backed away from calls to ban immigration and refugees.

## Controversies

- In 2021 she travelled out of Auckland under Alert Level 2 restrictions, which drew criticism, and she addressed anti-lockdown protests. Charges against her for breaching COVID-19 orders were dismissed in November 2024.
- Her 2011 bid for the Māori Women's Welfare League presidency involved Destiny-formed branches. The High Court excluded some of them, and the League later banned her from regional office for three years.
