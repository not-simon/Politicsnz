# OUR POLICIES

## Strong Families Build Strong Nations

## Empower Parents, Not Politics

We believe that the family is the ultimate cornerstone of society and the primary incubator for our nation's morals, which is why we will dismantle state dependency, strip away government over-reach, and restore parental authority to its rightful place.

Policy Commitments

\

- Establish a dedicated Ministry for Families to act as a watchdog against state over-reach and to champion proactive solutions for households.
- Protect the nuclear family by reversing policies that usurp parental rights, responsibilities, knowledge, or consent.
- Introduce Family Empowerment Grant 1, providing a $5,000 wealth and wellbeing investment for every child at birth to kickstart generational asset growth.
- Deliver Family Empowerment Grant 2, providing a weekly no-strings-attached payment to families with children under the age of one.
- Legislate the transition from the minimum wage to an annually adjusted Living Wage to protect working families from inflation.
- Slash state dependency by eliminating financial rules that disincentivise marriage or reward parental separation.
- Roll out tax credits for marriage milestones at 7, 15, and 20 years, alongside interest-free packages for first-home buyers.
- Stop forced state interventions by setting up a free 0800 helpline and placing hapu liaison personnel inside every Oranga Tamariki office.

• Unlock government land for affordable housing initiatives, reform the RMA for dual home builds, and provide $50,000 start-up grants for hapu family enterprises.

\

**Policy Overview**

\

The home is the original nursery for values, respect, responsibility, and intergenerational prosperity. Decades of woke politics have created a dangerous values void, where distant state agencies and unnecessary regulations have eroded the confidence of mothers and fathers to parent their own children. We are calling time on an over-reaching government system that acts like an imposter family. Our society is a direct reflection of our homes, and true restoration will not come from more bureaucratic handouts, but from inside the home itself.

\

Family breakdown and fatherlessness are the single greatest drivers of persistent child poverty, housing stress, and community instability across New Zealand. To break this cycle, we will move away from individual isolation and embrace the power of co-responsibility through thriving, intergenerational family structures. By replacing the minimum wage with a true Living Wage and opening up crown land for genuine homeownership, we will allow Kiwi families to escape the trap of renting and build permanent economic security for the next generation.

\

Through our unique Ministry for Families, we will aggressively protect parental rights and stop the standard practice of unnecessary state child uplifts. By establishing clear community support pipelines, local liaisons, and strong financial incentives like newborn grants and marriage milestone tax cuts, we will give the power back to everyday mums and dads. We are entirely optimistic about the future of our country, but that future depends on making family wellbeing the unchanging plumb-line for every single social policy we create.

## Relief for Kiwi Families

## Cost of Living and Economic Relief

We will provide immediate financial relief to hard-working Kiwi families by cutting taxes on essential income, protecting local jobs, and securing fair prices for locally grown food.

Policy Commitments

\

- Make the first $25,000 of personal income completely tax-free to support squeezed middle-class budgets.
- Abolish all income tax on superannuation, student loans, and welfare benefits.
- Introduce Farm Gate Prices to ensure New Zealanders get first priority and fair pricing on our own home-grown meat and dairy.
- Protect Kiwi jobs by mandating that foreign-owned companies employ a baseline percentage of New Zealand citizens.
- Apply a higher tax rate to foreign traders and businesses that fail to employ a required baseline of local Kiwi staff.
- Incentivise childbirth and support growing families by introducing a dedicated newborn birth payment.

\

**Policy Overview**

\

Middle-class Kiwi families are being pushed to the brink by a persistent cost of living crisis, where the prices of basic staples like dairy, meat, power, and gas continue to erode household savings. It is entirely unacceptable that families are struggling to afford food grown right in our own backyard. We will institute farm gate pricing models, ensuring our home-grown produce is made affordable to local consumers before it is shipped overseas.

\

To put money back into your pocket, we will restructure the tax system to support working people. Making the first $25,000 of income completely tax-free will provide immediate, meaningful relief to workers, while eliminating taxes on superannuation and student loans will protect our seniors and young people. We will also fiercely protect local employment by ensuring foreign corporations prioritise New Zealand citizens, penalising those who exploit our economy without investing back into our people.

## Schools Without Propaganda Education Excellence

We will return our schools to academic excellence and national unity by removing ideological agendas from the curriculum and investing heavily in foundational learning and our teachers.

Policy Commitments

\

- Completely remove ideologically driven gender theory, Relationship and Sexuality Education (RSE), and political propaganda from the national curriculum.
- Refocus classrooms on a knowledge-rich curriculum centred strictly on literacy, numeracy, science, and balanced history.
- Invest heavily in high-quality Early Childhood Education (ECE), prioritising early language, literacy, and social skills.
- Increase teacher salaries and reduce administrative burdens to attract and retain dedicated educators.
- Introduce a strengthened civic education programme to teach children about New Zealand's democratic institutions and responsibilities.
- Ensure early childhood environments remain completely free from inappropriate ideological, foreign religious, or overt cultural content.

\

**Policy Overview**

\

New Zealand's education standards have suffered a long-term decline on international benchmarks because the focus has shifted away from core academic achievement toward liberal social engineering. Classrooms should be places where children learn how to think, not what to think. We will conduct an independent review of the curriculum to strip out political bias, ensuring schools concentrate purely on the foundational skills required for future success.

\

We will also make substantial investments in the earliest years of development, where educational funding delivers the absolute highest return. By expanding specialist student support, raising teacher salaries, and enforcing political neutrality, we will give our educators the tools to succeed. Our schools will rebuild a shared national identity by teaching a balanced history and strong civic values, preparing young Kiwis to become responsible, proud citizens.

## Less Government, More Community Law and Order

We believe in restoring safety to our streets by shifting the focus from expanding government bureaucracy to empowering local communities and providing real, 24/7 support for families in need.

Policy Commitments

\

- Deputise Community Support Officers to actively support the Police in maintaining public safety.
- Fund and deploy proven grassroots organisations with proven success records to deliver 24/7 wrap-around services.
- Provide comprehensive family resources to address root issues such as truancy, addiction, debt, and housing instability.
- Tackle crime through an integrated approach combining local accountability, strict enforcement, and prevention.

\

**Policy Overview**

\

Every New Zealander deserves to feel safe in their home, business, and community. The current system relies too heavily on distant government agencies that fail to deliver results on the ground. We will change this by introducing less government and more community-led action to protect our neighbourhoods. By deputising Community Support Officers, we will provide our Police with the visible, practical backup they need to maintain public safety and restore order.

\

True prevention starts with the family unit. We will resource and deploy established groups which already work on the front line every day to help families overcome parenting challenges, truancy, drugs, alcohol, and debt. Instead of pouring tax dollars into failing state bureaucracies, we will invest directly into solutions that break the cycle of crime, hold individuals accountable, and bring long-term stability back to our local towns.

## Infrastructure for Local Growth Transport and Infrastructure

We will ensure that infrastructure and transport networks directly serve local communities so that rapid regional growth no longer leaves everyday Kiwis stranded or left behind.

Policy Commitments

\

- Prioritise transport links and infrastructure upgrades that align with local community growth.
- Deliver common sense economic growth focused on productive investment over expanding bureaucracy.
- Ensure rapidly growing regions receive their fair share of funding for roads, schools, and essential services.

\

**Policy Overview**

\

For too long, transportation planning has been dominated by central government mandates that ignore the practical realities of daily life for commuting families. Middle-class households are paying rising local council rates, yet they continue to face gridlock and declining road quality. We will ensure that local growth is always matched by immediate, high-quality infrastructure investment so local communities are never left behind.

\

Our transport policy is built on practical delivery, not ideological global agendas. We believe in building roads and transport networks that cut congestion, reduce commuting times, and allow families to spend more time together rather than stuck in traffic.

## One Nation, One People National Identity, Sovereignty, and Immigration

We stand for one unified country, free from global overreach and divisive race-based politics, where the rights and culture of Kiwi citizens are put first.

Policy Commitments

\

- Unify the country under our core principle: One Nation, One People, One Vote, One Government, One Flag, Under One God.
- Pause mass immigration immediately to review visa policies and relieve pressure on local infrastructure and communities.
- Restore absolute national sovereignty by leaving the United Nations and the Paris Accord.
- Extend the residency requirement for obtaining New Zealand citizenship to 10 years and end dual citizenship for new applicants.
- Declare English as the official language of New Zealand.
- End all race-based politics and He Puapua-driven policies across government.

\

**Policy Overview**

\

New Zealand is a sovereign nation, yet our laws, farming practices, and daily lives are increasingly dictated by unelected global bodies like the United Nations. We will completely suspend our commitment to UN regulations and leave the Paris Accord, returning the power to govern New Zealand back to New Zealanders. We will also declare English as our official language, ensuring a clear and shared foundation for national communication.

\

To protect our local communities and stop the strain on local infrastructure, we will pause mass immigration immediately. We will tighten citizenship criteria by extending the application period to 10 years and removing the option for dual citizenship for incoming applicants, ensuring that those who join us are fully committed to assimilating into Kiwi culture. True unity means ending the divisive, race-based policies that split our country into separate categories. We stand for equal rights under a single flag.

## Protecting Biological Reality Women's Rights and the Two-Gender Policy

We will uphold biological reality in law to safeguard the privacy, safety, and hard-won rights of women and protect our children from harmful, premature decisions.

Policy Commitments

\

- Legally define and protect biological sex strictly as male and female based on objective scientific reality.
- Ensure all single-sex spaces, including public toilets, changing rooms, women's refuges, and correctional facilities, are designated by biological sex.
- Protect fairness in women's sports by strictly restricting participation to biological categories.
- Prohibit all medical interventions for gender transition in minors.
- Introduce a Parental Rights Bill to fully restore parental authority over the health and wellbeing of children up to the age of 16 years.
- Review and repeal aspects of the Births, Deaths, Marriages, and Relationships Registration Act 2021 that undermine biological definitions.

\

**Policy Overview**

\

A stable and just society depends on shared understandings grounded in objective truth. The escalating confusion surrounding biological identity harms the safety and privacy of women and girls. We will pass clear legislation defining sex as male and female to ensure that women's sports remain fair and that private spaces like changing rooms and refuges remain completely secure.

\

Furthermore, we believe our children must be protected, not exposed to life-altering, irreversible medical procedures before they reach full maturity. We will completely ban gender transition medical interventions for minors and introduce a Parental Rights Bill. This law will ensure parents remain fully informed and retain ultimate decision-making authority over their children's wellbeing, pushing back against ideologically driven agendas that attempt to cut families out of the conversation.

## Frontline Service Delivery First Human Rights and Healthcare

We will build a high-quality, universally accessible healthcare system focused on frontline service delivery, professional retention, and protecting the sanctity of human life.

Policy Commitments

\

- Prioritise primary healthcare spending to improve GP access and reduce specialist waitlists across local towns.
- Increase the salary budgets for health professionals to retain our vital medical staff within the system.
- Completely remove all race-based targeted policies and spending within the healthcare sector.
- Fully fund Plunket and increase financial support for pre-natal and post-natal care for mothers and infants.
- Review and reform existing legislation that endangers the lives of unborn children.
- Boost funding for university health degrees and provide incentives for domestic students to enter medical professions.

\

**Policy Overview**

\

Our healthcare system is facing critical strain due to persistent specialist waitlists and severe shortages of local GPs. Instead of pouring money into top-heavy administration and divisive race-based spending, we will redirect health budgets directly to the frontline where it matters most. By increasing salaries for our healthcare professionals and boosting university training incentives, we will keep Kiwi doctors and nurses working right here in New Zealand.

\

We also firmly believe that a compassionate society must protect its most vulnerable, beginning in the womb. We will actively review legislation that endangers the unborn child, while expanding practical support for new families. This includes providing increased funding for essential pre-natal and post-natal care, as well as delivering full, stable funding for Plunket so mothers and their babies receive the best possible start in life.

## Democracy Reset Now MMP Fatigue and Democracy Reset

We will restore true democracy and public accountability by eliminating unelected List MPs, cutting the size of government, and placing power firmly back into the hands of local voters.

Policy Commitments

\

- Overhaul the MMP system to completely eliminate party lists, ensuring only candidates who win an electorate seat can serve in parliament.
- Reduce the total number of Members of Parliament to downsize government spending and bureaucracy.
- Introduce term limits for politicians to do away with career-driven politics.
- Mandate regular drug testing for all sitting MPs to ensure integrity and professional accountability.
- Ban taxpayer-funded alcohol at all MP functions and reject the introduction of digital IDs.

\

**Policy Overview**

\

The current MMP system has created an undemocratic environment where minor, unelected List MPs hold a disproportionate amount of power. These career politicians frequently alter national legislation and push aggressive agendas without ever securing a direct public mandate from the voters. We believe that if you cannot win the confidence of a local electorate, you have no business sitting in our parliament.

\

To clean up politics, we will put the government on a strict diet. We will reduce the overall number of MPs, implement limited terms, and enforce strict personal accountability rules, including regular drug testing and a total ban on taxpayer-funded alcohol at political functions. Parliament should be filled with dedicated public servants focused on their constituents, not career politicians insulated inside a Wellington bubble.
