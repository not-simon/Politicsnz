# Vision NZ (Vision New Zealand) — 2026 election policies

Every policy of **Vision NZ (Vision New Zealand)** for the 7 November 2026 general election that this archive could find: **35 policies** listed, backed by **1 full-text source document**.

- Registration: 4 December 2019 (Electoral Commission register)

## About this list

Vision NZ, led by Hannah Tamaki, publishes its platform on a single "Our Policies" page in nine sections, each with a list of "Policy Commitments" followed by a policy overview. Every listed commitment is entered below as a separate policy (the "Why" lines paraphrase the section overview).

**Method.** Pages and PDFs were downloaded on 2026-10-23 by [`tools/policies_minor_sites_fetch.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_sites_fetch.py) / [`tools/policies_minor_opportunity_fetch.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_opportunity_fetch.py) (raw copies in `raw/party-docs/vision-nz/`) and reduced to their main text in [`documents/`](documents/) (markup and whitespace normalised only). The list and summaries are built by [`tools/policies_minor_build.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_build.py) from [`tools/policies_minor_catalog.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_catalog.py).

policy.nz publishes no policy statements for this party, so every summary here is marked *archive*: written for this archive from the linked full text in one to three neutral sentences ("Why" = the party's own stated reason, attributed; "How" = what it says it would do). Party claims (costs, statistics) are reported as the party's claims, not verified.

**Gaps and caveats.** No costings, PDFs or fuller policy documents were found on vision.org.nz. The page gives no amounts for the weekly 'Family Empowerment Grant 2' or the newborn birth payment.
