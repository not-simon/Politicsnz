# NZ First Will Immediately Reverse This Week’s Decision By The Minister Of Consumer Affairs

*Friday, 16 October 2026, 1:13 pm Press Release: New Zealand First Party* — [https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PA2610/S00145/nz-first-will-immediately-reverse-this-weeks-decision-by-the-minister-of-consumer-affairs.htm](https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PA2610/S00145/nz-first-will-immediately-reverse-this-weeks-decision-by-the-minister-of-consumer-affairs.htm)

## Full text

NZFirst will immediately reverse this week’s decision by the Minister of Consumer Affairs before the end of this year.

Today we have visited Vulcan Aluminium in Hamilton to discuss the recent announcement by the Minister of Consumer Affairs to not impose a safeguard measure on imported aluminium extrusions flooding the market.

Thousands of New Zealand jobs are at stake.

The decision to not impose safeguards leaves NZ companies using aluminium extrusions with no trade remedy against many inferior products flooding the market.

The supply chain to local fabricators and wider industry is being threatened - as is our sovereign capability.

New Zealand has nine aluminium extrusion pressers serving the country and all of them are now at risk.

Australia, UK, US, EU, and Canada have all taken action and so should we.

We are a nationalist party who puts New Zealand and New Zealanders first.
