# New Zealand First Makes Mill Road Extension A Priority For South Auckland

*Monday, 17 August 2026, 4:27 pm Press Release: New Zealand First Party* — [https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PO2608/S00121/new-zealand-first-makes-mill-road-extension-a.htm](https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PO2608/S00121/new-zealand-first-makes-mill-road-extension-a.htm)

## Full text

Photo/Supplied.

George Ngatai, New Zealand First Candidate for Papakura

New Zealand First has reinforced its commitment to South Auckland infrastructure with the announcement by Party Leader Rt Hon Winston Peters at the party's Election Campaign Launch in Manukau that the extension and completion of Mill Road will be a priority for a future New Zealand First-led Government.

The announcement has been welcomed by New Zealand First Papakura candidate George Ngatai, who says the project is essential to the future growth and prosperity of Papakura, Takanini, Drury and the wider South Auckland region.

"For too long, South Auckland has experienced rapid population growth without the supporting infrastructure needed to keep communities moving. The commitment announced by Winston Peters sends a clear message that New Zealand First understands the challenges facing our region."

Mr Ngatai said residents regularly raise concerns about congestion, travel times, pressure on local roads and the lack of transport infrastructure to support new housing developments.

"People are frustrated. Families are spending more time in traffic and less time at home. Businesses are facing delays moving goods and services. We cannot continue building houses without ensuring roads and infrastructure are keeping pace."

The Mill Road corridor has long been identified as a strategic transport route capable of improving connectivity between Manukau, Takanini, Papakura and Drury while easing pressure on State Highway 1 and surrounding local roads.

"The announcement by Winston Peters recognises what local residents have been saying for years – infrastructure must come before growth, not after it."

Mr Ngatai said the recent opening of the Drury and Paerātā railway stations by the Leader was an important investment in public transport, but road infrastructure remained equally important.

"We need a transport system that works for everyone. Rail, buses and roads must complement each other. South Auckland families still rely heavily on their vehicles and we need roads that can safely and efficiently support that reality."

As the New Zealand First candidate for Papakura, Mr Ngatai said he would continue advocating strongly for the project and ensuring South Auckland receives its fair share of national infrastructure investment.

"Papakura is my home. It is where my family lives, where I work and where I serve the community every day. I support Winston Peters' commitment to Mill Road because I know firsthand the difference this investment will make for local families, workers and businesses." Other parties have spoken about it and have done nothing.

"New Zealand First is committed to delivering practical solutions, investing in infrastructure and ensuring that growing communities like Papakura are not left behind."
