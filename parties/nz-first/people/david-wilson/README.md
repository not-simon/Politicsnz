## Current status

NZ First list MP since 30 June 2025 (replacing Tanya Unkovich). Standing in **Henderson** and **#12** on NZ First's list for 2026.

## Biography

David Ashley Wilson was born in Kumeū and raised by adoptive parents in Glendowie; boarded at Saint Kentigern College. After working in retail he gained a BA in social policy and psychology, a master's in public policy, and in 2016 a PhD from AUT (supervised by Marilyn Waring) on Auckland governance and regional economic development. CEO of Northland's economic development agency Northland Inc (2013–2017 and again to 2019), later a consultant. Coached netball and basketball; board member of accessibility social enterprise The Be. In 2024 he lost $200,000 of retirement savings to an investment scam and spoke publicly about weak victim protections.

## Political career

- Joined NZ First in 2015; candidate in 2017 (Te Atatū, list #14), 2020 (Whangārei, #9) and 2023 (Upper Harbour, #9).
- Appointed by Shane Jones to the Provincial Growth Fund independent advisory panel (2018).
- Declared elected 30 June 2025.

## Record & notable actions

- In May 2026 joined a cross-party delegation to Taiwan; China banned the four MPs for a year, which PM Luxon called "entirely inappropriate".
