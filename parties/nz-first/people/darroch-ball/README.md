## Current status

Former MP (2014–2020); since the 2023 election chief of staff to Winston Peters. Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

Born and raised in Auckland in 1982, of Tongan descent through his mother; attended Liston College and became a father at 19, raising two children as a single parent. BSc in biological science (Auckland, 2005); seven years as an Army logistics officer; trained as a teacher and taught science at Waiopehu College (2014).

## Political career

- Joined NZ First at about 30; launched a Massey campus branch and became North Island vice-president (2013).
- Elected at list #10 in 2014 (Palmerston North candidate); re-elected 2017; chaired the Transport and Infrastructure Committee.
- Lost his seat in 2020; interim party president 2020–21; third in a 2021 Palmerston North City Council by-election.

## Record & notable actions

- As NZ First justice spokesperson (2017–2020) blocked Labour proposals including repeal of "three strikes" and some drug-law reforms; led an unsuccessful attempt (19–100) to require a referendum on the Abortion Legislation Bill.
- Protection for First Responders and Prison Officers Bill (2018), with a six-month minimum sentence for injuring first responders; passed first reading unanimously but stalled and was later discharged.
- Youth Employment Training and Education Bill (2017), for a Defence-Force youth programme; defeated at first reading.
- Visited the 2022 anti-mandate Parliament protest with Peters.

## Controversies

None of significance found in the sources consulted (the Sensible Sentencing Trust wound up under his leadership in 2022).
