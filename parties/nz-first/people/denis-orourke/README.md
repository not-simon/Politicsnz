## Current status

Former MP (2011–2017). Contested Banks Peninsula for NZ First in 2020 without success. Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

Born in Christchurch on 26 July 1946; educated at Christchurch West High School and the University of Canterbury (LLB). Practised law (legal drafting, statutory interpretation, commercial law) until 1992.

## Political career

- Labour member from about 1983 (sought several Labour candidacies); Christchurch City Councillor for Labour 1989–2004, chairing the Sustainable Transport and Utilities Committee. Left Labour in 2003.
- Joined NZ First in 2010; elected at list #7 in 2011 and re-elected in 2014 (contesting Port Hills both times); lost his seat in 2017 at list #13.

## Record & notable actions

- On council championed the Kate Valley landfill and set up the Recovered Materials Foundation, the start of kerbside recycling in Christchurch.
- Member of Jan Logie's cross-party LGBTI rights group (2015), although he voted against marriage equality in 2013 with the NZ First caucus; questioned the Bill of Rights consistency of NZ First's immigrant health insurance proposal.
