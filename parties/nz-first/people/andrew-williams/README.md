## Current status

Former MP (2011–2014). NZ First omitted him from its 2014 list; he retired to Taupō. Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

Born 1959. North Shore City councillor (2001–2004), Takapuna community board member (2004–2007) and RMA hearings commissioner (2001–2010). Married to Jane, a registered nurse.

## Political career

- Elected Mayor of North Shore City in 2007, serving until the council was abolished into the Auckland "Super City" on 31 October 2010. A vocal critic of the amalgamation, which he called a railroaded "power grab".
- Stood for the Super City mayoralty and Albany ward in 2010 (saying his aim was to cut John Banks' North Shore vote); elected to neither.
- NZ First candidate for North Shore in 2011 (about 828 electorate votes) and elected on the list; excluded from the 2014 list.

## Record & notable actions

- Argued for retaining existing councils with a strengthened regional council, broadly in line with the Royal Commission on Auckland Governance.

## Controversies

- In 2008 ambulance staff reported he "lashed out" while being treated after collapsing at a naval function (his wife attributed it to illness after an overseas trip); his mayoralty was criticised as "overbearing" and he was asked to leave a council committee after calling a councillor a "smart-arse". John Banks accidentally texted him calling him "a lunatic".
