## Current status

Former MP (2011–2014). Not re-elected in 2014 and left New Zealand in 2015. Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

Le'Aufa'amulia Asenati Lole-Taylor was born in Samoa and emigrated to New Zealand at 17; she holds the matai title Le'Aufa'amulia from Falese'ela. She chaired the Auckland City Pacific Islands Board, sat on Auckland DHB's Pacific health advisory committee, and worked as a Pacific regional adviser in the Department of Corrections. Three children and a stepson.

## Political career

- Labour candidate for Auckland City Council (Tamaki-Maungakiekie) in 2007; joined NZ First by 2008 and contested Maungakiekie (2008) and Manukau East (2011).
- Elected at list #8 in 2011; demoted to #16 in 2014 and not returned.

## Record & notable actions

- Sentencing (Protection of Children from Criminal Offending) Amendment Bill passed first reading in 2013 but was defeated in December 2014.
- Voted against marriage equality (2013).

## Controversies

- After leaving Parliament it was found that she and her husband Dennis Taylor had improperly accessed Corrections records (including the drink-driving record of a NZ First board member); Stuff reported she "broke rules by accessing records". The couple left New Zealand in 2015 while a police probe was under way.
- Known for outspoken tweets as an MP, including a 2014 dispute over her use of a sexual slang term.
