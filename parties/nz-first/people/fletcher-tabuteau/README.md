## Current status

Former MP (2014–2020) and former NZ First deputy leader. Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

Born in Rotorua in 1974 (Ngāti Ngāraranui, Ngāti Rangiwewehi, Ngāti Whakaue; French-Huguenot ancestry on his father's side). Educated at Rotorua Boys' High School, Waiariki Institute of Technology and the University of Waikato; taught maths and social studies at Rotorua Boys' for eight years and was head of the business school at Waiariki (2011–2014). Married to Karen; two children. Joined NZ First as a teenager at its 1993 launch.

## Political career

- Contested Rotorua six times (2002, 2005, 2011, 2014, 2017, 2020).
- Elected at list #4 in 2014; re-elected 2017.
- Parliamentary Under-Secretary to Winston Peters (Foreign Affairs; Disarmament) and Shane Jones (Regional Economic Development) 2017–2020, including responsibility for the aid programme.
- NZ First deputy leader from 27 February 2018 until after the 2020 defeat.

## Record & notable actions

- Fighting Foreign Corporate Control Bill (2015), aimed at limiting investor-state provisions in the TPP; defeated at first reading, but its thrust became government policy after 2017.
- KiwiFund Bill (2017), to create a government-owned KiwiSaver provider; withdrawn in 2018 after select committee recommended against it.
