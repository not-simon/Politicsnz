## Current status

Deceased (died 2 June 2024). She did not stand in 2017 after her husband Gordon died that January.

## Biography

Born in Wairoa on 23 February 1952. BEd and Postgraduate Diploma in Management Studies (Waikato); a primary and secondary teacher and company training officer. A long-time National Party member (Feilding branch secretary 1987–90) before joining NZ First in 1996. Lived in Cambridge; one son.

## Political career

- NZ First list MP 2002–2008 (contested North Shore, Piako and Waikato) and 2011–2017 (Waikato).
- NZ First whip throughout 2011–2017.

## Record & notable actions

- Electoral (Reduction in Number of Members of Parliament) Amendment Bill (2006), to cut Parliament to 100 MPs following the 1999 referendum; passed first reading 61–60 but defeated at second reading.
- SuperGold Health Check Bill (2013; three free GP visits a year for superannuitants, defeated 60–61) and Affordable Healthcare Bill (2015; defeated 46–75) — both transferred to Winston Peters' name. The Attorney-General found the latter's mandatory health insurance for immigrant parents unjustifiably discriminatory under the Bill of Rights.
- Voted against marriage equality (2013).

## Controversies

- Criticised for saying Asian immigrants "struggl[e] with the concept of a house".
