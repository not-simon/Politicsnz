## Current status

Former MP. Invercargill City Councillor since 2022, re-elected in 2025 (while again losing the mayoral race). Not a 2026 parliamentary candidate. In 2025 she took part in the unofficial cross-party "People's Select Committee" on pay-equity changes led by Marilyn Waring.

## Biography

Born in Palmerston North in 1976 (Ngāti Hine, Ngāpuhi and Scottish descent); placed in foster care five times as a child, describing in her maiden speech that she had lived "Once Were Warriors". Great-niece of Sir James Hēnare. A hairdresser in Invercargill, she was president of the NZ Association of Registered Hairdressers (2006–2012) and a director of the Hairdressing ITO. Two children.

## Political career

- Joined NZ First in 2011; national board from 2012; contested Invercargill in 2014 (third; list #12).
- Became a list MP on 28 April 2015 when Winston Peters won the Northland by-election; left Parliament in 2017.
- Her 2019 Invercargill mayoral nomination was rejected because a nominator's address was not correctly on the roll.

## Record & notable actions

- Commerce Committee member (2015–2017).
