## Current status

NZ First list MP since 2023 and party whip. Standing in **Kaikōura** and **#13** on NZ First's list for 2026.

## Biography

Grew up on an apple and cherry orchard at Rapaura, Marlborough; diploma in management from Nelson Polytechnic. After the Marlborough Farmers' Market declined his asparagus stall application in 2009 he led stallholders in founding a rival market. Married to Sally; four children; trains racehorses as a hobby.

## Political career

- Marlborough District Councillor 2010–2024, topping the Blenheim Ward poll in 2013 and 2016; runner-up for mayor in 2010, 2013 and 2019.
- NZ First candidate for Kaikōura in 2017, 2020 and 2023; elected at list #6 in 2023.
- NZ First whip; sits on Finance and Expenditure; deputy chair of the Justice Committee.

## Record & notable actions

- Focus on regional and Marlborough issues.

## Controversies

- **"Double dipping" (2024):** kept his council seat for a year after entering Parliament to avoid a by-election; said he would return his council pay, then on 6 May 2024 said he would keep it, then a day later after criticism said he would donate it to charity. Resigned from council on 31 October 2024.
