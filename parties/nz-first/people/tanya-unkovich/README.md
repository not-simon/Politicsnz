## Current status

Former MP (October 2023 – June 2025). Resigned on 27 June 2025 to focus on private-sector work. Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

Born in Dargaville to Croatian immigrant parents; BCom in accounting and commercial law (Auckland, 1984). Worked as an auditor, an Air New Zealand flight attendant and inflight-services auditor, ran her own accounting practice and consulted for MYOB; later a counsellor, life and business coach, public speaker and author of four books (including *From Grief to Greatness*, 2022). Widowed in 2005; married Grant Anderson in 2011.

## Political career

- NZ First candidate for Epsom in 2023 (sixth, 573 votes); elected at list #8.

## Record & notable actions

- **Fair Access to Bathrooms Bill** (May 2024): would require new public buildings to have single-sex and unisex toilets and make it an offence to use a toilet designated for the other sex.

## Controversies

- During the 2023 campaign she was criticised for participating in a "Nuremberg trials" Telegram group likening COVID-19 vaccines to Nazi war crimes.
- At a May 2024 "Unsilenced" conference she likened gender dysphoria in children to body dysmorphia; Labour MP Ingrid Leary called her an "anti-trans activist" at select committee and later apologised in the House after a privilege complaint.
