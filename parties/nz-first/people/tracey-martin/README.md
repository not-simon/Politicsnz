## Current status

Former MP (2011–2020) and former minister. Left NZ First in January 2021 (with Jenny Marcroft, who later rejoined). Holds public governance roles. Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

Born in Levin on 1 July 1964; her mother Anne Martin was a Social Credit and NZ First activist and later NZ First president; Martin has Ngāti Kahungunu ancestry. Worked as a debt collector, then spent 15 years as a stay-at-home parent in Warkworth, chairing the Mahurangi College board of trustees for over a decade. Married to winemaker Ben Dugdale; three children.

## Political career

- Involved with NZ First since 1993; board member from 2008; contested Rodney in 2008 and 2011.
- Rodney Local Board member 2010–2013.
- Elected at list #2 in 2011; deputy leader 2013–2015 (replaced by Ron Mark); re-elected 2014 and 2017.
- Minister for Children, Internal Affairs and Seniors and Associate Minister of Education in the 2017–2020 Labour–NZ First coalition; regarded as a key coalition go-between.
- Lost her seat when NZ First fell below the threshold in 2020.

## Record & notable actions

- Social Security (Clothing Allowances for Orphans and Unsupported Children) Amendment Act 2015 (her member's bill, passed unanimously).
- As Children's Minister ordered reviews after the 2019 attempted uplift of a baby in Hastings and scrapped the Children's Teams; Oranga Tamariki was heavily criticised during her tenure.
- Negotiated the abortion law reform with Labour (2019) and voted for it; Winston Peters later surprised her and Labour by demanding a referendum.
- Voted against marriage equality in 2013 with the rest of the NZ First caucus.

## Controversies

- In April 2025 Peters accused her of advancing the Births, Deaths, Marriages and Relationships Registration Act 2021 (sex self-identification) behind his back; Martin accused him of disinformation.
