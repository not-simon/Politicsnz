## Current status

NZ First candidate for **Waitaki** (confirmed 3 July 2026) and **#6** on the NZ First list — a potentially winnable position. Former MP (before the 2011–2026 period covered by this archive).

## Biography

Born in Wairoa on 26 June 1957 and raised in Whanganui; first-class honours in history (Otago) and an MA (Victoria); captained New Zealand debating teams; supported the 1981 Springbok tour as a student. A National Party researcher and press secretary (1985–89), including for Winston Peters; later a talkback host and long-time columnist.

## Political career

- National MP for Hawke's Bay 1990–1996; frequently voted against his party and promoted a voluntary euthanasia bill.
- Joined NZ First in April 1996 but resigned from Parliament later that year after a scandal over selecting a company part-owned by his wife for a contract; managed NZ First's 1996 campaign.
- Mayor of Whanganui 2004–2010; Whanganui District Council 2013–14; Otago Regional Council from 2016 (deputy chair until April 2022; re-elected 2025 on the Vision Otago ticket).

## Controversies

- The 1996 contract scandal that ended his parliamentary career; long-running clashes as Whanganui mayor (including over the Sarjeant Gallery); on the ORC opposed engagement with mana whenua and disputed the regional policy statement process.
