## Current status

NZ First candidate for **Tukituki** and **#9** on the list in 2026. Never an MP.

## Biography

Born 5 November 1974; educated at Lindisfarne College and the University of Otago (law and commerce). Loose forward who played 51 tests for the All Blacks (22 as captain), captained Otago at 19 and the Highlanders 1997–2003, and led New Zealand at the 1999 Rugby World Cup (semi-final loss to France). Played for Saracens in England until 2006. Since 2010 a director of Ngāti Kahungunu's asset holding company, including its seafood interests.
