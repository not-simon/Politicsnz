## Current status

NZ First candidate for **Takanini** and **#20** on the list in 2026 (announced June 2026). Never an MP.

## Biography

Born 25 June 1977 and raised in Point England, Glen Innes and Mount Wellington, then Hamilton. Says he overcame drug and alcohol addiction and became a Christian at 25. A youth worker in Flat Bush and Botany; lives in Papakura; married to Eona, with two children.

## Political career

- Conservative Party deputy leader from June 2017 (Manurewa candidate, last of six); New Conservative candidate for Takanini in 2020 (fourth, 939 votes).
- Leader of the New Conservatives for six weeks in late 2020, resigning on 31 December.

## Record & notable actions

- Has advocated restrictions on abortion and binding citizens-initiated referendums.

## Controversies

- Defended the 2018 speaking tour of Lauren Southern and Stefan Molyneux and attended a 2020 anti-lockdown march in Auckland.
