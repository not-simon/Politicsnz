## Current status

Former MP (2014–2017). Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

Born in Mumbai; BA (Hons) in political science and psychology (University of Mumbai); trained with the National Cadet Corps; worked for Air India in airport security and as cabin crew (to 2002). Migrated to New Zealand in 2002 with his family; his wife was killed in a car crash around 2006. Worked as a Department of Corrections case manager/senior corrections officer and was active in Indian community trusts.

## Political career

- Joined NZ First in 2006; contested Mount Roskill in 2011 and 2014; elected at list #11 in 2014.
- At #10 in 2017 he was not returned; contested Hutt South in 2020.

## Record & notable actions

- Questioned (with Denis O'Rourke) the Bill of Rights consistency of NZ First's proposal for compulsory health insurance for immigrant parents.

## Controversies

- Criticised in 2014 by the *Herald* and bloggers as NZ First's "MP of mystery" for not disclosing his employment during the campaign; he said his Corrections contract prevented it.
