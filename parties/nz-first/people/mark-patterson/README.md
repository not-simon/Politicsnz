## Current status

NZ First list MP (2017–2020, 2023–) and minister outside Cabinet (Rural Communities; Associate Agriculture and Regional Development). Standing in **Taieri** and **#4** on NZ First's list for 2026.

## Biography

Born in Canterbury in 1970 and raised on the family farm at Southbridge (owned by the family since 1905); attended Ellesmere College. Sheep and beef farmer at Lawrence, Otago, since 2008 with his wife Jude; directors of Ngapara Farms. A red-meat industry reform campaigner (deputy chair of the Meat Industry Excellence Group 2014–15) who opposed Silver Fern Farms' joint venture with China's Shanghai Maling — lobbying critics described as "anti-Chinese". Otago Federated Farmers chair 2021–2023.

## Political career

- Sought National's Clutha-Southland nomination in 2014 (lost to Todd Barclay); joined NZ First in 2015.
- Elected at list #7 in 2017 (Clutha-Southland candidate), NZ First's only South Island MP; lost in 2020 (Taieri).
- Re-elected at list #4 in 2023 (Taieri); minister from November 2023.

## Record & notable actions

- **New Zealand Superannuation and Retirement Income (Fair Residency) Amendment Act 2021**: his bill (later taken over by National's Andrew Bayly) raised the residency requirement for NZ Super from 10 to 20 years.
- Gore District Council (Otama Rural Water Supply) Act 2019; Farm Debt Mediation Bill (superseded by the government's 2019 Act).
- As minister, runs the wool portfolio ("woolshed roadshow"; 2025 requirement for government buildings to use wool rather than synthetic fibres).
- Voted for the End of Life Choice Act with the referendum condition; withdrew support for the 2020 abortion law at third reading after a referendum was rejected.

## Controversies

- He stood aside from Emissions Trading Scheme decisions because of a pecuniary interest (forestry), but only after taking part in ETS decisions in May 2024.
- Called for New Zealand citizenship to be required to buy land after the 2017 sale of Jericho Station to Chinese owners.
