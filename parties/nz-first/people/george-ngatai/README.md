## Current status

New Zealand First announced Ngatai as its Papakura candidate in July 2026. On 26 August 2026 he stepped down, saying that "after careful consideration" he had decided to step down, after screenshots posted by the social-media account Shubz Live purported to show him discussing a possible run for ACT and being introduced to ACT leader David Seymour and Opportunity leader Qiulae Wong. In a statement he admitted communicating with other parties during "a period of considerable uncertainty about whether I would have the opportunity to stand as a candidate", but said no meetings took place and he made no commitment to another party. Seymour said he remembered getting a message but "did not think it went anywhere". Ngatai said he would continue to support New Zealand First ([RNZ, 26 Aug 2026](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/politics_election-2026/1136184/nz-first-candidate-steps-down-after-earlier-shopping-himself-around-to-other-parties)).

Because he withdrew before nominations closed, he does not appear on the Electoral Commission's final candidate list (9 October 2026) or on policy.nz, and NZ First has no candidate in [Papakura](../../../../electorates/papakura.md). The Wikipedia candidate list records him as a withdrawn NZ First candidate who had contested Takanini for Vision NZ in 2020 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidates_in_the_2026_New_Zealand_general_election_by_electorate)).
