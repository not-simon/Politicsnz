## Current status

NZ First list MP since 2023. Standing in **Remutaka** and **#11** on NZ First's list for 2026.

## Biography

Born in Pembury, Kent, on 21 December 1961; moved to Wellington aged five and became a citizen in 1978. Educated at Wellington College and Victoria University (BA in history and economic history; BCom). Worked as a National Party parliamentary researcher in the late 1980s. Describes himself as a "Bluegreen" (conservative environmentalist). Married to Ann; two children. Long involved with the Karori Wildlife Sanctuary (Zealandia) trust.

## Political career

- Wellington City Councillor for nine terms (1992–2019; Western and Onslow-Western wards); served on council company boards including Wellington Airport.
- Ran for mayor in 2001 and 2016; won in 2019 by 62 votes over incumbent Justin Lester, backed by Sir Peter Jackson; lost heavily to Tory Whanau in 2022.
- Independent candidate in 1996; NZ First candidate in 2017 (list #18); NZ First's Mana candidate in 2023 (fourth), elected at list #7.

## Record & notable actions

- As mayor funded the Te Atakura – First to Zero climate plan, advanced the Let's Get Wellington Moving preferred option (second Mt Victoria tunnel, mass transit to Island Bay), and gave mana whenua iwi voting seats on council committees (2021).
- Opposed Three Waters (2021).

## Controversies

- In July 2021 he lit the Michael Fowler Centre in transgender-flag colours and joined a counter-rally while a group regarded by critics as trans-exclusionary spoke there — praised by opponents but at odds with later NZ First positions.
