## Current status

Former MP (2014–2020). Announced in June 2020 he would stand down; as of 2022 no longer a NZ First member. Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

A Tauranga hospitality businessman (bar owner) and chair of the Waikato-Bay of Plenty Hospitality Association. Elected to Tauranga City Council (Mt Maunganui/Pāpāmoa) in 2013.

## Political career

- Elected at list #6 in 2014 (Tauranga candidate); re-elected 2017; NZ First whip.
- Sat on select committees including Business, Economic Development, Officers of Parliament and Standing Orders.

## Record & notable actions

- Member's bill (February 2018) to make English an official language; NZ First later passed the English Language Act 2026.
- Led NZ First's "Respecting New Zealand Values" campaign to require migrants to sign a code of conduct.

## Controversies

- **Hospitality record:** convicted in the 1990s of assault (refusing a gang member entry to his bar), dangerous driving, and after-hours trading; his bar manager's licence was suspended in 1998 (restored 2000); later pubs drew scrutiny over pokie underage gambling and an unlimited-drinks promotion. Stuff reported he had been "ruled unfit to run a pub".
- **Tauranga bar incident (October 2019):** removed by security; staff alleged he accidentally hit a worker with a glass, which he denied. Peters promised exculpatory security footage that was never released.
- **NZ First Foundation:** reported in 2019 as the party's fundraiser handling large donors; the Serious Fraud Office investigated the foundation (those later charged were acquitted).
