## Current status

Deceased (died 26 August 2019 in Auckland, weeks after cardiac surgery).

## Biography

Rewiti Pomare Kingi "Pita" Paraone was born at Kawakawa on 30 November 1945 and educated at Motatau District High School and Bay of Islands College, with diplomas in business development (Auckland) and social work (Victoria), and study at Henley Management College. He had a long public-service career, including as a regional director of Te Puni Kōkiri. MNZM (1997) for services to Māori and the community. Married Elva Joyce Moore in 1966; three children.

## Political career

- NZ First board member/director 1999–2002; list MP 2002–2008 and 2014–2017.
- Chair of the Waitangi National Trust Board from 2009 (board member from 1997).
- Ranked #11 in 2017 and not returned; publicly encouraged NZ First to back a Labour-led government. Appointed chair of the Local Government Commission in November 2018.

## Record & notable actions

- NZ First spokesperson on Māori affairs, Treaty of Waitangi issues and Pacific Island affairs (2014–2017); advocated for Northland development.
