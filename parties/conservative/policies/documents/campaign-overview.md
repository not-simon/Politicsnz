# Conservative.NZ Policy Overview

### Conserving the good for a better New Zealand

Conservatives NZ stands for practical, common-sense government founded on personal responsibility, strong families, individual freedom, equal treatment and respect for New Zealand’s democratic traditions.

We believe government should protect the rights and safety of New Zealanders, provide essential public services and manage taxpayers’ money responsibly. It should not unnecessarily interfere in people’s lives, divide New Zealanders by race or identity, or impose an ever-growing burden of regulation and bureaucracy.

Our policies are guided by the following priorities:

### Smaller, more accountable government

We support lower taxes, responsible spending and fewer unnecessary regulations. Government agencies and local authorities must be transparent, efficient and accountable to the people they serve.

Public money should be directed towards essential services and critical infrastructure—not expanding bureaucracy.

### Strong families and communities

Strong families are the foundation of a healthy society. Our policies encourage personal responsibility, stable families and communities that care for their children, elderly, vulnerable and disadvantaged.

We believe government policy should strengthen families rather than create long-term dependency or undermine the role of parents.

### Freedom and individual responsibility

New Zealanders should be free to express their views, practise their beliefs, peacefully assemble and make responsible choices about their own lives.

With individual freedom comes personal responsibility and respect for the rights and freedoms of others.

### One people under one law

Every New Zealander should have the same rights, responsibilities and opportunities under the law.

We oppose policies that divide people according to race, ancestry, sex or identity. Employment, promotion and access to public services should be based on individual need, ability and merit.

### A strong and productive economy

New Zealand needs an economy that rewards work, enterprise, innovation and investment.

We support tax and regulatory settings that encourage businesses to grow, young New Zealanders to build their futures here, and our country to make responsible use of its natural resources.

### Safe communities and equal justice

The first responsibility of government is to protect its people.

We support properly resourced police, meaningful consequences for serious and repeat offending, support for victims, and a justice system that applies the law fairly and consistently.

### Protecting New Zealand’s future

We are committed to protecting New Zealand’s natural environment while ensuring environmental policy is practical, affordable and supported by credible evidence.

We want future generations to inherit a country that is safe, prosperous, democratic and united.

### Policies that put New Zealanders first

Across every area of government, Conservatives NZ will ask:

- Does this policy protect individual freedom?
- Does it strengthen families and communities?
- Does it treat New Zealanders equally?
- Does it make government more accountable?
- Is it a responsible use of taxpayers’ money?
- Will it leave New Zealand stronger for future generations?

These principles provide the foundation for the detailed policies presented throughout this website.
