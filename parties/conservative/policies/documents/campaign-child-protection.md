# Protecting Our Children

# Our Commitment

The Conservative Party believes every child has the right to grow up safe from abuse, exploitation, violence, neglect and unnecessary harm.

Children are among the most vulnerable members of our society. Government, families, schools, health professionals and the justice system all have a responsibility to protect them.

Our policy is based on a simple principle:

**The safety, wellbeing and long-term interests of the child must come first.**

We will strengthen protections for children, support parents in their responsibility for their children, hold serious offenders accountable, and ensure government agencies act quickly and effectively when children are at risk.

## Stronger Protection from Sexual Offending

Sexual offending against children is among the most serious crimes in our society. The consequences for victims can last a lifetime, and sentencing should properly recognise the severity and lasting impact of these offences.

The Conservative Party will:

- Increase penalties for serious sexual offending against children.
- Ensure repeat child sexual offenders face significantly longer prison sentences.
- Review sentence reductions for the most serious sexual offences against children to ensure sentences properly reflect the seriousness of the offending.
- Improve monitoring and management of high-risk sexual offenders following their release.
- Strengthen resources for investigating online child exploitation and identifying offenders.
- Ensure victims and their families have timely access to counselling, trauma support and appropriate long-term assistance.

## Protect Children from Medical Harm

Children deserve particularly strong safeguards when medical treatment may have irreversible or lifelong consequences.

The Conservative Party believes significant medical interventions involving minors must be based on high-quality medical evidence, genuine medical necessity and rigorous standards of informed consent.

We will:

- Restrict elective and irreversible medical procedures intended to alter a child's sex characteristics or reproductive capabilities where there is no clear medical necessity.
- Require comprehensive and appropriately independent assessment before significant medical intervention relating to gender distress in minors.
- Ensure parents or legal guardians are appropriately involved in significant healthcare decisions affecting their children, subject to necessary safeguards for the child's safety and existing legal obligations.
- Require treatment and clinical practice involving minors to be based on high-quality evidence and robust informed-consent standards.
- Establish appropriate independent review and complaints mechanisms for significant treatment involving minors.

## Protect Childhood from Sexual Exploitation

Children should be able to grow and develop without being targeted for sexual exploitation, grooming or inappropriate sexualisation.

The Conservative Party will:

- Increase penalties for producing, possessing, distributing or commercially exploiting child sexual abuse material.
- Strengthen laws and enforcement against online grooming and sexual exploitation of children.
- Improve the ability of law enforcement agencies to identify and investigate online offenders.
- Expand education and practical resources for parents about online risks, grooming, sextortion and exploitation.
- Work with technology providers and relevant agencies to improve the detection and reporting of child sexual abuse material.

## Safe Schools

Every child deserves to feel safe at school and every parent should be able to expect that schools will provide a secure environment focused on education.

The Conservative Party supports schools that:

- Maintain safe, orderly and respectful learning environments.
- Take bullying and intimidation seriously.
- Protect students from harassment, sexual harassment and sexual violence.
- Respond quickly when a child's safety is threatened.
- Keep parents appropriately informed about significant matters affecting their children.
- Focus strongly on academic achievement and core educational outcomes.
- Ensure health and relationship education is age-appropriate, transparent to parents and focused on children's health, safety and wellbeing rather than political or ideological advocacy.

## Mental Health and Trauma Support

Children experiencing mental distress, abuse or trauma should receive timely, professional and compassionate assistance.

The Conservative Party will:

- Improve access to appropriately qualified child psychologists and mental health professionals.
- Recognise the important role of parents and guardians in supporting children's mental health and involve them appropriately in assessment and treatment, subject to safeguards where a child's safety may be at risk.
- Reduce waiting times for youth mental health services.
- Expand specialist support for children who have experienced sexual abuse, violence or other serious trauma.
- Invest in early intervention where evidence shows it can prevent more serious long-term harm.

## Supporting Strong Families

Strong, stable and loving families provide one of the greatest protections available to children.

Government should support good parents rather than unnecessarily replacing or undermining their role.

The Conservative Party will:

- Support practical parenting education and family assistance.
- Strengthen early intervention when abuse, serious neglect or exploitation is identified.
- Improve coordination between families, schools, healthcare providers, Police and child protection agencies.
- Ensure agencies share critical information appropriately when a child is genuinely at risk.
- Prioritise the safety and wellbeing of children while respecting the rights and responsibilities of loving parents and guardians.

## Child Trafficking and Commercial Sexual Exploitation

New Zealand must take a zero-tolerance approach to the trafficking, sale and commercial sexual exploitation of children.

The Conservative Party will:

- Increase resources for investigating suspected child trafficking and organised exploitation.
- Strengthen cooperation between Police, Customs, immigration authorities and international enforcement agencies.
- Ensure severe penalties apply to those who traffic, procure, sell or commercially sexually exploit children.
- Strengthen measures to identify and protect children at risk of trafficking or exploitation.
- Provide appropriate long-term support for children rescued from trafficking or commercial sexual exploitation.

## Protecting Children from Inappropriate Sexualised Content

Children should not be deliberately targeted with sexually explicit or developmentally inappropriate material.

The Conservative Party will:

- Review standards governing sexually explicit or highly sexualised material directed towards children.
- Ensure material provided through schools and publicly funded children's programmes is age-appropriate.
- Support parents' right to know what sexuality and relationship material is being presented to their children.
- Require clear parental transparency around programmes dealing with sensitive sexual or gender-related subjects.
- Apply consistent child-safety standards regardless of the political, social or ideological viewpoint associated with the material.

## Our Principle

**Children deserve protection, parents deserve respect, victims deserve justice, and serious offenders must be held accountable.**

The Conservative Party will put the safety and wellbeing of New Zealand children at the centre of decisions affecting their lives.
