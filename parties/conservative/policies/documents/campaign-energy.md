# Energy

# We need access to cheap, reliable, and abundant energy

No country has ever achieved significant economic growth without access to cheap, reliable, and abundant energy. We are no different. This has been the case for every country since the Industrial Revolution.

| | | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| | | | | --- | --- | | | | | --- | | Renewables such as solar or wind have a part to play but cannot, and should not, be solely relied upon for electricity supply. - Solar and wind output can fluctuate extremely. This requires alternate generation to be already online in reserve (generators cannot turn on and off instantaneously). There is a mismatch between high solar production during the middle of the day when demand is lower, and peak electricity demand in the evening when supply is low. - Windmills, which are a blight on the landscape, are problematic too. The wind doesn’t always blow, and rotating blades are a danger to flying wildlife. Environmental concerns such as the disposal of old turbine blades and potential asbestos exposure are yet to be resolved. - Some turbines use asbestos for the turbine braking material linings, which is a significant health hazard to people in proximity. This danger is still not fully assessed in New Zealand. **New Zealand uses a Marginal Cost Pricing Model**: This wholesale spot market sets the price for all electricity by the most expensive generation at any one time. Regardless of where the power comes from, all generators get paid that same high price. - The Conservative Party would separate generation companies from retailing. This would allow retailers to source wholesale electricity from any provider. This incentivises competition, reducing electricity prices for homes and businesses. **Underinvestment in New Generation:** Generation capacity growth has been slow. The Conservative Party would encourage investment in new generation to provide “base load” supply. - Encourage natural gas power generation once our own commercial supply of gas is established, providing base load supply when hydro lakes are low, or when wholesale energy prices are high. Resulting in reduced wholesale prices. - Encourage the expansion of geothermal generation. - Encourage new clean coal generation. | | |
