# Right to Life and Legal Recognition of the Unborn Child in the Third Trimester

**Policy principle**

The Conservative Party believes that every human life has inherent dignity and value. We recognise that legal protection for unborn life is an important matter of public discussion and that changes to the law should be developed carefully, transparently and with respect for both the unborn life and the health and rights of pregnant women. We will introduce legislation recognising an unborn child from the beginning of the third trimester, defined for the purposes of the legislation as 28 weeks gestation, as a distinct human life entitled to enhanced legal protection.

**Policy commitments**

1. **Legal personhood:** Introduce an Unborn Child (Legal Personhood and Rights) Act recognising an unborn child as a legal person from the last trimester, with rights and protections expressly defined by legislation.
2. **Right to life:** Establish the right to life of the unborn child as a fundamental legal interest that Parliament and the courts must take into account when interpreting and applying the law.
3. **Protection from unlawful harm:** Strengthen legal protections against the intentional unlawful killing, assault or injury of an unborn child.
4. **Protection of pregnant women:** Ensure that legislation does not criminalise a pregnant woman for the natural outcome of her pregnancy, miscarriage, or seeking necessary medical care. Establish clear protections for healthcare professionals providing lawful and necessary treatment.
5. **Support for mothers and families:** Expand practical support for women facing crisis pregnancies, including access to counselling, maternity care, financial assistance and adoption information, so that no woman feels compelled to end a pregnancy because she lacks support.

**Legislative objective**

The Conservative Party will seek to establish a coherent legal framework in which the unborn child is recognised as a human life deserving of legal protection, with clearly defined rights, responsibilities and safeguards. The legislation will expressly address how those rights interact with the rights and medical needs of the pregnant woman.

The Conservative Party recognises that an unplanned pregnancy can bring financial hardship, emotional distress, housing insecurity and uncertainty about the future. Women facing these circumstances deserve practical assistance, compassionate support and genuine choices.

We will establish a **Pregnancy and Family Support Programme** to help women who are pregnant and do not feel able or prepared to raise a child.

Our policy commitments are:

**1. Pregnancy Support Payment**

Provide eligible pregnant women experiencing financial hardship with targeted financial assistance during pregnancy to help meet essential costs, including food, maternity clothing, transport and medical appointments.

**2. Housing security**

Work with housing providers and community organisations to improve access to safe, temporary accommodation for pregnant women who are homeless, escaping violence or at risk of losing their housing.

**3. A dedicated pregnancy support service**

Establish a confidential service connecting pregnant women with practical assistance, independent counselling, maternity care, budgeting support, accommodation and relevant government benefits.

**4. Support for single mothers and families**

Improve access to parenting assistance, childcare, financial advice and community support, recognising that women should not face the costs and responsibilities of raising a child without help.

**6. Adoption and alternative care**

Ensure that women who do not wish or feel able to raise a child can access independent information about lawful adoption and other available care arrangements. Counselling must be non-coercive, and decisions must be made freely and with appropriate safeguards.

**7. Practical support beyond birth**

Provide eligible families with a clear pathway to ongoing assistance after childbirth, including access to health services, early childhood support, parenting programmes and financial assistance.

**Our commitment**

We believe that protecting unborn life must be accompanied by a commitment to support women in practical ways. We will work towards a New Zealand where a woman facing an unplanned pregnancy is not left to manage financial hardship, homelessness or fear alone, and where she has access to meaningful support to make an informed decision about her future.
