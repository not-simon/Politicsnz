# Press Release

24 June 2026

 

CONSERVATIVE PARTY NZ ENVIRONMENT POLICY

 

With a Maritime Zone approximately 15 times the size New

Zealand’s land area, the Conservative Party is committed to protecting

that vast natural environment.

 

Sustainable Fisheries Policy, developed and supported by all

political parties since 1980's, demonstrated early positive outcomes

protecting over-fishing.

 

Several other countries adopt variations of the policy.

 

By 2005 faults in the integrity of management were exposed.

 

The Conservative Party accepts the validity of claims that the

Sustainable Fisheries Policy is now widely abused.

 

To protect our marine environment, the Conservative Party resolves to

fight for a complete overhaul of the policy.

 

Priorities:

 

- Promote the Kermadec Ocean Sanctuary Bill.
- To establish a permanent marine protected area in New Zealand’s exclusive economic zone around the Kermadec Islands and to preserve it in its natural state.
- To ensure compliance with a reviewed Sustainable Fisheries Policy,

 including surveillance cameras to be reinstalled on all commercial fishing vessels.

Presented by Ross Meurant, Senior Policy Advisor.
