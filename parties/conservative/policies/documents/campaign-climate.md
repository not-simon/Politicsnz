# CONSERVATIVE PARTY "CLIMATE" POLICY

## **Background**

"Climate" policies (for example "Net Zero", new energy taxes, restrictions on fossil fuel use) are not based on science, but on the conjuring up of scary scenarios ("Global Warming").  Even the chief global body (the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change) now admits that the scariest scenarios (upon which all "climate" policies are based) are not supported by the data.

The USA, China, India, the OPEC countries - and many others - are not bound by "climate" policies.  Closer to home, the state of Queensland has abandoned "climate" policies (likely soon followed by the rest of Australia).

In Europe we see the fruits of "climate" policies.  Germany - long the industrial powerhouse of Europe - is committing industrial suicide as a result of dogmatic adherence to "climate" policies.  In the UK, we can also see how the ideology of "climate" inflicts economic harm: for example North Sea oil and gas taxes (royalties) are now at a crippling 75% (in the name of "climate"), causing BP to sell off their interests there.  "Climate" ideology directly causes harm: for example the National Health Service in England has recently prioritised the restriction of air conditioning above patient outcomes (as a result many blood samples have had to be destroyed, and blood tests will have to be re-done).

Health, wealth, and economic growth are reliant on cheap and abundant energy.  This has not changed since the days of the Industrial Revolution.

The driving idea behind all "climate" policies is not "saving the planet", but economic and social control.

A striking feature of "climate" policies (for example higher food and energy prices) is that they hurt the poor the most.

## **Our Policies**

Conservate Party continue to say that New Zealand should leave the Paris Agreement (and any other international agreements relating to "climate").

All taxes and restrictions on fossil fuels which are based on "climate" ideology should be scrapped.

New Zealand should encourage the cheap and abundant supply of all kinds of energy.  No form of energy, however, should enjoy state subsidy.

## All legislation and regulations based on "climate" ideology should be repealed or withdrawn.

All departments (or parts of departments) in government which deal with "climate" policies should be scrapped.  All "climate" policies followed by government departments and state owned enterprises should be withdrawn.

The result will be reduced food and energy prices (especially important for the those who are already struggling day to day), and a much more welcoming environment for agriculture, industry and transport.  All households and businesses will benefit.  And of course, lower taxes and fewer regulations.
