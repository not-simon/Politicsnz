# Conservative NZ Tax Relief Policies.

### **SUPERANNUATION**

\
 Currently, NZ Super itself counts as taxable income, meaning the net amount you receive depends on your total taxable.

Complementary to reducing company tax and reviewing personal taxation,

Conservatives NZ will remove NZ Super as a taxable income.

### Super annuitants who continue work after 65 years of age, will not have their superannuation taxed. **ACCOMMODATION EXPENSES**

Accommodation expenses for boarders in private dwellings will be excluded from taxable income.

Our policy will remove income tax on genuine home-sharing and boarder income, subject to reasonable safeguards against large-scale commercial accommodation businesses using the exemption.
