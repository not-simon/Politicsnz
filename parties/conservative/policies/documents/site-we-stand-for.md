# What We Stand For

​

We are Conservatives because we seek to preserve and promote the things that made our society great.

​​

​We believe in:

- Strong families
- Personal responsibility and freedom from government coercion
- A thriving economy
- Limited government
- Freedom of speech and assembly
- Local decision making
- Low inflation and a strong, stable currency
- Safe neighbourhoods and streets
- National sovereignty
- Honest democratic process
- Equality before the law
- Valuing life from conception to natural death
- Protecting children from radical gender ideology
- Ending climate change alarmism​
