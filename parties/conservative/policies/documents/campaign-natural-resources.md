# Natural Resources

## Securing our Future with Natural Resources

New Zealand’s natural resources belong to the New Zealand people. Successive governments have failed to secure a fair share of these resources or have allowed extraction for the benefit of overseas interests.

The Conservative Party’s Natural Resource Policy will give New Zealand control of our natural resources, deliver high returns, and secure our self-reliance.

Norway has used its oil and gas resources and created the worlds largest sovereign wealth fund - now worth around US$2.3 trillion. The fund owns about 1.5% of the world’s share markets. This is the model our scheme is based on.

If New Zealanders want meaningful investment in Health, Education, Superannuation, Infrastructure, and to return to surplus, this is the way to do it. We would promote investment into New Zealand and create high paid jobs directly and indirectly. This will incentivise many New Zealanders, with these specialised skills, to return home from overseas. 

## Development of our Resources

Introduce a natural resource joint venture scheme. This would be based on a public / private partnership based on the Norway model. Partnering with industry, not fighting it.

·  Actively encourage commercial oil and gas exploration.

·  Hold the returns in a New Zealand Investment Fund allowing the fund to grow and build a nest egg for future generations.

## Resource Taxes / Regulations

·  Implement a fair and reasonable taxation / royalty scheme across all New Zealand resource projects. Ensuring local communities that support projects benefit from them.

·  Withdraw from the Paris accord and disestablish the Emission Trading Scheme (ETS).

·  Apply environmental bonds from investors to projects. Bonds to be a fair sum to provide ecological restoration at the end of a project’s natural life. In the event of a resource company becoming insolvent, the money is then already there. So, the taxpayer is not lumped with a potential clean-up bill.

## Fuel Security and a Stronger Industry

·  Procure a fleet oil tankers. This guarantees our refined fuel supply while our own refining infrastructure is constructed. It also adds to our nations fuel storage capacity via “floating storage”.

·  Remove excessive regulation that slows project approvals while maintaining environmental protections. These assets must be engineered and constructed to prevent any damage to New Zealand’s pristine environment.

·  Once commercially viable oil and gas is established, begin construction of new oil refineries.

These should be able to refine our oil and oil sourced from around the Pacific. Unlike Marsden Point refinery that was designed to process overseas crude.
