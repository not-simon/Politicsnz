# Conservative Party NZ (Conservatives NZ) — 2026 election policies

Every policy of **Conservative Party NZ (Conservatives NZ)** for the 7 November 2026 general election that this archive could find: **19 policies** listed, backed by **27 full-text source documents**.

- Party website: <https://www.conservatives.nz/ (2026 campaign site: https://conservatives.org.nz/)>
- Registration: 6 October 2011 (Electoral Commission register)

## About this list

The Conservative Party NZ (called the New Conservatives in 2020–2023; leader Helen Houghton) has two websites: the party site conservatives.nz (policy summary, principles, and policy press releases from 2025–26) and a separate 2026 campaign site conservatives.org.nz, linked from the main site, with 14 policy pages (Overview, Health, Economy, Bureaucracy, Environment, Immigration, Policing, Climate, Agriculture, Child Protection, Tax Relief, Right to Life, Natural Resources, Energy). Both are captured; policies are listed below with the campaign page as the main source.

**Method.** Pages and PDFs were downloaded on 2026-10-23 by [`tools/policies_minor_sites_fetch.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_sites_fetch.py) / [`tools/policies_minor_opportunity_fetch.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_opportunity_fetch.py) (raw copies in `raw/party-docs/conservative/`) and reduced to their main text in [`documents/`](documents/) (markup and whitespace normalised only). The list and summaries are built by [`tools/policies_minor_build.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_build.py) from [`tools/policies_minor_catalog.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_catalog.py).

policy.nz publishes no policy statements for this party, so every summary here is marked *archive*: written for this archive from the linked full text in one to three neutral sentences ("Why" = the party's own stated reason, attributed; "How" = what it says it would do). Party claims (costs, statistics) are reported as the party's claims, not verified.

**Gaps and caveats.** Wix blog posts on conservatives.nz are rendered client-side; their text was recovered from the page's embedded data. The campaign Energy page's layout table extracted poorly (text is present but jumbled). Tax wording differs: the economy page says 'lower tax rate', a 2025 press release says 'flat tax rate'.

## Community & inclusion

### Employment and services based on merit, 'one law for all', and 'a man is a man, and a woman is a woman' (CON-19)

**Why** (*archive*): The party opposes policies that divide people by race, ancestry, sex or identity.

**How** (*archive*): Listed among its 'policies that work' alongside limiting unskilled immigration and defending free speech and Judeo-Christian traditions.

Full text: [Our Policies](documents/site-policy.md) ([original](https://www.conservatives.nz/policy)); [Overview (2026 campaign site)](documents/campaign-overview.md) ([original](https://conservatives.org.nz/policies/overview/))

## Defence & foreign affairs

### Review all trade agreements and pursue a neutral foreign policy with a support- and reconstruction-focused military (CON-18)

**How** (*archive*): Seek a balance between free trade and protectionism ('Trade before War'); avoid being drawn into conflicts that do not threaten NZ territory.

Full text: [Economic and Trade Policy](documents/news-new-policies-announcement.md) ([original](https://www.conservatives.nz/news/new-policies-announcement))

## Economy

### Oppose converting farmland to carbon forestry and oppose the Gene Technology Bill (CON-10)

**How** (*archive*): No incentives for permanent carbon forestry on productive land; keep restrictions on GMO field trials and release; revisit the Planning and Natural Environment Bills to make them workable and protect property rights.

Full text: [Agriculture (2026 campaign site)](documents/campaign-agriculture.md) ([original](https://conservatives.org.nz/policies/agriculture/)); [Policy clarification on rural, environmental, and agricultural issues](documents/news-policy-clarification-on-rural-environmental-and-agricultural-issues.md) ([original](https://www.conservatives.nz/news/policy-clarification-on-rural%2C-environmental%2C-and-agricultural-issues))

### Encourage oil and gas exploration with a Norway-style resource joint venture and sovereign investment fund (CON-11)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says New Zealand has failed to secure a fair share of its resources.

**How** (*archive*): Public-private resource partnerships, royalties shared with local communities, environmental bonds, returns held in a New Zealand Investment Fund.

Full text: [Natural Resources (2026 campaign site)](documents/campaign-natural-resources.md) ([original](https://conservatives.org.nz/policies/natural-resources/))

## Environment

### Leave the Paris Agreement, scrap the ETS and repeal all 'climate'-based taxes, laws and agencies (CON-09)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says there is no climate emergency and that climate policies raise food and energy costs and hurt the poor most.

**How** (*archive*): Remove fossil-fuel taxes and restrictions, abolish climate units in government, end energy subsidies of any kind, and stop public funding of methane research such as AgriZero.

Full text: [Climate (2026 campaign site)](documents/campaign-climate.md) ([original](https://conservatives.org.nz/policies/climate/)); [Agriculture (2026 campaign site)](documents/campaign-agriculture.md) ([original](https://conservatives.org.nz/policies/agriculture/))

### Create a Kermadec Ocean Sanctuary and overhaul fisheries management with cameras on all commercial vessels (CON-14)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says the sustainable fisheries regime is widely abused.

**How** (*archive*): Promote the Kermadec Ocean Sanctuary Bill and reinstall surveillance cameras on commercial vessels.

Full text: [Environment (2026 campaign site)](documents/campaign-environment.md) ([original](https://conservatives.org.nz/policies/environment/)); [Has New Zealand Failed Its Oceans? Conservative Party Says Yes - Environment Policy update](documents/news-has-new-zealand-failed-its-oceans.md) ([original](https://www.conservatives.nz/news/has-new-zealand-failed-its-oceans))

## Health

### Make cutting hospital waiting times the top health priority, funded from bureaucracy, procurement and productivity savings (CON-04)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says the key issue is whether patients are treated in time, not how much is spent.

**How** (*archive*): Measure waits for elective surgery, specialists, diagnostics, cancer and ED; buy private capacity where it is quicker and competitive; grow the domestic workforce; link any extra funding to measurable results.

Full text: [Health (2026 campaign site)](documents/campaign-health.md) ([original](https://conservatives.org.nz/policies/health/))

### Restrict irreversible gender-related medical procedures for minors and require parental involvement (CON-16)

**How** (*archive*): Independent assessment before significant intervention, evidence-based practice, parental involvement subject to safeguards, and independent review mechanisms.

Full text: [Child Protection (2026 campaign site)](documents/campaign-child-protection.md) ([original](https://conservatives.org.nz/policies/child-protection/))

### Recognise an unborn child as a legal person from the third trimester (CON-17)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says every human life has inherent dignity.

**How** (*archive*): An Unborn Child (Legal Personhood and Rights) Act with a protected right to life, without criminalising pregnant women, plus a Pregnancy and Family Support Programme (support payment, housing security, support services, adoption options).

Full text: [Right to life (2026 campaign site)](documents/campaign-right-to-life.md) ([original](https://conservatives.org.nz/policies/right-to-life/))

## Incomes & employment

### Cut personal and company tax, align the company rate with the top personal rate, and lower GST to 12.5% (CON-01)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says lower taxes improve compliance and investment and that New Zealand's rates should be no higher than Australia's.

**How** (*archive*): Also tackle public debt; a 2025 release calls for a 'flat tax rate' for individuals and companies.

Full text: [Economy (2026 campaign site)](documents/campaign-economy-1.md) ([original](https://conservatives.org.nz/policies/economy-1/)); [Economic and Trade Policy](documents/news-new-policies-announcement.md) ([original](https://www.conservatives.nz/news/new-policies-announcement))

### Stop taxing NZ Super (including for over-65s still working) and boarders' income in private homes (CON-02)

**How** (*archive*): Remove NZ Super from taxable income, and exempt genuine home-sharing/boarder income with safeguards against commercial use.

Full text: [Tax Relief (2026 campaign site)](documents/campaign-superannuation.md) ([original](https://conservatives.org.nz/policies/superannuation/))

## Law, justice & government

### Let mayors become full-time council CEOs and give ministers power to appoint and sack senior public servants (CON-03)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says unelected bureaucrats and advisers usurp the mandate of elected representatives.

**How** (*archive*): Elected representatives at local and national level would get wide powers to dismiss obstructive or incompetent staff; legislate to curtail 'unconstitutional bureaucratic power'.

Full text: [Bureaucracy (2026 campaign site)](documents/campaign-bureaucracy.md) ([original](https://conservatives.org.nz/policies/bureaucracy/)); [Has New Zealand Lost Control of Its Own Government?](documents/news-has-new-zealand-lost-control-of-its-own-government.md) ([original](https://www.conservatives.nz/news/has-new-zealand-lost-control-of-its-own-government))

### Hold a Royal Commission of Inquiry into police under-performance and corruption (CON-06)

**Why** (*archive*): The party cites fabricated drink-drive tests and lost public trust.

**How** (*archive*): Also: refer prima facie criminal allegations against police and all deaths caused by police action to the courts; protect identities of police witnesses in international drug cases; review drone laws.

Full text: [Policing (2026 campaign site)](documents/campaign-policing.md) ([original](https://conservatives.org.nz/policies/policing/)); [Announcement: Police Policy - Commission Of Inquiry Into Under Performance And Corruption](documents/news-police-policy-announcement.md) ([original](https://www.conservatives.nz/news/police-policy-announcement))

### Strengthen the IPCA: double its staff, quadruple its budget, and let it prosecute and name officers (CON-07)

**How** (*archive*): As set out in its 'Sharpening the Teeth of the IPCA' statement.

Full text: [Law & Justice Policy Statement: Sharpening the Teeth of the IPCA](documents/news-sharpening-the-teeth-of-the-ipca.md) ([original](https://www.conservatives.nz/news/sharpening-the-teeth-of-the-ipca))

### Improve access to justice for self-represented litigants (CON-08)

**How** (*archive*): Procedural changes, support services, legal aid reform, tax deductions and cost awards for pro bono work, and wider Family Dispute Resolution.

Full text: [Improving Access to Justice for Self-Represented Litigants](documents/news-press-release-improving-access-to-justice-for-self-represented-litigan.md) ([original](https://www.conservatives.nz/news/press-release---improving-access-to-justice-for-self-represented-litigants))

### Increase penalties for child sexual offending and online exploitation (CON-15)

**How** (*archive*): Longer sentences for serious and repeat offenders, review of sentence reductions, better monitoring after release, more online-exploitation investigation resources and victim support.

Full text: [Child Protection (2026 campaign site)](documents/campaign-child-protection.md) ([original](https://conservatives.org.nz/policies/child-protection/))

## Migration

### Tighten immigration toward skilled migrants and cap other net migration by the supply of housing, doctors and school places (CON-05)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says population growing faster than 'social goods' makes everyone poorer.

**How** (*archive*): A stable points system; end 'golden' investor visas; NZ licence tests for foreign truck drivers; tougher document checks and audits; report GDP per capita.

Full text: [Immigration (2026 campaign site)](documents/campaign-immigration.md) ([original](https://conservatives.org.nz/policies/immigration/))

## Transport & infrastructure

### Buy a fleet of oil tankers and build new oil refineries for fuel security (CON-12)

**How** (*archive*): Tankers would guarantee supply and floating storage until refineries able to process local and Pacific crude are built; also bring back manufacturing.

Full text: [Natural Resources (2026 campaign site)](documents/campaign-natural-resources.md) ([original](https://conservatives.org.nz/policies/natural-resources/)); [Agriculture (2026 campaign site)](documents/campaign-agriculture.md) ([original](https://conservatives.org.nz/policies/agriculture/))

### Ensure 'cheap, reliable and abundant' energy without relying solely on solar and wind (CON-13)

**How** (*archive*): Renewables have a role but cannot be relied on alone because of variability.

Full text: [Energy (2026 campaign site)](documents/campaign-energy.md) ([original](https://conservatives.org.nz/policies/energy/))
