## About this list

Rather than a policy programme it published a 7 October 2026 media release proposing a national conversation about governance without parties, plus a list of principles. ## Law, justice & government

### Start a national conversation about governing without political parties ('Exit politics') (NAP-01)

**Why** (*archive*): NAP says parties force voters into 'package deals' and the system is 'better at producing political careers than producing good decisions'.

**How** (*archive*): Experiment with citizens' assemblies, deliberative democracy, local decision-making, participatory budgeting, digital consultation and rotating civic representation, with New Zealand as 'a laboratory for innovation in governance'.

Full text: [Not A Party has a radical proposal — leave politics behind (media release, 7 Oct 2026)](documents/media-release-2026-10-07-exit-politics.md) ([original](https://nap.org.nz/))

### Move peacefully to a society based on voluntary cooperation, guided by principles of self-sovereignty and self-government (NAP-02)

**How** (*archive*): Its listed principles: self-sovereignty, self-government, self-ownership, self-reliance, self-defence, self-control, self-respect, self-help, solidarity and 'safer communities together'.

Full text: [Principles](documents/principles.md) ([original](https://nap.org.nz/principles/)); [Beyond Democracy](documents/beyond-democracy.md) ([original](https://nap.org.nz/beyond-democracy/))
