## About this list

The Te Tai Tokerau Party was launched by Te Tai Tokerau MP Mariameno Kapa-Kingi in May 2026 after her split from Te Pāti Māori. Its whole platform is published on one page, "TTP Pātaka Policy", organised in five pillars (Whānau, Whare, Whenua, Tino Rangatiratanga, Ōhanga Ā-Rohe). Each named policy on that page is listed below. The page does not give costings except where noted.

## Community & inclusion

### Pass Te Ture Mokopuna (the Mokopuna Act) requiring government decisions to consider current and future generations of mokopuna (TTP-01)

**Why** (*archive*): The party calls this its flagship policy, modelled on Wales's Well-being of Future Generations Act 2015 and grounded in mātauranga Māori and Te Tiriti.

**How** (*archive*): Public bodies would be bound by a 'Mokopuna Principle', report annually against mokopuna wellbeing goals, carry out Mokopuna Impact Assessments on significant laws, Budgets and projects, and be monitored by an independent Commissioner for Mokopuna using co-designed national indicators.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

### Provide free solar power, home-delivered kai and paid cultural roles for kaumātua (TTP-07)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says many kaumātua live in energy poverty and are cut off from whānau and tribal roles.

**How** (*archive*): Prioritise elder-occupied homes for fully funded solar and batteries on community grids with zero-cost baseline electricity; monthly fresh-food prescription vouchers delivered to homes; paid mentorship roles for kaumātua and kuia; and paid, trained in-home carers drawn from local whānau ('Ringa Manaaki Dividend').

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

## Economy

### Build Local Business Incubators in Whangārei, Kaikohe and Kaitaia with a five-year 100% tax holiday for start-ups (TTP-19)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says young people leave the North because it lacks high-spec commercial infrastructure.

**How** (*archive*): Funded co-working spaces with fibre, fabrication labs and studios; businesses based in the incubators would pay no 'regional corporate income tax' for five years if local payroll and staffing targets are met; plus mentoring and seed grants.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

### Require government agencies to buy 10% (rising to 15% by 2030) of goods and services from Māori-owned and local businesses (TTP-20)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says state money spent in the regions should stay there.

**How** (*archive*): A 'Social & Indigenous Procurement Mandate' for schools, hospitals and construction, a 35% local Māori employment and 50% youth/apprenticeship target on regional projects, and 'Farm to School' purchasing of Māori-grown food.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

### Create a Tai Tokerau Fund modelled on the Provincial Growth Fund, specific to iwi (details in development) (TTP-22)

**How** (*archive*): The party lists a regional, iwi-specific fund modelled on the Provincial Growth Fund; its page says full detail is still in development.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

## Environment

### Set up a $150m-a-year Kaitiaki Guardians Network with statutory environmental enforcement powers (TTP-09)

**Why** (*archive*): The party wants Māori to move from being 'consulted' on the environment to being its legal 'eyes and ears'.

**How** (*archive*): A $150 million annual Crown appropriation for iwi- and hapū-led guardian teams with statutory power to monitor and enforce environmental rules in their rohe, including issuing protection notices for urupā, marae and biodiversity hotspots.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

### Create a Whenua Climate Restoration Fund giving iwi first right of refusal to buy back low-lying and coastal land, and a Māori Biodiversity Credit System (TTP-10)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says climate adaptation needs space and that iwi should control ecosystems that buffer against sea-level rise and food insecurity.

**How** (*archive*): A fund backing a compulsory first right of refusal for iwi to buy ecologically significant low-lying and coastal land, and Crown payments to whānau and iwi for carbon sequestration and ecosystem services from restored forests and wetlands.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

### Make marae official off-grid Civil Defence hubs with funded energy, water, stockpiles and training (TTP-11)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says marae already open their doors in emergencies long before state agencies arrive.

**How** (*archive*): Fund solar, batteries, rainwater collection and filtration at designated marae; stock emergency supplies and satellite communications; and legally designate marae as co-equal Civil Defence hubs under NEMA with funded emergency training for whānau.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

### Replace marine law with a Moana Ora Act protecting 30% of territorial waters through iwi/hapū-managed areas (TTP-12)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says current marine management is fragmented and ignores customary rights.

**How** (*archive*): Repeal and replace marine legislation with an act mandating Indigenous Protected and Conserved Areas, a binding 30% protection target co-designed and managed by iwi/hapū (including seasonal rāhui), an end to destructive bottom trawling and secured customary fishing rights.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

## Health

### Establish Te Aka Motuhake, a fully iwi-governed Māori health commissioning authority, plus a bonded iwi health scholarship scheme (TTP-05)

**Why** (*archive*): The party rejects the mainstreaming of indigenous health structures and wants to fix the Māori health workforce shortage.

**How** (*archive*): An autonomous, state-funded but iwi-governed health authority controlling clinical design, regional funding and provider licensing; an uncapped scholarship bonding graduates to Māori, iwi or rural providers for at least four years; and more diagnostic, preventative and dental services delivered from tribal hubs.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

## Housing

### Train local rangatahi to build hempcrete homes on papakāinga through a Rohe Construction Academy (TTP-08)

**Why** (*archive*): The party links overcrowded, damp housing to high rates of rheumatic fever and respiratory illness and says imported materials make building expensive.

**How** (*archive*): A funded trade academy in Te Tai Tokerau paying trainees a living wage; regional plants turning local hemp hurd and lime into certified building panels; and homes built on ancestral land and papakāinga, allocated first to overcrowded families and bypassing commercial developers.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

## Incomes & employment

### Wipe low-income whānau debt to MSD and IRD and replace the benefit system with a Universal Whānau Allowance (TTP-03)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says whānau cannot retrain or innovate while being penalised by MSD, and that the current system creates a poverty trap.

**How** (*archive*): An immediate amnesty on about $2 billion of debt owed by low-income whānau to MSD and IRD; a simplified, non-taxable Universal Whānau Allowance for people in training or caregiving; and an end to 'relationship status' policing in the benefit system.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

### Fund 'Backyard Sovereignty Kits' (garden beds, chickens, rain tanks) and deregulate small-scale local food trade (TTP-06)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says supermarket prices are crippling for Northland whānau and that home food production should be treated as regional infrastructure.

**How** (*archive*): Funded flat-pack kits with raised beds, soil, seeds, a predator-proof coop and laying hens; rain-water catchment and irrigation; mobile gardening mentors; and an exemption of hyper-local household food barter and sales from council licensing and transaction taxes.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

### Fund community-owned Kai Sovereignty food hubs and require Northland supermarkets to give 30% of shelf space to local produce (TTP-21)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says the supermarket duopoly trucks northern produce to Auckland and back with large markups.

**How** (*archive*): Fund regional processing hubs (kitchens, cold storage, butchery, packing), support direct-to-consumer networks, and require supermarkets in Te Tai Tokerau to allocate at least 30% of shelf space to locally grown unprocessed food bought from regional suppliers.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

## Law, justice & government

### Adopt a 'prison sinking lid' that diverts non-violent offenders with addiction or mental-health needs into hapū-run rehabilitation (TTP-04)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says each prisoner costs over $150,000 a year while reoffending stays high, and that rural communities lack preventative resources.

**How** (*archive*): Corrections savings from each bed vacated through health-focused diversion would be legally transferred to local hapū-led rehabilitation trusts running secure, alcohol- and drug-free 'healing sanctuaries', with mandatory restorative work programmes for those diverted.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

### Pass a Māori Justice & Autonomous Policing Act transferring youth-justice funding to iwi-governed authorities (TTP-18)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says state-controlled corrections and punitive policing entrench institutional inequity.

**How** (*archive*): Transfer regional youth-justice budgets to iwi-governed, marae-anchored authorities and fund iwi-administered community safety services under tribal jurisdiction, with restorative justice replacing incarceration.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

## Te ao Māori

### Create a Crown–Iwi Investment Fund and give mana whenua a 25% equity stake in major regional infrastructure (TTP-02)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says the state should co-invest in iwi- and hapū-owned assets so whānau become owners in the value chain rather than low-wage labour.

**How** (*archive*): A Crown–Iwi Investment Fund for 'transition infrastructure' (renewable energy, green hydrogen, digital); major regional projects such as wind farms would have to offer at least 25% equity to local mana whenua, funded by low-interest government loans.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

### Constitutionally protect He Whakaputanga and Te Tiriti, and repeal the Regulatory Standards Act 2025 and the Treaty Clauses Review (TTP-13)

**Why** (*archive*): The party notes that both founding documents were signed in Te Tai Tokerau and wants protection against legislative harm to them.

**How** (*archive*): Advance a Tiriti-centred constitution led by community voices, and, as a priority, vote to repeal the Regulatory Standards Act 2025 and the Treaty Clauses Review.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

### Increase and entrench Waitangi Tribunal funding and make its findings binding on the Crown beyond specific land types (TTP-14)

**Why** (*archive*): The party sees the Tribunal as a forum for justice relied on by whānau.

**How** (*archive*): Raise and entrench funding (amount to be set after talks with the Tribunal and whānau) and extend the Tribunal's binding jurisdiction over the Crown.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

### Fund tangata whenua-led Te Tiriti education in the curriculum, the public service and for all MPs (TTP-15)

**How** (*archive*): More funding for Te Tiriti education led by tangata whenua, embedded in the NZ Curriculum and in public-service professional development, including for all sitting MPs.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

### Redirect 20% of Crown revenue from conservation and public land into a Māori-governed land-buyback fund (TTP-16)

**Why** (*archive*): The party wants to replace lengthy land-return negotiations with automatic, revenue-backed mechanisms.

**How** (*archive*): Legislate a mandatory 20% redirection of Crown revenues from conservation and public lands into a Māori-governed Land Acquisition Fund, remove LINZ's disposal authority and automatically return unused public-works land to its original owners, with a right of first refusal to buy back private land.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))

### Create Te Whare Tohu Tātai, an independent Māori data and Crown-compliance commission (TTP-17)

**How** (*archive*): A statutory commission to monitor Crown compliance with all historical settlements, with ownership and control of all state data about Māori whānau and enforcement powers.

Full text: [TTP Pātaka Policy (Our Policies)](documents/ttp-pataka-policy.md) ([original](https://tetaitokerauparty.org.nz/our-policies/))
