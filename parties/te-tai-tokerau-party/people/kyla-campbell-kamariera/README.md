## Current status

**#1** on the list of the Te Tai Tokerau Party, which Mariameno Kapa-Kingi founded, for 2026. Never an MP.

## Biography

Raised in Mitimiti; of Te Rarawa, Te Aupōuri and Taranaki Tūturu descent. Her mother and grandmother were killed in 2017, and she had a brain tumour removed the same year. She studied for a master's in Māori and Indigenous Studies at the University of Waikato, where she was president of the Waikato Students' Union. In 2025 she was one of nine rangatahi Māori in the iwi delegation to the COP30 climate conference.

## Political career

- Ranked #23 on Te Pāti Māori's list in 2023.
- As co-chair of Te Pāti Māori's Te Tai Tokerau electorate committee in November 2025, she backed Kapa-Kingi against party president John Tamihere's call for her to step aside.
- Announced in September 2026 as a list candidate for the Te Tai Tokerau Party.
