### Why I'm standing

I believe that we have so many broken systems from councils, health, education, Police and the judicial system. There needs to be some accountability. Someone asking the hard questions all the time, not just at election time or at point Break, or when it's safe to jump on board after someone else has tested the waters.

### About me

I've lived in our community for a large part of my life. I grew up living on and around farming, in the second largest pine forest in NZ. I completed my Engineering trade in 1994. Then spent about 15 years logging and also engineering in-between. I spent 2 years in western Australia on large grain farm. I have owned a globally successful engineering business since 2009. I'm a solution provider to coal, cement, fishing, hops, gold, horticulture and road transport.
