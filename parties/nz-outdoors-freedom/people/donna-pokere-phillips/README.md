## Current status

NZ Outdoors & Freedom candidate for **Hamilton West** (#6 on its list) in 2026. Never an MP.

## Biography

Of Tainui, Ngāti Tūwharetoa and Taranaki descent; born in Taranaki; LLM from the University of Waikato (2004). Acted as legal adviser to Brian Te Huia, who alleged abuse in state care (2021).

## Political career

- Stood for the Alliance (Port Waikato, 1999), TOP (Hamilton West, 2017; TOP board member until 2019), Te Pāti Māori (Hauraki-Waikato, 2020) and Freedoms NZ (2023).
- Co-leader of NZ Outdoors & Freedom (2022–2025); fourth for Hamilton mayor (2022); sixth in the 2022 Hamilton West by-election.

## Controversies

- Wikipedia describes her views as conspiracy-driven; in 2020 she accused the government of "ethnic cleansing" over its COVID-19 response, and was arrested for trespass at a roading protest. In 2019 she publicly accused TOP leader Geoff Simmons over party finances, which he rejected.
