## History

The Outdoors Party stood four candidates in 2017 (0.06%). After the Ban 1080 Party deregistered, many of its
supporters joined, and anti-1080 lawyer **Sue Grey** became co-leader around the start of 2020. During 2020 it called the
COVID-19 lockdown "cruel and unreasonable" and compared the government to the Nazi Party; an Auckland rally in June 2020
featured 9/11 "false flag", flat-earth and anti-5G claims and the harassment of a young Asian woman (Grey said those
involved were not party members). Several candidates defected before the 2020 election (0.11%). [1]

It renamed itself NZ Outdoors & Freedom in April 2022. Grey won 1,030 votes (4th) in the 2022 Tauranga by-election.
In May 2023 it joined Brian Tamaki's [Freedoms NZ](../freedoms-nz/README.md) umbrella, Grey becoming co-leader with
Tamaki; the party vote in 2023 was therefore cast for Freedoms NZ (0.34%), while Grey stood for Outdoors & Freedom in
West Coast-Tasman (7th of 9). After Freedoms NZ deregistered in May 2025 the party continued on its own under Grey. [1][2]

In the 2026 campaign, a group of the party's anti-vaccination activists confronted Labour MP Ayesha Verrall at a
Wadestown candidates' meeting on 8 September; former ACT marketing director John Ansell reportedly accused her of mass
murder while livestreaming. [3]
