# Cannabis Policy

## Full text (verbatim)

## Cannabis Policy

## Cannabis for health

We support the medicinal use of cannabis plant, CBD and other cannabis products for humans, pets and livestock and acknowledge the important role of cannabinoids in supplementing the endocannabinoid system and promoting wellbeing.

## Cannabis cultivation for personal use

We do not believe cultivation or possession of cannabis for personal use should be a criminal offense.

## Support for New Zealand medicinal cannabis growers

We support home grown medicinal cannabis and will encourage small New Zealand owned companies.

\
We discourage the capture of cannabis by Big Phama and the imposition of excessive licensing fees that will block access to family and community based suppliers.

## Update legislation to support NZ grown hemp

We support NZ growers of hemp and the sale of hemp products as well as the development of new hemp products.
