## About this list

The NZ Outdoors & Freedom Party (leader Sue Grey) now brands itself 'freedom'. Its Policies page links ten policy pages: Direct Democracy (its 'new core pillar'), Agriculture, Housing, Open Source First, Cannabis, Anti-Bullying, the ACC Justice & Accountability statement, Education, Fisheries, and Outdoors Recreation & Wildlife. Each distinct commitment on those pages is listed below.

**Method.** Pages and PDFs were downloaded on 2026-10-23 by [`tools/policies_minor_sites_fetch.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_sites_fetch.py) / [`tools/policies_minor_opportunity_fetch.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_opportunity_fetch.py) (raw copies in `raw/party-docs/nz-outdoors-freedom/`) and reduced to their main text in [`documents/`](documents/) (markup and whitespace normalised only). The list and summaries are built by [`tools/policies_minor_build.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_build.py) from [`tools/policies_minor_catalog.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_catalog.py).

policy.nz publishes no policy statements for this party, so every summary here is marked *archive*: written for this archive from the linked full text in one to three neutral sentences ("Why" = the party's own stated reason, attributed; "How" = what it says it would do). Party claims (costs, statistics) are reported as the party's claims, not verified.

**Gaps and caveats.** The home page renders almost no text without JavaScript (captured as-is). Pages are undated; costings appear only in the ACC statement. Positions on 1080, fluoride and 5G described in media coverage were not found on the current policy pages, so are not listed.

## Defence & foreign affairs

### Leave the CPTPP (TPPA) and RCEP and pursue 'fair trade' bilateral deals (OFP-06)

**How** (*archive*): Withdraw from agreements that limit New Zealand's ability to legislate, increase Pacific trade, strengthen anti-monopoly law and place a moratorium on sales of farmland, forestry, housing and infrastructure to non-tax-residents.

Full text: [Agriculture Policy](documents/agriculture-policy.md) ([original](https://freedom.kiwi/agriculture-policy))

## Economy

### Adopt an 'Open Source First' rule for government software procurement (OFP-10)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says dependence on multinational software vendors is a hidden import cost and a resilience risk.

**How** (*archive*): Agencies must check for a suitable open-source option before buying proprietary software, compare whole-of-life costs, and use New Zealand providers for support.

Full text: [Open Source First: Keeping New Zealand' s Digital Wealth in New Zealand](documents/open-source-policy.md) ([original](https://freedom.kiwi/open-source-policy))

### Replace the fisheries Quota Management System with a LegaSea-style system keeping fisheries in public ownership (OFP-17)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says the QMS has depleted fish stocks, privatised a public resource and priced locals out.

**How** (*archive*): Tendered commercial permits with no private sale of fishing rights, catches landed and processed in NZ, only NZ-owned operators, fleet separation in the territorial sea, and more recreational and customary inshore access.

Full text: [Fisheries Policy](documents/nzofp-fisheries-policy.md) ([original](https://freedom.kiwi/nzofp-fisheries-policy))

## Education

### Make early childhood centres and primary schools Wi-Fi free, using wired connections (OFP-15)

**How** (*archive*): Minimise Wi-Fi in high schools too and work with Chorus on wired fibre connections.

Full text: [Education Policy](documents/education-policy.md) ([original](https://freedom.kiwi/education-policy))

### Invest heavily in education with an outdoors- and community-focused curriculum, implementing the Tomorrow's Schools Review (OFP-16)

Full text: [Education Policy](documents/education-policy.md) ([original](https://freedom.kiwi/education-policy))

## Environment

### Withdraw from the Paris Agreement and focus on flood management and carbon-rich soils (OFP-04)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says farm animals cannot release more carbon than they eat and that extreme weather is 'nature, not climate change'.

**How** (*archive*): Exit Paris (it says staying would cost more than leaving), maintain river flows and channels to reduce flood risk, and support ecological agriculture with minimal synthetic inputs.

Full text: [Agriculture Policy](documents/agriculture-policy.md) ([original](https://freedom.kiwi/agriculture-policy))

### Replace the EPA with an independent Health-Environment Protection Agency and apply the precautionary principle to new technology (OFP-05)

**How** (*archive*): New technologies and products only with full liability for the business introducing them; independent biosecurity at every entry point.

Full text: [Agriculture Policy](documents/agriculture-policy.md) ([original](https://freedom.kiwi/agriculture-policy))

### Replace DOC with a Department of Outdoors – Wildlife, Recreation & Nature (DOWRN) (OFP-18)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says governments have undervalued New Zealanders' outdoors culture and access.

**How** (*archive*): A department promoting access to all public land, coasts and rivers, poison-free conservation (trapping rather than poisons), food gathering on public land, and early public engagement on access decisions.

Full text: [Recreation & Wildlife Policy](documents/outdoors-recreation-and-wildlife-policy.md) ([original](https://freedom.kiwi/outdoors-recreation-and-wildlife-policy))

## Health

### Decriminalise personal cannabis use and cultivation and support medicinal cannabis and hemp (OFP-11)

**How** (*archive*): No criminal offence for personal possession or growing; support home-grown medicinal cannabis and small NZ suppliers over 'Big Pharma', with lower licensing barriers; update law to support hemp products.

Full text: [Cannabis Policy](documents/cannabis-policy.md) ([original](https://freedom.kiwi/cannabis-policy))

### Create a nationwide anti-bullying strategy treating bullying as a public-health issue (OFP-12)

**How** (*archive*): Awareness programmes in schools and communities, leadership training for teachers and managers, bullying questions in routine health screening, and treatment of psychological effects.

Full text: [Anti Bullying Policy](documents/anti-bullying-policy.md) ([original](https://freedom.kiwi/anti-bullying-policy))

### Make one doctor's injury diagnosis final for ACC and abolish ACC's review and dispute machinery (OFP-13)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says ACC 'expert shopping' is used to decline claims.

**How** (*archive*): Disestablish ACC review specialists and investigators, cancel contracts with FairWay and ICRA, stop paying external consultants to contest diagnoses; it claims $61.5m a year savings to redirect to surgery, rehabilitation and disability support.

Full text: [ACC Policy Statement](documents/the-acc-justice-accountability-policy-statement.md) ([original](https://freedom.kiwi/the-acc-justice-accountability-policy-statement))

### Pursue Pfizer for breach of contract over Covid-19 vaccines and recover ACC vaccine-injury costs (OFP-14)

**How** (*archive*): Release the full NZ Pfizer contract, bring a Crown claim to recover the $16.8m ACC has paid for vaccine injuries, guarantee ACC compensation for long-term vaccine harm, give ACC a statutory right of recourse against manufacturers, and review ACC law to cover distress and loss of enjoyment of life.

Full text: [ACC Policy Statement](documents/the-acc-justice-accountability-policy-statement.md) ([original](https://freedom.kiwi/the-acc-justice-accountability-policy-statement))

## Housing

### Free up land and revise the RMA to speed house building, with a mix of public and private housing (OFP-07)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says every citizen should have access to an affordable, warm, dry home.

**How** (*archive*): Identify land for subdivision, sequence servicing and construction, and have the Ministry of Housing take a hands-on role.

Full text: [Housing Policy](documents/housing-policy.md) ([original](https://freedom.kiwi/housing-policy))

### Require high standards for new homes (R5 insulation, rainwater and greywater systems, energy efficiency) and fund hempcrete building (OFP-08)

**How** (*archive*): Also solar or micro-generation, green space for gardens, recycling of building materials, lower consent costs for sustainable materials, and a funding scheme for hempcrete homes.

Full text: [Housing Policy](documents/housing-policy.md) ([original](https://freedom.kiwi/housing-policy))

### Fully fund night shelters and low-cost living spaces for homeless people (OFP-09)

**How** (*archive*): Shelters would provide two nutritious meals a day and supplements; the party also wants to restore New Zealand's leadership in social housing.

Full text: [Housing Policy](documents/housing-policy.md) ([original](https://freedom.kiwi/housing-policy))

## Law, justice & government

### Entrench the NZ Bill of Rights Act as supreme law (OFP-01)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says Covid-era measures showed fundamental rights could be overridden by a simple parliamentary majority and that select-committee consultation is 'a toothless illusion'.

**How** (*archive*): Make the Bill of Rights the constitutional bedrock so no government or emergency declaration can override rights such as free speech, movement, assembly and the right to refuse medical treatment.

Full text: [DIRECT DEMOcracy](documents/direct-democracy.md) ([original](https://freedom.kiwi/direct-democracy))

### Introduce Swiss-style binding citizens' referendums: a citizens' veto on laws and citizen-initiated laws (OFP-02)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says Covid-era measures showed fundamental rights could be overridden by a simple parliamentary majority and that select-committee consultation is 'a toothless illusion'.

**How** (*archive*): Citizens could gather signatures to force a binding nationwide vote to strike down a law, or to put their own draft law on the ballot, with a lower signature threshold than the current 10% for non-binding referendums.

Full text: [DIRECT DEMOcracy](documents/direct-democracy.md) ([original](https://freedom.kiwi/direct-democracy))

### Decentralise government under a 'subsidiarity' principle and let regions keep part of the GST and tax raised locally (OFP-03)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says Covid-era measures showed fundamental rights could be overridden by a simple parliamentary majority and that select-committee consultation is 'a toothless illusion'.

**How** (*archive*): Return decisions on local infrastructure, land management and regional development to communities, shrink central ministries and give regions financial autonomy.

Full text: [DIRECT DEMOcracy](documents/direct-democracy.md) ([original](https://freedom.kiwi/direct-democracy))
