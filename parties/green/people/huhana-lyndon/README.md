## Current status

Green list MP since 2023. Standing in the **Te Tai Tokerau** Māori electorate and ranked **#7** on the Green list for 2026.

## Biography

Hūhana Melanie Lyndon (Ngātiwai, Ngāti Hine, Ngāti Whātua, Waikato-Tainui, Hauraki) was raised in the Pīpīwai valley west of Whangārei. She holds a BA (Victoria University of Wellington), a postgraduate diploma in education (Auckland) and an MBA (Massey). She was chief executive of the Ngāti Hine Forestry Trust (2019–2021) and of the Ngātiwai Trust Board (from 2021). Mother of three daughters.

## Political career

- Fifth in a 2017 Whangārei District Council by-election (Denby Ward).
- Green candidate for Te Tai Tokerau in 2023 (third, 4,187 votes); elected on the list.
- Green spokesperson for health, Whānau Ora, Māori development and forestry from November 2023.

## Record & notable actions

- Before Parliament, wrote about marae carrying the burden of the Cyclone Gabrielle response in Northland.
