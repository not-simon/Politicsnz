## Current status

Former MP (2008–2017). Announced on 15 December 2016, alongside Steffan Browning, that she would not seek re-election in 2017. Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

Born in 1953. Since the early 1990s Delahunty has worked educating people about Te Tiriti o Waitangi, which she credits with leading her into politics; she acknowledges Whaitiri (Betty) Williams and Mitzi Nairn as influences. Her sister is the playwright Sarah Delahunty.

## Political career

- Female co-convenor of the Green Party 2003–2005; narrowly missed entering Parliament on the list at several elections.
- Elected as a list MP in 2008 (ranked 8; also stood in East Coast), re-elected in 2011 (ranked 4) and 2014 (ranked 6).
- Retired at the 2017 election.

## Record & notable actions

- Her Customs and Excise (Sustainable Forestry) Amendment Bill (2009), to ban imports of illegally or unsustainably produced timber, was defeated at first reading.
- Held a wide range of portfolios including education, water, toxics, mining and Te Tiriti o Waitangi.
