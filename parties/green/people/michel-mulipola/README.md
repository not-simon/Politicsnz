## Current status

Green candidate for **Māngere** (#49 on the list) in 2026. Never an MP.

## Biography

Born 1981 and raised in Māngere, of Samoan descent (Lefaga, Vaimoso, Manono); great-grandson of a secretary of the Mau movement. A self-taught comic book artist known as "Bloody Samoan" (Gibson Award 2006; Storylines Notable Book for *Samoan Heroes*, 2016) and a professional wrestler (NZ tag-team champion 2016 with Haku). Worked as a story artist and cultural consultant on Disney's *Moana 2* (2023) and runs comic workshops for young people.
