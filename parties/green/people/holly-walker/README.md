## Current status

Former MP (2011–2014). Writer, book reviewer and public servant; completed a PhD at Victoria University's International Institute of Modern Letters in 2022. Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

Born in Lower Hutt on 15 November 1982 and raised by a mother on the Domestic Purposes Benefit. BA (Hons) in English and Politics from Otago, editor of *Critic Te Ārohi* (2005), Rhodes Scholar (2007) and MPhil/master's in development studies from Oxford (2009). She worked as a Green Party media adviser, a Treaty settlements analyst, and a political adviser to Green MPs, and co-convened the Young Greens in 2010. In a civil union with David Haines; two daughters. Author of *The Whole Intimate Mess: Motherhood, Politics, and Women's Writing* (2017) and co-editor of *Reconnecting Aotearoa* (2023).

## Political career

- Elected as a Green list MP in 2011 (list #12; Hutt South candidate).
- Withdrew from the party list three months before the 2014 election citing "a recent unexpected change in my family life"; her 2017 memoir described post-natal anxiety, a chronically ill husband and domestic unrest. Stood for Hutt South for the party vote only.

## Record & notable actions

- Promoted the Lobbying Disclosure Bill (drawn April 2012), modelled on Canadian law to make lobbying of MPs transparent; it passed first reading but was rejected at select committee in August 2013.
- Launched the Greens' "Home for Life" rent-to-buy housing scheme (2013).
- Her parenting experiences in Parliament fed into rule changes about babies in the House.

## Controversies

- As *Critic* editor in 2005 she published an "Offensive Issue" containing a fictional diary of a drug-rapist; the Classification Office banned the issue in 2006. She initially defended it, but in 2012 called its publication "a mistake".
