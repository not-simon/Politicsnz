## Current status

Former MP. Doyle (they/them) resigned effective 3 October 2025 citing safety concerns, describing Parliament in their farewell as "a hostile and toxic place that wasn't built for people like [them]". Replaced by Mike Davidson. Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

Benjamin Cody Doyle grew up in Whangaparāoa and is of Ngāpuhi, Scottish and Irish descent. A secondary teacher (Auckland Girls' Grammar, Manurewa High), head of Māori at Melville High School and head of social sciences at Rototuna Senior High School; later a kaupapa Māori researcher at the University of Waikato, a community organiser for artists and rainbow communities, and a staff member at Burnett Foundation Aotearoa. Non-binary, takatāpui and disabled; lives in Hamilton with their partner and child.

## Political career

- Green candidate for Hamilton West in 2023 (third, 3,230 votes; list #18, not elected).
- Declared elected on 22 October 2024 after Darleen Tana's removal — New Zealand's first non-binary MP.
- Announced their resignation on 5 September 2025.

## Record & notable actions

- In 2023 opposed allowing anti-trans activist Kellie-Jay Keen-Minshull (Posie Parker) into New Zealand.

## Controversies

- **Instagram posts (2025):** in early 2025 businessman and former NZ First member Rhys Williams led a social-media campaign over a 2023 photo album on Doyle's personal Instagram (account "biblebeltbussy", captioned "bussy galore") that included a photo of Doyle with their child. Doyle said the posts had no sexual meaning, acknowledged "political naivety" in not deleting them, and reported "a significant number of threats" to their life and child.
- The ensuing campaign of threats, break-ins and assaults led police to charge more than ten people by September 2025 (and a 19-year-old in October 2025 for death threats). In June 2026 the Broadcasting Standards Authority ordered Newstalk ZB to broadcast a censure over "dehumanising" on-air comments about Doyle by Heather du Plessis-Allan and Barry Soper.
