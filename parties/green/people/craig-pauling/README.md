## Current status

Green candidate for **Banks Peninsula** and **#14** on the Green list for 2026. Never an MP.

## Biography

Of Ngāi Tahu, Ngāti Mutunga and European descent; educated at Aranui High School and Lincoln University (environmental management), with postgraduate study in social science and ecology; worked at Manaaki Whenua Landcare Research. National and international waka ama champion; speaks te reo Māori; father of three.

## Political career

- Elected to Environment Canterbury in 2019 and 2022; lost the 2022 chair vote to Peter Scott by lot after a tie, becoming deputy chair.
- Acted as chair during Scott's 2024 controversies and was elected chair in October 2024; did not stand in 2025.
