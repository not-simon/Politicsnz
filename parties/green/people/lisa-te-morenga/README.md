## Current status

Green candidate for **Te Tai Tonga** (#46 on the list) in 2026. Never an MP.

## Biography

Of Ngāti Whātua Ōrākei, Te Uri o Hua, Ngāpuhi and Te Rarawa descent. Began in forestry science (Canterbury), then switched to human nutrition (PhD, Otago, 2010). Worked at Otago's Edgar Diabetes and Obesity Research Centre and Victoria University before Massey, where she became a full professor in 2024. Her 2012 BMJ review on dietary sugar and weight gain influenced international nutrition policy (including WHO sugar guidelines); Royal Society Te Apārangi Hamilton Award (2019); Rutherford Discovery Fellowship (2021). Involved with Health Coalition Aotearoa.

## Political career

- Green Party member since 2009; announced as Te Tai Tonga candidate in June 2026.
