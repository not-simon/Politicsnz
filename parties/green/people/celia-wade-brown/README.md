## Current status

Green list MP (since January 2024) who is **retiring** at the 2026 election, having announced on 28 January 2026 that she would not stand again. Lives in the Mangatārere Valley near Carterton.

## Biography

Born in Paddington, London, on 12 July 1956, daughter of a British military officer; grew up in a council flat. After a gap year in Ghana she gained an honours degree in philosophy at the University of Nottingham and worked for IBM before moving to Wellington in 1983. A founding member of the New Zealand Internet Society (InternetNZ) and founder of walking advocacy group Living Streets Aotearoa (2002). Married to Alastair Nicholson; two sons.

## Political career

- Wellington City Councillor for the Southern Ward 1994–1998 and 2001–2010.
- **Mayor of Wellington 2010–2016**: defeated incumbent Kerry Prendergast by 176 votes in 2010 and was re-elected over John Morrison in 2013; stood as an independent although a Green member. Did not seek a third term.
- Green/Alliance list candidate in 1996, 1999 and 2002; Green candidate for Wairarapa in 2020 and 2023 (list #15 in 2023, one place short).
- Became an MP on 19 January 2024 when Golriz Ghahraman resigned.

## Record & notable actions

- As mayor introduced a living wage for council staff and contractors (2014), raised the cycling budget from $70,000 a year to $37 million over four years (with government funding), and backed Pukeahu National War Memorial Park and pest-control/forest restoration; Wellington joined the Biophilic Cities network (2013).
- As MP, campaigned for compulsory microchipping and registration of cats.

## Controversies

- The Island Bay cycleway divided the community; she later said its handling was "a mistake".
- Peace Action Wellington criticised her in 2015, as an executive of Mayors for Peace, for the council venue hosting a Lockheed Martin-sponsored defence conference.
