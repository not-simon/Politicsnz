## Current status

Former MP (2011–2017). Retired at the 2017 election. Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

Born in Clyde, Central Otago, on 3 July 1954. Before Parliament he was spokesperson for the Soil & Health Association (organic growers' organisation) from 2003.

## Political career

- Green list candidate in 2002 (#19), 2005 (#11) and 2008 (#12); contested Kaikōura.
- Elected in 2011 (list #10). Returned in 2014 as the Greens' 14th and last list MP after special votes (he would have missed out on preliminary results).
- Did not seek re-election in 2017.

## Record & notable actions

- Focused on organics, food safety and opposition to genetic engineering, reflecting his Soil & Health background.

## Controversies

- In November 2014 the Greens stripped him of the natural health products portfolio after he signed and shared an online petition supporting homeopathy to treat Ebola. Co-leader Metiria Turei called it "a mistake"; Prime Minister John Key called the idea "mad". Co-leader Russel Norman stated the party took "an evidence-based approach".
