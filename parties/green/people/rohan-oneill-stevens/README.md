## Current status

Green candidate for **Nelson** (#16 on the list) in 2026. Never an MP.

## Biography

Born 2000, of Māori (Ngāti Apakura) and European descent; Nelson College and a scholarship to UWC Robert Bosch College, Germany (2017).

## Political career

- Elected to Nelson City Council in 2019 aged 19 — its youngest councillor; topped the at-large poll in 2022 and placed third for mayor; appointed deputy mayor by National's former MP Nick Smith to "provide balance".
- Led the council's $78 million "Bridge to Better" city revitalisation; co-chaired LGNZ's Young Elected Members committee; did not stand in 2025.
- Convened the Greens' 2023 national campaign.

## Record & notable actions

- Advocates housing density (voted against the regional Future Development Strategy for "lowballing intensification"), free public transport for young people, and a sinking-lid policy on pokie machines.

None of significance found in the sources consulted.
