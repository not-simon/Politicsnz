## Current status

Green list MP since 6 March 2024. Standing in **Epsom** and ranked **#8** on the Green list for 2026.

## Biography

Lawrence Xi Xu-Nan was born in Tianjin, China, on 12 December 1986 and settled in Auckland with his mother in 1997, aged 11; his surname combines his father's (Xu) and mother's (Nan). Educated at Pakuranga Intermediate and Pakuranga College and the University of Auckland (BSc/BA in pharmacology and ancient history, master's, and a PhD in Egyptology). He is queer and neurodivergent.

## Political career

- Green Party male policy co-convenor 2020–2023 and party co-convenor from 2023.
- Contested Pakuranga in 2020 (list #17) and stood in 2023 (list #16), missing out both times.
- Declared elected on 6 March 2024 following the death of Efeso Collins.

## Record & notable actions

- According to *The Post* (2026), he had the longest cumulative speaking time of any MP in the 54th Parliament (an estimated ~420,000 words).

## Controversies

- In July 2026 Foreign Minister Winston Peters told him to "go back to your own country" during a debate. The Race Relations Commissioner, PM Christopher Luxon (who called the comments "very offensive and completely inappropriate"), Labour and the Chinese Embassy criticised Peters; Xu-Nan called the remark a "desperate attempt to distract" and "scapegoating" of migrants. (Xu-Nan was the subject, not the instigator.)
