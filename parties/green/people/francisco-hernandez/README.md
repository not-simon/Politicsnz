## Current status

Green list MP since 7 May 2024 (replacing James Shaw). Standing in **Dunedin** and ranked **#10** on the Green list for 2026.

## Biography

Francisco Bagkus Hernandez was born in Manila; his father Rossano Hernandez, a politician aligned with President Joseph Estrada, brought the family to New Zealand after Estrada's fall in 2001, when Francisco was about 12. Educated at Liston College, Auckland, and the University of Otago (BA in politics, Master of Entrepreneurship). Catholic.

## Political career

- Labour member from 2008, joined the Greens in 2014. OUSA president in 2013; unsuccessful candidate for Dunedin City Council (2013) and Henderson-Massey Local Board (2016).
- Worked as a Green parliamentary staffer and policy adviser, at the Climate Change Commission (waste), and as chief climate-change adviser at Otago Regional Council.
- Green candidate for Dunedin in 2023, not elected; became an MP on 6–7 May 2024 on Shaw's resignation.
- Named *The Post*'s "Rookie on the rise" in December 2025.

## Record & notable actions

- Describes himself as a "market-socialist" (maiden speech, May 2024).

## Controversies

None of significance; in 2023 he was the target of an online attack by convicted white supremacist Philip Arps.
