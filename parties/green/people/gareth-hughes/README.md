## Current status

Former MP (2010–2020). Elected chair of the board of animal-rights organisation SAFE in 2021 and is New Zealand lead for the Wellbeing Economy Alliance; according to Wikipedia he is no longer a Green Party member. Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

Born 31 October 1981 and raised in Gisborne; studied religious studies, history and politics at Victoria University of Wellington. Worked for Greenpeace in Australia and New Zealand (2000–2005) and then for the Green Party on climate change; coordinated Greenpeace's 2009 "Sign On" climate campaign. In 2004 he was arrested after chaining himself, dressed as Ronald McDonald, to the gates of a McDonald's distribution centre in protest at GM chicken feed. Married with two children. Author of *A Gentle Radical* (2022), a biography of Jeanette Fitzsimons.

## Political career

- Green candidate in Ōhāriu in 2008 (list #11); entered Parliament on 16 February 2010 on Jeanette Fitzsimons' retirement, then the youngest MP.
- Re-elected 2011, 2014 and 2017 (contested East Coast in 2017); stood unsuccessfully for male co-leader in 2015 against James Shaw.
- Not given a ministerial role in 2017; served as Green musterer (whip). Announced in 2019 he would retire, and was based in Dunedin for his final period; left Parliament at the 2020 election.

## Record & notable actions

- Declared support for a New Zealand republic in his 2010 maiden speech.
- Spokesperson at various times for housing, ICT, tertiary education, GE, food, oceans, transport, energy and youth.
- Voted for the Marriage (Definition of Marriage) Amendment Act 2013.

## Controversies

None of significance during his parliamentary career found in the sources consulted (pre-Parliament protest arrest noted above).
