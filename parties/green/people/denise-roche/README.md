## Current status

Former MP (2011–2017). Not re-elected in 2017 (list #13). Won a Waitematā Local Board by-election for City Vision in 2018. Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

Born in Helensville on 9 July 1963, the eighth of nine children, of Ngāti Huri and Scottish descent. She left school without qualifications and later studied as an adult (diploma in labour studies 2000; graduate diploma in not-for-profit management 2007). Long background in union work; worked for the Waste Resource Trust on Waiheke Island, where she farmed with partner John Stansfield from 1997.

## Political career

- Auckland City Councillor for the Hauraki Gulf Islands 2007–2010 (won by 11 votes); top-polling Waiheke Local Board member in 2010.
- Green candidate for Auckland Central in 2008 (13%).
- Elected a Green list MP in 2011 (list #11); re-elected 2014; not returned in 2017.

## Record & notable actions

- In November 2015, after Prime Minister John Key accused Labour of "backing the rapists" detained on Christmas Island, Roche and several other women MPs who were survivors of sexual violence walked out in protest after being ejected for raising points of order; Roche told the House she had "endured years of sexual assault as a child".
