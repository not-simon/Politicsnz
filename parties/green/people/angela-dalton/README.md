## Current status

Green candidate for **Papakura** (#26 on the Green list) in 2026. Never an MP.

## Biography

A long-serving South Auckland community representative: school board member (Weymouth Primary, James Cook High), co-founder of the Weymouth Netball Club and member of the Manukau Enhancement Initiative steering committee.

## Political career

- Manurewa Community Board (elected 2007), Manurewa Local Board (2010–2019).
- Auckland Councillor for Manurewa-Papakura 2019–2025, first on the Manurewa-Papakura Action Team ticket and from 2022 as an independent; did not stand in 2025.

## Controversies

None of significance found in the sources consulted.
