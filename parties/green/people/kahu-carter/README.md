## Current status

Green list MP since 2023 and deputy musterer. Standing in **Christchurch Central** (open seat after Labour's Duncan Webb retired) and ranked **#11** on the Green list for 2026.

## Biography

Of Ngāti Maniapoto and Tainui descent; born 1983 or 1984 and raised in rural Waikato until 12, then on Auckland's North Shore. BA from the University of Auckland; a zero-waste advocate who worked for the Ministry for the Environment for three years; briefly studied acting (Meisner technique) in New York in 2013. Moved to Christchurch in 2021. Has two adult sons.

## Political career

- Encouraged to stand by Chlöe Swarbrick; Green candidate for Christchurch Central in 2023 (lost to Labour's Duncan Webb). Confirmed elected as the Greens' 14th list MP after the final count on 3 November 2023.

## Record & notable actions

- Copyright (Parody and Satire) Amendment Bill (drawn November 2024), to allow copyrighted works to be used for parody, satire and memes.
- A "Good Samaritan" drug bill (drawn September 2025) so people calling emergency services during an overdose are not prosecuted for low-level drug offences; supported by the NZ Drug Foundation.
- Waste Minimisation (Food Waste) Amendment Bill (July 2026), modelled on French law, requiring supermarkets to donate surplus food to charities.

## Controversies

- Before politics she appeared in a 2014 Telecom "Gigatown" promotional video that was criticised as racist; the criticism was directed at Telecom.
