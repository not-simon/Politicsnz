## Current status

Green list MP since 2023. Standing in **Kenepuru** and ranked **#6** on the Green list for 2026.

## Biography

Ngoc-Lan Thi Pham was born in 1985 or 1986 to a Vietnamese Buddhist father and a mother of English and Irish descent, and grew up in Brooklyn, Wellington. BSc in ecology (Massey, 2009) and MSc in ecology (Otago, 2014). A freshwater ecologist specialising in Canterbury rivers, she worked for the Department of Conservation and as a resource-management and freshwater commissioner. Married to Emerson, with two children; the family moved from Christchurch to Wellington in 2024.

## Political career

- Elected to Environment Canterbury in 2016 (topping the Christchurch constituency poll while working on Raoul Island) and re-elected in 2019 for Christchurch Central (The Common Good ticket); served until 2022.
- Green candidate for Banks Peninsula in 2023 (third), elected at list #6.

## Record & notable actions

- Her member's bill to ban mining on public conservation land was drawn from the ballot in May 2026; the Aggregate and Quarry Association raised concern about its broad definition of mining.
- Long-standing advocate on river pollution and freshwater quality in Canterbury.
