## Current status

Former MP (2011–2023). Retired at the 2023 election. Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

Heather Janet Logie was born in Invercargill on 26 October 1969 and graduated from the University of Otago with a BA in politics. She was Women's Coordinator for the New Zealand Union of Students' Associations (1993–1996), taught English in Japan on the JET Programme, and worked for Women's Refuge, the Hutt Valley Youth Health Service, the YWCA and the New Zealand Centre for Sustainable Cities. She described herself in her maiden speech as a "lefty, feminist lesbian" and lives in Cannons Creek, Porirua.

## Political career

- Green candidate for Mana from the 2010 by-election onward; list MP from 2011 to 2023 (four terms).
- Member of the Social Services and Community Committee 2011–2018, chairing it for 18 months.
- Parliamentary Under-Secretary to Justice Minister Andrew Little (domestic and sexual violence) 2017–2020, setting up the cross-agency programme to end family and sexual violence. Not reappointed in 2020 (Marama Davidson became the responsible minister).

## Record & notable actions

- **Domestic Violence—Victims' Protection Act 2018**: her member's bill, enacted in July 2018, gives workers affected by domestic violence up to 10 days' paid leave and workplace protections.
- Equal Pay Amendment Bill (2017), requiring publication of gender pay data, defeated at first reading.
- Set up a cross-party LGBTI rights group of MPs in 2015.
- Voted for marriage equality (2013), the End of Life Choice Act (2019) and the Abortion Legislation Act (2020).
