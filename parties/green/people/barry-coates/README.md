## Current status

Former MP (October 2016 – September 2017). Chief executive of Mindful Money, which campaigns for ethical KiwiSaver and fund investment. Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

Born 18 October 1956. BCom (Auckland) and Master's in Public and Private Management (Yale). Worked for the Samoan government promoting small business (1978–1980), at WWF-UK (1991–1996, including as an NGO observer at the 1992 Rio Earth Summit), as executive director of the UK's World Development Movement (1996–2003) and of Oxfam New Zealand (2003–2014). Coordinated the "It's Our Future" campaign against the Trans-Pacific Partnership.

## Political career

- Green candidate for Mount Roskill in 2014.
- Sworn in as a list MP on 7 October 2016, replacing Kevin Hague.
- Ranked #10 in 2017 and not re-elected when the Greens won 8 seats.

## Record & notable actions

- Through Mindful Money, pressed KiwiSaver providers to divest from nuclear-weapons manufacturers (reported exposure fell from ~$100 million in 2019 to under $10 million by 2024) and, after the Gaza war, from companies linked to Israeli settlements; the Israel Institute of New Zealand accused the group of an "undue fixation" on Israel, which Coates rejected.

## Controversies

- In July 2017 he wrote on *The Daily Blog* that the Greens might force a second election rather than accept a Labour–NZ First government without them; co-leader James Shaw said that was "absolutely not" party policy, and Winston Peters called the comments "the height of stupidity".
