## Our Vision for Aotearoa 2026

Our vision is an Aotearoa that honours Te Tiriti o Waitangi, where power and wealth sit with our communities, and where people and the rest of nature thrive together. **Aotearoa has enough for all of us**. Our government should work for people and planet, not the greed of corporations and their shareholders.

### Our Priorities

#### Affordable Lives

The Greens have the plan to make housing, transport, groceries and power bills more affordable.

#### Healthy Nature

A Green Government will protect and restore our nature. That means clean swimmable rivers, beaches, and drinkable water.

#### Real Democracy

Decisions and laws should be made for and by New Zealanders, not lobbyists and corporations.
