# Greens Announce Paid Placements For Nurses, Teachers, And Social Workers

**Friday, 16 October 2026, 12:24 pm**
**Press Release: [Green Party](https://info.scoop.co.nz/Green_Party)**

16 October 2026

The Green Party has announced it will provide students a universal weekly payment while they complete practical training to become nurses, teachers, and other essential health and social support workers.

"Students should not have to go broke while they train for the jobs New Zealand needs. Our paid placements policy will make sure they can afford the essentials while training,” says Green Party Co-leader Marama Davidson.

The Green Party will also support students who need to relocate for their placement, making training more accessible for students in the regions.

“We will prioritise sectors where unpaid placements mean that some students have no choice but to drop out. Unpaid placements push students out of sectors where the workforce is struggling to keep up with demand, like health and education.”

The Green Party’s paid placements policy will:

· **Support students to afford the basics while training** by providing them with a universal weekly payment of $380, available on top of any pre-existing student loan or student allowance, for the duration of their compulsory placements.

· **Make it easier for students who need to relocate for their placement** by covering some of their costs with a relocation payment of $1500.

· **Improve student retention and widen access** so students from all backgrounds and financial situations can train for the crucial rolesNew Zealand needs more of.

Green Party Co-leader Chlöe Swarbrick says, “Imagine being required to spend 30 hours a week working and not being paid for it.”

“People training to be nurses, teachers and social workers are currently forced to do hundreds or thousands of hours for free in order to get into a profession that no one is doing for the money or the glory in the first place. That’s messed up. It doesn’t have to be that way. The Greens will fix it.”

“When our country is crying out for more teachers and nurses and social workers, the Greens will ensure that people are supported in that study and paid for their work.

“This is about what our country chooses to value. We can fix the tax system, and we can support our essential workforce. That’s real economic development,” says Chlöe Swarbrick.

### NOTES

1. The Green Party policy will pay a weekly stipend to students\* required to undertake practicum/placements to gain their qualifications.

2. \*Paid placements will be available to students studying the following courses and will be administered through Studylink: Clinical Psychology, Psychotherapy, Counselling, Addiction practitioners, Medicine, Nursing, Midwifery, Paramedicine, Occupational therapy, Anaesthetic technicians, Audiology, Dietitians, Medical Imaging Techs, Medical Lab Scientists, Oral health & dental tech, Dentistry, Pharmacy, Physiotherapy, Radiation therapy, Speech-Language Therapists, Teaching, Social Work, Social Work, Veterinary.

3. The weekly stipend is available to students in addition to any pre-existing support through the Student Loan Living Costs or Student Allowance mechanisms, and any other mechanism.
