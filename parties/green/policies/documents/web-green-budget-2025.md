# Green Budget 2025

Read about the Green alternative budget for 2025 that shows how Aotearoa could be a place where everyone has what they need.

### As was promised in te Tiriti o Waitangi, Aotearoa can be a place where everyone has what they need.

This alternative budget for 2025 imagines the future we could create together if we put people at the heart of decision-making. The challenges of climate change and wealth inequality need shared solutions that give back control to communities – not big corporations.

The Green Alternative Budget 2025 showed how we could have:

|  |
| --- |
| - Free GPs & Free Dental  - Early Childhood Education for whānau, not profit  - Protection and restoration of nature  - Clean energy for our climate  - Hoki whenua mai: returning whenua and recognizing the enduring tino rangatiratanga that tangata whenua have.  - A fair tax system to pay for it |

#### A better world is possible, let us show you how.
