At a time when Aotearoa
New Zealand desperately
needs more nurses, teachers,
social workers, and other
skilled professionals, students
should be able to cover the
basics while they complete
their training.
Thousands of students across Aotearoa
New Zealand are required to complete
practical placements to qualify for some of
our most important professions.
Many students are required to spend up
to 30 hours a week in their placement,
meaning they cannot support their studies
with a part-time job. Unpaid placements
create and entrench deep inequities.
This is not just unfair. It is a major barrier
to entering the very professions Aotearoa
New Zealand urgently needs more people
to join.
Meanwhile, our communities are paying
the price for critical workforce shortages
with clogged emergency departments and
long waitlists for appointments. We cannot
solve these shortages while making it harder
for people to complete the training those
professions require.

This will apply to:
Clinical psychology. Psychotherapy.
Counselling. Addiction practitioners.
Medicine. Nursing. Midwifery.
Paramedicine. Occupational therapy.
Anaesthetic technicians. Audiology.
Dietitians. Medical imaging techs.
Medical lab scientists. Oral health &
dental tech. Dentistry. Pharmacy.
Physiotherapy. Radiation therapy.
Speech-language therapists. Teaching.
Social work. Veterinary.

Apprenticeships
There is important work to be done in
Aotearoa, and it’s time to support the
apprentices who do it.
If we want to build more homes, scale up
renewable energy, fix our pipes and get our
infrastructure going, we need skilled builders,
electricians, plumbers and mechanics to
make it happen.
Right now, too many apprentices are being
pushed out of the training they need to
complete. Low pay, insecure work, and a
lack of support means the drop out rates for
apprenticeships are high, and getting through
qualifications comes with added pressure.

The Green Party will end the expectation
that students should work for free as part
of getting a qualification. Supporting
students to train for the jobs we need is
an investment in our future workforce and
a more resilient Aotearoa New Zealand.

Paid Placements & Apprenticeships Policy 2026

Instead of focusing on gaining skills and learning
their trade, apprentices are worried about paying
the rent, filling up the car, growing student debt,
and trying to get through training without going
into hardship.
If we want people to enter trades and
complete apprenticeships, it’s time to
create a better pathway.
The Green Party will invest in the apprentices
we need by making sure they’re paid properly,
can get the right tools and materials, access
pastoral care, and ensure apprentices can
finish their qualifications and have confidence
entering the workforce.

The Green
Party will:
Paid placements
- Make sure students can afford to
pay for the essentials while on
placements, with a universal weekly
stipend of $380.

- Support students who need to move
from the place they live to complete a
placement, with a relocation payment
of $1500.

- Prioritise sectors where unpaid
placements are pushing students
out and making workforce
shortages worse, including in
health and education.
