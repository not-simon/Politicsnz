# Green Party To Abolish Training Wage And Provide More Support For Apprentices

**Saturday, 17 October 2026, 2:14 pm**
**Press Release: [Green Party](https://info.scoop.co.nz/Green_Party)**

The Green Party will abolish the training wage, so people aren’t forced to live in poverty while they learn on the job.

“A strong economy needs a strong workforce. We will ensure apprentices are paid properly for their work, supporting more tradies on and into the job,” says Green Party Co-leader Chlöe Swarbrick.

"We need to more homes and renewable energy, which means we need a skilled workforce of builders, electricians, plumbers, mechanics, and other tradespeople to make it happen.”

“But apprentice numbers are at a 15-year low. Low pay, insecure work, and a lack of pastoral support mean that instead of focusing on learning their trade, our apprentices spend too much time worried about making ends meet. We can fix that.”

“Along with abolishing the training wage, the Greens will increase the toolbox allowance to $2,000, so that apprentices can get the gear they need and focus on on-the-job learning.”

“We used to make things in this beautiful country, and we can again. We must invest in our people and our infrastructure, because you cannot cut your way to resilience. The Greens are proudly the only party bringing real economic development policy to this election.”

Green Party Co-leader Marama Davidson says, “the challenges are even greater for our Māori and Pasifika whānau who face additional financial and structural barriers to training."

"Funding wraparound support that helps people complete their qualifications means that trades training can be a pathway to a thriving career for everyone.”

### To support our future workforce, the Green Party will:

- **Abolish the training wage**, making sure apprentices and trainees can afford to pay for the essentials.
- **Introduce an apprentice equipment grant of $2,000, over the duration of an apprenticeship,** so that apprentices can afford to cover tools, uniforms, and course materials.
- **Prioritise support for Māori and Pasifika apprentices** with a $30m annual funding uplift for Māori and Pasifika-led training. This will strengthen pastoral and learning support for apprentices, increase funding for job-matching services to support people into meaningful work, and provide wraparound support for newly-qualified workers in their first job.
- **Ensure that employers actively support apprentices to complete their training** with a completion bonus of $2,000 for employers who actively support apprentices to progress through their qualifications.

This is the final component of the Green Party’s jobs package, ‘Good Jobs for All of Us.’

“The Green Party has a plan that will create good jobs in our vital industries. We can support our people and communities by incentivising the skills we need right now and for years to come.”

“The Greens' jobs policy will create thousands of jobs in our most important sectors, creating an economy that works for all of us,” says Marama Davidson.

Note: [“Paid Placements & Apprenticeships For our future workforce” policy document](https://img.scoop.co.nz/media/pdfs/2610/Green_Party__Paid_Placements__Apprenticeships_for_our_future_workforce_2026.pdf) - The costings have been independently verified by Infometrics.

*The Training Wage is currently set at $19.16 an hour, and apprentices are specifically allowed to be paid this rate, below the Minimum Wage of $23.95 an hour. The Greens would remove this exemption.*
