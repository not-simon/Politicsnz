Drink Swim Fish policy 2026

Ko te wai te toto o te
whenua, ko te whenua te
toto o te tangata. Water is
the blood of the land, and
land is the blood of the
people.
From our mountain glaciers to our
wild coasts, Aotearoa is defined
by water. Water has always
shaped our land, our lives, and
our livelihoods.
Clean, healthy water is a human right
and a way of life for all of us in Aotearoa.
But this way of life is being taken from us.
Every year, up to 100,000 people become
sick from unsafe drinking water. Our
favourite swimming spots are often too
polluted to swim in, and our moana is being
emptied out, pushing marine life to the brink
of collapse.
The weak rules in place now allow for
out of control pollution and damaging
fishing practices that mean human and
environmental health pay for short term
corporate profits.
The Green Party will fix the rules, so the
health of our communities and the health of
our environment always come first. We will
make sure all New Zealanders can drink,
swim, and fish in the places they love.

We will start by
Protecting drinking water by
setting strong environmental
limits and lowering nitrate limits.
Protecting rivers and coasts
by strengthening rules for
wastewater discharges.
Protecting our moana by
phasing out destructive
bottom trawling.

Drink Swim Fish policy 2026

The health of our communities and our
economy depends on the health of our
environment.
Rules that allow overfishing and pollution
mean we have unsafe drinking water, polluted
rivers and a depleted moana. This is not how
life in Aotearoa should be.
The Green Party will fix the rules so
everyone’s drinking water is safe, local
swimming holes and beaches don’t
have warning signs at the car park, and
kaimoana is plentiful.
This means tackling pollution before it
reaches our waterways; restoring natural
ecosystems that keep water clean; protecting
biodiversity; and ending activities that harm
communities and the environment.
By rebuilding the health of our moana, rivers,
lakes and estuaries, the Green Party will make
sure everyone in Aotearoa can drink from,
swim in, and fish from our waters.
We’re putting the brakes on pollution
and exploitation by fixing the rules.
Because our rivers, lakes and moana
are for all of us.

The Green Party will:
• Set strong, science-based
national standards for
freshwater that protect both the health of
our communities and the health of our rivers,
lakes and aquifers, including by lowering the
nitrate limit for drinking water.
• Strengthen
rules for wastewater
discharges to ensure our rivers, lakes
and moana are swimmable.
• Empower the drinking water regulator
to monitor, report on and enforce
protections for drinking water sources.
• Phase out synthetic
nitrogen fertiliser and
take action to reduce stocking rates on a
catchment basis to improve the health of
rivers and drinking water quality.
• Protect at least 30% of
New Zealand’s moana to restore
marine ecosystems and support abundant
fisheries by reforming marine-protected area
legislation to ensure it is fit-for-purpose, Tiriticonsistent, and tikanga-led.
• Support kaitiakitanga Māori
in marine management and iwiand hapū-led conservation and restoration
initiatives.
• Phase out destructive fishing methods,
including bottom trawling on seamounts,
set netting and dredging.
• Review the Quota Management System
and move towards ecosystem-based
fisheries management that prioritises
abundance and long-term sustainability.
