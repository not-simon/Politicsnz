# Hauraki Gulf / Tīkapa Moana Marine Protection (Restoration of High Protection Areas) Amendment Bill (Green Party draft bill)

<!-- page 1 -->

Hauraki Gulf / Tīpaka Moana Marine Protection
(Restoration of High Protection Areas) Amendment Bill
Explanatory note
General policy statement
This Bill amends the Hauraki Gulf / Tīkapa Moana Marine Protection Act 2025 to
remove the Act’s current exemption which allows authorised persons to undertake
ring net fishing in the high protection areas.
Clause by clause analysis
Clause 1 is the Title clause.
Clause 2 is the commencement clause and provides for the Bill to come into force on
the day after Royal assent.
Clause 3 states that the Bill amends the Hauraki Gulf / Tīpaka Moana Marine Protec‐
tion Act 2025.
Clauses 4 and 5 contain consequential amendments arising from the amendments in
clauses 6 and 7.
Clause 6 repeals section 23 of the Act, which provides that a person may undertake
ring net fishing in the Kawau Bay High Protection Area and the Rangitoto and Motu‐
tapu High Protection Area if the person is authorised by the Director General.
Clause 7 replaces section 74 of the Act, which provides for the making of regulations
that prescribe the restrictions and conditions that apply to authorised persons under‐
taking ring net fishing, with a new regulation-making power that allows regulations to
be made to provide for additional management actions in relation to activities that
occur within high protection areas.
Clause 8 replaces the cross-heading above section 75 to reflect the changes made by
clause 9.

Explanatory note

Clause 9 repeals sections 76 and 77, which provided for a review of the operation of
the ring net fishing provisions, and empowered the making of an Order in Council to
repeal those provisions following the review.
Clause 10 and the Schedule provide for transitional matters. In particular, the revoca‐
tion of existing authorisations under section 24 and confirmation that no compensa‐
tion is payable in respect of that revocation.

74

Regulations for additional management actions in high protection areas

(1)

The Governor-General may, by Order in Council made on the recommendation
of the Minister, make regulations that provide for additional management
actions in relation to activities that occur within high protection areas.

(2)

Before making a recommendation under subsection (1), the Minister must be
satisfied that—

(3)

2

(a)

biodiversity objectives and associated restrictions have been established
by regulations made under section 73 in relation to the high protection
area; and

(b)

there is evidence that the restrictions are not sufficient to achieve the
biodiversity objectives; and

(c)

the additional management actions are necessary to achieve the biodiver‐
sity objectives.

The Minister must not make a recommendation under subsection (1) unless
the Minister is satisfied that the proposals for regulations were developed in
consultation with iwi that exercise kaitiakitanga in the relevant high protection
area.
