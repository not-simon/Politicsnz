## Current status

Leader of the Alliance and its #1 list candidate; standing in **Dunedin** in 2026. In June 2026 the Maritime Union of New Zealand voted to affiliate to the Alliance, which Billot called a "gamechanger". Never an MP.

## Biography

Born in Dunedin in 1972 and raised in Warrington; BA in political science from Otago (1994); co-editor of *Critic* (1995); musician (Alpha Plan, Age of Dog). Communications officer for the Maritime Union (2003–2016 and again by 2022) and publicist for Otago University Press (2017). Poet with collections including *Ambient Terror* (2017) and *The Sets* (2021).

## Political career

- Founding member of Jim Anderton's NewLabour Party (1989), which formed the Alliance in 1991.
- Alliance candidate for Dunedin North in 2005, 2008 and 2011; party president 2006, co-leader 2007–2008; stepped down as spokesman in 2014, later re-elected leader in February 2026.
