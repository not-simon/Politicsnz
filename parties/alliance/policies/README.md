# Alliance Party of Aotearoa New Zealand — 2026 election policies

Every policy of **Alliance Party of Aotearoa New Zealand** for the 7 November 2026 general election that this archive could find: **27 policies** listed, backed by **37 full-text source documents**.

- Registration: 5 August 2026 (re-registered; Electoral Commission register)

## About this list

The relaunched Alliance (leader Victor Billot) released its 2026 platform as a series of policy announcements (September–October 2026), each with a full policy PDF: tax and costings, workers' rights, environment, health, transport, social security, gender equality, small business, justice, child poverty, housing, retirement, Te Tiriti and constitution, trade, public ownership, and foreign affairs and defence. All 17 PDFs and all policy posts are captured. The main commitments of each document are listed below.

**Method.** Pages and PDFs were downloaded on 2026-10-23 by [`tools/policies_minor_sites_fetch.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_sites_fetch.py) / [`tools/policies_minor_opportunity_fetch.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_opportunity_fetch.py) (raw copies in `raw/party-docs/alliance/`) and reduced to their main text in [`documents/`](documents/) (markup and whitespace normalised only). The list and summaries are built by [`tools/policies_minor_build.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_build.py) from [`tools/policies_minor_catalog.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_catalog.py).

policy.nz publishes no policy statements for this party, so every summary here is marked *archive*: written for this archive from the linked full text in one to three neutral sentences ("Why" = the party's own stated reason, attributed; "How" = what it says it would do). Party claims (costs, statistics) are reported as the party's claims, not verified.

**Gaps and caveats.** Only the tax package has a separate costing document. Policy PDFs were extracted with pdftotext (page numbers kept). Earlier 2026 posts responding to news are captured where they state policy (Universal Child Benefit, conference).

## Defence & foreign affairs

### Refuse trade deals with investor–state dispute settlement and seek to remove it from existing deals (ALL-25)

**How** (*archive*): Trade policy that protects jobs, public services and the ability to regulate; retain tariffs as a sparing option; Māori consent on agreements affecting Māori interests; stronger buy-local procurement.

Full text: [Alliance Trade Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-trade-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-trade-policy-2026.pdf)); [Open, Not Owned: Alliance Party Announces Trade Policy](documents/post-2026-09-28-open-not-owned-alliance-trade-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/open-not-owned-alliance-trade-policy/))

### Make New Zealand non-aligned, restructure defence for peacekeeping and disaster response, and review foreign military and intelligence facilities (ALL-27)

**How** (*archive*): Withdraw from inconsistent military alliances, remove offensive capabilities, strengthen the nuclear-free Pacific, support Palestinian self-determination and right of return, cancel unsustainable Global South debt, raise aid toward 0.7% of GNI and ease Pacific migration barriers.

Full text: [Alliance Foreign Affairs, Defence and International Relations Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-foreign-affairs-defence-international-relations-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/alliance-foreign-affairs-defence-international-relations-policy-2026.pdf)); [A Commitment to Peace: Alliance Party Releases Foreign Affairs, Defence, and International Relations Policy](documents/post-2026-10-04-alliance-foreign-affairs-defence-international-rel.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-foreign-affairs-defence-international-relations-policy/))

## Environment

### Replace the Emissions Trading Scheme with a carbon tax (ALL-10)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says a carbon tax sets a transparent price that free allocation and offsets cannot undermine.

**How** (*archive*): Revenue directed to renewables, public transport and emissions reduction; mandatory energy-efficiency standards for buildings, appliances, vehicles and industry.

Full text: [Alliance Environmental, Climate, and Sustainable Economy Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-environment-climate-sustainable-economy-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-environment-climate-sustainable-economy-policy-2026.pdf)); [Public Power, Clean Water, Polluters Pay: A Just Transition in Our Environment Policy](documents/post-2026-09-08-alliance-environment-climate-sustainable-economy-p.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-environment-climate-sustainable-economy-policy/))

### Apply a strict polluter-pays principle and move to a zero-waste, circular economy (ALL-11)

**How** (*archive*): Polluters pay full repair costs; ban non-recyclable plastic packaging, reusable packaging such as glass bottles, right to repair; keep water in public ownership with stronger pollution controls; a Genuine Progress Index alongside GDP.

Full text: [Alliance Environmental, Climate, and Sustainable Economy Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-environment-climate-sustainable-economy-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-environment-climate-sustainable-economy-policy-2026.pdf)); [Public Power, Clean Water, Polluters Pay: A Just Transition in Our Environment Policy](documents/post-2026-09-08-alliance-environment-climate-sustainable-economy-p.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-environment-climate-sustainable-economy-policy/))

### Ban mining in national parks, conservation land and the Coromandel, and give the Conservation Minister a veto (ALL-12)

**How** (*archive*): Restoration bonds for permitted mining, precaution on genetic modification, limits on sales of land and beaches to overseas buyers, and a reaffirmed nuclear-free policy.

Full text: [Alliance Environmental, Climate, and Sustainable Economy Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-environment-climate-sustainable-economy-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-environment-climate-sustainable-economy-policy-2026.pdf)); [Public Power, Clean Water, Polluters Pay: A Just Transition in Our Environment Policy](documents/post-2026-09-08-alliance-environment-climate-sustainable-economy-p.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-environment-climate-sustainable-economy-policy/))

## Health

### Create a public Community Health Service with free GP, dental, optical, audiology and mental-health care (ALL-13)

**Why** (*archive*): The Alliance says primary care is a fragmented network of struggling private practices.

**How** (*archive*): Fund care teams rather than appointments, bring allied services into public provision, give community health workers hospital-equivalent pay, uncap nurse-practitioner training, 'GP rescue' by taking over practice overheads, and local Community Health Boards.

Full text: [Alliance Health Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-health-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-health-policy-2026.pdf)); [Our Health Policy Eliminates Costs for GP Visits, Dental, Optical, and Audiology Services](documents/post-2026-09-10-alliance-health-policy-free-gp-dental-optical-audi.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-health-policy-free-gp-dental-optical-audiology/))

## Housing

### Clear the social housing waitlist in five years through a national state-house rebuilding programme (ALL-22)

**Why** (*archive*): The Alliance says housing is a fundamental human right.

**How** (*archive*): Funding Kāinga Ora for large-scale building with local materials and labour, cap Kāinga Ora rents and state-backed mortgage payments at 25% of net household income, no-deposit low-interest state loans for low-income workers, co-operative housing, stronger tenancy protections and universal insulation.

Full text: [Alliance Housing Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-housing-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-housing-policy-2026.pdf)); [The Right to a Roof: Our Housing Policy](documents/post-2026-09-23-the-right-to-a-roof-alliance-housing-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/the-right-to-a-roof-alliance-housing-policy/))

## Incomes & employment

### Make the first $20,000 of everyone's income tax-free (ALL-01)

**Why** (*archive*): The Alliance says income is taxed from the first dollar that pays for food, power and rent while wealth is lightly taxed.

**How** (*archive*): A universal, automatic threshold; it says everyone earning under about $178,150 would pay less. Upper rates of 34% ($78,100–$150,000), 39% ($150,000–$250,000) and 47% above $250,000.

Full text: [Alliance Tax Justice and Cost of Living Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-tax-justice-cost-of-living-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-tax-justice-cost-of-living-policy-2026.pdf)); [Our Tax Plan Makes First $20,000 Income Tax-Free, Funded by Wealth Tax](documents/post-2026-09-06-alliance-tax-plan-first-20000-income-tax-free.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-tax-plan-first-20000-income-tax-free/)); [Alliance Tax Policy Costings 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-tax-policy-costings.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-tax-policy-costings-2026.pdf))

### Introduce a 2.5% annual wealth tax on net wealth over $2 million, with a trust tax (ALL-02)

**How** (*archive*): A couple would pay nothing on combined net wealth up to $4 million; the party estimates $6.59 billion a year and would resource IRD to enforce it.

Full text: [Alliance Tax Justice and Cost of Living Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-tax-justice-cost-of-living-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-tax-justice-cost-of-living-policy-2026.pdf)); [Our Tax Plan Makes First $20,000 Income Tax-Free, Funded by Wealth Tax](documents/post-2026-09-06-alliance-tax-plan-first-20000-income-tax-free.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-tax-plan-first-20000-income-tax-free/)); [Alliance Tax Policy Costings 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-tax-policy-costings.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-tax-policy-costings-2026.pdf))

### Introduce a 33% capital gains tax on realised gains, excluding the family home (ALL-03)

**How** (*archive*): Covers investment property, shares and business assets, applying only to gains after it starts; revenue estimated to build from $0.54bn to over $2bn a year.

Full text: [Alliance Tax Justice and Cost of Living Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-tax-justice-cost-of-living-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-tax-justice-cost-of-living-policy-2026.pdf)); [Our Tax Plan Makes First $20,000 Income Tax-Free, Funded by Wealth Tax](documents/post-2026-09-06-alliance-tax-plan-first-20000-income-tax-free.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-tax-plan-first-20000-income-tax-free/)); [Alliance Tax Policy Costings 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-tax-policy-costings.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-tax-policy-costings-2026.pdf))

### Make full employment a central goal, with a 35-hour week and five weeks' annual leave (ALL-05)

**Why** (*archive*): The Alliance says work is a basic right.

**How** (*archive*): 35 hours without loss of pay, penal rates for overtime, a right to refuse unreasonable hours, and public job creation in infrastructure, housing, conservation and services.

Full text: [Alliance Workers’ Rights and Employment Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-workers-rights-employment-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-workers-rights-employment-policy-2026.pdf)); [Full Employment and a 35-Hour Week in Our Workers’ Rights Policy](documents/post-2026-09-07-alliance-full-employment-35-hour-week-workers-righ.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-full-employment-35-hour-week-workers-rights-policy/))

### Raise the adult minimum wage immediately to two-thirds of the average wage and index it to inflation (ALL-06)

**How** (*archive*): Apply the adult rate to workers of all ages and adjust annually for the cost of living.

Full text: [Alliance Workers’ Rights and Employment Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-workers-rights-employment-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-workers-rights-employment-policy-2026.pdf)); [Full Employment and a 35-Hour Week in Our Workers’ Rights Policy](documents/post-2026-09-07-alliance-full-employment-35-hour-week-workers-righ.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-full-employment-35-hour-week-workers-rights-policy/))

### Provide 52 weeks' paid parental leave for all workers and paid compassionate leave (ALL-07)

**How** (*archive*): Including part-time, casual and seasonal workers.

Full text: [Alliance Workers’ Rights and Employment Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-workers-rights-employment-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-workers-rights-employment-policy-2026.pdf)); [Full Employment and a 35-Hour Week in Our Workers’ Rights Policy](documents/post-2026-09-07-alliance-full-employment-35-hour-week-workers-righ.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-full-employment-35-hour-week-workers-rights-policy/)); [Women, Gender Equality, and Economic Security (PDF)](documents/pdf-women-gender-equality-economic-security-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-women-gender-equality-economic-security-policy-2026.pdf)); [52 Weeks’ Paid Parental Leave in Our Gender Equality Plan](documents/post-2026-09-16-alliance-52-weeks-paid-parental-leave-gender-equal.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-52-weeks-paid-parental-leave-gender-equality/))

### Restore national collective bargaining, a broad right to strike and Work Councils in firms with over 50 staff (ALL-08)

**How** (*archive*): Coordinated wage bargaining, protection for solidarity and some political strikes, worker voice on restructuring and technology, a Minimum Employment Code covering contractors and labour hire, tougher action on wage theft, and a national retraining and redeployment programme.

Full text: [Alliance Workers’ Rights and Employment Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-workers-rights-employment-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-workers-rights-employment-policy-2026.pdf)); [Full Employment and a 35-Hour Week in Our Workers’ Rights Policy](documents/post-2026-09-07-alliance-full-employment-35-hour-week-workers-righ.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-full-employment-35-hour-week-workers-rights-policy/))

### Introduce a Universal Benefit and abolish benefit sanctions, stand-downs and beneficiary debt (ALL-16)

**Why** (*archive*): The Alliance says welfare should provide security, not punishment.

**How** (*archive*): An adequate universal base payment, individualised entitlements regardless of relationship status, write-off of MSD debt, quarterly indexation to wages or CPI (whichever is higher), and payments to unpaid carers.

Full text: [Alliance Welfare Family and Social Security Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-welfare-family-social-security-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-welfare-family-social-security-policy-2026.pdf)); [Our Plan to Rebuild Universal Social Security](documents/post-2026-09-14-alliance-plan-to-rebuild-universal-social-security.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-plan-to-rebuild-universal-social-security/))

### Restore a Universal Child Benefit paid to every child's main carer, plus a Universal Early Years Benefit for under-threes (ALL-17)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says Working for Families and the In-Work Tax Credit leave out some of the families most in need.

**How** (*archive*): Non-means-tested, paid directly to the primary carer and indexed to average weekly earnings, as part of a plan to eliminate child poverty.

Full text: [Alliance Universal Child Benefit and Child Poverty Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-universal-child-benefit-and-child-poverty-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-universal-child-benefit-and-child-poverty-policy-2026.pdf)); [Alliance Party Plan to Eliminate Child Poverty](documents/post-2026-09-23-alliance-plan-to-eliminate-child-poverty.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-plan-to-eliminate-child-poverty/)); [Alliance Calls for Universal Child Benefit](documents/post-2026-08-19-alliance-calls-for-universal-child-benefit.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-calls-for-universal-child-benefit/)); [Alliance Welfare Family and Social Security Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-welfare-family-social-security-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-welfare-family-social-security-policy-2026.pdf)); [Our Plan to Rebuild Universal Social Security](documents/post-2026-09-14-alliance-plan-to-rebuild-universal-social-security.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-plan-to-rebuild-universal-social-security/))

### Keep NZ Super at 65, raise the married rate to 72.5% of the after-tax average wage, and end means-testing for long-term aged care (ALL-23)

**How** (*archive*): Also a Ministry for Senior Citizens, independent advocates for older people, subsidised transport and internet, and strengthening KiwiSaver.

Full text: [Alliance Retirement and Aged Care Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-retirement-and-aged-care-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-retirement-and-aged-care-policy-2026.pdf)); [Alliance Party Commits to 65 Retirement Age, Funding Aged Care Support, Supercharging KiwiSaver](documents/post-2026-09-25-alliance-retirement-aged-care-kiwisaver-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-retirement-aged-care-kiwisaver-policy/))

## Law, justice & government

### 'Three strikes and into treatment': rehabilitation-focused justice with no private prisons (ALL-21)

**Why** (*archive*): The Alliance says justice is about prevention, rehabilitation and support for victims, not just punishment.

**How** (*archive*): Community care orders and habilitation centres, restorative and marae-based justice, a victim compensation scheme, prisons kept public, expanded legal aid and public defenders, and recognition of tikanga.

Full text: [Alliance Justice Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-justice-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-justice-policy-2026.pdf)); [Justice Policy: “Three Strikes and into Treatment”](documents/post-2026-09-21-alliance-justice-policy-three-strikes-into-treatme.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-justice-policy-three-strikes-into-treatment/))

### Lower the MMP threshold to 3.5% and hold a public review toward a written constitution grounded in Te Tiriti (ALL-24)

**How** (*archive*): Strengthen the Waitangi Tribunal, consider Matike Mai recommendations, citizens' initiatives, a democratic decision on the monarchy, stronger select committees, OIA reform, conflict-of-interest rules and donation transparency and limits.

Full text: [Alliance Te Tiriti o Waitangi, Democracy, and Constitutional Policy 2026 (PDF)](documents/pdf-te-tiriti-democracy-constitutional-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alliance-te-tiriti-democracy-constitutional-policy-2026.pdf)); [Alliance Party: Redistribute Power, Honour Te Tiriti o Waitangi, Move Forward in Unity](documents/post-2026-09-26-alliance-te-tiriti-democracy-constitutional-policy.md) ([original](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-te-tiriti-democracy-constitutional-policy/))

## Transport & infrastructure

### Put all electricity generation, transmission, distribution and retail into full public ownership (ALL-09)

**Why** (*archive*): The Alliance says public ownership allows long-term planning and affordable power.

**How** (*archive*): A 'reverse privatisation' programme buying back strategic assets at fair value, focused on renewables.
