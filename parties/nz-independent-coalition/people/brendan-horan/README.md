## Current status

Former MP (2011–2014). Not a 2026 candidate. Came fifth in the Whakatāne-Ōhope ward of Whakatāne District Council in 2025.

## Biography

Born 9 July 1961; of Ngāti Maniapoto descent. A former TVNZ *One News* weather presenter (2005–2007), professional lifeguard and coach, NZ Surf Lifesaving Iron Man champion, water polo representative for New Zealand and Australia, and outrigger canoe champion. Married with two children. In 2012 he performed CPR that helped save an Indonesian MP who collapsed at a UN conference in Japan.

## Political career

- NZ First candidate for East Coast (2008) and Tauranga (2011, third by 96 votes); elected at list #6 in 2011.
- Expelled from NZ First by Winston Peters on 4 December 2012 (announced in Parliament before Horan was told); continued as an independent.
- Founded the NZ Independent Coalition in January 2014; came fifth in Bay of Plenty in 2014 and left Parliament. The party was deregistered in 2016.

## Record & notable actions

- One term in Parliament; no member's bills noted in the sources consulted.

## Controversies

- **Family estate allegations (2012):** accused of taking money from his dying mother's bank account for gambling, which he denied; he admitted 144 calls to the TAB on his parliamentary phone but denied a gambling problem. In 2016 police said there was no evidence to support charges, and he said the allegations had damaged his job prospects.
