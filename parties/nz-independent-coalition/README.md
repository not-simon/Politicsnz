# NZ Independent Coalition

| **Ideology** | Electorate-driven representation (MPs to poll electorates and vote accordingly); higher pensions; free tertiary education |
| **Seats** | None won (Horan sat as an independent MP until 2014) |

## History

Brendan Horan was expelled from NZ First in December 2012 amid accusations (which he denied) of taking money from his
dying mother's bank account. He formed the party for the 2014 election on the slogan "safe children, safe whanau, safe
society". It won 872 votes (0.04%) and Horan left Parliament. [1][2]

## MPs

None elected under its banner. Leader Brendan Horan was already a sitting MP (elected on NZ First's list in 2011; independent from December 2012) and left Parliament at the 2014 election.
