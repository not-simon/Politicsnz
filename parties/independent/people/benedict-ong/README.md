## Current status

Independent candidate for **Dunedin** in 2026; first-term Dunedin City Councillor. Never an MP.

## Biography

Says he was born in Dunedin and raised largely in Australia; BCom in finance from UNSW (2004). Worked in banking in Singapore (Rabobank M&A 2007–2010; RBC wealth management 2013–2017; UOB). Fellow councillor Lee Vandervis has disputed Ong's descriptions of senior banking roles.

## Political career

- Elected to Dunedin City Council in October 2025 (2,596 votes) and eighth in the mayoral race; the highest-spending candidate ($44,098).

## Controversies

- **Council code-of-conduct disputes (2025–26):** his complaint alleging discriminatory comments by Cr John Chambers was dismissed by an independent investigator for lacking substance; the chief executive's complaint against Ong (over public comments and alleged conduct toward staff) proceeded, his Civic Centre access was restricted, and on 26 March 2026 the council censured him, stripped key roles and voted 10–2 to ask him to resign. He refused, reading from Kafka's *The Trial* at the meeting.
