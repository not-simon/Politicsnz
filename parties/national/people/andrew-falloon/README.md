## Current status

Former MP. He resigned on 21 July 2020 (see Controversies). His activities since are not detailed in the sources consulted.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born on 29 August 1983 in Ashburton. He boarded at Christchurch Boys' High School and studied political science and economics at the University of Canterbury.
- **Early politics:** stood list-only for ACT in 2005 and 2008.
- **Parliamentary staff:** from 2007, a parliamentary researcher, then a staffer in the offices of Rodney Hide, Phil Heatley and Steven Joyce.

## Political career

- **Rangitata (2017):** won the seat for National, beating Labour's Jo Luxton by 6,331 votes.
- **Spokesperson:** South Island regional development under Simon Bridges, then biosecurity under Todd Muller and Judith Collins.

**Roles held:**

- MP for Rangitata (2017–2020)
- National spokesperson for biosecurity; South Island regional development

## Record & notable actions

- **Conscience votes:** a social liberal; voted for the End of Life Choice and abortion bills.

## Controversies

- **Resignation (July 2020):**
  - On 20 July 2020 he announced he would not seek re-election, citing personal reasons.
  - It was then reported that he had sent a pornographic image to a 19-year-old student. He claimed someone else had used his phone.
  - Judith Collins said she "no longer trusted his story" after reports of further explicit messages to other women, and he resigned effective immediately on 21 July.
  - At least five women reported similar behaviour. Police investigated but did not lay charges.
