## Current status

Former MP. He resigned in May 2013 after the Hanmer Springs incident (see Controversies). He runs a private property investment company, The Mighty Rocket Group, which was later renamed St Pauls Asset Management. ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/business/property/120228206/former-national-mp-aaron-gilmore-is-in-dispute-with-the-st-pauls-apartment-body-corporate-over-legal-fees))

## Biography

- **Early life:** born on 26 August 1973 in Christchurch, to shopkeeper parents. He attended Shirley Boys' High School.
- **Education:** MCom in economics from the University of Canterbury (1995).
- **Career:**
  - Analyst at the Ministry of Transport, then at Treasury's Crown Company Monitoring Advisory Unit.
  - Ernst & Young and Cameron & Partners (utility advisory).
  - Corporate development manager at General Cable.

## Political career

- **First term (2008–11):** contested Christchurch East in 2008 and 2011, losing to Lianne Dalziel both times. He was a list MP from 2008 to 2011.
- **2011 election:** appeared to be returned on election night, but lost his place when special votes gave National one seat fewer.
- **Return (February 2013):** re-entered Parliament as the next person on National's list when Lockwood Smith resigned to become High Commissioner to the UK. ([Stuff](http://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/8310260/Aaron-Gilmore-back-in-Parliament))
- **Resignation:** announced on 12 May 2013; his membership ended on 27 May. Claudette Hauiti replaced him.

**Roles held:**

- List MP (2008–2011; Feb–May 2013)

## Controversies

- **Hanmer Springs incident (2013):** it was alleged that after a barman refused to serve him more alcohol, Gilmore asked "Do you know who I am?" and threatened to have the Prime Minister get the barman sacked. He resigned under pressure from senior ministers after it emerged he had misled John Key. ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/8639144/Lawyer-stands-by-allegations))
- **MBIE emails:** emails released in May 2013 showed that while an MBIE contractor in 2012 he had told a Treasury manager "this sort of thing will come back to haunt you". The ministry called the emails "inappropriate". ([Scoop](http://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/HL1305/S00083/mbie-releases-aaron-gilmore-emails-text-version.htm))
- **Parliamentary CV:** his CV on Parliament's website wrongly listed him as a CFA Institute member.
