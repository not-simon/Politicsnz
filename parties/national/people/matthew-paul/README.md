## Biography

Sources: [National Party candidate profile](https://www.national.org.nz/team/matthewpaul). The party profile is self-published, so the details below are as the party describes them.

- **Current role:** managing director of an environmental and landscape consultancy. He founded his first arboricultural business in 2008.
- **Background:** grew up in Bombay, Papakura and Takanini, and attended Rosehill College.
- **Education:** studied horticulture and arboriculture at Wintec; Executive MBA from Massey (2025).
- **Party roles:** Port Waikato electorate chair; co-chair of National's Bluegreens.
- **Personal:** married to Stacey; two children.
