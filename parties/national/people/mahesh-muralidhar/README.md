## Biography

Sources: [National Party candidate profile](https://www.national.org.nz/team/maheshmuralidhar). The party profile is self-published, so the details below are as the party describes them.

- **Current role:** chief executive of the venture firm Phase One Ventures; board member of Ministry of Awesome.
- **Career:** an early executive at Canva, with senior tech roles in Asia and Australia.
- **Background:** born in Kerala, India, and spent part of his childhood in Singapore. He came to New Zealand in the 1990s and attended Napier Boys' High School.
- **Education:**
  - BSc (Statistics) and Graduate Diploma in Arts from Auckland.
  - MBA from UNSW.
  - Postgraduate Diploma in Public Policy from Auckland.
- **Personal:** lives in Mission Bay with his wife, Praveena.
