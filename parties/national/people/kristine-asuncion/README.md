## Biography

Sources: [National Party candidate profile](https://www.national.org.nz/team/kristineasuncion). The party profile is self-published, so the details below are as the party describes them.

- **Background:** born in the Philippines; moved to New Zealand in 2008. She attended Verdon College.
- **Education:** BCom and a Postgraduate Diploma in Entrepreneurship from Otago.
- **Career:**
  - Contract milker with her husband for more than a decade.
  - HR professional and Dunedin business owner.
- **Community roles:** leadership roles within Federated Farmers; chair of Filipino Dairy Workers of New Zealand.
