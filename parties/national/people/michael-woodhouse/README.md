## Current status

Former MP. Before the 2023 election he withdrew his name from National's list, objecting to his proposed ranking, and then lost the Dunedin electorate. Since April 2024 he has been chief executive of Forté Health, a private hospital in Christchurch.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in 1965 in South Dunedin, the fifth of nine children.
- **Early career:** worked at the National Bank and played club rugby in Scotland and England.
- **Education:** commerce and accounting at the University of Otago, and a Master of Health Administration from UNSW.
- **Career:** accountant, then roles at Dunedin Hospital and ACC. He was CEO of Mercy Hospital Dunedin (2001–08).

## Political career

- **List MP (2008–23):** never won a seat, standing in Dunedin North and then Dunedin.
- **Whip:** senior whip from 2011.
- **Ministerial career:**
  - Immigration (2013–17).
  - Police (2014–15).
  - Workplace Relations and Safety (2014–17).
  - Revenue (2015–16).
  - ACC (2016–17).
- **Opposition:**
  - Health spokesperson (2017–20).
  - Finance spokesperson (2020–21).
  - Shadow Leader of the House.

**Roles held:**

- Minister of Immigration (2013–2017)
- Minister of Police (2014–2015)
- Minister for Workplace Relations and Safety (2014–2017)
- Minister of Revenue (2015–2016)
- Minister for ACC (2016–2017)
- Minister of Veterans' Affairs (2013–2014)
- National senior whip (2011–2013)
- National spokesperson for health (2017–2020), finance (2020–2021); Shadow Leader of the House (2020–2021, 2023)
- List MP (2008–2023)
- CEO, Mercy Hospital Dunedin (2001–2008)

## Record & notable actions

- **Health and Safety at Work Act 2015:** passed the Act, a major post-Pike River reform.
- **Easter trading (2016):** passed the Shop Trading Hours Amendment, which lets councils allow Easter Sunday trading.
- **Conscience votes:**
  - Voted against marriage equality, End of Life Choice and abortion reform.
  - Was one of the eight MPs who voted against the 2022 conversion-practices ban (see table).

## Controversies

- **"Worm farms" (2015):** his health and safety risk classifications were ridiculed for listing worm farming and mini-golf as high-risk while omitting dairy farming. ([Stuff](http://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/71305806/government-deems-minigolf-and-worm-farming-more-risky-than-cattle-farming))
- **COVID claims (2020):** claimed a homeless man had talked his way into a managed-isolation hotel. The Director-General of Health called the story likely "an urban myth", though later inquiries found a man of no fixed abode had stayed in MIQ after returning from Australia.
- **Patient data (2020):** admitted he had received COVID-19 patient details from Michelle Boag and deleted them. Collins then removed him as health spokesperson. ([TVNZ](https://www.tvnz.co.nz/one-news/new-zealand/national-mp-michael-woodhouse-admits-receiving-similar-covid-19-leak-michelle-boag))
- **Toilet-seat photo (2012):** was photographed with a toilet seat bearing Labour MP Clare Curran's picture. This was reported in 2020. ([ODT](https://www.odt.co.nz/star-news/star-national/muller-mum-toilet-seat-photo))
- **List complaint (2023):** said "being male" had cost him a high list placing.
