## Current status

Former MP and Deputy Speaker. He retired at the 2014 election. He was appointed to the Landcorp board in 2015 and elected to the Southland Regional Council in 2016. He was made a Companion of the Queen's Service Order (QSO) in 2015. ([Stuff](http://www.stuff.co.nz/southland-times/news/69401139/eric-roy-appointed-to-landcorp-board))

## Biography

- **Early life:** born on 27 June 1948 in Gore.
- **Career:** farmer and company director, active in Federated Farmers.
- **Other roles:** Justice of the Peace.

## Political career

- **Awarua (1993):** first elected for the seat.
- **List MP (1996–2002):** lost the merged Invercargill seat to Labour's Mark Peck but stayed in Parliament on the list.
- **Assistant Speaker:** 1998–2002.
- **2002:** out of Parliament.
- **Invercargill (2005–14):** won the seat in 2005 and held it in 2008 and 2011.
- **Deputy Speaker:** in the 50th Parliament.

**Roles held:**

- Deputy Speaker (2011–2014)
- Assistant Speaker (1998–2002)
- MP for Invercargill (2005–2014)
- MP for Awarua (1993–1996)
- List MP (1996–2002)

## Record & notable actions

- **Conscience votes:** voted against marriage equality (2013).
