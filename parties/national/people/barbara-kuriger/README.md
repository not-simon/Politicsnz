## Current status

Deputy Speaker since December 2023. She is MP for Taranaki-King Country (since 2014) and #42 on National's 2026 list. In December 2024, while presiding, she ruled on the Clerk's advice that the Fast-track Approvals Bill's project lists conferred a private benefit. Speaker Brownlee was recalled to the chair and overruled her. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/536285/fast-track-projects-speaker-rules-no-private-benefit-in-list))

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in 1961 in Ōpunake and raised on a dairy farm.
- **Farming:** dairy farmer with her husband Louis. They were Taranaki Sharemilkers of the Year and are shareholders and directors of family farming businesses.
- **Boards:** DairyNZ, Primary ITO, NZ Young Farmers, Taratahi, Venture Taranaki and the Dairy Women's Network, among others.
- **Award:** Dairy Woman of the Year (2012).
- **Personal:** three children.

## Political career

- **2014:** succeeded Shane Ardern in Taranaki-King Country with a 16,773 majority. She has held the seat since, with a 14,355 majority in 2023.
- **Whip:** junior whip (2014–17) and senior whip (2018–20).
- **Spokesperson roles:** agriculture, energy and food safety (2020); agriculture, biosecurity and food safety under Luxon (2021–22).
- **October 2022:** resigned her portfolios over conflicts of interest (see Controversies).
- **2023:** conservation spokesperson outside the shadow cabinet; then Deputy Speaker.

**Roles held:**

- Deputy Speaker of the House (2023–)
- Senior Whip, National Party (2018–2020)
- Junior Whip (2014–2017)
- Chair, Governance and Administration Committee (2020–2021); Primary Production Committee (2020)
- National spokesperson for agriculture, biosecurity, food safety (2020–2022); conservation (2023)
- MP for Taranaki-King Country (2014–)

## Controversies

- **Taratahi (2015):** criticised over her 2012–14 board membership of the Taratahi Agricultural Training Centre, which was under SFO investigation. She denied knowing of any wrongdoing. ([Stuff](http://www.stuff.co.nz/taranaki-daily-news/news/72120447/kuriger-must-have-known-about-taratahi-rorts--cunliffe))
- **Family farm and MPI (2020–22):**
  - The family company Oxbow Dairies faced animal-welfare charges. Her son Tony pleaded guilty in 2020. She had been a director until 2014.
  - In October 2022 she gave up the agriculture, biosecurity and food safety portfolios after admitting she had blurred the line between her family's dispute with MPI and her role as spokesperson. ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/130174859/national-mp-barbara-kuriger-drops-agriculture-portfolios-over-blurred-line-in-family-dispute))
  - Newsroom's OIA-released emails showed a pattern of personal attacks on MPI officials. An independent review by Mike Heron KC cleared MPI and found the investigation was not politically motivated. ([Newsroom](https://www.newsroom.co.nz/mps-emails-show-pattern-of-personal-attacks-on-ministry))
  - She declined to resign from Parliament. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/482723/kuriger-says-no-plans-to-step-down-after-family-scandal-aired))
