## Current status

MP for Rangitīkei since 2023 and the first woman to hold the seat. She is #31 on National's 2026 list. As National's junior whip, she deputised for Senior Whip Stuart Smith as scrutineer at the 21 April 2026 confidence vote. She kept the result confidential but said she had voted for Luxon. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_2026_New_Zealand_National_Party_leadership_election))

## Biography

- **Early life:** born Susan Dossor. She holds a BA from the University of Otago.
- **Farming:** with her husband Richard she has run Tunnel Hill farm at Turakina since 1993, along with the award-winning Coastal Lamb brands.
- **Electorate staff:** worked in the Rangitīkei electorate offices of Simon Power and Ian McKelvie for over 20 years. She calls McKelvie her mentor.
- **Community:** trustee of the Whanganui Community Foundation and Sport Whanganui.

## Political career

- **2022:** selected in November 2022 to succeed Ian McKelvie.
- **2023:** won Rangitīkei by 9,785 votes over Labour's Zulfiqar Butt. ([Electoral Commission](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-41.html))

**Roles held:**

- MP for Rangitīkei (2023–)
- National Junior Whip

## Record & notable actions

- **Local priorities:** campaigned on restoring a 24-hour police presence in Feilding and on Ruapehu Alpine Lifts' financial problems.
- **Positions:** describes herself as "fiscally conservative and socially liberal… pro-choice". She said she would cross the floor if National tried to change abortion law. ([The Spinoff](https://thespinoff.co.nz/politics/04-10-2023/suze-redmayne-once-voted-green-now-she-wants-to-keep-rangitikei-blue))
- She was not in Parliament for the 2013–2022 conscience votes.
