## Current status

Deceased. Chester Borrows died on 27 February 2023 of head and neck squamous-cell cancer, aged 65. ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/former-mp-chester-borrows-dies-after-cancer-battle/O657NWKO3JDE7CFX457CQRQFTU/))

## Biography

- Born Kerry James Borrows on 20 June 1957 and raised in Nelson.
- **Police career:**
  - Served in Nelson, Wellington and Auckland, and as the sole-charge officer at Pātea.
  - Received a Queen's Commendation for Brave Conduct (1978) for trying to arrest an armed murderer.
- **Law:** LLB from Victoria University (2002); then a lawyer in Hāwera.
- **Church:** later an Anglican archdeacon.
- **Honours:** QSO (2018).

## Political career

- **Whanganui:** lost the seat in 1999 and 2002, won it from Labour's Jill Pettis in 2005, and held it until he retired in 2017.
- **Minister:** Courts, outside Cabinet, plus associate Justice and Social Development (2011–14).
- **Deputy Speaker:** 2014–17.
- **After Parliament:** chaired the Labour government's Safe and Effective Justice Advisory Group (2018).

**Roles held:**

- Deputy Speaker (2014–2017)
- Minister for Courts (2011–2014)
- Associate Minister of Justice; Social Development (2011–2014)
- Chair, Justice and Electoral Committee
- MP for Whanganui (2005–2017)
- Chair, Safe and Effective Justice Advisory Group (2018–c.2020)

## Record & notable actions

- **"Smart on crime":** known for a "smart on crime" rather than "tough on crime" approach.
- **Section 59 amendment (2007):** proposed an amendment to the anti-smacking law to allow light correction. It was defeated 63–58.
- **DNA sampling:** said his proudest achievement was persuading the government (2008) to take DNA samples from arrested people.
- **Criticism of his party:**
  - Spoke out against Donald Trump's refugee policies (2017).
  - Later criticised National's gang-patch ban and boot-camp proposals as headline-driven (2022). ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/300610969/it-will-not-work-excop-and-national-mp-says-partys-gang-policy-is-made-for-headlines))
- **Conscience votes:** voted against marriage equality (2013).

- **Gang mentor (2014):** publicly disagreed with Corrections Minister Judith Collins over her decision to stand down a gang member, a friend of his, from prison mentoring work.
