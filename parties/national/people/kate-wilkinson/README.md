## Current status

Former MP. She retired at the 2014 election and was appointed a Commissioner of the Environment Court in May 2015. She lives on her family farm at Swannanoa.

## Biography

- Born 3 August 1957 and raised on a mixed cropping farm at Chertsey, Mid Canterbury.
- LLB from the University of Canterbury.
- Lawyer at Christchurch firm Harman & Co for 25 years, becoming a partner in 1984.

## Political career

- **List MP (2005–11):** entered Parliament on the list.
- **Waimakariri:** lost the seat to Clayton Cosgrove by 390 votes in 2008, then beat him by 642 votes in 2011.
- **Ministerial career:**
  - Conservation and Food Safety ministries from 2008.
  - Minister of Labour (2011–12).
- **2012–13:** resigned as Labour Minister in November 2012 after the Pike River Royal Commission report, and was dropped from Cabinet in January 2013.
- **Retirement:** retired in 2014; Matt Doocey succeeded her.

**Roles held:**

- Minister of Conservation (2010–2013)
- Minister of Labour (2011–2012)
- Minister for Food Safety (2008–2013)
- Associate Minister of Immigration (2008–2013)
- MP for Waimakariri (2011–2014)
- List MP (2005–2011)
- Commissioner, Environment Court (2015–)

## Controversies

- **Folic acid:** criticised by paediatricians for not mandating folic acid in bread. ([TVNZ](http://tvnz.co.nz/national-news/folic-acid-decision-missed-opportunity-5056538))
- **Pike River resignation (2012):** the Royal Commission found major failings in the Department of Labour's mine regulation. Prime Minister John Key described her resignation as "the honourable thing to do".
