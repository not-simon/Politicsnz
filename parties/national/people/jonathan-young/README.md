## Current status

Former MP. He lost New Plymouth to Labour's Glen Bennett by 2,555 votes in 2020, and his list place of #22 was too low to return him. His activities since are not detailed in the sources consulted.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in 1958, son of National MP and minister Venn Young. He attended New Plymouth Boys' High and Hāwera High.
- **Ministry:** senior minister of CityChurch Waitakere for 18 years.
- **Other work:** primary teacher, financial administrator, and development work in Cambodia.

## Political career

- **New Plymouth (2008):** took the seat from Harry Duynhoven by 105 votes.
- **Re-elected:** 2011, 2014 and 2017.
- **2020:** defeated.

**Roles held:**

- MP for New Plymouth (2008–2020)

## Record & notable actions

- **Conscience votes:** voted against marriage equality ("marriage had always been a heterosexual institution"), End of Life Choice and abortion reform.

## Controversies

- **"Ex-lesbian" remark (2008):** criticised for saying "one of my associates was an ex-lesbian". He was also criticised for links to Teen Challenge and for objecting to homosexuality being presented "as a normal alternative".
- **Internet as "Skynet" (2011):** likened the internet to Skynet during the copyright-amendment debate.
