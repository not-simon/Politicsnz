## Current status

Former MP. He retired at the 2014 election and returned to his central North Island farms. Barbara Kuriger succeeded him.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born on 26 January 1960 and raised in Ōpunake.
- **Farming:** a dairy farmer.
- **Family:** a distant cousin of Jacinda Ardern.

## Political career

- **Taranaki-King Country (1998):** won the by-election that followed Jim Bolger's retirement, and held the seat until 2014.
- **Committee chair:** Primary Production Committee (2008–14).

**Roles held:**

- MP for Taranaki-King Country (1998–2014)
- Chair, Primary Production Committee (2008–2014)

## Record & notable actions

- **Fonterra:** a driving force behind the 2001 legislation that created Fonterra.
- **Conscience votes:** voted against marriage equality (2013).

**Conscience votes** (recorded third-reading votes):

| Bill | Vote |
|---|---|
| [Marriage (Definition of Marriage) Amendment Bill — third reading, 17 Apr 2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marriage_(Definition_of_Marriage)_Amendment_Act_2013) | against |

## Controversies

- **Tractor protest (2003):** drove a tractor up Parliament's steps to protest the proposed agricultural emissions research levy, the "fart tax". He was charged with disorderly conduct; police withdrew the charge after a judge called the case a waste of resources.
