## Current status

Backbench MP for Maungakiekie since 2023. He is #43 on National's 2026 list and is re-contesting the seat. In 2026 he co-sponsored, with Labour's Camilla Belich, a modern-slavery reporting bill that passed its first reading on 29 April 2026 with every party except ACT in support. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/585319/watch-national-and-labour-mps-team-up-to-get-slavery-bill-heard-after-act-objects))

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in Masterton and attended Wairarapa College. BCom from Victoria University of Wellington.
- **Career:**
  - General manager of Parenting with Confidence (later Parents Inc and The Parenting Place).
  - Co-founded the conservative think tank the Maxim Institute with Bruce Logan in 2001, and was its CEO from 2005.
  - CEO of The Parenting Place (2015–20).
  - Founding chair of the Manurewa poverty charity Te Whakaora Tangata, and co-founder of the Christian education body the Venn Foundation.

## Political career

- **2023:** selected for Maungakiekie in March 2023. He beat Labour minister Priyanca Radhakrishnan by 4,617 votes. ([Electoral Commission](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-23.html))
- **Maiden speech:** opened by reciting Psalm 1 in te reo Māori.

**Roles held:**

- MP for Maungakiekie (2023–)
- CEO, Maxim Institute (2005–c.2013)
- CEO, The Parenting Place (2015–2020)

## Record & notable actions

- **Gaza (November 2023):** told a constituent he was urging the government internally to call for an immediate ceasefire. ([Newshub](https://www.newshub.co.nz/home/politics/2023/11/incoming-national-mp-greg-fleming-says-he-is-urging-govt-to-call-for-immediate-ceasefire-in-gaza.html))
- **Fireworks (2025):** received the 90,000-signature petition calling for a ban on public fireworks sales.
- **Māori media funding (December 2025):** publicly agreed that a planned 25% cut to Te Māngai Pāho would be harmful and lobbied against it. Budget 2026 later allocated NZ$48m over four years to Māori-language broadcasting. ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/politics/360908147/incoming-25-funding-cut-maori-media-will-be-catastrophic-even-national-mp-agrees))
- **Modern-slavery bill (2026):** see Current status.
- He was not in Parliament for the 2013–2022 conscience votes.

## Controversies

- **Maxim Institute positions:** while at Maxim he campaigned against the Prostitution Reform Act 2003 and the Civil Union Act 2004, comparing civil unions to polygamy and incest. When this resurfaced in April 2023, he said his views had changed. ([Newshub](https://www.newshub.co.nz/home/politics/2023/04/national-candidate-greg-fleming-compared-civil-unions-to-polygamy-and-incest.html), [1News](https://www.1news.co.nz/2023/04/04/national-candidate-previously-compared-civil-unions-to-incest/))
- **Parenting Place (2020):** The Spinoff reported on The Parenting Place's past material relating to conversion practices during his time in its leadership. ([The Spinoff](https://thespinoff.co.nz/society/10-10-2020/parenting-place-conversion-therapy))
