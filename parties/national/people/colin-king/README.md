## Current status

Former MP. He lost the Kaikōura selection to Stuart Smith in 2013. He later worked as a consultant in the primary-industries and tertiary-education sectors, and lost the 2016 Marlborough mayoralty to John Leggett.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born on 19 December 1949 in Canterbury. He farms in Marlborough.
- **Shearing:** won the Golden Shears Open in 1982, 1987 and 1988, the only left-handed shearer to win it. He was made MNZM in 2000 for services to shearing and the wool industry.
- **Meat Board:** a member of the NZ Meat Board from 2000.
- **Personal:** married; four adult children.

## Political career

- **Kaikōura (2005):** won the seat by 4,675 votes. He held it in 2008 and 2011.
- **Deselection (2013):** lost the selection to Stuart Smith. He declined to appear with other departing MPs at the party conference.

**Roles held:**

- MP for Kaikōura (2005–2014)
