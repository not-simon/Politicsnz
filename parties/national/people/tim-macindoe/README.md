## Current status

**Mayor of Hamilton** since October 2025. He won the 2025 mayoral election on a platform of lower rates and a "slimmed-down" council. ([Hamilton City Council](https://hamilton.govt.nz/your-council/news/latest-news/new-faces-new-term-hamiltons-next-council-gets-underway), [The Spinoff](https://thespinoff.co.nz/politics/08-10-2025/the-battle-for-hamilton-a-former-climate-activist-takes-on-a-former-national-mp)) He had been elected to the council's East Ward at a February 2024 by-election, replacing Ryan Hamilton. While a councillor he also lobbied for the University of Waikato's proposed medical school.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in 1961 in Auckland. He boarded at King's College.
- **Education:** BA (Hons) in history from Otago; LLB from Waikato (2009).
- **Teaching:** secondary teacher for 17 years, including six as deputy principal of St Peter's School, Cambridge.
- **Before Parliament:** chief executive of Arts Waikato.
- **Personal:** married to Anne; two daughters. An Anglican.

## Political career

- **Early attempts:** stood for United in 1996, then for National in 1999, 2002 and 2005, without success.
- **Hamilton West (2008):** won the seat from Labour's Martin Gallagher, and held it in 2011, 2014 and 2017.
- **Whip:** junior whip from 2013 and senior whip from 2014.
- **Minister (April–October 2017):** Customs, Associate Education and Associate Transport.
- **Opposition:** spokesperson for ACC, seniors and civil defence, and shadow attorney-general (2019–20).
- **2020:** lost Hamilton West to Gaurav Sharma by 6,267 votes; at #23 on the list he was not returned.

**Roles held:**

- Mayor of Hamilton (2025–)
- Hamilton City Councillor, East Ward (2024–2025)
- Minister of Customs (May–Oct 2017)
- Chief Government Whip (2014–2017)
- Deputy Chief Government Whip (2013–2014)
- MP for Hamilton West (2008–2020)
- Shadow Attorney-General (2019–2020)
