## Current status

Former MP. She retired at the 2023 election, as announced in May 2022. Miles Anderson succeeded her in Waitaki. Her current activities are not detailed in the sources consulted.

## Biography

- Born Jacqueline Hay on 13 May 1957 in Palmerston North.
- Before politics she worked as an actor (including hosting *Play School*), a radio announcer and in education.
- Waitaki District councillor for the Oamaru ward.

## Political career

- **Otago (2005):** won the seat back from Labour's David Parker by 1,995 votes.
- **Waitaki (2008–2023):** held the successor seat. Her majority was 16,668 in 2014 and 3,281 in 2020.
- **Ministerial roles:** Commerce and Consumer Affairs and Small Business, outside Cabinet (December 2016 – October 2017).
- **Assistant Speaker:** 2020–2023.

**Roles held:**

- Assistant Speaker (2020–2023)
- Minister of Commerce and Consumer Affairs; Small Business (2016–2017)
- Parliamentary Private Secretary (2014–2016)
- MP for Otago (2005–2008)
- MP for Waitaki (2008–2023)
- Waitaki District Councillor

## Record & notable actions

- **Drug law:** campaigned to ban BZP "party pills" (2007) and Salvia divinorum.
- **Conscience votes:**
  - Voted for marriage equality (2013).
  - Voted against the End of Life Choice and abortion bills (see table).

## Controversies

- **Bridges complaint (2021):** in November 2021 Judith Collins used Dean's earlier complaint about a 2016 remark by Simon Bridges to demote him, which led to Collins' own removal. Dean said she was "surprised and disappointed", that this had not been her intention, and that she had long since accepted Bridges' apology.
