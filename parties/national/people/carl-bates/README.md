## Current status

Backbench MP for Whanganui since 2023. He is #28 on National's 2026 list and is re-contesting the seat.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born on 13 March 1983 and raised in Whanganui; attended Whanganui High School.
- **Education:** BBS in accountancy from Massey University.
- **Career:**
  - Director of an aged-care facility, the Arena Manawatū stadium company and UCOL.
  - Founded Sirdar Global Group, a board-training firm, and ran it in New Zealand and Africa until 2020.
  - Self-published two business books.
- **Personal:** married to Candice, whom he met while living in South Africa; three children.

## Political career

- **Early involvement:** joined National at 13 and was a Youth MP in 2000.
- **2023:** list #47. He won Whanganui from first-term Labour MP Steph Lewis by 5,512 votes, campaigning on reversing Three Waters and the RMA reforms. ([Electoral Commission](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-62.html))

**Roles held:**

- MP for Whanganui (2023–)

## Record & notable actions

- Backbench MP.
- He was not in Parliament for the 2013–2022 conscience votes.

## Controversies

- **Pecuniary interests (2025):**
  - In September 2025 the NZ Herald reported that soon after his election he moved shares in family property companies owning 25 properties into a trust. The properties were not on the Register of Pecuniary Interests.
  - Bates said the Registrar had advised him such holdings did not need to be declared.
  - On 25 September 2025 the Registrar opened an inquiry. ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/mp-put-25-properties-in-family-trust-before-facing-financial-declaration-rules/5SNYJCC3LZB4LHPHLLLHBA7EZA/), [Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/politics/360835388/parliament-watchdog-investigate-national-mp-over-his-trusts-25-rental-homes))
  - The outcome of the inquiry is not given in the sources consulted.
