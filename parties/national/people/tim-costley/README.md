## Current status

Backbench MP for Ōtaki since 2023. He sits on the Foreign Affairs, Defence and Trade Committee and the Governance and Administration Committee. He is #37 on National's 2026 list and is contesting Kāpiti.

## Biography

- **Early life:** raised in Palmerston North by teacher parents and attended Palmerston North Boys' High School.
- **Education:** BSc in mathematical physics from Massey University, and a master's in international defence and security from Cranfield University.
- **RNZAF (from 2001):**
  - Flew Iroquois and NH90 helicopters, serving in the Solomon Islands, East Timor and Afghanistan and on rescue and humanitarian missions.
  - Equerry to Prince William in 2014.
  - Retired as a wing commander, commanding the Flying Training Wing at Ohakea.
- **Charity:** founded the Missing Wingman Trust for RNZAF families.
- **Personal:** married to Emma; three daughters.

## Political career

- **2020:** lost Ōtaki to Labour's Terisa Ngobi by 2,988 votes.
- **2023:** beat Ngobi by 6,271 votes. ([Electoral Commission](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-34.html))

**Roles held:**

- MP for Ōtaki (2023–)
- RNZAF pilot from 2001, retired as Wing Commander

## Record & notable actions

- Backbench MP on the defence and governance committees.
- He was not in Parliament for the 2013–2022 conscience votes.

## Controversies

- **Grant Robertson remark (early 2024 per RNZ; Wikipedia dates it March 2025):** apologised to Robertson, a gay former finance minister, after saying in the House "I don't like the thought of Grant Robertson inspecting me in the shower". ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/510900/national-mp-tim-costley-apologises-to-grant-robertson-over-shower-comment))
- **Accommodation allowance (June 2024):** he claimed more than NZ$36,000 in accommodation allowance to live in a Wellington apartment he owned. Luxon defended the claim, citing the late finishes and the commute to Waikanae. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/519212/christopher-luxon-defends-mp-tim-costley-claiming-allowance-to-live-in-own-flat))
