## Current status

Since the 2 April 2026 reshuffle he has been Minister of Commerce and Consumer Affairs, Minister for Small Business and Manufacturing, and Associate Minister of Immigration. He is MP for Upper Harbour and #19 on National's 2026 list. ([Beehive](https://www.beehive.govt.nz/release/pm-refreshes-ministerial-team))

## Biography

- **Early life:** born on 8 March 1973 in Hāwera. He attended Wanganui Collegiate and Massey University (BA in history and sociology, 1994), and studied journalism at WITT.
- **Journalism:** founded and edited the Dunedin community paper *Inside Otago* (1996–98).
- **Political staff:** National Party researcher; press secretary to Jenny Shipley and to ACT's Rodney Hide; communications adviser to Auckland Mayor John Banks (2002–04).
- **Business:** CEO of the Newmarket Business Association (2005–10), and later ran a communications firm.
- **Personal:** three children.

## Political career

- **Local government:**
  - Elected Auckland Councillor for Ōrākei in 2010 as an independent, beating Citizens & Ratepayers' deputy leader. C&R members called it a "betrayal". ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/politics/brewers-stance-dubbed-betrayal/SGVMQYDYRPGI7Y6BRPV74ZHNNE/))
  - Re-elected unopposed in 2013 and did not stand in 2016.
  - Rodney Local Board member (2016–19).
- **National Party:** a member since 1996.
- **2023:** won Upper Harbour from Labour's Vanushi Walters by 11,192 votes. ([Electoral Commission](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-55.html))
- **Parliament:** chaired the Finance and Expenditure Committee from January 2025, then became a minister in April 2026.

**Roles held:**

- Minister of Commerce and Consumer Affairs (2026–)
- Minister for Small Business and Manufacturing (2026–)
- Associate Minister of Immigration (2026–)
- Chair, Finance and Expenditure Committee (2025–2026)
- MP for Upper Harbour (2023–)
- Auckland Councillor, Ōrākei ward (2010–2016)
- Rodney Local Board member (2016–2019)

## Record & notable actions

- **Member's bill:** his Life Jackets for Children and Young Persons Bill (2025) would require boat users aged 15 and under to wear life jackets. ([Parliament](https://bills.parliament.nz/v/6/2e5a2466-5ed8-4380-1880-08dd77d42c62?Tab=hansard))
- **Council record:** a prominent critic of Mayor Len Brown, including over undeclared gifts.
- He was not in Parliament for the 2013–2022 conscience votes.

## Controversies

- **"Stale, pale, males" (2023):** celebrated his win over New Zealand's first Sri Lankan-born MP as a victory for "stale, pale, males". He apologised, calling it "a poor attempt at humour". ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/502875/new-national-mp-cameron-brewer-celebrated-victory-for-stale-pale-males-after-defeat-of-sri-lankan-born-rival))
- **Undeclared gifts (2013):** having criticised Len Brown over gifts, he was himself found not to have declared some gifts and hospitality.
