## Current status

He is a regional councillor. He was elected to Horizons Regional Council at a 2018 by-election and re-elected in 2019, 2022 and 2025, and has served as deputy chair.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in 1966 in Upper Hutt.
- **Education:** Bachelor of Social Work from Massey University (1997).
- **Personal:** married; three children.

## Political career

- **Local government:** Palmerston North City Councillor from 2001 and mayor from 2007, when he defeated Heather Tanguay.
- **2014 election:** lost Palmerston North to Iain Lees-Galloway, but was confirmed as a list MP at #51 once final results were in.
- **Retirement:** announced in late 2016 that he would not stand in 2017.

**Roles held:**

- List MP (2014–2017)
- Mayor of Palmerston North (2007–2014)
- Horizons Regional Councillor (2018–)

## Record & notable actions

No significant legislative record in the sources consulted. He was not in Parliament for any of the major conscience votes.

## Controversies

- **Delayed resignation as mayor (2014):** criticised for keeping the mayoralty, and two salaries, until his list seat was confirmed. The delay pushed the mayoral by-election into February 2015.
