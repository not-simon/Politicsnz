## Current status

List MP since 14 December 2023. She sits on the Finance and Expenditure Committee and has been deputy chair of the Regulations Review Committee since January 2025. She is #15 on National's 2026 list (list-only).

## Biography

- **Early life:** born Nancy Nan Lu in 1987 in Guangzhou, China, and moved to New Zealand with her parents in 1997. She attended Avondale Primary, Pakuranga Intermediate and Macleans College.
- **Education:** BCom in international business and accounting from the University of Auckland, and a Master of Public Administration from Harvard Kennedy School (2023).
- **Career:** chartered accountant at PwC, EY and Fonterra, working in New Zealand, Hong Kong and mainland China.
- **Boards:** Chinese New Settlers Services Trust and ContainerCo (NZ) Ltd.
- **Personal:** married with two daughters.

## Political career

- **2020:** ranked #26 as a list-only candidate, the highest-ranked new National candidate and National's only Chinese candidate that year. She was not elected because of National's result. ([Newsroom](https://www.newsroom.co.nz/nancy-lu-biggest-surprise-in-national-list-rankings))
- **2023:** ranked #20 and was the first unelected National list candidate.
  - The death of a candidate turned Port Waikato into a by-election, so Andrew Bayly temporarily took a list seat.
  - When Bayly won the by-election on 25 November 2023, Lu took the vacated list seat on 14 December 2023. ([The Spinoff](https://thespinoff.co.nz/politics/24-11-2023/next-on-the-list-nancy-lus-political-future-hangs-on-port-waikato), [NZ Gazette](https://gazette.govt.nz/notice/id/2023-au5838))
