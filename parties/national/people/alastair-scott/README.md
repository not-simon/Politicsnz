## Current status

Former MP. He announced in June 2019 that he would not stand in 2020. He owns the Matahiwi Estate winery in the Wairarapa.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born on 18 September 1965 in Wellington. He attended Wellington College and Massey University.
- **Business:** owner of the Matahiwi Estate winery.
- **Personal:** partner Robyn Noble-Campbell; each has three children.

## Political career

- **Wairarapa (2014):** beat Jo Hayes for National's selection, then won the seat over Labour's Kieran McAnulty, with NZ First's Ron Mark a close third.
- **2017:** held the seat with a reduced majority.
- **Retirement:** announced on 25 June 2019 that he would retire at the 2020 election.

**Roles held:**

- MP for Wairarapa (2014–2020)
