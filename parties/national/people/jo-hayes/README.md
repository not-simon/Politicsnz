## Current status

Former MP. Three days after the 2020 election she was named general manager of the Rangitāne Tū Mai Rā Trust. ([Rangitāne](https://tumaira.iwi.nz/new-gm-announced-to-strengthen-momentum-at-rangitane/)) She stood unsuccessfully for Masterton mayor in 2022, and in 2025 took part in the unofficial "People's Select Committee" on pay equity.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in 1959. She is of Ngāti Porou, Te Āti Haunui-a-Pāpārangi and Rangitāne ki Wairarapa descent, and was raised in farming.
- **Career:** worked in health and for the Māori development organisation Ngā Tai O te Awa.
- **Personal:** married, with two sons.

## Political career

- **2011:** lost Dunedin South to Clare Curran.
- **List MP (January 2014):** entered Parliament when Katrina Shanks resigned.
- **Electorate contests:** lost Christchurch East to Poto Williams in 2014 and 2017, and Mana in 2020 (by 16,224 votes). She was not returned in 2020.
- **Roles:**
  - Chaired the Social Services Committee and was Third Whip.
  - Māori development and Whānau Ora spokesperson in opposition.

**Roles held:**

- List MP (2014–2020)
- Chair, Social Services Committee (2014–2017)
- Third Whip (51st Parliament)
- National spokesperson for Māori development; Whānau Ora (2017–2020)

## Record & notable actions

- **Member's bill:** her Minors (Court Consent to Relationships) Legislation Bill requires Family Court approval for marriages of 16- and 17-year-olds. It passed unanimously in August 2018.
- **Conscience votes:** voted against the End of Life Choice and abortion bills.
