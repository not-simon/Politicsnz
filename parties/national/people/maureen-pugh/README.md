## Current status

MP for West Coast-Tasman and Assistant Speaker since December 2023. She is retiring at the 2026 election, as announced on 23 January 2026.

In May 2026 she visited Taiwan with a cross-party group of MPs, and China then banned the four from entering China, Hong Kong and Macau for a year. Luxon called the ban "entirely inappropriate". In September 2026, presiding as Assistant Speaker, she ejected Debbie Ngarewa-Packer and Willie Jackson from the chamber. ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/politics/360987562/four-new-zealand-mps-banned-china-one-year-after-taiwan-visit), [RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/597368/new-zealand-to-raise-china-s-entirely-inappropriate-ban-on-four-mps-directly-christopher-luxon-says), [Te Ao](https://www.teaonews.co.nz/2026/09/17/te-pati-maori-co-leader-ejected-from-parliament-after-c-word-clash/))

## Biography

- Born in 1958.
- Westland District councillor from 1998 and the district's first woman mayor (2004–13). She was returned unopposed in 2007.

## Political career

- **The "Yo-yo MP":** in 2014 and again in 2017 she gained a list seat on preliminary results but lost it on special votes. She entered Parliament mid-term both times:
  - February 2016, replacing Tim Groser;
  - March 2018, replacing Bill English.
- **2020:** list #19. She kept her seat and was elected Junior Whip.
- **2023:** won West Coast-Tasman from Labour's Damien O'Connor by 1,017 votes. She then became Assistant Speaker. ([Electoral Commission](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-61.html))

**Roles held:**

- Assistant Speaker of the House (2023–)
- Junior Whip, National Party (2020–2023)
- MP for West Coast-Tasman (2023–2026)
- List MP (2016–2017; 2018–2023)
- Mayor of Westland (2004–2013)
- Westland District Councillor (1998–2004)

## Record & notable actions

- Pushed for local renewable generation to cut the West Coast's high power prices.
- **Conscience votes:** voted against the End of Life Choice and abortion bills (see table).

## Controversies

- **"Fucking useless" (2018):** a recording released by Jami-Lee Ross caught leader Simon Bridges calling her "fucking useless". Bridges apologised. ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/business/107920843/simon-bridges-describes-mp-colleague-as-fing-useless-in-recorded-conversation.html))
- **Medicines (2016):** said she never takes medication and relies on a healthy immune system. She later clarified that she supports pharmaceuticals such as Panadol.
- **Convoy protest (2022):** posted support for the anti-mandate Convoy 2022 and then deleted it, saying she had not realised many protesters were anti-vaccine. ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/300514726/convoy-protest-national-mp-maureen-pugh-deletes-post-thanking-protesters-says-she-didnt-know-they-were-antivax))
- **Climate change (2023):** declined to say she accepted human-caused climate change. After Luxon said climate denial was not acceptable, she reversed her position; RNZ's Guyon Espiner likened her retraction to a "hostage video". ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/131287875/nationals-maureen-pugh-waiting-for-evidence-on-human-impact-on-climate-change))
- **Assault by protester (June 2024):** she was allegedly assaulted by an anti-fast-track protester at Tākaka. ([Newshub](https://www.newshub.co.nz/home/politics/2024/06/national-mp-maureen-pugh-allegedly-assaulted-in-t-kaka-by-anti-fast-track-protester.html))
- **Conflict of interest (as mayor):** accused of failing to declare a financial conflict of interest.
