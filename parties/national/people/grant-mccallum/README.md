## Current status

Backbench MP for Northland since 2023. He is #34 on National's 2026 list and is re-contesting Northland.

## Biography

- **Background:** his father, Ron McCallum, chaired National's Kaipara and Whangārei electorates. McCallum moved to Northland in the 1970s.
- **Education and farming:** agricultural commerce graduate of Lincoln College (1988). He farms at Maungaturoto.
- **Community and party roles:**
  - Chair of the Kauri Museum at Matakohe.
  - Vice-president of Northland Federated Farmers.
  - National Party board member (2005–15).
  - On the executive of the BlueGreens since 2001.

## Political career

- **2015:** lost the Northland by-election selection to Mark Osborne.
- **2020:** chaired Matt King's unsuccessful campaign.
- **2023:** list #68. He won Northland from Labour's Willow-Jean Prime by 6,087 votes. ([Electoral Commission](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-32.html))

**Roles held:**

- MP for Northland (2023–)
- National Party board member (2005–2015)
- Executive, National's BlueGreens (since 2001)

## Record & notable actions

- Identified the cost of living and the regulatory burden on farmers as his electorate's main issues.
- He was not in Parliament for the 2013–2022 conscience votes.

## Controversies

- **Edited clip (2019):** he posted a misleadingly edited clip of Environment Minister David Parker on Twitter, which falsely suggested Parker said he hated farmers. (Reported by the NZ Herald; see the Wikipedia article's citations.)
