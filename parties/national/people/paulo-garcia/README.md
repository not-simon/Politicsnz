## Current status

MP for New Lynn, retiring at the 2026 election, as announced on 16 December 2025. He is deputy chair of the Social Services and Community Committee. ([The Post](https://www.thepost.co.nz/politics/360917227/national-mp-paulo-garcia-standing-down-election))

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in 1965 in San Juan, Metro Manila, Philippines. Graduate of the University of the Philippines, and attended the Academy of American and International Law in Texas.
- **Law in the Philippines:** practised commercial law for ten years.
- **Law in New Zealand:** immigration lawyer, mainly investor migration. He founded Garcia Law.
- **Community roles:** Honorary Consul of the Philippines in Auckland from 2012, and helped set up the NZ Philippines Business Council.

## Political career

- **2017:** contested New Lynn and lost to Deborah Russell; list #50.
- **2019:** entered Parliament on 16 May 2019, replacing Nuk Korako. He was the first MP of Filipino descent and the first Philippines-born member of any foreign national legislature.
- **2020:** list-only candidate; lost his seat.
- **2023:** won New Lynn from Russell by 1,013 votes. ([Electoral Commission](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-28.html))

**Roles held:**

- MP for New Lynn (2023–2026)
- List MP (2019–2020)
- Honorary Consul of the Philippines in Auckland (from 2012)

## Record & notable actions

- **"Coward punch" bill (February 2024):** his member's bill would create an offence of coward punching that causes serious injury or death. UFC champion Israel Adesanya backed it, and the government adopted it as its own in June 2025. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/565530/justice-minister-paul-goldsmith-announces-harsher-penalties-for-one-punch-attacks))
- **Conscience votes:** voted against the End of Life Choice and abortion bills (see table).
