## Current status

MP for Waikato since 2017. He has chaired the Foreign Affairs, Defence and Trade Committee since 2023. He is #48 on National's 2026 list and is re-contesting the seat.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in 1985, one of five children of two primary-school teachers. He grew up in Matamata and attended Matamata College.
- **Education:** psychology degree from the University of Waikato.
- **Farming and young farmers:** dairy, sheep and beef farmer. He was active in NZ Young Farmers (junior vice-president in 2007) and was runner-up for Young Farmer of the Year in 2011.
- **Career and service:**
  - Agribusiness manager at the National Bank/ANZ and Rabobank from 2009.
  - Territorial Force member.
  - St John volunteer.
- **Family:** his great-great-grandfather John Stevens was a Liberal MP.
- **Personal:** married to Hilary; two children. In February 2022 a fall left him with two broken arms and a fractured spine.

## Political career

- **2017:** succeeded Lindsay Tisch in Waikato with about 61% of the vote, and was comfortably re-elected in 2020 and 2023. ([Electoral Commission](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-56.html))
- **Opposition roles:** third whip (2018), then spokesperson in a range of portfolios, including building and construction, defence and veterans.

**Roles held:**

- MP for Waikato (2017–)
- Chair, Foreign Affairs, Defence and Trade Committee (2023–)
- National third whip (2018)
- National spokesperson for building and construction, biosecurity, defence, veterans, ACC and others (2017–2023)

## Record & notable actions

- Campaigned for rural broadband and a Waikato medical school to train rural GPs.
- **Conscience votes:**
  - Voted for the End of Life Choice Bill, unlike most National MPs.
  - Supported the abortion bill at first and second readings but voted against it at third reading (see table).

## Controversies

- **Privileges Committee (August 2023):**
  - The committee found he had behaved in a way that was "aggressive… hostile, unprofessional… [with] an element that was objectively threatening" towards Labour MP Shanan Halbert after a select committee meeting.
  - He was found in contempt and censured by the House.
  - Luxon removed all his portfolios, and he apologised. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/496507/national-mp-tim-van-de-molen-stood-down-from-all-portfolios), [NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/politics/national-mp-tim-van-de-molen-censured-by-parliament-for-threatening-behaviour/YT6GBGMFRJEKPLBJO2JGSSWX5Q/))
