## Current status

Former MP. He lost Panmure-Ōtāhuhu to Jenny Salesa in 2020, and his list place of #24 was too low to return him. His activities since are not detailed in the sources consulted.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born on 20 February 1964 in Delhi. He holds a BCom from the University of Delhi.
- **Career:** worked in his family's freight business.
- **New Zealand:** married Irvinder Kaur in 1989 and moved to New Zealand in 2001.
- **Honours:** Pravasi Bharatiya Samman award (2015).

## Political career

- **List MP (2008–20):** New Zealand's first Indian and first Sikh MP. He contested Manukau East four times.
- **Committee chair:** Law and Order Committee (2015–17).
- **Other roles:** parliamentary private secretary to the Police Minister; internal affairs spokesperson (2017–20).

**Roles held:**

- List MP (2008–2020)
- Chair, Law and Order Committee (2015–2017)
- National spokesperson for internal affairs (2017–2020); ethnic communities (2020)

## Record & notable actions

- **Member's bill:** his Military Manoeuvres Act Repeal Bill became law in 2012.
- **Kirpan bill:** drafted a bill to let Sikhs carry kirpans; it was never drawn.
- **Conscience votes:** voted against marriage equality, End of Life Choice and abortion reform.

## Controversies

- **Job-offer allegations (2009):** police and Immigration NZ investigated claims of false job offers to Indian migrants. Police cleared him, though the main complainant had left the country and was not interviewed.
- **Marriage-equality rally (2012):** told a rally that most National MPs had voted against the bill and linked this to Christianity. He later conceded that most had voted for it.
- **"Fridges" remark (2016):** compared international students to faulty fridges being sent back.
