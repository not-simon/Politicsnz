## Current status

Former MP. He retired at the 2014 election, and Maureen Pugh replaced him as National's West Coast-Tasman candidate. According to Wikipedia he and his wife farm Highland cattle near Lake Brunner.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born on 7 March 1945 in Prestwick, Scotland. He attended Sandhurst.
- **New Zealand:** naturalised in 1980. He worked in exporting on the West Coast.
- **Other roles:** Uniting Church lay preacher and wedding celebrant.

## Political career

- **List MP (2005):** entered Parliament on the list after losing West Coast-Tasman.
- **West Coast-Tasman (2008):** won the seat from Labour's Damien O'Connor.
- **2011:** lost the seat back to O'Connor but was returned on the list.
- **Retirement:** announced in October 2013.

**Roles held:**

- MP for West Coast-Tasman (2008–2011)
- List MP (2005–2008, 2011–2014)

## Record & notable actions

- **Marriage equality:** a strong supporter of the 2013 bill; his second-reading speech was widely praised.
- **Celebrant:** officiated at Tau Henare's wedding in Parliament (2012).
