## Current status

Former MP and diplomat. He resigned from Parliament on 19 December 2015 to become Ambassador to the United States, serving from early 2016 to August 2018. Since 2019 he has run a trade consultancy and comments publicly on trade; in 2023, for example, he welcomed the NZ–EU FTA.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born 6 March 1950 in Perth, Scotland, to actor parents, and came to New Zealand in 1958. He was a child actor and attended Hutt Valley High School.
- **Education:** studied economic history at Victoria University of Wellington, where he was president of the socialist society.
- **Diplomatic career (30+ years):**
  - Negotiator on the Closer Economic Relations agreement with Australia.
  - Adviser to Prime Ministers Muldoon and Lange.
  - Chief negotiator in the GATT Uruguay Round.
  - Ambassador to Indonesia (1994–97).
  - Head of the Asia 2000 Foundation.
  - Ambassador to the WTO (2002–05), where he chaired its agriculture negotiations.
- **Personal:** married three times. He converted to Islam to marry his second wife.

## Political career

- **Entry:** in 2005 he stood list-only for National at #13, angering the Labour government, which removed its support for his WTO roles.
- **Ministerial roles:** Minister of Trade (2008–15), Conservation (2008–10) and Climate Change (2011–15).
- **WTO bid:** an unsuccessful candidate for WTO Director-General in 2013. ([3 News](http://www.3news.co.nz/Groser-misses-out-on-top-WTO-job/tabid/1607/articleID/295630/Default.aspx))

**Roles held:**

- Minister of Trade (2008–2015)
- Minister for Climate Change Issues (2011–2015)
- Minister of Conservation (2008–2010)
- Ambassador to the United States (2016–2018)
- Ambassador to the WTO (2002–2005)
- Ambassador to Indonesia (1994–1997)
- List MP (2005–2015)

## Record & notable actions

- **Trade agreements:** the ASEAN–Australia–NZ FTA came into force in 2010. He also negotiated the Trans-Pacific Partnership, which he called his proudest achievement.
- **Conscience votes:** voted for marriage equality (2013).

## Controversies

- **GCSB surveillance (2013):** an Inspector-General of Intelligence and Security inquiry in 2017 confirmed that the GCSB gathered intelligence on rival WTO candidates to support his bid, with his approval. It found the surveillance lawful but criticised the record-keeping. ([The Intercept](https://theintercept.com/2015/03/22/new-zealand-gcsb-spying-wto-director-general/))
- **TPP criticism (2015):** said opponents of the TPP were whipping reasonable people "into a frenzy".
