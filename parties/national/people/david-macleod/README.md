## Current status

Backbench MP for New Plymouth since 2023. He is #45 on National's 2026 list and is re-contesting the seat.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in Hāwera, of Ngāti Mutunga, Ngāi Tahu and Ngāti Porou descent, and raised on the family farm at Manaia.
- **Business:** trained as an electrician with Greaves Electrical, becoming a partner (1991) and managing director (1999). He expanded the firm into lines work and sold it in 2023.
- **Taranaki Regional Council:**
  - Councillor from 2000 and chair from 2007 to 2022.
  - Opposed a Māori constituency in 2011. In 2021 he supported one, after all eight Taranaki iwi backed it, while calling the system "flawed". ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/te-manu-korihi/439912/taranaki-regional-council-votes-to-establish-maori-ward))
- **Directorships:**
  - Port Taranaki (2001–23).
  - Fonterra (2011–17), as its first Māori director.
  - Predator Free 2050 board.
- **Family and personal:** his godfather was National MP Venn Young. He is married to Leasa and has three children.

## Political career

- **2023 selection:** chosen in October 2022; placed at list #67 in the expectation he would win his seat.
- **2023 election:** beat Labour's Glen Bennett by 6,991 votes. He was then made chair of the Environment Committee.

**Roles held:**

- MP for New Plymouth (2023–)
- Chair, Environment Committee (Dec 2023–May 2024)
- Chair, Taranaki Regional Council (2007–2022)
- Fonterra director (2011–2017)
- Port Taranaki director (2001–2023)
- Predator Free 2050 board (2016–2022; acting chair 2021)

## Record & notable actions

- Campaigned on Taranaki roads, workforce immigration and the oil and gas sector.
- He was not in Parliament for the 2013–2022 conscience votes.

## Controversies

- **Undeclared donations (2024):**
  - Stood down from his committee roles on 21 May 2024 after failing to declare 19 donations totalling NZ$178,394 in his candidate return. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/517356/politics-live-national-mp-david-macleod-stood-down-after-failing-to-declare-donations))
  - The Electoral Commission referred the matter to Police, and he called it a genuine mistake. ([The Press](https://www.thepress.co.nz/politics/350303895/national-mp-david-macleod-referred-police-over-donations))
  - Police took no further action (29 August 2024). ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/526511/police-drop-investigation-into-national-mp))
- **Council advertising (2019):** a rival complained to the Auditor-General that council advertising featuring him gave him an unfair advantage. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/national/401179/taranaki-regional-council-chair-david-macleod-under-fire-for-unfair-ads))
- **Campaign signs (2023):** breached signage rules during the campaign.
- **Fonterra election (2011):** criticised for taking a farmer-elected board seat when he was not a farmer.
