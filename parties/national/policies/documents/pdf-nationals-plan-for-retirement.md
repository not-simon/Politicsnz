Fixing the Basics. Building the Future.

National’s plan for
Retirement

Fixing the Basics. Building the Future.

National’s plan for Retirement
Seniors have spent a lifetime contributing to
New Zealand, and they deserve the security,
respect, and opportunities to enjoy the retirement
they have earned.
From Monday 26 October, Kiwi seniors will be able
to vote on New Zealand’s future.
National is committed to recognising all that our
older generations have given to this country and
supporting them in retirement and into their old age. As
a society, we must ensure you feel included and valued
to live with dignity, respect and the security you deserve.
This election, there is a clear choice between sticking
with National’s plan for a more secure future, no matter
the stage of your life, with a growing economy, less
debt, more jobs and higher wages.
Or going back to Labour – propped up by the Greens,
Te Pāti Māori and TOP – who will undo the progress
New Zealand has made over the last three years,
leaving you to pay for it through at least nine new
taxes costing households $2,263 each year.
Many retirees have spent a lifetime paying off
their homes or have bought a rental property to top
up their Super to support them in retirement. Now,
these investments are at risk with TOP’s proposed
Land Tax – and Labour’s own Capital Gains Tax.

Authorised by J de Joux, ps@national.org.nz

The Land Tax would mean that if you have $500,000
worth of land in an urban centre, you will owe $8,750
a year in extra tax, on top of your rates bill.
And Labour’s Capital Gains Tax means an extra 28 per
cent could be taken off investments that have built your
retirement security.
There is no new money being proposed for seniors
to help offset these costs, leaving you thousands of
dollars worse off.
It’s unfair, and National won’t let it happen.
Don’t put it all at risk. By sticking to National’s plan,
we will deliver a better future for retirees. A future
where your finances are protected. A future where
we invest responsibly in health, so you have peace
of mind knowing you’ll get the care you need when
you need it.
A Labour-led coalition will undo the progress
we’ve made, putting our economy and your
savings at risk with reckless spending, more
borrowing and higher taxes; leaving no money
to invest in the frontline services you need.

National’s track record…
Increased superannuation to give couples an extra
$180 every fortnight in their back pockets.
Helped more seniors with their rates, ensuring
that every SuperGold Card holder earning only
NZ Superannuation, with rates higher than $2000,
can access the full rebate scheme.
Protected the Winter Energy Payment to help
superannuitants with their power bills.
Reduced inflation from record highs under Labour,
by getting government spending under control,
taking pressure off the cost of living.
Froze petrol tax for three years and scrapped the
Auckland Regional Fuel Tax, helping you get to where
you need to go, cheaper.
Made it easier for Kiwis to build a simple granny flat
by removing the need for both building and resource
consents in most cases for units up to 70 square metres,
supporting inter-generational living arrangements.
Progressed an upgrade to the SuperGold Card to
enable seniors to use it as a free, official form of photo
identification, making it easier to access banking, legal
and other essential services.
Invested a record amount in our Health system –
$34.2 billion this year. We are also providing
more elective surgeries like cataracts, hip and
knee replacements to shorten waiting times.
Targeted free prescriptions for seniors and extended
prescriptions for long-term, stable conditions to up to
12 months, saving you time and money.

Funded 66 new medicines, including 33 extra cancer
medicines, thanks to our $604 million funding boost,
with more medicines to come.
Hired over 2,100 new nurses and 900 more doctors,
and introduced a wave of hiring incentives to recruit
more GPs and nurse practitioners.
Provided a 24/7 digital health service so you can
contact a doctor or nurse using your phone, laptop,
or tablet at any time, day or night.
Increased numbers of urgent and after-hours clinics
across the country, including new 24/7 services.
Reduced violent crime and ram raids so you can feel
safer on the streets and in your own home.
Cracked down on gangs, banned gang patches in
public, and gave Police more powers and tools to
tackle criminals.
Introduced tougher sentences and brought back
consequences for crime such as restoring Three Strikes
for repeat offenders.
Ensured your grandchildren get the education
they deserve, requiring all kids to do an hour each
of reading, writing and maths every day at school,
as well as banning cell phones in schools.
Funded more accessible housing, with over
22 per cent of social and affordable houses funded
by this government being accessible, which
benefits seniors.

Our 2026 election policies for seniors
A re-elected National government will:
• Boost seniors housing by ring-fencing at least
20 per cent of all Housing Flexible Fund places
going forward for seniors’ housing – whether that
be affordable rentals (like Abbeyfield), social homes,
or other innovative solutions.
• Expand the granny flat rules so more can be built,
faster and cheaper, by allowing an attached garage
of up to 40sqm in addition to the 70sqm granny flat,
speeding up processing times by requiring PIMs
to be processed within five working days for all
standalone dwellings and limit fees to cost recovery,
removing the requirement for a primary dwelling,
exploring a new licensing class for builders to ensure
quality construction, and allowing tiny homes on
wheels to also access these rules.
• Strengthen protections against elder abuse and
financial exploitation by reviewing the Enduring
Powers of Attorney framework and considering
further measures including law changes to better
protect seniors from economic harm.
• Establish a 0800-SENIORS number, to give seniors
a single free helpline covering any issue to help
them navigate government services and receive
the Government support they are entitled to. The
helpline will sit in MSD and be managed within
existing baselines.

• Improve access to healthcare for seniors by
expanding access to medicines and cancer
screening, including extending free cervical
screening to age 69 and improving access to
breast cancer screening, helping older
New Zealanders stay healthier for longer.
• Increase your KiwiSaver by requiring employers
to continue KiwiSaver contributions for employees
over 65 from 1 July 2027. This change means a
65-year-old earning $70,000 per year in 2027, who
chooses to work three more years and retires at
age 68 will retire with an additional $6300 in 2030.
• Strengthen grocery competition, helping with the
price of food at the checkout by cracking down on
the supermarket duopoly.
• Low interest energy loans so you can install solar,
insulation, heat pumps and home batteries, saving
you money now on your electricity bills.
• Keep council rates affordable by introducing a Rates
Cap and refocus councils on core services.
• No new taxes, protecting your assets from Capital
Gains Tax and a Land Tax, which are two of the nine
new taxes Labour and the left coalition are proposing.
• Protect the country’s finances by returning to
surplus in 2028/29 and government debt below
40 per cent of GDP over time, supporting the funding
of public services.

Fixing the Basics. Building the Future. Party Vote National.
