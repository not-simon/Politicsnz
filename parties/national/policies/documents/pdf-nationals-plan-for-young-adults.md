Fixing the Basics. Building the Future.

National’s plan for
Young Adults

Fixing the Basics. Building the Future.

National’s plan for Young Adults
From Monday 26 October, Kiwis will be able to vote
on New Zealand’s future.
National has a plan to help you secure your future,
whether you’re a Kiwi kid, a young person starting
out, a parent raising your family, or in your retirement.
This election, there is a clear choice between sticking
with National’s plan for a more secure future, no matter
the stage of your life, with a growing economy, less
debt, more jobs and higher wages.
Or going back to Labour – propped up by the Greens,
Te Pati Māori and TOP – who will undo the progress
New Zealand has made over the past three years,
leaving you to pay for it through at least nine new
taxes costing households $2,263 each year.

Authorised by J de Joux, ps@national.org.nz

Under our plan for young adults, we’ll deliver a future
where you have the best opportunity to get ahead
in life without having to move abroad. A future with
a stronger economy, meaning more jobs and higher
wages. A future where it is easier to pay off a student
loan and buy your first home. A future where you get
the services you rely on – all with no new taxes.
Labour will undo the progress we’ve made in
the economy and spend recklessly, meaning
more taxes and more borrowing, leaving young
Kiwis to pay off our debt. That will raise inflation,
which in turn leads to higher unemployment and
higher interest rates on your mortgage.

What we’ve delivered to date
Tax relief: National introduced personal income tax
cuts in July 2024, which increased the take home pay
of hard-working Kiwis.
Support for young jobseekers: National introduced
community job coaches for up to 10,000 young
people looking for work. This helps our young people
with career planning, barriers to work and pathways
to employment.
Driver licence assistance: National is helping jobseekers
obtain licences, which is an important employment
requirement in many industries. More than 21,000
young people have already benefitted.

The results so far
National believes a strong economy is the foundation
for a successful life for young people.
We have made a lot of progress. New Zealand is
growing faster than the US, the UK, Canada, and
Australia. Exports are up $20 billion, inflation is down
from a 32-year high, and 220,000 new jobs are
expected with wages growing faster than prices.
More Kiwis are returning home, with a 14 per cent
increase in Kiwis moving back to New Zealand from
Australia last year.
We have made it easier to buy a first home with 5 per cent
deposits and first home buyers were at a record 26 per
cent of the property market in 2026 – that is 24,800
young Kiwis into their first homes in the past year. And
for tenants, rent increases are at a 25-year low, whereas
under Labour, rents went up $180 per week.

Our 2026 election policies for
Young Adults
• Lower student loan repayments: National will
reduce the student loan repayment rate for Kiwis
living in the country, leaving graduates with more
take-home pay. This gives young people more
income for rent, savings, a first home deposit or
starting a business.
• Wider access to 5 per cent home loan deposits:
National will expand access to the First Home Loan
Scheme so more eligible buyers can purchase with
a 5 per cent deposit, rather than the conventional
20 per cent.
• Paid Parental Leave extension: National is extending
paid parental leave from 26-30 weeks to give
parents more time with their newborn.
• Stronger KiwiSaver and $1500 Baby Boost:
KiwiSaver will become compulsory, contribution
settings will be higher, and a $1500 Baby Boost
contribution will be made for each eligible newborn.
• A welfare system focused on work: Provide
more intensive employment support for long-term
jobseekers, alongside stronger expectations.
• No new taxes: Give workers, households and
businesses greater certainty.

Fixing the Basics. Building the Future.
Party Vote National.
