## National’s plan for Kiwi kids

Under our plan for Kiwi kids every child will get the best possible start in life.

National has a plan to help you secure your future, whether you’re a young person starting out, a parent raising your family, or in your retirement.

Under our plan for Kiwi kids, every child will get the best possible start in life. By sticking to National’s plan, we will deliver a future where children get the education they need to set them up for success; and an economy that has been responsibly managed so they are not burdened with our debt.

Building on the progress made over the past three years, National’s Plan for Kiwi Kids will continue to focus on ensuring every child in New Zealand gets the best possible start in life, with practical policies that support families, strengthen education, and help young people reach their potential and build a prosperous future.
