## Full text (reading order)

Delivering on our 2023 Hunting and Fishing
commitments
National supports the right of New Zealanders to hunt and ﬁsh. Tens of thousands
of Kiwis grow up ﬁshing in our lakes and rivers or in the sea. Many learn to hunt
with their friends and family.
Hunters help to control animal numbers, protect
biodiversity by eradicating pests, and provide food for
their families. Hunting and ﬁshing also foster tourism
and are pillars of New Zealand’s culture and heritage.

National believes conservation and recreation can
work in harmony, and our plan supports the rights of
New Zealanders to continue to hunt and ﬁsh while
protecting and enhancing our natural environment.

1.

Establish a Minister for Hunting
and Fishing

Complete.

2.

Strengthen the Game Animal
Council and designate ‘herds
of special interest’

Complete. The Game Animal Council (Herds of Special Interest)
Amendment Act 2026 clariﬁed the ability to designate a herd of special
interest in national parks will be passed by July. Two herds of special
interest (wapiti and sika) were designated in July, with the Game
Animal Council responsible for implementing the Sika Herd of Special
Interest, the ﬁrst time the GAC has been designated this responsibility.

3.

Change the law so game
animals are not pests

Complete as not strictly required. Game animals are not classiﬁed as
pests under the law, however a bill to provide express recognition of
game animals as valued introduced species to be managed in line
with their recreational value has been drafted as a member’s bill.

4.

Guarantee access to public
land for hunting and ﬁshing

Complete. The Access Charter for Recreational Hunting and Fishing
on Public Conservation Land and Waters was announced on
2 November 2024.

5.

Establish the ‘Huts of
Recreational Importance’
Partnership to maintain huts in
the DOC estate

Complete. Through the Community Hut Programme, the Backcountry
Trust received $4.2m in partnership with DOC to maintain and enhance
30 recreational hut projects in January 2025.

6.

Support Fish & Game New
Zealand and protect trout and
salmon ﬁshing

Partially complete. Cabinet agreed on policy decisions to modernise
and strengthen Fish and Game, and enacting legislation awaits ﬁrst
reading before the election.

7.

Not introduce recreational
licences for game animal
hunting or sea ﬁshing

Complete.

Authorised by J de Joux, ps@national.org.nz
