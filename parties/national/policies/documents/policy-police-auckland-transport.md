## More Police on Auckland’s buses and trains

So you feel safe catching public transport

Auckland’s public transport network has just got a lot bigger. The City Rail Link adds three underground stations and lifts peak capacity through the city centre by around half. That is a big win for Auckland, and it means more passengers, later services, and enclosed platforms below ground where help needs to be close at hand.

Most journeys on Auckland’s public transport network pass without incident, but too many Aucklanders now think twice before taking the train after dark, or before letting their teenager catch the bus home from a late shift. Every Aucklander should be able to catch a bus, train or ferry and feel safe doing it.

As Auckland’s public transport network grows, National will ensure that safety measures grow with it by establishing a dedicated Auckland Transport Police Team to improve safety across Auckland’s public transport network.

## What we'll do

1

### Establish a dedicated Auckland Transport Police Team to improve safety across Auckland’s public transport network.

2

### Fund New Zealand Police to provide a visible presence on buses, trains, ferries, stations and interchanges.

3

### Focus on preventing anti-social behaviour, protecting passengers and transport staff, responding quickly to incidents, and bolstering confidence in safety on public transport.

4

### Deter crime before it happens and make sure that when a serious incident does occur, it gets an immediate police response.

5

### Recruit an additional officers to fulfil this function.

6

### Establish the team in Auckland in the first instance, with expansion to other centres considered if it succeeds.
