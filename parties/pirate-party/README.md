# Pirate Party of New Zealand

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status (Oct 2026)** | **Inactive; never registered** |
| **Founded** | c. 2009–2010 (modelled on Sweden's Pirate Party; member of Pirate Parties International) |
| **Ideology** | Copyright and patent reform, internet privacy |
| **Seats** | None |

## History

As an unregistered party it could not contest the party vote. In 2011 it stood in the Botany by-election (32 votes)
and in Hamilton East (137 votes) and Wellington Central (277 votes) at the general election, after being conditionally
awarded $20,000 of broadcasting funding and having its logo registered. It stood no candidates in 2014 and failed to
gain registration for 2017. [1]
