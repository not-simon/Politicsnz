## Current status

**#1** on the Free Palestine party list for 2026. Never an MP.

## Biography

A veteran left-wing activist and retired teacher who lives in Christchurch. He became national chair of Halt All Racist Tours (HART) in 1980 and was a leading figure in the 1981 Springbok Tour protests, during which the SIS listed him as a "subversive". He has been active in the PPTA, the Quality Public Education Coalition and Unite Union, and wrote a weekly column for *The Press*.

## Political career

- Mana Movement co-vice-president. Ran for Auckland mayor on a Mana ticket in 2013 (fifth of 17) and was #4 for Internet Mana in 2014.
- Ran for Christchurch mayor in 2016 for the Keep Our Assets group; not elected.

## Record & notable actions

- As chair of Palestine Solidarity Network Aotearoa, he backs the Boycott, Divestment and Sanctions (BDS) movement. He opposed New Zealand joining the IHRA as an observer (2022), and in 2024 helped persuade Christchurch City Council to exclude companies involved in Israeli settlements from its procurement.
- In 2025 the IPCA found police had unlawfully pepper-sprayed and arrested him at a 2024 Lyttelton protest. Police paid him $10,000 compensation in June 2026.

## Controversies

- In January 2025 he set up a "genocide hotline" for reporting Israeli soldiers holidaying in New Zealand. The Jewish Council, the Chief Human Rights Commissioner and the Government condemned it, and the Human Rights Commission received about 100 complaints. Minto denied that it was antisemitic.
- Arrested at a 2010 tennis protest against Israeli player Shahar Pe'er; his disorderly-behaviour conviction was overturned on appeal.
