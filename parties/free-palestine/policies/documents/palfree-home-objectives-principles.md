A political party committed to justice, international law, and a democratic national state with equal rights for all citizens.

What We Are Doing

## Our Objectives

The Palestine Free from the River to the Sea Party is committed to building a mass democratic movement for justice in Aotearoa New Zealand and internationally.

We aim to change public opinion and put sustained parliamentary pressure on the New Zealand government to uphold its obligations under international law with respect to Palestine.

We centre the Nakba — the 1948 ethnic cleansing of Palestine — as the historical root of the ongoing crisis, directly challenging Western narratives that erase or minimise Palestinian dispossession.

- **Mass Democratic Movement**

 Build a broad, democratic party that unites supporters of Palestine from all traditions, allowing different ideas and perspectives to be discussed in an environment of tolerance and respect.
- **International Accountability**

 Pressure the New Zealand government to support the prosecution of war crimes and genocide, uphold UN resolutions, and exercise its independent voice in international forums.
- **Global Solidarity**

 Build links in solidarity with all those resisting inequality and injustice around the world, recognising that Palestinian liberation is bound up with the broader struggle against imperialism.

Who We Are

## Our Founding Principles

01

A just peace in Palestine depends upon the return of Palestinian refugees to their homeland and the dismantling of the Zionist structure of the state of Israel, recognizing that the further partitioning of Palestine in order to create the so-called Two-State Solution would only lead to further injustice and suffering.

02

Acceptance of the primacy of international law and United Nations resolutions as the basis for the ending of military occupation and all forms of ethnic discrimination in Israel.

03

The international community's responsibility for upholding the Universal Declaration of Human Rights and the urgent need for the state of Israel to be called to account for its gross abuses of Palestinian human rights.

04

Justice requires the establishment of a single state in Palestine, bi-national, secular and democratic, with full and equal citizenship for all with ethnic and religious rights protected in a democratic constitution.

05

Unconditional and complete solidarity for the Palestinian resistance in all forms accordant with International Humanitarian Law.

06

That New Zealand commit to an independent foreign policy by withdrawing itself from any formal security alliances with the U.S. and by distancing our foreign policy from the orbit of Anglo-U.S. economic hegemony and military interventionism.
