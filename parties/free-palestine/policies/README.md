## About this list

Free Palestine is a single-issue party registered in August 2026 (its website calls it the 'Palestine Free from the River to the Sea Party'). Its platform consists of three objectives and six founding principles on its one-page website, the same principles in its June 2026 constitution lodged with the Electoral Commission, and a six-point list in its August 2026 registration press release. Each principle/objective is listed below.

policy.nz publishes no policy statements for this party, so every summary here is marked *archive*: written for this archive from the linked full text in one to three neutral sentences ("Why" = the party's own stated reason, attributed; "How" = what it says it would do). **Gaps and caveats.** The party has no published policies on domestic issues. The website's membership, enrolment and donation sections are omitted from the captured text.

## Defence & foreign affairs

### Support the right of return of Palestinian refugees and the dismantling of Israel's current state structure, rejecting a two-state solution (FPP-01)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says a just peace depends on refugees' return and that further partition would bring more injustice.

**How** (*archive*): Founding principle 1 (website) / constitution clause 2.

Full text: [Palestine Free from the River to the Sea Party — Objectives and Founding Principles](documents/palfree-home-objectives-principles.md) ([original](https://palfree.nz/)); [Free Palestine Party Constitution (June 2026, as lodged with the Electoral Commission)](documents/constitution-june-2026.md) ([original](https://elections.nz/assets/Party-rules/FPP-Constitution-June-2026.pdf)); [The Free Palestine Party: The Newest Political Party In Aotearoa](documents/release-2026-08-newest-political-party.md) ([original](https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PO2608/S00073/the-free-palestine-party-the-newest-political-party-in-aotearoa.htm))

### Establish a single secular, democratic, bi-national state in all of historic Palestine with equal citizenship (FPP-02)

**How** (*archive*): Founding principle 4: one state with full and equal citizenship and ethnic and religious rights protected in a democratic constitution.

Full text: [Palestine Free from the River to the Sea Party — Objectives and Founding Principles](documents/palfree-home-objectives-principles.md) ([original](https://palfree.nz/)); [Free Palestine Party Constitution (June 2026, as lodged with the Electoral Commission)](documents/constitution-june-2026.md) ([original](https://elections.nz/assets/Party-rules/FPP-Constitution-June-2026.pdf)); [The Free Palestine Party: The Newest Political Party In Aotearoa](documents/release-2026-08-newest-political-party.md) ([original](https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PO2608/S00073/the-free-palestine-party-the-newest-political-party-in-aotearoa.htm))

### Press the New Zealand government to uphold international law and UN resolutions on Palestine and support war-crimes and genocide prosecutions (FPP-03)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says its aim is to change public opinion and put sustained parliamentary pressure on the government.

**How** (*archive*): Accept the primacy of international law and UN resolutions; call Israel to account under the Universal Declaration of Human Rights; have New Zealand support prosecutions and use its independent voice internationally.

Full text: [Palestine Free from the River to the Sea Party — Objectives and Founding Principles](documents/palfree-home-objectives-principles.md) ([original](https://palfree.nz/)); [Free Palestine Party Constitution (June 2026, as lodged with the Electoral Commission)](documents/constitution-june-2026.md) ([original](https://elections.nz/assets/Party-rules/FPP-Constitution-June-2026.pdf)); [The Free Palestine Party: The Newest Political Party In Aotearoa](documents/release-2026-08-newest-political-party.md) ([original](https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PO2608/S00073/the-free-palestine-party-the-newest-political-party-in-aotearoa.htm))

### Adopt an independent foreign policy and withdraw from formal military and security alliances with the United States (FPP-05)

**How** (*archive*): Founding principle 6: leave any formal US security alliances and distance foreign policy from 'Anglo-U.S. economic hegemony and military interventionism'.
