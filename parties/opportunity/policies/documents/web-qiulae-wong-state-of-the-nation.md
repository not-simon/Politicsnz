## Qiulae Wong: State of the Nation

### *It’s time to stand up, stop pretending and start building.*

On Saturday 21 February, Opportunity leader Qiulae Wong will talk directly to Kiwis - openly and honestly - about the challenges we face.

Join the livestream to hear her vision for an Aotearoa New Zealand full of opportunity, where no-one gets left behind.

While the road to a united, fairer and more prosperous New Zealand will be long and difficult, it starts here.
