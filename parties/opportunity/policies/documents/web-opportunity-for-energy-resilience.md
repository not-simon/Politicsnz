In the warm hospitality of the [**Invercargill Workingmen's Club**](https://iwmc.co.nz/), we are hosting a discussion and Q&A about energy resilience in the deep south of Aotearoa, New Zealand.\
\
Energy-specialist **[Nathan Surendran](https://www.schema.nz/)**(Schema Consulting) will join Opportunity Party's Southland candidate, [**Bianca Beebe**](https://www.opportunity.org.nz/candidate-bianca-beebe), to host a conversation about these important issues in the lead up to the 2026 General Election.

Join us with your questions and comments.

Tea, coffee and biscuits will be available (for your energy) alongside the Workingmen's Club bar and restaurant.

Opportunity Party's Abundant Energy Policy can be found **[here](https://www.opportunity.org.nz/abundant-energy)**.

Schema Consulting's website can be found **[here](https://www.schema.nz/)**.

The more people who turn up, the better the discussion.
