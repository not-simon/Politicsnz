## Time for an honest conversation about transport funding

Opportunity says New Zealanders struggling with high fuel prices deserve cost-of-living relief and honesty about how the country will fund the transport system it needs.

\
“People are hurting. Fuel is expensive, groceries are expensive, and household budgets are stretched,” says Opportunity Leader Qiulae Wong.

\
“We’ve proposed a Citizens Income funded by a Land Value Tax that would help 70% of Kiwis deal with the increasing cost-of-living. That’s our idea and we’re ready to work with other parties who have ideas to achieve the same goal.

\
“We need to be honest that freezing fuel tax doesn’t make the cost of transport infrastructure disappear.

\
“We have to stop kicking the can down the unfunded road. There are always trade-offs. Who pays and how? What won’t get built if we don’t raise the money?

\
“We also need to prepare for a low-carbon economy. As fossil fuel use falls, fuel tax will become a less reliable way to fund our transport needs.

\
“National and Labour are telling voters what they won’t do. Now they need to explain what they will do, what it will cost, and who will pay.”

\
“New Zealanders deserve that honest conversation, and that’s what they’ll get with Opportunity in Parliament,” says Wong.
