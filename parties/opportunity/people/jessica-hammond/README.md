## Current status

Opportunity (TOP) candidate for **Wellington North** and **#3** on the list in 2026. Never an MP.

## Biography

A Wellington public servant, playwright and blogger.

## Political career

- TOP candidate for Ōhāriu in 2017 (third, 7.3%), 2020 (third, 10.2%) and 2023 (fourth); third in the 2018 TOP leadership vote.
- Campaigned on housing (rental warrant of fitness, medium-density housing) and a TOP policy to share GST revenue with councils for infrastructure.
