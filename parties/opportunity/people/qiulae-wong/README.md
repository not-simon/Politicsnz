## Current status

Leader of the Opportunity Party ("Opportunity"/TOP) since November 2025, **#1** on its list and candidate for **Mount Albert** in 2026. Never an MP. In the 13 October 2026 TVNZ multi-party debate she ruled out serving in a government with Winston Peters as Foreign Minister, calling his remarks to Lawrence Xu-Nan "clearly racist"; RNZ described her as a possible "kingmaker".

## Biography

Born in Fiji in May 1988, of Chinese and European descent, and raised in One Tree Hill, Auckland; her mother Margaret Malcolm was a senior adviser to Judith Collins and chief executive of the Law Association. Law and politics graduate (Auckland); worked with the Ethical Fashion Group/Common Objective and as a sustainable business consultant at KPMG.

## Political career

- Chosen by the party board in 2025 as its fifth leader despite low name recognition; the party shortened its name to "Opportunity".

## Record & notable actions

- Platform: a land value tax; a means-tested "citizen's income" replacing most benefits and superannuation (not paid to those earning over $350,000); a flat income tax; citizens' assemblies ("Citizen's Voice"); lobbying and donations reform; low-interest loans for household electrification.
