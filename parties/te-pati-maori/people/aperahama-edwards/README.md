## Current status

Te Pāti Māori candidate for **Te Tai Tokerau** in 2026, contesting the seat against sitting MP Mariameno Kapa-Kingi (now Te Tai Tokerau Party). Never an MP.

## Biography

Chairman of the Ngātiwai Trust Board since 31 July 2020 (trustee for four years before, and chair of its Treaty claims committee). Part of the team behind Hinemoana Halo, a carbon-offset ocean-conservation scheme that secured investment at COP28.

## Controversies

- Removed from Parliament's public gallery in 2025 for interjecting during the foreshore and seabed (MACA) amendment debate; at Waitangi in 2025 he twice took the microphone from David Seymour, saying Seymour was "fanning the flames of hurt and pain".
