## Current status

Former MP (2014–2017) and former Māori Party co-leader. Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

Born in Cannons Creek, Porirua, the youngest of five; her father was a Pākehā teacher and her mother founded a pre-school. Educated in Christchurch (Christchurch Girls' High School). A teacher with 26 years in education before Parliament. Lives in Masterton; nine children. Describes herself as "a badge wearing Mormon". Cousin of NZ First's Ron Mark. Appeared on *Dancing with the Stars* (2018).

## Political career

- Contested Ikaroa-Rāwhiti in 2014 and became the Māori Party's first-ever list MP.
- Co-leader with Te Ururoa Flavell from November 2014, succeeding Tariana Turia, while the party supported the National-led government.
- Lost her seat in 2017 when Labour won all seven Māori electorates; said New Zealand had voted for a return to "the age of colonisation".
- Resigned as co-leader in September 2018.

## Record & notable actions

- Co-led the Māori Party in its final term of support for the National government (2014–2017).

## Controversies

- Her 2018 resignation followed controversy over her business activities; in 2019 liquidators of her failed consulting company hired agents to locate her.
- Admitted a drink-driving charge in Hamilton (2018) and was sentenced and fined in 2019.
- Expressed support for the 2022 anti-mandate protest at Parliament.
