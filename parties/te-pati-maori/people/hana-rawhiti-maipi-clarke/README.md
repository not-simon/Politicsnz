## Current status

Te Pāti Māori MP for **Hauraki-Waikato** since 2023, standing again in Hauraki-Waikato in 2026. In November 2025 she likened the party's internal conflict to "a divorce between two parents" and urged reconciliation.

## Biography

Hana-Rawhiti Kareariki Maipi-Clarke (born 2002; Waikato, Ngāpuhi, Ngāti Porou, Te Āti Awa, Ngāi Tahu) is the daughter of broadcaster Potaka Maipi, granddaughter of activist Taitimu Maipi and grand-niece of te reo campaigner Hana Te Hemara. Educated at Te Wharekura o Rākaumangamanga, Huntly; at 17 she published *Maahina*, a book on maramataka (the Māori lunar calendar). Named to the BBC 100 Women (2024) and Time100 Next (2025); One Young World Politician of the Year (2024).

## Political career

- Chosen as TPM's Hauraki-Waikato candidate in 2023 (list #4) after a 2022 speech on Parliament's steps marking 50 years of the Māori language petition.
- Defeated Labour's Nanaia Mahuta by 2,911 votes in 2023, aged 21 — the youngest MP in 170 years.

## Record & notable actions

- Her maiden speech (December 2023), in which she said the government had "attacked my whole world from every corner", went viral internationally.
- Supports lowering the voting age to 16.

## Controversies

- **Treaty Principles Bill haka (14 November 2024):** tore up the bill and led a haka during its first reading; the Speaker suspended the House and "named" her (24-hour suspension). Footage was viewed hundreds of millions of times. After TPM boycotted the Privileges Committee, Parliament suspended her for seven days on 5 June 2025; she called it "grossly unjust".
- During the 2023 campaign TPM reported five incidents to police (stolen hoarding, alleged break-in) as politically motivated; police found no criminality or racial motive, and she later clarified her "ram raid" description as meaning her privacy was violated.
