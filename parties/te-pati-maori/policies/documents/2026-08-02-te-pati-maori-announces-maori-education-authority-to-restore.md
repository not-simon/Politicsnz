# Te Pāti Māori Announces Māori Education Authority To Restore Māori Success In Education

*Sunday, 2 August 2026, 2:23 pm Press Release: Te Pati Maori* — [https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PA2608/S00006/te-pati-maori-announces-maori-education-authority-to-restore-maori-success-in-education.htm](https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PA2608/S00006/te-pati-maori-announces-maori-education-authority-to-restore-maori-success-in-education.htm)

## Full text

Te Pāti Māori today unveiled its commitment to establish a Māori Education Authority, with Te Tai Tonga candidate Lisa Marie Murch announcing the policy during her debut election debate on Marae.

The policy commits to restoring Māori authority over Māori education by strengthening Māori governance across the education system, investing in kōhanga reo, kura kaupapa Māori, wharekura and wānanga, embedding mātauranga Māori throughout the curriculum, and ensuring every tamaiti has the opportunity to succeed as who they are.

Lisa Marie Murch said the policy is about giving every Māori child the opportunity to thrive.

"Enhancing educational success is my bottom line because every mokopuna deserves the opportunity to succeed as who they are."

"Te Pāti Māori will establish a Māori Education Authority. We will strengthen education for tamariki across all education sectors, support our teachers, and ensure our tamariki grow up proud in their identity, language and culture. Education isn't just about the classroom, it's about the future of our people and ensuring success for generations to come."

Te Pāti Māori Co-Leader Rawiri Waititi said the current education system continues to reflect the legacy of assimilation rather than Māori aspiration.

"For generations our tamariki have been expected to succeed in a system that was never designed for them. A Māori Education Authority puts decision making back where it belongs, with Māori. When our tamariki are grounded in their identity, language and culture, they do not just participate in education, they excel."

Te Pāti Māori Co-Leader Debbie Ngarewa-Packer said Māori leadership in education is essential to achieving equitable outcomes.

"Education is one of the greatest expressions of tino rangatiratanga. Our tamariki deserve an education system that celebrates who they are, values mātauranga Māori, supports our kaiako and walks alongside whānau. This policy invests in generations of Māori success, not just the next school term."

Te Pāti Māori's education policy is founded on the principle that Māori learners thrive when Māori lead, whānau are central to educational success, and education must uphold identity while reflecting Aotearoa's diverse knowledge systems.

The Māori Education Authority forms the cornerstone of Te Pāti Māori's wider education policy, which also includes expanding school lunches, free public transport for all students, free school counselling, making te reo Māori a core curriculum subject through Year 10, increasing student support, and strengthening the education workforce.
