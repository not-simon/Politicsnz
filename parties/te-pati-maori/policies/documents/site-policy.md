### Policy

Te Pāti Māori will look after you the way a Marae looks after all people. Everyone will have a home, be cared for, protected, connected, empowered, and sovereign. Together, we will be the kaitiaki our whenua needs us to be.

That means:

- Fairness in wealth and resources

- Safe, secure housing

- Māori-led health, education, disability and justice

- Protection of whenua, wai and climate

- Te Tiriti-based constitutional transformation
