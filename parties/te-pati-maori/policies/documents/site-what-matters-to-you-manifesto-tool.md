# “What matters to you?” policy tool (content from the Te Pāti Māori Policy Manifesto 2026)

Source: <https://www.maoriparty.org.nz/what-matters-to-you> — Common Crawl capture 2026-10-08T18:53:45Z

## Full text

## Part 1 — policies by area (“Pick your top three”)


### Money in my pocket (Pūtea)

*No one should be paid less than what it costs to live.*

- **No tax on the first $30,000 you earn** — Less tax for 97% of people. Nine in ten people get around $4,000 more a year on average.
- **The minimum wage goes straight up to the living wage** — That's $28.95 an hour. Youth rates and starting out rates end.
- **GST off kai, paid back to you as a tax credit** — For anyone earning $60,000 or less. Worth up to eight weeks of kai every year. Around 3 million people get it.
- **Double baseline benefits** — Remove sanctions and work test obligations, and cancel benefit debt owed to the Crown.
- **Free public transport** — Free right away for tamariki, students and Community Services Card holders. Half price for everyone else, then free for everyone within five years.
- **Lower power bills** — Help for whānau with solar, batteries, better heating, and low cost or zero interest finance.
- **Break the supermarket duopoly** — $100 million to help Māori collectives set up their own supermarket chains.
- **Company tax back to 33%** — And a 5% tax on profits sent offshore.
- **Get paid fairly for power you make** — If you generate or store power and send it back to the grid, you get a fair price for it.
- **Protect workers** — Pay equity for Māori, and stronger union rights and collective bargaining.

### A warm and safe home (Kāinga)

*Everyone has the right to a warm, dry and secure home.*

- **Freeze rents** — After the freeze, rents can only go up by inflation each year, unless the owner makes big improvements.
- **End no cause evictions in our first 100 days** — More security for whānau who rent.
- **Build 2,000 homes on ancestral Māori land within four years** — Built by Māori workers, apprentices and trainees, supporting papakāinga.
- **Build more state houses and stop selling them off cheap to investors**
- **Half of all new social housing goes to whānau Māori** — Māori make up half the social housing waiting list.
- **End youth homelessness** — A new agency, Rangatahi Ora, and a legal duty on government to help any young person without a home.
- **Tax homes left empty** — A 2% yearly tax on homes left empty for more than three months.
- **Every new government build made accessible for all whānau**

### Our tamariki and mokopuna (Mokopuna)

*Mokopuna Māori raised with love, safety, identity and belonging.*

- **End state care of mokopuna Māori** — Replace it with care led by Māori and grounded in whakapapa. End uplifting pēpi and mokopuna without whānau, hapū and iwi consent.
- **A Mokopuna Māori Authority** — Takes over the care and protection of mokopuna Māori from Oranga Tamariki, with about $1 billion a year moved across to it.
- **Free school lunches in every kura and public school**
- **Free counselling in schools** — And mental wellbeing becomes a core part of what's taught.
- **Carry out all 138 recommendations from the Royal Commission into Abuse in Care**
- **Close military style bootcamps straight away** — Youth justice residences closed by 2030.
- **Free public transport for tamariki**
- **Every law and policy decision must uphold the rights of mokopuna** — Their right to their language and culture, good health, education, and a voice in decisions about them.

### Learning and training (Ako)

*Māori learners thrive when Māori lead.*

- **A Māori Education Authority** — Long term, stable funding for kōhanga reo, kura kaupapa Māori, wharekura and wānanga.
- **A Te Kōhanga Reo Act, with guaranteed funding every year** — Plus $20 million a year for buildings and a one off $50 million for the roughly 80 kōhanga reo needing new builds or upgrades.
- **$1.25 billion over five years for kura property** — That is $250 million every year to develop kura and grow Māori medium education.
- **Te reo Māori a core subject from primary school to Year 10** — Plus compulsory teaching of Aotearoa history, tikanga and te ao Māori.
- **Te Ahu o Te Reo Māori restored** — Funded at $30 million a year.
- **20 new kōhanga reo over three years** — Built with the Trust setting the priorities.
- **Write off the living cost part of every student loan** — And work towards full write offs for people who live and work here for five years.
- **A student allowance for every student, at double the rate** — Scrap the parental income test, and restore allowances for postgraduate, part time and long course students.
- **Free public transport for all students, including tertiary**
- **Teachers' pay rises that keep up with inflation** — Support for Māori teachers, kaiako and support staff, and action on pay and workload.
- **Second chance and adult education**
- **Scholarships and trade pathways** — Kai sovereignty scholarships for rangatahi in food science, marine biology and primary industries, and apprenticeships in building and energy.

### Being well (Oranga)

*Māori die seven years earlier than non Māori. That has to change.*

- **Free doctor visits and free dental care for all whānau** — Free primary healthcare and free dental care.
- **$500 million a year for kaupapa Māori mental health** — Plus free mental health services in the community, and addiction treated as a health issue.
- **Clinics on marae and mobile health units** — And free delivery of medication.
- **Earlier bowel screening** — From age 45 for everyone, and from 35 for Māori and Pacific peoples.
- **Māori leading Māori health** — A quarter of all health and ACC funding moved to systems led by Māori.
- **A Mana Hauā Authority led by Māori for disabled whānau** — Support that keeps Tangata Hauā with their whānau, and recognises unpaid care as real work.
- **Protect rongoā Māori and fund more medicines through PHARMAC**
- **Gender affirming healthcare in the public system** — Led by takatāpui and culturally safe, with protection for intersex tamariki from gender normalising surgery.

### Our whenua, wai and moana (Taiao)

*When the land is harmed, the people are harmed.*

- **Ban seabed mining** — End new oil and gas exploration, and support workers through a fair change.
- **Repeal the Fast Track Approvals Act** — And cancel mining consents that were fast tracked.
- **End mining on conservation land**
- **Safe, affordable drinking water for every whānau** — And no selling or trading of water rights. Water is a taonga, not a product.
- **Mana whenua decide about freshwater** — End Crown only control of water.
- **$1 billion to put solar and insulation on marae, kura, papakāinga and homes**
- **Marae and hapū funded as hubs for disasters** — Plus a $100 million fund to rebuild and clean up marae, urupā and papakāinga.
- **$400 million to help Māori farmers move to regenerative farming** — Including small, diverse whenua blocks growing kai for local people.
- **A $100 million Community Kai Fund** — For home gardens, marae māra kai, and food farms led by Māori and communities.

### Our reo and who we are (Tuakiri)

*Te reo Māori should be part of everyday life, not something you have to ask for.*

- **Te reo Māori compulsory in education** — And public agencies required to work in both languages.
- **Fund reo led by whānau and marae** — Support iwi language plans and protect our regional dialects.
- **Stronger Māori media and broadcasting in te reo**
- **$19 million base funding for Te Matatini** — Plus $10 million for kapa haka in communities, hapū and iwi.
- **A Toi Māori authority led by Māori** — Funded the same as Creative NZ, plus a guaranteed income scheme for artists.
- **A $100 million Whānau Pakari fund for sport** — Māori sports academies, scholarships for Māori athletes, and funding for Pā Wars.
- **Māori teams competing overseas as Aotearoa Māori**
- **Pathways for takatāpui into te reo fluency and leadership on marae** — And funding for creative and cultural expression led by takatāpui.

### Making our own decisions (Mana motuhake)

*Te Tiriti can't be settled. It's a living agreement that must be honoured.*

- **Land back** — Return the foreshore and seabed, conservation land, and Crown and council land to mana whenua.
- **Make Waitangi Tribunal recommendations binding on the Crown**
- **An independent Te Tiriti Commission** — With the power to stop Crown decisions that seriously breach Te Tiriti.
- **Protect the Māori seats for good** — Entrench the Māori electorates and require Māori wards in every council.
- **A quarter of all government spending on contracts goes to Māori businesses** — Plus a $4.5 billion fund to grow Māori land, marae and business.
- **Overseas buyers can lease our land but never own it**
- **Māori data controlled by Māori**

### Justice and safety (Tika)

*Justice is about balance and healing, not just punishment.*

- **An independent Māori Justice Authority** — Funded with half of the Corrections, Police and Courts budgets.
- **Abolish prisons by 2040** — Replaced with community based, restorative alternatives grounded in tikanga. Keeping people safe through prevention, not punishment.
- **Drug use treated as a health issue, not a crime** — Convictions for drug use and possession wiped, and double the funding to prevent and treat alcohol and drug harm.
- **Legal help led by Māori** — Independent Māori legal aid, kaupapa Māori community law centres, and more support for Māori Wardens.
- **Raise the age of criminal responsibility from 10 to 16**
- **Body cameras for every police officer** — And a truly independent police watchdog grounded in te ao Māori.
- **Repeal three strikes and the gang laws**
- **Lower the voting age to 16** — Enrol any time, and switch between the Māori and general rolls any time.
- **Aotearoa becomes a neutral country** — No fighting in foreign wars unless we're directly threatened. The Defence Force focuses on disaster response and the Pacific.

## Part 2 — what changes for you (“Which of these sounds like you or your whānau?”)


### Renting

- **Rents frozen** — Then only allowed to rise by inflation each year.
- **No more no cause evictions** — Gone in our first 100 days.

### Working for wages

- **No tax on your first $30,000**
- **Minimum wage raised to the living wage** — $28.95 an hour.

### Money's tight

### On a benefit

- **Baseline benefits doubled** — Sanctions and work test obligations removed.
- **Benefit debt to the Crown cancelled** — And hardship grants that don't need to be paid back.
- **Special Needs Grants that cover cultural costs** — Including tangihanga and unveilings.

- **Living cost part of your student loan written off**
- **A student allowance for every student, at double the rate** — And free public transport for students, including tertiary.

### Running a business

- **A $4.5 billion Oranga Whenua Oranga Tangata Fund** — Investment and grants for Māori land, marae and business.

### Farming or on whenua Māori

- **$400 million to help Māori farmers go regenerative**
- **Support for farms to make their own power**
- **The right to sell homegrown and homekill kai locally** — Including roadside kai, without interference or tax.

### Living rurally

- **The right to sell homegrown and homekill kai locally** — Including roadside kai, without interference or tax.

- **More land set aside for rural and urban food farms**

### Kaitiaki for our hapū or marae

### Toi Māori and creativity

## Part 3 — “Everything else we’ll do”


### Entrench Te Tiriti o Waitangi

*Honouring Te Tiriti becomes a legal duty on the Crown, not a choice for whoever is in government.*

- **An independent Te Tiriti Commission** — It can stop Crown decisions that seriously breach Te Tiriti. If the Crown disagrees, it has to go to the High Court.
- **Waitangi Tribunal recommendations made binding on the Crown** — With clear timeframes for putting them in place.
- **$220 million over four years for Māori to lead our constitutional future** — Kōrero in every rohe, working towards constitutional transformation by 2040.

### Energy sovereignty

*From customers to owners. Lower power bills now, and our people owning the power system for the future.*

- **Cut the power** — Help for whānau to lower bills with solar, batteries, better heating and hot water, and low cost or zero interest finance.
- **Build the power** — A $1 billion Energy Sovereignty Fund for renewable power, community batteries and energy jobs owned by our people.
- **Own the power** — Whānau, hapū, iwi, marae and communities supported to own energy assets, and paid fairly for power they send back to the grid.

### A fairer tax system

*Less tax for 97% of people, and those with the most wealth pay their fair share. The money goes to housing, health, education, climate resilience and kaupapa Māori solutions.*

- **No tax on the first $30,000 you earn** — Less income tax for 97% of people. Nine in ten people get around $4,000 more a year on average.
- **GST off kai, paid back to you as a tax credit** — For people earning $60,000 or less. Worth up to eight weeks of kai a year.
- **A wealth tax on the richest 3%** — Only on net wealth over $2 million per person. 1.5% between $2 million and $5 million, 2% between $5 million and $10 million, and 2.5% over $10 million.
- **Companies and speculators pay their share** — Company tax back to 33%, a 5% tax on profits sent offshore, a 33% land banking tax, a 2% tax on empty homes, and 5% stamp duty on home sales, except for first home buyers under $1 million.
- **$1 billion to crack down on tax fraud and avoidance** — More investment in Inland Revenue, the Serious Fraud Office, the Financial Markets Authority and the Financial Intelligence Unit.
