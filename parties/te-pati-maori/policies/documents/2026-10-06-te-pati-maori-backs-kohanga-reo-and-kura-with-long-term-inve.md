# Te Pāti Māori Backs Kōhanga Reo And Kura With Long-Term Investment

*Tuesday, 6 October 2026, 7:49 pm Press Release: Te Pati Maori* — [https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PA2610/S00052/te-pati-maori-backs-kohanga-reo-and-kura-with-long-term-investment.htm](https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PA2610/S00052/te-pati-maori-backs-kohanga-reo-and-kura-with-long-term-investment.htm)

## Full text

Te Pāti Māori will back the future of Māori-medium education with long-term investment in kōhanga reo and kura Māori, giving whānau certainty to build, grow and strengthen te reo Māori for future generations, Co-leader Rawiri Waititi and Hauraki-Waikato MP Hana-Rāwhiti Maipi-Clarke announced today in Rotorua.

“Kōhanga reo and kura Māori are the heartbeat of te reo Māori revitalisation. They need secure, long-term investment that reflects what they mean to our tamariki, our whānau and the future of Aotearoa.

“This is tino rangatiratanga in education. Māori-medium education must be able to plan, build and grow with certainty,” said Rawiri Waititi.

“From new kōhanga reo to upgraded kura, this is about giving whānau the support and certainty they need to raise tamariki in te reo Māori.

“Our tamariki deserve to learn in environments that are fit for purpose, with the buildings, kaiako and resources needed to support them to thrive,” said Hana-Rāwhiti Maipi-Clarke.

Te Pāti Māori will establish a Te Kōhanga Reo Act and lock in secure annual baseline funding for kōhanga reo.

It will invest $20 million a year in a dedicated capital fund, alongside a $50 million investment to address the backlog of around 80 kōhanga reo needing new builds or refurbishment.

The policy will fund 20 new kōhanga reo over three years, with priorities set alongside Te Kōhanga Reo National Trust to ensure investment reaches the communities that need it most.

For kura Māori, Te Pāti Māori will commit $1.25 billion over five years through a ring-fenced property investment plan. This includes $250 million a year to upgrade kura property and support the continued growth of Māori-medium education.

Te Pāti Māori will also restore Te Ahu o Te Reo Māori, with $30 million a year to strengthen te reo Māori capability across the education workforce.
