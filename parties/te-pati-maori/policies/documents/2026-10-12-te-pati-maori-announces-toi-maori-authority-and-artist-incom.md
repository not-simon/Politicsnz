# Te Pāti Māori Announces Toi Māori Authority And Artist Income Programme

*Monday, 12 October 2026, 3:17 pm Press Release: Te Pati Maori* — [https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PA2610/S00101/te-pati-maori-announces-toi-maori-authority-and-artist-income-programme.htm](https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PA2610/S00101/te-pati-maori-announces-toi-maori-authority-and-artist-income-programme.htm)

## Full text

Today in Ōtautahi, Te Pāti Māori has announced the establishment of an independent Toi Māori Authority and a continuing, two-year rotational income programme for Māori artists contributing to mātauranga Māori.

The authority will be independent of Creative New Zealand, resourced at parity with it, and governed by tohunga toi from across Māori art forms and iwi.

The income programme will support 1,000 artists at a time, providing paid work for 30 hours a week over two years. Places will then rotate to a new cohort of artists.

Co-Leader Rawiri Waititi said colonisation targeted Māori tohunga, artists, and storytellers because they carried the knowledge and identity of their people.

“Our identity is inseparable from our art forms. Successive governments have allowed the harm to Toi Māori to continue. This is about putting decisions in Māori hands and properly backing the artists who carry our mātauranga forward.

“When our creativity is strong, our people are strong.”

This investment will strengthen and protect the transmission of mātauranga Māori at home and internationally, it will support the oranga and livelihoods of our artists, and it will ensure that all mokopuna of Aotearoa grow up surrounded by mātauranga Māori in all of its forms.
