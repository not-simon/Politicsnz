### Tax

### What we’ll do

- Make sure the wealthiest pay their fair share.

- Use that revenue to fund housing, health, education and whānau support.

### Why it matters

When the system rewards the top and squeezes everyone else, poverty becomes permanent. A country that protects the wealthy while whānau go without is not fair, it is rigged. Tax is one of the ways we return power, resources, and opportunity back to the people who keep this country moving.
