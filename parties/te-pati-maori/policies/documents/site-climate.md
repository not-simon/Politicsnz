### Climate

Published by Te Pāti Māori

June 03, 2026

### What we’ll do

- Protect whenua and moana from extractive industries (including banning seabed mining).

- Invest in climate resilience for communities most at risk.

- Back regenerative and sustainable approaches aligned with Māori values.

### Why it matters

Our whenua, awa, moana, marae, wāhi tapu, mahinga kai and whakapapa are not resources to be exploited. They are our inheritance, our responsibility, and our future. Climate justice is about protecting our right to live safely, affordably, and sovereign on our own whenua.
