Military
Neutrality
Te Pāti Māori declares that Aotearoa must
become a militarily neutral nation grounded
in peace, mana motuhake, and indigenous
solidarity.
Aotearoa can no longer act as a Pacific spy base
for imperialist powers who threaten the Mana
Motuhake of every nation in the Pacific and who uses
our geographical location in the world to uphold
imperialism across other regions.
We must determine our own independent foreign
and defence policy, and our defence force must
prioritise protection, resilience, and Pacific support.
Military neutrality is not isolation. It is an active
sovereignty. It is choosing peace, protecting our
people, and standing in solidarity with indigenous
communities worldwide.

Summary

Te Pāti Māori Policy Manifesto 2026
61
For generations, Māori have been called to
fight in wars that were not our own.
From the Pioneer Battalion to the Māori
Battalion, and through conflicts in Korea,
Malaya, Vietnam and Afghanistan, Māori
service has been marked by sacrifice. Yet,
on return, our people were met with no
land allocations, no housing support, and no
enduring benefit for our communities.
This reflects a broader truth: participation in
imperial wars has not advanced the wellbeing
or sovereignty of Māori.
Our foreign affairs and defence policies have
been shaped externally through alliances
driven by global powers. This undermines
our ability to act in accordance with our own
values as tangata whenua.
We must also remember that colonisation
is a global empire. Our Mana Motuhake
is inherently tied to the liberation of all
indigenous peoples across the world.
The changing global environment demands
a reset toward peace, diplomacy, and
indigenous solidarity.
We must work to achieve a demilitarised
Pacific, free from the influence of foreign
militaries and their destructive legacies.
Our sovereignty cannot be dictated by foreign
powers. We reject involvement in conflicts
with other indigenous peoples on their own
lands and oppose Aotearoa being used as a
base for foreign military intelligence alliances.
While we advance a policy of military
neutrality, we understand that lasting peace
cannot be achieved without justice. We are
committed to upholding international human
rights and holding war criminals to account.
Military neutrality is an expression of mana
motuhake in action.
Context
Our Position

Military Neutrality
62
62
Te Pāti Māori will...
1. • Declare Aotearoa a Militarily Neutral
Nation
• Legislate neutrality and prohibit
participation in foreign wars unless directly
threatened.
2. Reset Foreign Policy
• Develop an independent foreign policy
grounded in Māori values and prioritise
Pacific and indigenous relationships.
3. • Withdraw from Compromising Military
Alignments
• Review and withdraw from arrangements
that undermine sovereignty.
4. Transform the Role of the Defence Force
• Refocus on protection, disaster response,
and Pacific support.
5. Embed Indigenous Values
• Ensure decisions reflect manaakitanga,
whanaungatanga, and rangatiratanga.
6. Promote Global Peace Leadership
• Position Aotearoa as a neutral mediator
and advocate for indigenous rights.
7. Uphold International Human Rights
• Sanction nations involved in illegal wars and
cut diplomatic relations with government’s
actively committing war crimes.
Our Commitment
Te Pāti Māori is committed to asserting tino
rangatiratanga over foreign and defence
policy, protecting our people, and leading
with peace.
We will not trade our mana for alliances that
struggle to recognise that mana in the first
place.
