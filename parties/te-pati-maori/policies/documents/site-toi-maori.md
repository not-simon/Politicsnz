### Toi Māori

### What we’ll do

- Invest in Toi Māori and Māori creative industries.

- Protect Māori cultural rights and expressions.

- Back Māori-led arts infrastructure, funding, and pathways.

### Why it matters

Toi Māori is not decoration. It is truth-telling, resistance, healing, identity, and legacy. Our art carries the stories others tried to silence and projects our imagination into the future.
