# Te Pāti Māori Launches New Tool Putting Whānau Priorities First

*Friday, 9 October 2026, 1:46 pm Press Release: Te Pati Maori* — [https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PA2610/S00079/te-pati-maori-launches-new-tool-putting-whanau-priorities-first.htm](https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PA2610/S00079/te-pati-maori-launches-new-tool-putting-whanau-priorities-first.htm)

## Full text

Te Pāti Māori has launched a new interactive online tool that helps whānau quickly see how Te Pāti Māori policies align with the issues that matter most to them.

The "What Matters to You?" tool asks users to select their top three priorities, from affordability and housing to health, te reo Māori, the environment, justice and mana motuhake. It then provides a personalised summary of the policies and actions Te Pāti Māori would take in those areas.

Te Pāti Māori Co-Leader Rawiri Waititi said too often politics expects people to fit around political agendas rather than the other way around.

"Whānau shouldn't have to dig through policy documents to understand what's on offer. This tool starts with what matters to them and shows what Te Pāti Māori would do about it.

"Whether it's putting more money back into people's pockets, protecting our reo, improving health outcomes or strengthening Māori authority, this tool makes our kaupapa easy to access and understand."

Users can also select circumstances that apply to them, such as renting, studying, working for wages, or receiving a benefit, helping show how policy proposals could affect their lives.

"It takes less than a minute to complete, nothing is stored, and nothing is sent anywhere. It's simply a way for whānau to explore our kaupapa and see how it connects to their everyday lives."
