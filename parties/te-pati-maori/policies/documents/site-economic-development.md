### Economic development

Published by Te Pāti Māori

June 03, 2026

### What we’ll do

- Build Māori-led institutions and funds that grow Māori enterprise.

- Support trade, innovation, and long-term Māori wealth building.

- Protect Māori data and knowledge (data sovereignty).

### Why it matters

Māori prosperity cannot rely on permission from systems built to exclude us. Economic power is political power. This is about restoring what was taken, growing Māori wealth, and building a future where our people thrive on our own terms.
