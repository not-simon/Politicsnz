# Te Pāti Māori Puts Its Money Where Its Mouth Is With One-Person Party List

*Sunday, 4 October 2026, 2:21 pm Press Release: Te Pati Maori* — [https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PA2610/S00026/te-pati-maori-puts-its-money-where-its-mouth-is-with-one-person-party-list.htm](https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PA2610/S00026/te-pati-maori-puts-its-money-where-its-mouth-is-with-one-person-party-list.htm)

## Full text

Te Pāti Māori has today announced its Party List for the 2026 General Election, reaffirming its candidate-vote strategy by selecting just one list candidate outside its seven Māori electorate candidates.

Lance Norman, General Secretary of Te Pāti Māori, was selected unanimously as the Party’s additional list candidate.

“This list proves we are putting our money where our mouth is,” Te Pāti Māori Co-Leader Rawiri Waititi said.

“We made a deliberate decision to put collective Māori representation ahead of building a long party list.

“Our strategy is clear. We want more Māori in Parliament, a one-term Government, and an independent Māori voice that is accountable to our people.”

Co-Leader Debbie Ngarewa-Packer said the Party was standing by the strategy it had taken to the motu.

“We said this election is bigger than any one party. Our list reflects that commitment.

“We are backing our seven candidates to win the Māori electorates and standing by the strategy we announced to our people.”
