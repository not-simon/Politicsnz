### Housing

### What we’ll do

- Build and support more public and affordable homes.

- Make it easier to build homes on Māori/ancestral land.

- Stop speculation and land-banking (including action on vacant properties).

### Why it matters

A warm, safe, secure home is not a luxury. It is the ground everything else stands on: health, education, safety, stability, and hope. Our people deserve homes, not waiting lists, overcrowding, or speculation.
