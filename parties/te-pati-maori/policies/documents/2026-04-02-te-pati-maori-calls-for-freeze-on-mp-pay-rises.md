# Te Pāti Māori Calls For Freeze On MP Pay Rises

*Thursday, 2 April 2026, 11:46 am Press Release: Te Pati Maori* — [https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PA2604/S00015/te-pati-maori-calls-for-freeze-on-mp-pay-rises.htm](https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PA2604/S00015/te-pati-maori-calls-for-freeze-on-mp-pay-rises.htm)

## Full text

Te Pāti Māori is calling for an immediate freeze on all salary increases for MPs, Ministers, and the Prime Minister, as politicians prepare to receive a pay bump later this year.

“At a time when whānau are being stretched to breaking point just to put kai on the table, politicians should not be giving themselves a raise, “ said Rawiri Waititi, Te Pāti Māori Co-leader and Finance spokesperson

“It’s daylight robbery.”

Te Pāti Māori says accepting pay rises during one of the toughest economic periods in recent memory is out of touch with the reality facing everyday families.

“We cannot sit here and take more money while our people are struggling to survive. It goes against everything we stand for. It is morally wrong. It is ethically wrong. And it makes no sense economically either.”

The party says the focus should instead be on addressing growing inequality across Aotearoa.

“The government is cashing in on the extra tax revenue from rising fuel and kai costs but has no intention of redistributing it to where it’s needed most.”

“The last thing this country needs is politicians lining their own pockets while whānau go without.”

In May 2025, the Government used urgency to wipe 33 pay equity claims. A year later, after telling women workers there is no money for fairness, politicians are lining up for a pay rise. That is not leadership. It is a disgrace.

Te Pāti Māori is calling for an immediate freeze on MP pay rises and for Ministers and the Prime Minister to take a deeper cut in solidarity with the people they are meant to serve.

We will back pay equity claims, not political privilege.

The money should go to workers, wāhine and whānau, not to politicians who are protected from the worst of this economy.
