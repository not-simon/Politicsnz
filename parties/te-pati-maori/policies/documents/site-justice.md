### Justice

### What we’ll do

- Build Māori-led justice authority and community solutions.

- Reform laws that criminalise poverty and survival.

- Move away from mass imprisonment and toward long-term transformation.

### Why it matters

A system that cages Māori generation after generation is not failing by accident. True justice means ending the machinery that criminalises poverty, trauma, and survival, and building Māori-led solutions that restore mana, safety, and futures.
