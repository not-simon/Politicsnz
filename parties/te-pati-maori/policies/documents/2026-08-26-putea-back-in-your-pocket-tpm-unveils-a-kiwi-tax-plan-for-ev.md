# Pūtea Back In Your Pocket: TPM Unveils A Kiwi Tax Plan For Everyone

*Wednesday, 26 August 2026, 3:38 pm Press Release: Te Pati Maori* — [https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PA2608/S00238/putea-back-in-your-pocket-tpm-unveils-a-kiwi-tax-plan-for-everyone.htm](https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PA2608/S00238/putea-back-in-your-pocket-tpm-unveils-a-kiwi-tax-plan-for-everyone.htm)

Attachments linked in the release: [https://img.scoop.co.nz/media/pdfs/2608/A_Kiwi_Tax_Plan.pdf](https://img.scoop.co.nz/media/pdfs/2608/A_Kiwi_Tax_Plan.pdf)

## Full text

Te Pāti Māori has unveiled an economic plan for the many, not the few, putting more pūtea into the pockets of almost everyone in Aotearoa.

Under the plan, 4.5 million people (97%) will pay less income tax, with the first $30,000 earned tax-free.

Around 4.2 million people (90%) will receive, on average, an additional $4,000 a year.

“This is a policy for everyone. Whether you are a cleaner, a nurse, a teacher, a tradie, a pensioner or raising a whānau, we want you to keep more of what you earn.

“People across Aotearoa are feeling the squeeze. The kai bill is up. Housing costs are up. Power is up. Everything is taking a bigger bite out of the household budget.

“Our answer is simple: put pūtea back in people’s pockets and give Aotearoa some room to breathe.”

The plan also delivers targeted support with kai costs for 3 million people (64%), providing people earning $60,000 or less with a tax credit worth up to eight weeks of kai each year.

The other side of the equation is fairness.

The targeted wealth tax will affect only the wealthiest 3% of people, while 97% will pay no wealth tax at all. The wider plan also asks corporations, offshore profit transfers, vacant property and land banking to contribute more.

“This isn't about Māori versus non-Māori, or workers versus business. It's about building an economy that works for the overwhelming majority of people who call Aotearoa home.

“4.5 million people (97%) pay less income tax. The wealthiest 3% contribute more. That's what a fair economy looks like.”

We have named this the Kiwi Tax Plan because it is a tax policy for everyone, ensuring everyone in Aotearoa has an opportunity to thrive.
