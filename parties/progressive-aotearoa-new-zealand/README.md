## About

An unregistered party based on Prabhat Ranjan Sarkar's Progressive Utilization Theory (economic democracy; turning large businesses into co-operatives). It stood two electorate candidates in 2020 (Dyer won 50 votes in Nelson) and Dyer alone in 2023. In August 2026 it announced three candidates. Not to be confused with Jim Anderton's former Progressive Party.

Wikipedia notes that a candidate standing under a party name "is not necessarily an indication that the party exists
beyond that single candidate". We found no independent coverage of this group's programme beyond what is listed here;
where the candidate supplied a statement to policy.nz it appears in their person profile.
