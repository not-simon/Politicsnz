## Community & inclusion

### Guarantee access to food, clothing, housing, education and healthcare (PPA-01)

**Why** (*archive*): The Progressive Party says economic and political power should serve people rather than concentrated wealth (its stated philosophy draws on economic democracy and 'neohumanism').

**How** (*archive*): Listed in the health, housing and education sections as guarantees for everyone.

Full text: [Policies](documents/policies.md) ([original](https://www.progressiveparty.co.nz/policies-3/))

## Economy

### Shift farming to co-operatives and plant-based protein (PPA-10)

**How** (*archive*): Incentivise farmers to move to plant protein (including lab-grown meat) and regenerative, ethical farming models; co-operatives at all levels of agriculture.

Full text: [Policies](documents/policies.md) ([original](https://www.progressiveparty.co.nz/policies-3/))

### Run businesses with more than nine employees as worker co-operatives and state-manage monopolistic key industries (PPA-12)

**How** (*archive*): A Minister, Ministry and Centre for Cooperative Studies; railways, power and mining state-managed; a nationwide co-operative supermarket to cut living costs.

Full text: [Policies](documents/policies.md) ([original](https://www.progressiveparty.co.nz/policies-3/))

## Education

### Move schooling from national standards to child-centred, experiential learning including meditation and mindfulness (PPA-07)

**How** (*archive*): Learning based on each child's needs, parents as active partners, restorative behaviour management and a Hauora (holistic wellbeing) approach.

Full text: [Policies](documents/policies.md) ([original](https://www.progressiveparty.co.nz/policies-3/))

## Environment

### Establish a Ministry for Animal and Plant Welfare and extend legal advocacy to nature (PPA-09)

**How** (*archive*): Building on Te Urewera's legal personhood and animals' legal recognition as sentient.

Full text: [Policies](documents/policies.md) ([original](https://www.progressiveparty.co.nz/policies-3/))

### Build a government-backed renewables industry and prepare to move infrastructure 10 metres above sea level (PPA-11)

**How** (*archive*): Electrify transport, convert sewage and organic waste to biogas and biodiesel, raise eco-housing standards, phase out gas appliances, and redirect foreign aid toward emissions cuts in the Pacific.

Full text: [Policies](documents/policies.md) ([original](https://www.progressiveparty.co.nz/policies-3/))

## Health

### Create an independent National Health Board outside the Health Ministry to oversee Te Whatu Ora (PPA-02)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says this would counterbalance political influence on health priorities.

**How** (*archive*): A board of health experts with elected and appointed members, plus co-operative primary and residential care, Māori-for-Māori services and higher taxes on harmful products ring-fenced for health.

Full text: [Policies](documents/policies.md) ([original](https://www.progressiveparty.co.nz/policies-3/))

### Legalise cannabis as a health issue and support the End-of-Life Choice Act (PPA-03)

**How** (*archive*): Tax revenue from cannabis would go to health, with research on benefits and harms; the party also lists voluntary vaccination and a review of Covid-era measures for future pandemics.

Full text: [Policies](documents/policies.md) ([original](https://www.progressiveparty.co.nz/policies-3/))

## Housing

### Offer state-funded mortgages, a state-owned house-building enterprise and limit home ownership to NZ residents (PPA-04)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says leaving housing to the market 'amounts to a dereliction of duty'.

**How** (*archive*): Also long-term leasing, co-operative housing and co-housing options, and a capital gains tax to curb speculation.

Full text: [Policies](documents/policies.md) ([original](https://www.progressiveparty.co.nz/policies-3/))

## Incomes & employment

### Phase out income tax in favour of taxes on production, remove GST from fresh produce and introduce a capital gains tax (PPA-13)

**How** (*archive*): Income tax would become a mechanism capping salaries at a maximum linked to the minimum wage, set by a Wages and Salary Commission.

Full text: [Policies](documents/policies.md) ([original](https://www.progressiveparty.co.nz/policies-3/))

## Law, justice & government

### Focus prisons on rehabilitation, education and restorative justice, and end for-profit prisons (PPA-08)

**Why** (*archive*): The party views crime as rooted in social and economic conditions.

**How** (*archive*): Skills training and tikanga Māori in prisons, mental-health services, restorative justice, community reintegration support, and terminating private prison arrangements.

Full text: [Policies](documents/policies.md) ([original](https://www.progressiveparty.co.nz/policies-3/))

### Create Social Boards of respected community members to advise local and national government (PPA-14)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says they would moderate lobby groups' influence.

Full text: [Policies](documents/policies.md) ([original](https://www.progressiveparty.co.nz/policies-3/))

## Te ao Māori

### Make te reo Māori compulsory in primary and secondary schools, aiming for a bilingual Aotearoa (PPA-05)

**How** (*archive*): Language parity with English, plus full teaching of New Zealand history and the constitutional documents (Te Tiriti and He Whakaputanga).

Full text: [Policies](documents/policies.md) ([original](https://www.progressiveparty.co.nz/policies-3/))

### Entrench the Māori seats in Parliament (PPA-06)

**How** (*archive*): Give the Māori seats the same entrenched protection as general seats.

Full text: [Policies](documents/policies.md) ([original](https://www.progressiveparty.co.nz/policies-3/))
