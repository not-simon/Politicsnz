## About

Part of the international Money Free movement, which advocates a resource-based economy in which all work is voluntary (inspired by Jacque Fresco's Venus Project). Never registered: it could not verify 500 members for 2014. Results: five electorate candidates in 2014; four in 2017 (293 votes in total); two in 2020 and two in 2023. Osmaston is a frequent mayoral candidate.

Wikipedia notes that a candidate standing under a party name "is not necessarily an indication that the party exists
beyond that single candidate". We found no independent coverage of this group's programme beyond what is listed here;
where the candidate supplied a statement to policy.nz it appears in their person profile.
