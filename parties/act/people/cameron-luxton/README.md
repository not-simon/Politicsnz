## Current status

ACT list MP since 2023. Standing in **Mount Maunganui** and **#12** on ACT's list for 2026.

## Biography

Raised in Pāpāmoa and educated at Mount Maunganui College. Spent seven years dairy farming at Galatea (Rotorua Modern Apprentice of the Year 2013; Bay of Plenty Dairy Trainee 2014) and has worked as a self-employed builder. Married to Susan; two children.

## Political career

- Contested Tauranga in 2020 (fourth) and the 2022 Tauranga by-election (third, 10.2%).
- Contested Bay of Plenty in 2023 (third) and was elected at list #11.

## Record & notable actions

- Member's bill to remove Easter trading and alcohol restrictions; defeated 74–49 on a conscience vote in December 2024.
- Member's bill (April 2026) to strip voting rights from unelected appointees (e.g. iwi/hapū representatives) on council committees, which ACT has pledged to pass within its first 100 days if re-elected.

## Controversies

- During the 2022 by-election he likened Tauranga commission chair Anne Tolley to Marie Antoinette, saying "off with [her head]"; ACT defended the remark as a figure of speech.
