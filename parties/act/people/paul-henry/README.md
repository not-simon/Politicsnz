## Current status

Never an MP. ACT candidate at **#4** on the party list for the 7 November 2026 election — a position likely to win a seat if ACT polls near its 2023 level.

## Biography

Born Paul Henry Hopes in Auckland on 4 August 1960; moved to Bristol with his mother at 11. Began at the BBC, then worked on National Radio, 2ZD Wairarapa and his own Today FM (1991–92), Radio Pacific and Radio Live. Co-hosted TVNZ's *Breakfast* (2004–2010), Network Ten's *Breakfast* in Sydney (2012), *The Paul Henry Show* (2014) and *Paul Henry* on TV3/Radio Live (2015–2016), then semi-retired; later host of *The Traitors NZ* and *The Chase NZ*. Author of *What Was I Thinking* (2011) and *Outraged* (2013). Appointed to the TVNZ board in 2025.

## Political career

- National candidate for Wairarapa in 1999.
- Spoke at ACT's 2024 "Change Makers" event, calling ACT "the last cab left on the rank heading in the right direction"; announced as an ACT candidate in 2026.

## Controversies

- Resigned from TVNZ in 2010 after mocking the name of Indian politician Sheila Dikshit and questioning whether Governor-General Sir Anand Satyanand "looks and sounds like a New Zealander"; New Zealand's government apologised to India.
- In July 2026 said on Radio Hauraki, in a "shoot, shag or marry" game, that he would "shoot" Labour leader Chris Hipkins; Hipkins and Ayesha Verrall called it offensive and akin to a death threat, Henry called it "manufactured outrage", and David Seymour and Nicole McKee defended it as a risqué game.
