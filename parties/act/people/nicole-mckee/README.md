## Current status

ACT list MP since 2020, Minister for Courts and Associate Minister of Justice (Firearms), and ACT deputy leader since 28 June 2026. Standing in **Wellington Bays** and **#2** on ACT's list for 2026.

## Biography

Born in Lower Hutt (1970s), grew up partly in Rotorua; of Ngāpuhi descent. Her partner died in a car crash a week before their daughter was born when she was 24. She came to public attention as spokesperson for the Council of Licensed Firearms Owners, opposing the gun-law reforms passed after the 2019 Christchurch mosque attacks, and joined ACT in June 2020. Married to Duncan; four children; lives in Hataitai. Enjoys hunting and wool spinning.

## Political career

- Contested Rongotai in 2020 and 2023; elected at list #3 in 2020 and re-elected in 2023.
- ACT spokesperson for firearms, conservation, justice and veterans (2020–2023).
- Minister for Courts and Associate Minister of Justice (Firearms) from November 2023.

## Record & notable actions

- **Three strikes reinstated**: the Sentencing (Reinstating Three Strikes) Amendment Act passed on 10 December 2024 (modified from the 2010 version, applying to sentences of two years or more with more judicial discretion).
- **Arms Act rewrite**: unveiled in November 2025 and passed in late July 2026 — moves firearms licensing from Police to a new agency, keeps the semi-automatic ban and the registry, adds new offences.
- Eased rules for shooting clubs and ranges (2024); proposed tougher shoplifting and trespass penalties (2025) and liquor-licensing liberalisation (2025).
- Set up a $1.8m-a-year Ministerial retail crime advisory group (2024), which disbanded early in 2026 after three of five members resigned amid disagreements with chair Sunny Kaushal.

## Controversies

- In August 2024 the NZ Police Association asked the PM to strip her of the firearms portfolio, calling her a gun lobbyist and objecting to its exclusion from consultation; Gun Control NZ, Muslim groups and Labour criticised the short consultation on range rules. McKee said Police had been consulted.
