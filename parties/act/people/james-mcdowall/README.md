## Current status

Former ACT list MP (2020–2023); did not stand in 2023. Since January 2024 Head of Advocacy at the Motor Trade Association. Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

Born in Pukekohe on 6 January 1988. Studied management at Waikato and Maastricht universities; PhD in marketing from Waikato (2018). Co-owned an immigration law firm with his wife Sushan, a lawyer, and worked for mental-health provider the Wise Group. Lives in Hamilton; speaks Cantonese.

## Political career

- ACT member since after the 2005 election; candidate for Hamilton East in 2017.
- Elected at list #6 in 2020 (Waikato candidate). Third in the 2022 Hamilton West by-election (about 10%).
- Did not seek re-election in 2023.

## Record & notable actions

- Led ACT's firearms policy in response to the 2019 Arms Amendment Act; described himself in his maiden speech as a libertarian and firearms enthusiast.
- As immigration spokesperson criticised COVID-era border settings and their effect on seasonal workers, and Labour's migrant-exploitation law changes.
- Supported the law allowing people to change the sex on their birth certificate by self-declaration.
- Swore his oath (2020) and read the parliamentary prayer (2022) in Cantonese, which drew wide attention in Hong Kong.
