## Current status

ACT list MP since 2020. In June 2026 he withdrew from ACT's party list because of kidney failure (and a heart attack in December 2025 that made him ineligible for the transplant list) and the toll of his son's death in 2024; according to the Wikipedia candidate list and policy.nz he remains ACT's **Northland** electorate candidate. RNZ reported he would leave Parliament; voters should check the official candidate list for his final status.

## Biography

A Ruawai dairy farmer who has farmed in Northland for about 30 years. Married with three children; his 22-year-old son Brody died suddenly in May 2024. Has spoken publicly about depression and kidney disease.

## Political career

- Contested Northland in 2020 (fourth) and 2023 (sixth); list MP from 2020.
- ACT spokesperson for primary industries, regional development and biosecurity in his first term.

## Record & notable actions

- Prominent voice on rural regulation and farmer mental health; argued some climate-policy messaging "perpetuat[ed] an environment of fear" for farmers.
- Member's bill (July 2024) to stop councils considering climate-change effects in resource consents, supported by Federated Farmers.
- Supported the End of Life Choice Act.

## Controversies

- In November 2021 Newshub reported 2019 social-media comments in which he called Jacinda Ardern a "feckless wench" and "vacuous teenager" and praised Donald Trump; he expressed regret for "flippant remarks" made before he was an MP.
