## Current status

ACT list MP since 2020 and Parliamentary Under-Secretary for Infrastructure and RMA Reform. Standing in **Kaipara ki Mahurangi** and **#8** on ACT's list for 2026.

## Biography

Educated at Auckland Grammar School and Unitec (civil engineering). Worked as a civil and environmental engineer for Auckland City Council and ran his own engineering firm. While working for MWH Global in Fiji (2016–17) he was deported after the firm's dispute with the Fiji Roads Authority; he says it followed his criticism of Chinese contractors' workmanship. He has said he was shot in the foot at 17 after a friend's cannabis deal went wrong. Three sons, the youngest of whom has Down syndrome.

## Political career

- Joined ACT in December 2019; contested Te Atatū in 2020 and 2023; list MP from 2020.
- Under-Secretary for Infrastructure and RMA reform from November 2023, working with Minister Chris Bishop.

## Record & notable actions

- Co-led replacement of the Resource Management Act 1991 with a **Planning Act** and a **Natural Environment Act** (announced March 2025; passed third readings on 22 September 2026).
- Strong supporter of Israel and critic of Iran.

## Controversies

- **Privileges breach (2023):** the Privileges Committee found on 26 July 2023 that he had committed a "clear breach" of select-committee confidentiality by disclosing an Environment Committee vote on the Natural and Built Environment Bill; he apologised and no further action was taken.
- **"Terro dress-up" post (September 2025):** described pro-Palestinian protesters as playing "terro dress-up" and motivated by antisemitism; the Greens criticised him, ACT leader David Seymour defended him on free-speech grounds, and he amended a slur in a related statement.
