## Current status

Former ACT list MP (2020–2023). Standing for ACT in **Christchurch East** in 2026 at **#39** on the list. Came third in the South Canterbury constituency of Environment Canterbury in 2025 (ACT Local).

## Biography

Grew up in Invercargill; worked for 14 years as a laboratory technician at Canterbury District Health Board; diagnosed with dyslexia at 21; diploma in marketing from Christchurch Polytechnic. Runs businesses with her husband Richard (residential rentals; building-exterior cleaning). A licensed gun owner.

## Political career

- ACT candidate for Christchurch Central (2008, 2011, 2014) and Christchurch East (2017, 2020, 2023).
- Elected at list #9 in 2020; dropped to #14 in 2023 and not re-elected.

## Record & notable actions

- Advocated deregulation and small business interests; argued the post-Christchurch gun laws were rushed and would not have prevented the attack.

None of significance found in the sources consulted.
