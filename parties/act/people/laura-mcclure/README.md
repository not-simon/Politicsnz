## Current status

ACT list MP since 2023 (elected under her then married name, Laura Trask). Standing in **Wigram** and **#9** on ACT's list for 2026.

## Biography

Laura Barbara McClure was born in Christchurch on 3 August 1985, eldest of three; attended Burnside High School, began an arts degree at Canterbury and trained as a pharmacy technician, working in Christchurch and Wellington. With her husband she ran the Auckland branch of her father's fire-safety evacuation business; she is a registered fire evacuation consultant. Two children. Began volunteering for ACT in 2020 and worked for MP Toni Severin.

## Political career

- Contested Banks Peninsula in 2023 (fourth, 2,073 votes) and was elected at list #10.

## Record & notable actions

- **Deepfake Digital Harm and Exploitation Bill** (drawn 23 October 2025): would extend the definition of intimate visual recordings and the Harmful Digital Communications Act to cover AI "deepfake" images.
- Sponsors a bill to abolish the Broadcasting Standards Authority; criticised the BSA's March 2026 ruling asserting jurisdiction over online station The Platform.
- Has criticised the education system as "incredibly woke" and argues government spending drives inflation.
- In May 2026 joined a cross-party delegation to Taiwan; China then banned the four MPs from China, Hong Kong and Macau for a year, and she refused to apologise.
