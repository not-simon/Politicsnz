## Current status

ACT list MP since 2023 and ACT whip. Standing in **Southland** and **#6** on ACT's list for 2026.

## Biography

Born in Lumsden and educated at James Hargest College, Invercargill, and the University of Otago (law). Worked for about 17 years in the pharmaceutical industry, including for Johnson & Johnson and Vertex Pharmaceuticals, mostly in Australia. An ACT member since the party's formation; worked for the party 1997–2000 and served on its board. Gay; lives in Queenstown with his Australian partner Alex.

## Political career

- Campaigned for ACT at elections from 1996 to 2005.
- Contested Southland in 2023 (fourth, 2,807 votes) and was elected at list #4.
- ACT whip and multi-portfolio spokesperson; from 2024 Parliamentary Private Secretary to Associate Health Minister David Seymour on Pharmac and Medsafe.

## Record & notable actions

- Parole (Mandatory Completion of Rehabilitative Programmes) Amendment Bill (introduced February 2024), barring parole consideration for offenders who have not completed rehabilitation plans; the Justice Committee could not agree to recommend it, and the Parole Board chair and Chief Ombudsman criticised the original draft.
- Argued for a "productivity focus" in Pharmac decision-making.

## Controversies

- In April 2024 Labour's Rachel Boyack called for his removal as arts spokesperson after he said taxpayers should not fund the arts and struggled to name a New Zealand author in an interview.
- In May 2024 he sold shares in health companies (Chimeric Therapeutics, CSL, Johnson & Johnson) after Labour alleged a conflict of interest with his health PPS role.
