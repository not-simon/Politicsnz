## Current status

Former ACT list MP (2020–2023). Did not seek re-election in 2023. Not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography

Born in Northern Ireland and raised in Enniskillen during the Troubles. Worked in FMCG corporate sales, banking (Virgin Group, ASB) and governance/advisory roles, and runs a consultancy. Moved to New Zealand in 2002; lives in Auckland with his daughter.

## Political career

- First-time candidate in Botany in 2020 (third); elected at list #10 after Stephen Berry withdrew.
- Did not stand in 2023; his valedictory was cancelled because of illness.

## Record & notable actions

- Overseas Investment (Exempt Investment from OECD Countries) Amendment Bill, to exempt OECD investors (except for residential land) from Overseas Investment Office approval; defeated 43–77 at first reading in September 2023.
- Described himself as liberal centre-right and, in his final speech, "anarcho-capitalist"; opposed the Fair Digital News Bargaining Bill.

## Controversies

- In July 2022 he said New Zealand was "like North Korea but without the nukes" in its foreign-investment rules.
