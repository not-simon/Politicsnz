## Current status

Former ACT list MP (2020–2023). Standing again for ACT in **Nelson** in 2026, at **#46** on the party list. Stood unsuccessfully as an independent for Nelson City Council in 2025.

## Biography

Grew up in a unionist, Labour-supporting household. Worked as a police officer for 14 years, owned a pub (he owns The Honest Lawyer in Nelson) and was a special-education teacher at Nayland College. Plays trumpet in jazz bands and played rugby for over 30 years.

## Political career

- Joined ACT at the end of 2019; contested Nelson in 2020 (fourth) and was elected on the list when ACT won 10 seats.
- ACT spokesperson for small business, workplace relations and safety, education and police.
- Contested Nelson again in 2023 (fourth, 2,692 votes); at list #17 he was not re-elected.

## Record & notable actions

- Launched ACT's 2022 truancy policy (daily attendance reporting tied to school funding; infringement regime for parents).
- Supported 90-day employment trials, charter schools and reducing regulation on small business.

## Controversies

- In 2020 former students criticised a discussion group he ran at Nayland College about climate-change "hysteria"; he rejected being labelled a climate-change denier.
