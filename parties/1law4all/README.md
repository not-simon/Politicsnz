| **Founded** | June 2013; registered 24 July 2014 |
| **Ideology** | Opposed Treaty of Waitangi references in law, the Waitangi Tribunal, Māori electorates and the UN Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples |
| **Seats** | None |

## History

Registered for the 2014 election, it failed to lodge a candidate list after four of five board members resigned
shortly before nominations closed. It had earlier removed references to Allan Titford from its website after his
conviction for rape and arson. As an unregistered group it distributed pamphlets in 2017 and 2019; the Advertising
Standards Authority ordered it to stop the 2019 material as likely to cause "serious offence". [1]
