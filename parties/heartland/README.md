# Heartland New Zealand Party (HeartlandNZ)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status (Oct 2026)** | **Deregistered** at its own request in June 2023; did not stand in 2023 or 2026 |
| **Founded** | 2019/2020 (sources differ); registered 6 August 2020 |
| **Leader** | Mark Ball (former Franklin District mayor) |
| **Ideology** | Rural party opposing the Emissions Trading Scheme, the Paris Agreement, the oil-and-gas exploration ban and agricultural environmental regulation |
| **Seats** | None |

## History

Backed at its founding by Hamilton entrepreneur Harry Mowbray, Heartland ran a five-person list in 2020 and won 914
party votes (0.03%), the fewest of any registered party, though Ball polled 8,462 electorate votes (21%, third) in Port
Waikato. [1][2]
