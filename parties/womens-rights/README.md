## History

Jill Ovens, a trade unionist and former Alliance co-leader, resigned from the Labour Party in 2023 over its support for
transgender rights and founded the Women's Rights Party. policy.nz notes that co-leader Dawn Trenberth, also a trade
unionist, had been a Labour member, and that both "left the Labour Party in 2023, feeling alienated by increased
hostility to trans-critical feminist perspectives". [1][2]

In 2023 the party (Ovens ranked first) won **0.09%** (2,513 votes); Ovens then came seventh (188 votes, 1%) in the 2023
Port Waikato by-election. [1][3]
