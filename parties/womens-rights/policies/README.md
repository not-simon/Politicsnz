## About this list

The Women's Rights Party, a sex-based-rights feminist party, publishes 13 'policy proposal' pages (each with a Proposal and Rationale), an 'Our Priorities' page and a downloadable full policy document (Word file dated 4 Nov 2025, which also contains members' policy-amendment proposals). Each distinct policy is listed below.

**Method.** Pages and PDFs were downloaded on 2026-10-23 by [`tools/policies_minor_sites_fetch.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_sites_fetch.py) / [`tools/policies_minor_opportunity_fetch.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_opportunity_fetch.py) (raw copies in `raw/party-docs/womens-rights/`) and reduced to their main text in [`documents/`](documents/) (markup and whitespace normalised only). The list and summaries are built by [`tools/policies_minor_build.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_build.py) from [`tools/policies_minor_catalog.py`](../../../tools/policies_minor_catalog.py).

policy.nz publishes no policy statements for this party, so every summary here is marked *archive*: written for this archive from the linked full text in one to three neutral sentences ("Why" = the party's own stated reason, attributed; "How" = what it says it would do). Party claims (costs, statistics) are reported as the party's claims, not verified.

**Gaps and caveats.** The full policy document is a Word file converted with pandoc; its proposal forms are tables, kept as HTML tables. No 2026-specific manifesto or costings were found; the web policy pages are undated.

## Community & inclusion

### Record biological sex on birth certificates and repeal sex self-identification (WRP-02)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says women's rights must be based on biological sex rather than gender identity.

**How** (*archive*): Amend the Births, Deaths, Marriages and Relationships Registration Act 2021 to remove 'nominated sex'; sex could be corrected only if originally recorded wrongly (e.g. undetected DSD).

Full text: [Women's Rights Party Full Policy (Word document, 4 Nov 2025)](documents/full-policy-docx-2025.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/POLICY-Womens-Rights-Party-2025-041125.docx))

### Oppose adding 'gender identity and gender expression' to the Human Rights Act (WRP-03)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says it could undermine sex-based exceptions.

Full text: [POLICY – Human Rights Act – Women's Rights Party](documents/proposal-human-rights-act.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/policy-proposal/human-rights-act/)); [Women's Rights Party Full Policy (Word document, 4 Nov 2025)](documents/full-policy-docx-2025.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/POLICY-Womens-Rights-Party-2025-041125.docx))

### Remove 'gender, gender identity and gender expression' from the Conversion Practices Prohibition Act (WRP-04)

**Why** (*archive*): It supports protecting lesbian, gay and bisexual people from conversion practices but says 'gender' is not a clear concept for law.

Full text: [Conversion Practices – Women's Rights Party](documents/proposal-conversion-practices.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/policy-proposal/conversion-practices/)); [Women's Rights Party Full Policy (Word document, 4 Nov 2025)](documents/full-policy-docx-2025.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/POLICY-Womens-Rights-Party-2025-041125.docx))

### Keep commercial surrogacy illegal, ban international surrogacy by New Zealanders and lengthen the birth mother's decision period (WRP-07)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says surrogacy cannot be free of exploitation and having a baby by surrogacy is not a human right.

**How** (*archive*): Keep the birth mother as first legal parent with longer than seven days to decide; children should be able to learn their origins.

Full text: [Women's Rights Party Full Policy (Word document, 4 Nov 2025)](documents/full-policy-docx-2025.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/POLICY-Womens-Rights-Party-2025-041125.docx))

### Protect single-sex spaces and keep biological males out of women's prisons (WRP-09)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says women's rights must be based on biological sex rather than gender identity.

**How** (*archive*): Single-sex toilets, changing rooms, refuges, wards, sports and women-only groups (including online); school privacy from the opposite sex; supportive conditions for pregnant prisoners and mothers.

Full text: [Protecting Women’s and Children’s Spaces and Safety – Women's Rights Party](documents/proposal-exit-services-for-women-leaving-gangs-religious-cults-and-se.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/policy-proposal/exit-services-for-women-leaving-gangs-religious-cults-and-services-to-support-detransitioners/)); [Women's Rights Party Full Policy (Word document, 4 Nov 2025)](documents/full-policy-docx-2025.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/POLICY-Womens-Rights-Party-2025-041125.docx))

### Fund women-only refuges, exit services for women leaving gangs or cults, and support for detransitioners (WRP-10)

**How** (*archive*): More resources to leave domestic violence and accessible, affordable, enforced protection orders.

Full text: [Protecting Women’s and Children’s Spaces and Safety – Women's Rights Party](documents/proposal-exit-services-for-women-leaving-gangs-religious-cults-and-se.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/policy-proposal/exit-services-for-women-leaving-gangs-religious-cults-and-services-to-support-detransitioners/))

### Use language in law, health and education that defines women as adult human females and motherhood as exclusively female (WRP-17)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says women's rights must be based on biological sex rather than gender identity.

**How** (*archive*): Keep words such as 'mother', 'woman' and 'breastfeeding' in maternity services.

Full text: [Our Priorities](documents/our-priorities.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/our-priorities/)); [Women's Rights Party Full Policy (Word document, 4 Nov 2025)](documents/full-policy-docx-2025.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/POLICY-Womens-Rights-Party-2025-041125.docx)); [Advocating for Women – Women's Rights Party](documents/proposal-advocating-for-women.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/policy-proposal/advocating-for-women/))

## Education

### Require parents to be told what is taught in sexuality education and stop schools supporting 'social transition' (WRP-11)

**Why** (*archive*): The party broadly supports the new RSE Framework because it recognises biological sex.

**How** (*archive*): Full disclosure to parents, no outside agencies such as InsideOut, teach that under-16s cannot consent, refer distressed children to professionals with parents involved.

Full text: [Policy — Educating our Young People – Women's Rights Party](documents/proposal-educating-our-young-people.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/policy-proposal/educating-our-young-people/)); [Women's Rights Party Full Policy (Word document, 4 Nov 2025)](documents/full-policy-docx-2025.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/POLICY-Womens-Rights-Party-2025-041125.docx))

## Health

### Ban puberty blockers and cross-sex hormones for under-18s with 'sex-related distress' and restrict funded gender surgery to over-25s (WRP-01)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says women's rights must be based on biological sex rather than gender identity.

**How** (*archive*): Also an inquiry into Health NZ's contracting of PATHA to write gender-affirming care guidelines, which it notes scored poorly in the Cass Report assessment.

Full text: [Puberty blockers, hormones and surgery – Women's Rights Party](documents/proposal-puberty-blockers-hormones-and-surgery.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/policy-proposal/puberty-blockers-hormones-and-surgery/)); [Women's Rights Party Full Policy (Word document, 4 Nov 2025)](documents/full-policy-docx-2025.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/POLICY-Womens-Rights-Party-2025-041125.docx))

## Incomes & employment

### Extend paid parental leave from six to 12 months, reform Working for Families and fund continuity of midwifery care (WRP-12)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says mothering and caring is work that contributes to society.

**How** (*archive*): Partner leave in addition to the mother's, CPAG-style WFF reform removing discrimination against beneficiary families, wrap-around support for vulnerable mothers, free or affordable childcare and a review of for-profit ECE.

Full text: [Caring for Mothers and Children – Women's Rights Party](documents/proposal-working-for-families-reform.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/policy-proposal/working-for-families-reform/)); [Women's Rights Party Full Policy (Word document, 4 Nov 2025)](documents/full-policy-docx-2025.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/POLICY-Womens-Rights-Party-2025-041125.docx))

### Deliver pay equity in the public and private sectors and reinstate Fair Pay Agreements (WRP-13)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says about 150,000 women's pay-equity claims were extinguished by the 2025 law change.

**How** (*archive*): A strong onus on employers and publication of average hourly pay by occupation, sex and ethnicity.

Full text: [Pay equity – Women's Rights Party](documents/proposal-pay-equity.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/policy-proposal/pay-equity/)); [Women's Rights Party Full Policy (Word document, 4 Nov 2025)](documents/full-policy-docx-2025.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/POLICY-Womens-Rights-Party-2025-041125.docx))

### Raise minimum wages and benefits and provide free financial advice to secure older women's incomes (WRP-14)

**Why** (*archive*): The party says older women are the fastest-growing group in poverty globally.

**How** (*archive*): Living incomes, affordable housing and work with the Office for Seniors on solutions to the retirement savings gap.

Full text: [Financial Stability for Women in Older Age – Women's Rights Party](documents/proposal-financial-stability-for-women-in-older-age.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/policy-proposal/financial-stability-for-women-in-older-age/)); [Women's Rights Party Full Policy (Word document, 4 Nov 2025)](documents/full-policy-docx-2025.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/POLICY-Womens-Rights-Party-2025-041125.docx))

## Law, justice & government

### Replace the Prostitution Reform Act with the 'Nordic' Equality Model criminalising buyers and brothel owners (WRP-05)

**How** (*archive*): Decriminalise the prostituted person, criminalise exploiters, fund exit services; meanwhile keep section 19 (barring temporary-visa holders from sex work) to deter trafficking.

Full text: [Prostitution Reform – Women's Rights Party](documents/proposal-prostitution.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/policy-proposal/prostitution/)); [Women's Rights Party Full Policy (Word document, 4 Nov 2025)](documents/full-policy-docx-2025.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/POLICY-Womens-Rights-Party-2025-041125.docx))

### Ban the 'rough sex' defence in murder and serious-harm cases (WRP-06)

**Why** (*archive*): The party opposes pornography involving strangulation and violence.

Full text: [“Rough Sex” murder defence – Women's Rights Party](documents/proposal-rough-sex-murder-defence.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/policy-proposal/rough-sex-murder-defence/)); [Women's Rights Party Full Policy (Word document, 4 Nov 2025)](documents/full-policy-docx-2025.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/POLICY-Womens-Rights-Party-2025-041125.docx))

### Add 'sex' to the Sentencing Act's aggravating factors instead of creating new hate-crime laws (WRP-08)

**How** (*archive*): Amend section 9(1)(h) to include sex, oppose 'gender identity' as an aggravating factor and oppose new hate-crime offences.

Full text: [Women's Rights Party Full Policy (Word document, 4 Nov 2025)](documents/full-policy-docx-2025.md) ([original](https://womensrightsparty.nz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/POLICY-Womens-Rights-Party-2025-041125.docx))
