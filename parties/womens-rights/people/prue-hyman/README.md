## Current status

**#5** on the Women's Rights Party list in 2026 (she was also #5 in 2023). Never an MP.

## Biography

Born 23 March 1943. A feminist economist with an MA from Oxford, where she campaigned for women's admission to the Oxford Union. She moved to New Zealand in 1969 and taught at Victoria University of Wellington, rising to associate professor of economics and gender & women's studies. She resigned when the university abolished gender and women's studies in a 2008–2010 restructuring. She wrote a 2000 report on the culture of the New Zealand Police and *Women and Economics* (2014), and played cricket for Middlesex and Wellington women's teams.

## Record & notable actions

- Has written widely on pay equity and living wages. She criticises the transgender rights movement and argues for classifying people by sex rather than gender.
