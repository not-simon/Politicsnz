## Current status

Founder, national secretary and co-leader of the Women's Rights Party, and **#1** on its 2026 list. Never an MP.

## Biography

A trade unionist. She was president of the Association of Staff in Tertiary Education, convened the CTU Women's Council, became northern secretary of the Service & Food Workers Union in 2006, and later co-led the midwives' union MERAS. Her partner is Len Richards.

## Political career

- Alliance candidate in Mount Albert against Helen Clark (1999, 2002). Alliance president from 2004, and co-leader and #1 on the list in 2005 (Manukau East).
- Left the Alliance in 2006 and joined Labour, serving as a regional representative on the Labour Party Council. Stood unsuccessfully for local boards as a Labour candidate (2013, 2022).
- Left Labour in 2023 over its support for transgender rights and founded the Women's Rights Party. It won 0.08% in 2023, and she came seventh in the 2023 Port Waikato by-election.

## Controversies

- Described by Wikipedia as an anti-transgender activist. In 2021 she opposed the Conversion Practices Prohibition Legislation Bill, arguing it would bar therapy that explores alternatives to gender transition for young people.
