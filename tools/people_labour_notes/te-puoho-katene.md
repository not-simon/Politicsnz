## Current status
Labour candidate for Te Tai Hauāuru, ranked 34 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography
Kātene (Ngāti Toa Rangatira, Ngāti Whātua, Ngāti Tama) was born and raised in Takapūwāhia, Porirua, and is a chief executive. His Labour profile says he studied at Stanford Business School, was a Fulbright Scholar and an Obama Foundation Leader, and works on Māori economic development and indigenous values in investment. ([Labour candidate profile](https://www.labour.org.nz/tepuohokatene), self-description; occupation also from the policy.nz questionnaire)
