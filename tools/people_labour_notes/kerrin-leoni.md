## Current status
On 22 October 2026 Te Ao Māori News released video of Leoni wearing Labour campaign branding at a Mongrel Mob gathering in Papakura on 3 October, where she urged attendees to vote ([Te Ao Māori News](https://www.teaonews.co.nz/2026/10/22/video-shows-labour-candidate-kerrin-leoni-at-mongrel-mob-event/)). She said it was a family function with relatives who are gang members and denied campaigning at a gang pad ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/politics_election-2026/2087989/labour-candidate-kerrin-leoni-clarifies-whanau-function-with-gang-members)).

Chris Hipkins initially accepted her account. On 23 October Leoni resigned as a Labour candidate, and Hipkins said the footage was "inconsistent with the standards" expected of Labour candidates. Because nominations had closed, her name remains on the ballot but without Labour's endorsement ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/politics/election-2026-live-updates-kerrin-leoni-resigns-as-labour-candidate-after-campaigning-with-mongrel-mob/R5IJCEB4TNF3LETYQ5JOSBPH7A/)). The sources consulted do not say how her party-list place (43) is affected.

## Biography
Leoni is of Ngāti Pāoa, Ngāi Takoto, Ngāti Kurī, Italian and Irish descent. She has a master's from AUT (2007) and one in economics and international politics from King's College London, and ran a contracting business during ten years in London ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/auckland-councillor-kerrin-leoni-first-out-of-the-blocks-to-express-interest-in-standing-for-mayor/4RA5YGHUHRAAJEYYZVXCCKBJDI/)).

## Political career
- Waitematā Local Board member (2019–2022) on the City Vision ticket.
- Labour candidate for Waikato in 2020 (list #66), losing to National's Tim van de Molen.
- Auckland councillor for Whau (2022–2025), the first Māori woman elected to Auckland Council ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/national/476911/auckland-s-first-wahine-maori-councillor-wants-mana-whenua-to-have-more-say)); ran for mayor in 2025 and lost to Wayne Brown.
- Selected in March 2026 for Tāmaki Makaurau against Te Pāti Māori's Oriini Kaipara ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/590449/labour-selects-kerrin-leoni-to-contest-for-tamaki-makaurau-seat)).

## Controversies
