## Current status
Labour list-only candidate, ranked 72 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography
Howe works in Māori media and te reo Māori revitalisation. His Labour profile says he presented the children's show *Pūkana*, co-founded the music group MAIMOA, and founded Mauri Media, whose productions include *Waiata Nation*.
