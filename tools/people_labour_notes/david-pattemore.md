## Current status
Labour candidate for Taranaki-King Country, ranked 71 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography
Pattemore lives in the Waikato. His Labour profile says he worked in conservation before completing a PhD in ecology and evolutionary biology at Princeton University and later worked on crop pollination; he volunteers with Sanctuary Mountain and a Waharoa community garden. ([Labour candidate profile](https://www.labour.org.nz/davidpattemore), self-description; occupation also from the policy.nz questionnaire)
