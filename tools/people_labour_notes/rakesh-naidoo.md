## Current status
Labour list-only candidate, ranked 13 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography
Naidoo was born and raised in South Africa under apartheid and moved to New Zealand more than 25 years ago. When selected he was a Police superintendent and the force's ethnic, iwi and community relations manager. He was appointed MNZM in 2023 for services to New Zealand Police and ethnic communities, and his governance roles have included NZ Football, Sport NZ and Gandhi Nivas ([Labour candidate profile](https://www.labour.org.nz/rakeshnaidoo)).

When Labour released its list on 8 June 2026, Police Commissioner Richard Chambers criticised Naidoo for not disclosing his intention to stand earlier, saying it was "untenable" for him to continue in a senior Police role. Chris Hipkins and Ginny Andersen defended his candidacy ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/597578/untenable-for-labour-s-new-candidate-to-continue-as-superintendent-police-commissioner)).

## Controversies
