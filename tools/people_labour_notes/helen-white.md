## Current status
White is No. 29 in the shadow cabinet and is defending Mt Albert at No. 38 on the list ([policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/party-vote/parties/labour-party)).

## Biography
White grew up in Kawerau and Freemans Bay, Auckland, and is an employment-law barrister who early in her career worked at the EPMU alongside Andrew Little ([Ponsonby News](http://www.ponsonbynews.co.nz/article-detail/show/511/)). She has three children.

## Political career
- Lost the 2009 Mt Albert by-election selection to David Shearer; contested Auckland Central in 2017 and 2020, losing in 2020 to Green MP Chlöe Swarbrick by 1,068 votes, and entered Parliament on the list in 2020 ([Newshub](https://www.newshub.co.nz/home/politics/2020/07/chloe-swarbrick-defends-herself-from-labour-s-auckland-central-candidate-helen-white.html)).
- **Mt Albert MP since 2023:** selected to succeed Jacinda Ardern (over Camilla Belich); held the seat by 18 votes after National's Melissa Lee sought a recount — down from Ardern's 21,000+ majority in 2020 ([1News](https://www.1news.co.nz/2023/11/15/labours-mt-albert-margin-shrinks-to-18-votes-after-recount/)).

## Controversies
- Criticised in 2020 for dismissing Swarbrick as a "celebrity" candidate ([Newshub](https://www.newshub.co.nz/home/politics/2020/07/chloe-swarbrick-defends-herself-from-labour-s-auckland-central-candidate-helen-white.html)).
