## Current status
Labour candidate for East Coast Bays, ranked 69 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography
McEnroe is a technology entrepreneur originally from Yorkshire, England. His Labour profile says he has more than 30 years' experience in digital transformation across fintech, healthcare, aviation and supply chains, including digital trade infrastructure for exporters.
