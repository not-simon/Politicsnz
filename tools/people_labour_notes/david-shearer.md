## Current status
Shearer left Parliament in December 2016 when UN Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon appointed him head of the UN Mission in South Sudan, a post he held until January 2021 ([UN](https://www.un.org/press/en/2016/sga1691.doc.htm)). Lincoln University awarded him an honorary doctorate in 2023. No reliable source was found for his role in 2026; he is not a candidate.

## Biography
Born 28 July 1957 and raised in Papatoetoe (head boy of Papatoetoe High School), Shearer holds a BSc (Auckland) and MSc (Hons) in resource management (Canterbury) and taught at Massey and Onehunga high schools. He spent nearly 20 years in humanitarian and UN work — Save the Children in Somalia and Rwanda, UN OCHA in the Balkans and Jerusalem, and as UN Deputy Special Representative for Iraq (2007) — and was named New Zealander of the Year with his wife Anuschka Meyer in 1992 and appointed MBE in 1993 ([NZ Herald](http://www.nzherald.co.nz/politics/news/article.cfm?c_id=280&objectid=10568104)).

## Political career
- **Mt Albert MP 2009–2016:** won the June 2009 by-election to replace Helen Clark (majority 9,718 over National's Melissa Lee), increasing it in 2011 and 2014 ([2014](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2014/electorate-27.html)).
- **Labour leader and Leader of the Opposition, 13 Dec 2011 – 15 Sep 2013:** won the caucus vote over David Cunliffe; struggled with low public recognition and caucus dissent, and resigned on 22 August 2013 saying he no longer had the full confidence of colleagues ([Stuff](http://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/9074568/David-Shearer-quits)).
- Foreign affairs spokesperson under Cunliffe and Little.

## Controversies
- **Undeclared bank account (2013):** admitted not declaring a US bank account holding his UN salary, saying he had forgotten ([3 News](http://www.3news.co.nz/Banks-calls-for-Shearers-resignation/tabid/1607/articleID/290920/Default.aspx)).
- **"Man ban" (2013):** a proposal for women-only selection shortlists drew backlash; Shearer opposed it and it was dropped ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/8894880/Labours-man-ban-canned)).
- A 2013 stunt holding two dead snapper in Parliament was poorly received shortly before his resignation ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/shearer-nets-a-few-jokes-with-snapper-stunt/J5XSIRIK5H7DJW4P65EQUTJDVA/)).
