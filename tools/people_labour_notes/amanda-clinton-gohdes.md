## Current status
Labour candidate for New Plymouth, ranked 59 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography
Clinton-Gohdes served two terms on the New Plymouth District Council. Her Labour profile describes a background in science, law, education and local government; it says she was raised by a solo mother and lives in Taranaki with her husband and two sons.
