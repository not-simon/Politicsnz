## Current status
Tangaere-Manuel is No. 20 in the shadow cabinet, Labour's assistant whip since March 2025, and since March 2026 emergency management and Natural Hazards Commission spokesperson alongside forestry, Māori economy and sport ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/politics/360949687/hipkins-reshuffles-labour-portfolios-henare-departs-and-plan-hatched-maori-seats-race)). She is defending Ikaroa-Rāwhiti — Labour's only Māori electorate — at No. 9 on the list ([policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/party-vote/parties/labour-party)).

## Biography
Born in Te Puia Springs and raised in Tikitiki (Ngāti Porou), she was head girl of Ngata Memorial College ([The Spinoff](https://thespinoff.co.nz/politics/10-10-2023/cushla-tangaere-manuel-can-sing-but-are-the-voters-listening)). She was a broadcaster at TVNZ and Whakaata Māori (including *Marae* and presenting *Māorioke*), then for nine years chief executive of the East Coast Rugby Football Union (to 2022), and later New Zealand Rugby's Māori rugby programme manager ([NPEC](https://npec.co.nz/wp/2022/01/06/tangaere-manuel-npecs-longest-serving-ceo-farewelled/)).

## Political career
- **Ikaroa-Rāwhiti MP since 2023:** selected after Meka Whaitiri defected, she stood electorate-only and defeated Whaitiri (by then Te Pāti Māori) by about 2,800 votes — one of only two new Labour MPs elected in 2023 ([2023 results](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-67.html); [RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/500285/devastated-labour-mps-prepare-for-spell-in-political-wilderness)).
- Spokesperson for tourism, forestry and cyclone recovery (2023); member of Hipkins' economic team from March 2025.
