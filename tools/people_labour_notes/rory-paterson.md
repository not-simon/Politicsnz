## Current status
Labour candidate for West Coast-Tasman, ranked 54 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography
Paterson is a West Coaster and a father of four. His Labour profile says he joined Labour at 15 and has worked as a funeral director, primary school teacher, deputy principal and in tertiary education, with more than a decade in school governance. ([Labour candidate profile](https://www.labour.org.nz/rorypaterson), self-description; occupation also from the policy.nz questionnaire)
