## Current status
Labour candidate for Waiariki, ranked 44 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography
Boynton (Ngāi Tūhoe, Tamakaimoana, Ngāti Awa, Te Rarawa, Ngāti Kahu) is a Māori ward councillor on the Whakatāne District Council. Her Labour profile says she has worked in social services, housing and community development. ([Labour candidate profile](https://www.labour.org.nz/toniboynton), self-description; occupation also from the policy.nz questionnaire)
