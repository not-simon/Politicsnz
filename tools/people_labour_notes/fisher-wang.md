## Current status
Labour candidate for Rotorua, ranked 68 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography
Wang was born and raised in Rotorua. His Labour profile says that when first elected to the Rotorua Lakes Council in 2019 he was the youngest councillor in the district's history, and that he topped the poll in 2022 and 2025. ([Labour candidate profile](https://www.labour.org.nz/fisherwang), self-description; occupation also from the policy.nz questionnaire)
