## Current status
Labour candidate for Whangaparāoa, ranked 47 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography
Muller Pallarès grew up on the Hibiscus Coast and attended Gulf Harbour School and Whangaparāoa College. Her Labour profile describes her as an environmental educator who works with schools, marae, community organisations and businesses on sustainable development. ([Labour candidate profile](https://www.labour.org.nz/estefaniamullerpallarès), self-description; occupation also from the policy.nz questionnaire)
