## Current status
Henare announced on 3 February 2026 that he would not seek the Tāmaki Makaurau nomination or a list place and would leave Parliament; his resignation took effect on 15 March 2026 and Dan Rosewarne took his list seat ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/politics/waitangi-2026-senior-labour-mp-peeni-henare-leaving-the-party-report/ZAWGQIRAHNHCPMZZW6CXCSKTWY/); [NZ Gazette](https://gazette.govt.nz/notice/id/2026-ps1387)). In his valedictory speech he urged MPs to move away from "gotcha-style politics" ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/589302/peeni-henare-tells-mps-to-move-away-from-gotcha-style-politics-in-valedictory-speech)). In July 2026 New Zealand Rugby appointed him Kaihautū Māori ([Te Ao Māori News](https://www.teaonews.co.nz/2026/07/23/former-minister-peeni-henare-appointed-kaihautu-maori-for-new-zealand-rugby/)).

## Biography
Henare (Ngāti Hine) is a great-grandson of Taurekareka Hēnare (MP for Northern Maori 1914–1938) and son of Māori Language Commission head Erima Henare; former MP Tau Henare is his second cousin ([Northern Advocate](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/northern-advocate/news/grandson-aims-to-keep-parliament-seat-in-family/BTMULIR4F6LCJ3EGMT6DSKINIE/)). His partner is Skye Kimura, former chief executive of consultancy Tātou.

## Political career
- **Tāmaki Makaurau MP 2014–2023:** won in 2014; held it in 2017 and narrowly in 2020 (927 after a recount requested by Te Pāti Māori's John Tamihere); lost it in 2023 to Te Pāti Māori's Takutai Tarsh Kemp by 42 votes (confirmed by a judicial recount) and returned on the list ([1News](https://www.1news.co.nz/2023/11/15/takutai-kemp-extends-lead-in-tamaki-makaurau-recount/)). Lost the September 2025 Tāmaki Makaurau by-election (after Kemp's death) to Te Pāti Māori's Oriini Kaipara, 3,093 votes to 6,031 on preliminary results ([1News](https://www.1news.co.nz/2025/09/06/oriini-kaipara-wins-tamaki-makaurau-byelection/)).
- **Minister 2017–2023:** Whānau Ora (2017–2023), Civil Defence (2019–2020), Defence (2020–2023, in Cabinet), associate health (Māori), housing and tourism; from January 2023 ACC, Tourism and Environment ([DPMC 2020](https://dpmc.govt.nz/sites/default/files/2020-11/ministerial-list-2-nov-2020.pdf)).
- **Opposition:** defence, sport, associate health; later economic development and Māori–Crown relations, and briefly foreign affairs after David Parker's retirement (2025).

## Record & notable actions
- In November 2022 became the first New Zealand Cabinet minister to visit Ukraine after the Russian invasion ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/politics/peeni-henare-becomes-first-minister-to-visit-ukraine-since-conflict/PBWO6GRR7BE5ROICB6PNK5MI4Y/)).
- Member's bill to ban imports made with slave labour (defeated at first reading, 2016).

## Controversies
- **Tātou contracts (2023):** National sought a review of about $600,000 of government contracts awarded to his partner's firm; Henare had declared the interest in 2018. A 2024 PwC review found the Ministry of Health had not properly managed conflicts of interest on its contracts ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/national/508184/health-ministry-admits-failings-in-handling-contracts-it-awarded-to-firm-with-links-to-peeni-henare)).
- **"Gun" metaphor (Feb 2024):** a te reo speech line "I lift my gun, and I let the shots do the talking" drew ACT criticism; he and Hipkins said it was figurative ([Newshub](https://www.newshub.co.nz/home/politics/2024/02/chris-hipkins-defends-peeni-henare-s-use-of-gun-metaphor-at-waitangi.html)).
- **Treaty Principles Bill haka (Nov 2024):** referred to the Privileges Committee for joining the haka that halted the vote; the committee found his conduct disorderly but not contempt ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/547574/labour-s-peeni-henare-not-in-contempt-over-disorderly-actions-during-treaty-principles-reading)).
- **Gang patches (Aug 2025):** in a by-election debate backed repealing the gang-patch ban, contradicting party policy; Carmel Sepuloni said he was mistaken ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/politics/labours-peeni-henare-mistaken-to-claim-party-would-repeal-gang-patch-ban-says-deputy-leader/M3Y75EETSNEFNFIKD67DEQ7AY4/)).
