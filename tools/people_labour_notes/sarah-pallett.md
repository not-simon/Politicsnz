## Current status
Pallett lost Ilam in 2023 to National's Hamish Campbell, finishing third behind TOP's Raf Manji ([Electoral Commission](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-15.html)). No reliable source was found for her activities in 2026.

## Biography
Born in Jersey, Channel Islands, Pallett was a midwife for ten years and then a midwifery lecturer at Ara Institute of Canterbury, where she was president of the academic staff union.

## Political career
- **MP for Ilam 2020–2023**, unseating National's Gerry Brownlee by 3,463 votes ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/123128975/election-2020-sarah-pallett-promises-more-inclusive-style-after-defeating-gerry-brownlee-in-ilam)).
- Member of the Health Committee; deputy chair of the Petitions Committee.
