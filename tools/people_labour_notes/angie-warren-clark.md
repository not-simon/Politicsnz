## Current status
Warren-Clark lost Whangārei to National's Shane Reti in 2023 and was too low on the list to return ([Electoral Commission](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-64.html)). No reliable source was found for her activities in 2026.

## Biography
Born in 1971, Warren-Clark holds a Waikato law degree (admitted 1998) and managed Tauranga Women's Refuge, criticising government funding of the refuge as "appalling" ([NZ Herald](http://www.nzherald.co.nz/politics/news/article.cfm?c_id=280&objectid=11929498)).

## Political career
- **List MP 2017–2023**, entering on special votes in 2017; lost Bay of Plenty to Todd Muller in 2017 and 2020 (by 3,415 votes).
