## Current status
Ngobi lost Ōtaki to National's Tim Costley by 6,271 votes in 2023 ([Electoral Commission](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-34.html)). No reliable source was found for her activities in 2026.

## Biography
Born in Levin, of Samoan and Scottish descent, Ngobi worked for 16 years in government and non-profit organisations before entering Parliament ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/300135120/election-2020-labours-taki-candidate-takes-the-seat-off-national)).

## Political career
- **MP for Ōtaki 2020–2023**, defeating Tim Costley by 2,988 votes in 2020 ([Electoral Commission](https://electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2020/electorate-details-34.html)).
