## Current status
Lubeck announced in December 2022 that she would not contest 2023, citing health, travel and family ([1News](https://www.1news.co.nz/2022/12/13/six-labour-mps-including-david-clark-to-retire-at-election/)). No reliable source was found for her activities in 2026.

## Biography
Born in the Netherlands in 1965, Lubeck emigrated in 1989, became an Air New Zealand flight attendant and was president of the flight attendants' union FARSA from 2009, leading its merger into E tū. She qualified in law at AUT (2014).

## Political career
- **List MP 2017–2023**; contested Rodney (2017) and Kaipara ki Mahurangi (2020).
- Chaired the Education and Workforce Committee (2020–2023).

## Record & notable actions
- Drafted a 2018 member's bill to ban conversion therapy; Labour adopted the policy, leading to the Conversion Practices Prohibition Legislation Act 2022 ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/labour-mp-lodges-members-bill-which-would-ban-controversial-conversion-therapy/7POOJHBPAXI4EE6AVFVRQIRG4E/)).
