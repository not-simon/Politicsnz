## Current status
Labour candidate for Tāmaki, ranked 29 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography
Harris is a lawyer, campaigner and writer. His Labour profile says he was a Rhodes Scholar who studied at the University of Oxford and appears regularly in the courts, including the Supreme Court; he has opened a community office in Glen Innes. ([Labour candidate profile](https://www.labour.org.nz/maxharris), self-description; occupation also from the policy.nz questionnaire)
