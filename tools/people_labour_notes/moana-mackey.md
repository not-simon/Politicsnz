## Current status
Mackey was not returned in 2014 and declined to re-enter Parliament in 2017 ([NZ Herald](http://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/news/article.cfm?c_id=1&objectid=11804547)). Since 2015 she has held advisory roles in Wellington City Council's city planning team ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/dominion-post/news/98153727/councils-our-city-tomorrow-set-to-tackle-future-challenges-head-on)).

## Biography
Born 28 February 1974 in Auckland and raised in Gisborne, Mackey has a biochemistry degree from Victoria University and worked as an environmental laboratory scientist. She was Young Labour president (1999–2000). She and her mother Janet Mackey were the first mother–daughter pair to sit in Parliament together ([NZ History](https://nzhistory.govt.nz/page/moana-mackey-joins-mother-janet-parliament)).

## Political career
- **List MP 2003–2014**, entering on Graham Kelly's departure; lost East Coast to National's Anne Tolley in 2005 (by 1,219 votes) and 2008 (by 6,413).
