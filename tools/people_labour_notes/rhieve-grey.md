## Current status
Labour candidate for Mt Maunganui, ranked 65 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography
Grey (Ngāti Tūwharetoa, Ngāti Porou, Ngāti Raukawa, Te Arawa) is a Rhodes Scholar with postgraduate degrees in public policy from the University of Oxford. His Labour profile says he co-founded the History of Aotearoa Charitable Trust, is associate director of Tupu Angitū (the commercial arm of the Lake Taupō Forest Trust), and was one of the New Zealanders chosen to attend King Charles III's coronation. ([Labour candidate profile](https://www.labour.org.nz/rhievegrey), self-description; occupation also from the policy.nz questionnaire)
