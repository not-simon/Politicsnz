## Current status
Moroney became chief executive of Community Law Centres Aotearoa in 2018 and stood for Hamilton City Council's East ward in the 2025 local elections ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/568837/two-former-labour-mps-vie-for-seats-on-hamilton-city-council)); the result is not recorded in the sources consulted.

## Biography
Born 8 May 1964 and raised on a Waikato farm (Walton), Moroney was a journalist and worked for the NZ Nurses Organisation for 12 years. Her brother Michael Moroney is a Melbourne Cup-winning racehorse trainer.

## Political career
- **List MP 2005–2017** (contested Piako and Hamilton East); junior government whip (2007–2008); chief whip under David Cunliffe (2013); shadow cabinet under Andrew Little (transport, 2015).
- Announced in April 2017 she would not stand after Labour's council denied her a winnable list position ([Stuff](http://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/92061647/labour-mp-sue-moroney-to-step-down-at-2017-election)).

## Record & notable actions
- Her member's bill on meal and rest breaks underpinned the Employment Relations (Breaks and Infant Feeding) Act 2008.
- **Paid parental leave:** her bills to extend paid parental leave to six months were repeatedly blocked — defeated on a 60–60 vote, and a revived version passed its first reading 61–60 in September 2015 against the National government's opposition ([Stuff](http://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/66673532/paid-parental-leave-bill-finally-defeated)).
