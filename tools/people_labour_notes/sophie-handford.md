## Current status
Handford was selected in November 2025 as Labour's candidate for Kāpiti ([The Post](https://www.thepost.co.nz/politics/360891809/climate-activist-turned-councillor-named-labours-kapiti-candidate)) and is ranked 26. On policy.nz she lists her occupation as NZ Nature Fund.

## Biography
Born in 2000 or 2001, Handford grew up on the Kāpiti Coast and was head girl of Kāpiti College in 2018. In 2018–2019 she founded School Strike 4 Climate NZ, whose 27 September 2019 nationwide strike drew an estimated 170,000 people ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2019/9/27/thousands-in-new-zealand-kick-start-new-wave-of-climate-protests)). Her awards include the 2019 Wellingtonian of the Year supreme award (shared) and a 2023 Prime Minister's Scholarship for Asia.

## Political career
- Elected to the Kāpiti Coast District Council (Paekākāriki–Raumati ward) at 18 in 2019 ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/kapiti-news/news/18-year-old-wins-seat-on-kapiti-coast-district-council/3Q3TLN6NBPC3FWGH6M4UQOULSA/)); re-elected 2022; chaired the Strategy, Operations and Finance Committee; did not stand in 2025.
- Youth MP for Kris Faafoi in 2019.
