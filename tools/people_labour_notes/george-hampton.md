## Current status
Labour candidate for Christchurch Central, ranked 52 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography
Hampton was born and raised in Christchurch. His Labour profile says he has worked on climate and energy at the United Nations and as a New Zealand diplomat, and is co-owner of Mr Whippy New Zealand. (He is not the subject of the Wikipedia article "George Hampton".)
