## Current status
McAnulty is Labour's Shadow Leader of the House and its housing, infrastructure and public investment spokesperson (No. 7 in the shadow cabinet), and is contesting Wairarapa again at No. 10 on the list ([policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/party-vote/parties/labour-party)). His member's bill allowing on-licence premises open on Good Friday, Anzac Day morning and Christmas Day to sell alcohol under normal conditions passed its third reading 66–55 on a conscience vote on 1 April 2026 ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/591281/bill-to-ease-holiday-alcohol-restrictions-passes-final-reading-in-parliament)).

## Biography
Born in Eketāhuna in 1985 and adopted into a long-established Wairarapa Labour family, McAnulty was head boy of Chanel College (2002) and gained a BA, PGDipArts and an MA in politics from the University of Otago ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/politics/kieran-mcanulty-why-the-wairarapa-mp-is-rural-to-the-core/SVEH6NYJJZHJNFPGBO4CSZF7PU/)). He worked as a TAB bookmaker and an economic development adviser at Masterton District Council, and was a volunteer firefighter ([maiden statement](https://www.parliament.nz/en/pb/hansard-debates/rhr/document/HansS_20171116_064200000/mcanulty-kieran)). He was treasurer of the New Zealand Republic campaign. In January 2024 he married Gia Garrick, a former press secretary to the prime minister. His battered red Mazda ute became a campaign trademark until he sold it for charity in 2021 ([1News](https://www.1news.co.nz/2021/09/19/labours-mcanulty-swapping-famous-red-ute-for-hybrid-wagon/)).

## Political career
- **Wairarapa:** lost to National's Alastair Scott in 2014 and 2017 (entering Parliament from list position 38 in 2017); won the seat in 2020 by 6,545 votes over Mike Butterick; lost it to Butterick in 2023 and returned on the list ([2020](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2020/electorate-details-58.html); [2023](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-58.html)).
- **Whip:** junior whip (2017–2020), chief whip (2020–2022) ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/hawkes-bay-today/news/wairarapa-mp-elected-chief-government-whip/YNOLUT76J4HXT2JUW4H27U3X44/)).
- **Minister, 2022–2023:** Minister for Emergency Management and Racing (outside Cabinet) from June 2022; in Cabinet from 31 January 2023 as Minister of Local Government, Rural Communities and Deputy Leader of the House; Regional Development from July 2023 ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/131110270/prime-minister-chris-hipkins-reveals-his-new-cabinet)).
- **Opposition:** No. 7 in the shadow cabinet, with housing, local government and regional development (2023); Shadow Leader of the House; from March 2025 housing, infrastructure and public investment.

## Record & notable actions
- **Cyclone Gabrielle:** declared a national state of emergency on 14 February 2023 — only the third in New Zealand's history ([Beehive](https://www.beehive.govt.nz/release/state-national-emergency-declared)).
- **Three Waters reset (April 2023):** restructured the reform into ten regional water entities while keeping the council/mana whenua co-governance model ([Beehive](https://www.beehive.govt.nz/release/major-shakeup-will-see-affordable-water-reforms-led-and-delivered-regionally)); the reforms were repealed by the next government.
- Led the Local Electoral (Advertising) Amendment Act 2022 through the House.
- **Conscience votes:** supports marriage equality, voted for the Abortion Legislation Act 2020, and voted yes in both 2020 referendums (cannabis and euthanasia) ([Parliament Hansard](https://www.parliament.nz/en/pb/hansard-debates/rhr/combined/HansDeb_20200318_20200318_24)).
- **Member's bill:** his bill easing alcohol trading restrictions on Good Friday, Anzac Day and Christmas Day (excluding bottle stores and supermarkets) became law with royal assent on 2 April 2026 ([ODT](https://www.odt.co.nz/news/dunedin/mixed-response-alcohol-act-changes)).

## Controversies
- **Gaurav Sharma allegations (2022):** expelled MP Gaurav Sharma accused McAnulty, then chief whip, of bullying; Labour and Ardern rejected the claims ([The Spinoff](https://thespinoff.co.nz/live-updates/12-08-2022)).
- **Accommodation allowance (2024):** The Post reported he claimed a $36,400-a-year allowance to rent a Lower Hutt flat from his wife while owning a home in Masterton ([The Post](https://www.thepost.co.nz/politics/350305871/labours-kieran-mcanulty-claims-housing-perk-live-wifes-apartment)).
- **Ejections from the House:** in 2019 (mocking Simon Bridges) and on 19 February 2026, after accusing Speaker Gerry Brownlee of "double standards" over Winston Peters' remarks about the name Aotearoa ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/politics/labour-mp-kieran-mcanulty-booted-from-the-house-over-speakers-handling-of-winston-peters-racist-remark/MR2AVPBXJBHCJH7AFODN3MICJA/)).
