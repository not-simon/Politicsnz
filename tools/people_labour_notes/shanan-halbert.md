## Current status
Halbert holds tertiary education and (since March 2026) Whānau Ora (No. 28 in the shadow cabinet) and is contesting Northcote again at No. 19 on the list ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/politics/360949687/hipkins-reshuffles-labour-portfolios-henare-departs-and-plan-hatched-maori-seats-race); [policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/party-vote/parties/labour-party)).

## Biography
Born 24 April 1982 (Rongowhakaata, Ngāti Whitikaupeka) and raised in Napier, Halbert has a BA in education and Māori from the University of Auckland. He worked at Glenfield College (setting up a Health Sciences Academy) and Hato Petera College, and was head of relationships at Te Wānanga o Aotearoa ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/300131313/election-2020-labours-shanan-halbert-takes-northcote-seat-from-national)).

## Political career
- Lost Northcote in 2017 and the 2018 by-election (to National's Dan Bidois); **Northcote MP 2020–2023** (won by 2,534; lost to Bidois by 9,270 in 2023) ([2020](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2020/electorate-details-31.html); [2023](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-31.html)).
- Returned as a list MP in February 2024 after Kelvin Davis resigned ([NZ Gazette](https://gazette.govt.nz/notice/id/2024-au488)); spokesperson for Auckland and Rainbow issues, then tertiary education.

## Controversies
- **Campaign flyers:** Labour apologised for a 2020 flyer claiming 600 homes built in Northcote when about 74 had been; a 2023 complaint over housing claims was dismissed by the Advertising Standards Authority ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/300969021/advertising-watchdog-dismisses-complaint-against-labour-northcote-mp)).
- **Staff allegations (Sept 2023):** former staff alleged bullying; he denied it and said no formal complaints had been made ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/politics/election-2023-labour-mp-shanan-halbert-rocked-by-bullying-allegations/XOHX4DUHSRHY3AD6JJ5HHZ2DYA/)).
