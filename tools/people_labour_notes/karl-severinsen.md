## Current status
Labour candidate for Rangitīkei, ranked 66 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography
Severinsen grew up in rural Hawke's Bay in a single-parent home. His Labour profile says he was an officer in the NZ Army before training in social work, has 23 years' experience in youth justice, and is a general manager in mental health; he lives in Manawatū. ([Labour candidate profile](https://www.labour.org.nz/2026-campaign/our-candidates/karl-severinsen/), self-description; occupation also from the policy.nz questionnaire)
