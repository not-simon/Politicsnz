## Current status
Labour candidate for Port Waikato (electorate only, not on the party list) at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography
Brachi is a high school teacher in South Auckland and sits on a school board. His Labour profile says he worked previously in the fuel industry, newspaper advertising and technology media, and as a film and television special-effects artist, before retraining as a teacher 13 years ago. ([Labour candidate profile](https://www.labour.org.nz/mattbrachi), self-description; occupation also from the policy.nz questionnaire)
