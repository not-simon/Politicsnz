## Current status
Sharma returned to medicine after his 2022 by-election defeat and opened a general practice in Frankton, Hamilton in 2024 ([Waikato Times](https://www.waikatotimes.co.nz/nz-news/360499557/gaurav-sharma-doctor-general-practice-not-house)). He is not a 2026 candidate.

## Biography
Born in 1987 in Himachal Pradesh, India, Sharma moved to New Zealand at 12, attended Auckland Grammar School, and graduated MBChB from the University of Auckland (2011). He interned at the WHO, worked in Auckland hospitals, and completed an MBA at George Washington University as a Fulbright scholar (2015–2017) before working as a GP in Hamilton ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/129561032/gaurav-sharma-the-political-rookie-who-struck-out-at-his-own-party)).

## Political career
- Lost Hamilton West in 2017; won it in 2020 with 52% of the vote — Labour's first electorate MP of Indian origin; took his oath in te reo Māori and Sanskrit ([2020 results](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2020/electorate-details-13.html)).
- **Expulsion:** in August 2022 he published allegations of bullying by Labour whips and Parliamentary Service; Labour's caucus suspended him (16 Aug) and then expelled him from caucus (23 Aug); the party's NZ Council expelled him from Labour on 20 October 2022 for bringing the party into disrepute ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/473325/hamilton-west-mp-gaurav-sharma-expelled-from-labour-caucus); [RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/477028/gaurav-sharma-expelled-from-labour-party)).
- Resigned from Parliament on 18 October 2022, triggering the 10 December 2022 Hamilton West by-election; launched the unregistered New Zealand Momentum Party and finished fourth with 1,242 votes; National's Tama Potaka won ([Electoral Commission](https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2022/hamilton-west-by-election-official-results/)).

## Controversies
- **Bullying allegations (2022):** his claims against whips Duncan Webb and Kieran McAnulty, the PM's office and the Parliamentary Service were disputed by Ardern, Labour MPs and the Parliamentary Service; The Spinoff noted he produced little evidence. Former staff said they had felt bullied by him and that three staff had left his office in his first year; he said they had performance issues ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/parliament-bullying-claims-labour-mp-gaurav-sharmas-ex-staffer-speaks-out-after-being-left-in-tears/6Y6DUYVDRNWS6DPEVT7B4JT24Q/); [The Spinoff](https://thespinoff.co.nz/live-updates/20-10-2022/gaurav-sharma-expelled-from-labour-party)).
- He accused Ardern of lying and released a secret recording of a call with a Labour MP ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/473116/jacinda-ardern-is-lying-labour-mp-gaurav-sharma-questions-pm-s-credibility)).
