## Current status
Labour candidate for Rangitata, ranked 64 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography
Malama is a Tokelauan–Tongan woman raised in Timaru. Her Labour profile says she is a school attendance officer, directs the region's Pasifika Festival, and has worked in factories, on fishing boats and in cleaning. ([Labour candidate profile](https://www.labour.org.nz/sangemalama), self-description; occupation also from the policy.nz questionnaire)
