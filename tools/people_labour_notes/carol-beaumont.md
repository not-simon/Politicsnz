## Current status
Beaumont was elected Labour Party vice-president in 2021, succeeding Tracey McLellan ([Labour](https://www.labour.org.nz/party_info)), and was appointed ONZM in 2021 for services to the union movement and women's rights ([DPMC](https://dpmc.govt.nz/publications/queens-birthday-honours-list-2021)). Whether she still holds a party office in October 2026 was not confirmed from the sources consulted.

## Biography
Born 6 October 1960, Beaumont worked for the NZ University Students' Association, the NZ Nurses Organisation and the Council of Trade Unions, where she was secretary from 2003 to 2008.

## Political career
- **List MP 2008–2011 and 2013–2014**, returning in March 2013 on Charles Chauvel's resignation ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/130264/carol-beaumont-takes-a-new-seat-in-parlliament)); lost Maungakiekie to National's Sam Lotu-Iiga in 2008, 2011 and 2014.
- Spokesperson for consumer affairs, then women's affairs.

## Record & notable actions
- Took over the Credit Reforms (Responsible Lending) Bill, which was defeated at first reading in July 2010.
