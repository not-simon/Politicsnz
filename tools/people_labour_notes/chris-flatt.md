## Current status
Labour candidate for Taupō, ranked 20 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography
Flatt has been national secretary of the NZ Dairy Workers Union Te Rūnanga Waiū since 2012. His Labour profile says he is deputy chair of Trust Waikato and has 20 years of union leadership and primary-industry experience. ([Labour candidate profile](https://www.labour.org.nz/chrisflatt), self-description; occupation also from the policy.nz questionnaire)
