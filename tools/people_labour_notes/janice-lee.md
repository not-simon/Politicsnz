## Current status
Labour candidate for Invercargill, ranked 62 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography
Lee (Ngāi Tahu, Kāti Māmoe, Waitaha, Ngāti Porou) founded Koha Kai, an Invercargill social enterprise that provides vocational training for people with disabilities. Her Labour profile says she chairs Te Kāwai Trust, which is developing housing for kaumātua and tāngata whaikaha, and is a member of the Invercargill Chamber of Commerce. ([Labour candidate profile](https://www.labour.org.nz/2026-campaign/our-candidates/janice-lee/), self-description; occupation also from the policy.nz questionnaire)
