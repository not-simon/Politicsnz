## Current status
Roberts is Labour's 2026 candidate for Whanganui, ranked 46 (policy.nz candidate data).

## Biography
A teacher of economics and drama for 20 years, including at Stratford High School, Roberts was president of the PPTA (2013–2017), clashing with Education Minister Hekia Parata over Novopay and charter schools ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/417455/former-ppta-president-angela-roberts-to-run-for-labour)).

## Political career
- **List MP 2020–2023** (ranked 50); lost Taranaki-King Country to National's Barbara Kuriger in 2020 (by 3,134 votes) and 2023 (by 14,355).
- Deputy chair of the Primary Production Committee (2023).

## Record & notable actions
- Was slapped and shaken by a man after a campaign meeting in Inglewood in September 2023, and spoke about growing aggression in politics ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/499039/completely-unacceptable-labour-candidate-angela-roberts-slapped-following-political-debate)).
