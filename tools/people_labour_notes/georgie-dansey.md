## Current status
Dansey entered Parliament on 9 February 2026 when Adrian Rurawhe retired and became rainbow issues and regulation spokesperson in March 2026 ([NZ Gazette](https://gazette.govt.nz/notice/id/2026-au491); [Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/politics/360949687/hipkins-reshuffles-labour-portfolios-henare-departs-and-plan-hatched-maori-seats-race)). She is Labour's Hamilton East candidate at No. 33 ([policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/party-vote/parties/labour-party)).

## Biography
Born in 1986 (Ngāti Tūwharetoa), Dansey was a teacher and education-sector unionist, chief executive of the Independent Schools Education Association, and owns a Te Awamutu fitness business ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/130337901/labour-selects-georgie-dansey-to-run-in-hamilton-west-byelection)). She and her partner Ruby have three children; she identifies as queer ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/politics/360927759/who-labours-soon-be-new-list-mp-georgie-dansey)). Her great-uncle Harry Dansey was Race Relations Conciliator.

## Political career
- Last-ranked Labour list candidate in 2020; lost the December 2022 Hamilton West by-election to National's Tama Potaka; contested Hamilton East in 2023 (list No. 31, not elected) and was then elected to Labour's policy council ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/politics/hamilton-west-byelection-nationals-tama-potaka-wins-seat-flip-a-blow-for-government/ZUJTXCVBWZG27LF5FOHZOQW2NE/)).

## Controversies
- A day after her 2022 by-election selection she was seen at a union protest that ambushed Labour minister Andrew Little; she said she attended as a union representative and left when it became an ambush ([Newshub](https://www.newshub.co.nz/home/politics/2022/11/labour-hamilton-west-by-election-candidate-georgie-dansey-denies-protesting-labour-minister-andrew-little-day-after-selection.html)).
