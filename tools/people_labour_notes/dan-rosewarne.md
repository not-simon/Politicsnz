## Current status
Rosewarne re-entered Parliament when Peeni Henare resigned (declared elected 16 March 2026) and holds rural communities and small business (No. 33 in the shadow cabinet) ([NZ Gazette](https://gazette.govt.nz/notice/id/2026-au1406)). He is contesting Waimakariri for the fourth time, at No. 36 on the list ([policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/party-vote/parties/labour-party)).

## Biography
Born in 1981 and raised in Wellington (of Samoan ancestry), Rosewarne trained as a mechanic and joined the Army in 1999, serving in Afghanistan (2005, 2012) and the Solomon Islands (2008) and in the Kaikōura earthquake response; he was commissioned in 2017 and retired as a captain in 2022 ([Newsroom](https://www.newsroom.co.nz/from-military-combat-to-parliament-meet-labours-working-class-mp)). He survived leukaemia in 2014 with state-funded treatment, which he credits for bringing him to Labour. He lives in Waimakariri with his wife and two children.

## Political career
- Lost Waimakariri to National's Matt Doocey in 2017, 2020 (cutting the majority to 1,507) and 2023.
- List MP July 2022 – October 2023 after Kris Faafoi resigned; not re-elected from list position 32 in 2023.

## Controversies
- After losing in 2023 he publicly lamented losing MP perks such as Koru Club access, drawing criticism from breakfast-TV hosts ([Newshub](https://www.newshub.co.nz/home/politics/2023/10/election-2023-am-hosts-slam-bizarre-reactions-from-ousted-labour-mps-jan-tinetti-dan-rosewarne.html)).
