## Current status
Lewis lost Whanganui to National's Carl Bates by 5,512 votes in 2023 ([Electoral Commission](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-62.html)). No reliable source was found for her activities in 2026.

## Biography
Raised in Whanganui and Waverley, Lewis studied law at Victoria University and worked at ACC (as a union delegate) and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

## Political career
- **MP for Whanganui 2020–2023**, defeating National's Harete Hipango by 8,191 votes ([Electoral Commission](https://electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2020/electorate-details-62.html)).

## Record & notable actions
- In February 2022, while pregnant, she was harassed by anti-mandate protesters outside Parliament, who threatened to lynch or kidnap her ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/covid-19-convoy-protest-at-parliament-pregnant-mp-steph-lewis-protesters-threatened-to-lynch-or-kidnap-me-staff/M47SWG3ZSOQEHYH7D34AAC7OVM/)).
