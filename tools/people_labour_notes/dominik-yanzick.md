## Current status
Labour candidate for Wigram, ranked 53 on the party list at the 7 November 2026 election (policy.nz candidate data). Not previously an MP.

## Biography
Yanzick grew up in Otekaieke, North Otago, the son of migrants from Germany and the United States. His Labour profile says he worked in milking sheds and as a farm hand, then in agritech at Lincoln, and is now a technology project manager.
