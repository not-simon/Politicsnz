## Current status

Former MP. She retired at the 2017 election and returned to the East Coast. Under the Labour government she was appointed to:
- the Royal Commission of Inquiry into the COVID-19 response (December 2022). She resigned from it in November 2023.
- lead the 2023 ministerial inquiry into forestry land use after Cyclone Gabrielle. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/national/480128/jacinda-ardern-ayesha-verrall-announce-royal-commission-of-inquiry-into-covid-19-response))

## Biography

- **Early life and family:**
  - Born 1 November 1958 in Ruatoria, of Ngāti Porou and Ngāi Tahu descent.
  - Her great-great-grandfather Tame Parata was an MP (1885–1911).
- **Education:** BA and MA (Māori studies) from the University of Waikato. She wrote her thesis in te reo Māori. She was the first Māori woman president of the Waikato students' association (1980) and protested against the 1981 Springbok tour.
- **Public service:**
  - Diplomat with Foreign Affairs.
  - Worked in Geoffrey Palmer's office on Treaty policy and in David Lange's policy unit.
  - Deputy chief executive of Te Puni Kōkiri.
- **Consultancy:** later ran a consultancy with her husband, Sir Wira Gardiner.

## Political career

- **Joined National:** in 2001.
- **Electorate contests:** stood in Wellington Central (2002) and Mana. She entered Parliament as a list MP in 2008.
- **Early ministerial roles:** Women's Affairs and Energy from 2010.
- **Minister of Education:** 2011 to 2 May 2017, when Nikki Kaye took over before Parata retired.

## Record & notable actions

- **Education reforms:**
  - Introduced "communities of learning" (Kāhui Ako) to get schools working together.
  - Legislated for online learning.
  - Began replacing the decile system with an equity index.
  - Māori and Pasifika NCEA Level 2 achievement rose during her tenure.
- **Conscience votes:** voted for marriage equality (2013).

## Controversies

- **2012–13 difficulties:**
  - Reversed a Budget 2012 plan to increase class sizes after a public backlash. ([Newshub](http://www.newshub.co.nz/politics/parata-defends-class-size-backdown-2012060809))
  - Her handling of school closures and mergers in post-quake Christchurch was widely criticised. ([ODT](https://www.odt.co.nz/opinion/editorial/christchurch-school-debacle))
  - The Novopay teacher-pay failures were moved to Steven Joyce.
  - The Secretary of Education resigned. ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/secretary-of-education-lesley-longstone-resigns/IFBEGIN56MAGSMZMVHQPUKG7DA/))
- **Pre-parliamentary scrutiny:**
  - A 1995 State Services inquiry into vehicle purchases at Te Puni Kōkiri cleared her.
  - Consultancy fees paid by government agencies were criticised by opposition MPs.
