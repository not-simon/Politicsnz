## Current status

He is no longer with National. Ngaro led the Christian-conservative NewZeal party from July 2023 and resigned in March 2026. On 22 March 2026 he announced he would stand for **New Zealand First** and brought NewZeal into alignment with that party. He is #5 on NZ First's 2026 list and its candidate for Glendene. His NZ First 2026 candidate profile is at [`parties/nz-first/people/alfred-ngaro`](../../../nz-first/people/alfred-ngaro/).

## Biography

- **Early life:** raised in Te Atatū by Cook Islands parents (a cleaner and a labourer). He attended Henderson High School and has some Jewish ancestry.
- **Training:** trained as an electrician and earned a theology degree from the Bible College of NZ (Laidlaw).
- **Community work:** community-development consultant, including on the Tāmaki Transformation Programme; chaired the Auckland DHB Pacific committee.
- **Personal:** married to Mokauina; four children.

## Political career

- **2010:** lost an Auckland Council race for Citizens & Ratepayers.
- **List MP (2011–20):** the first MP of Cook Islands descent.
- **Minister:** Pacific Peoples and Community and Voluntary Sector, outside Cabinet (2016–17).
- **2020:** lost his seat.
- **Leaving National:** in 2023 he said National's refusal to bloc-oppose the 2021 births registration law and the 2022 conversion-practices ban was part of why he left.
- **NewZeal:** led the party at the 2023 election (no seats) and the 2025 Tāmaki by-election.

## Record & notable actions

- **Conscience votes:** voted against marriage equality, End of Life Choice and abortion reform (see table).
- **Advocacy:** a self-described Christian Zionist; co-founded the Indigenous Coalition for Israel (2021).

## Controversies

- **Holocaust comparison (2019):** shared a post likening abortion to the Holocaust, then apologised.
- **Assault allegations:**
  - A 2009 allegation that he punched a teacher was not upheld; the Employment Court doubted the complainant's reliability.
  - He was cleared of assault allegations made in 2024.
