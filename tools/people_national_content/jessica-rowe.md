## Current status

Standing for National at the 7 November 2026 election in **Kaipara ki Mahurangi**, ranked **#68** on the party list published by the Electoral Commission. Not a current or former MP.

## Biography

Sources: [National Party candidate profile](https://www.national.org.nz/team/jessicarowe). The party profile is self-published, so the details below are as the party describes them.

- **Background:** raised on her family's farm in Riverhead; attended Coatesville Primary School.
- **Career:** former journalist. After a Master's in Global Diplomacy she worked for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Trade on Asia and Pacific issues.
- **Candidacy:** selected for Kaipara ki Mahurangi after the sitting National MP, Chris Penk, withdrew from the 2026 election. See [`chris-penk`](../chris-penk/).
