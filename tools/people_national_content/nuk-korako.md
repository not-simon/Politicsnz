## Current status

Former MP. After the 2022 local elections he was appointed a Ngāi Tahu representative on Environment Canterbury.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in 1954; Ngāi Tahu, with whakapapa to Rāpaki. He attended Rangiora High School and Lincoln College.
- **Career:** tourism businessman and Ngāi Tahu board member.
- **Personal:** married to Christine; four sons.

## Political career

- **Christchurch City Council (2013):** lost the Banks Peninsula ward by five votes.
- **List MP (2014):** lost Port Hills to Labour's Ruth Dyson but entered Parliament on the list. He lost to Dyson again in Banks Peninsula in 2017.
- **Roles:** chaired the Māori Affairs Committee (2014–17), then was Māori affairs and Treaty negotiations spokesperson (2017–19).
- **Retirement:** retired in May 2019, and Paulo Garcia replaced him.

## Record & notable actions

- **Lost property bill (2016):** his Airport Authorities (Publicising Lost Property Sales) Amendment Bill was criticised as trivial. Its changes were later made through a Statutes Amendment Bill.
- **Conscience votes:** voted against cannabis and euthanasia reform. He had left Parliament before the End of Life Choice third reading.
