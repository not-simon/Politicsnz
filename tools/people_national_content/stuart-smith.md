## Current status

National's Senior Whip and Chief Government Whip since March 2025. He is MP for Kaikōura (since 2014) and #21 on National's 2026 list.

In April 2026 the NZ Herald reported that, as whip, he had tried before Easter to warn Luxon about flagging caucus support. Luxon denied being approached. Smith was absent from the 21 April confidence vote, where he would normally have been a scrutineer. ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/politics/christopher-luxon-evaded-mp-who-tried-to-tell-him-of-flagging-support-faces-possible-challenge-this-fortnight-sources/CVCFDGUEYJHO3DVBSPZ3FPVX3Y/), [RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/592637/christopher-luxon-adamant-he-ll-remain-national-leader-amid-speculation))

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in 1963 on a South Canterbury farm near Methven. His father was a shearer who later bought a farm.
- **Wine industry:**
  - Bought into his wife's family's Marlborough vineyard in 1994. Their Fairhall Downes Estate winery was sold in 2015.
  - Chaired New Zealand Winegrowers from 2006 to 2012, during an industry downturn.
- **Personal:** married to Julie, a former occupational therapist; three children.

## Political career

- **Selection:** beat sitting MP Colin King for the Kaikōura nomination in 2013.
- **Elections:** won Kaikōura in 2014 by 12,570 votes and has held it since (2017, 2020, 2023).
- **Spokesperson roles in opposition:** civil defence, EQC, immigration, viticulture, climate change and energy.
- **Since 2023:**
  - Chaired the Finance and Expenditure Committee (December 2023 – February 2025).
  - Senior Whip from March 2025.

## Record & notable actions

- **Member's bills:**
  - His Insurance (Prompt Settlement of Claims for Uninhabitable Residential Property) Bill, a response to the Kaikōura and Canterbury quakes, reached select committee in 2020. He withdrew it after the committee found the government was already doing similar work. ([BusinessDesk](https://businessdesk.co.nz/article/policy/uninhabitable-home-insurance-law-change-dropped))
  - His Sale and Supply of Alcohol (Cellar Door Tasting) Amendment Bill passed its first reading 105–9 in 2023.
- **Conscience votes:** voted for the End of Life Choice Bill and was recorded absent for the abortion bill's third reading, having supported the bill earlier (see table).
