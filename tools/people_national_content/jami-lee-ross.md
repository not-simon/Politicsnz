## Current status

Former MP; no longer in National. He was expelled from the National caucus on 16 October 2018 and sat as an independent. He then co-led Advance New Zealand, which won no seats in 2020 and was deregistered in 2021.

Later developments:
- **2022:** acquitted in the SFO donation-splitting trial. The judge found it possible he had lied about acting on Simon Bridges' instructions in order to bring Bridges down. Businessman Zhang Yikun and two others were convicted.
- **Business:** he has since run businesses including an escort agency.
- **2025:** stood unsuccessfully for the Howick Local Board.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in 1985 and raised by his grandmother in Manukau. He is of Ngāti Porou descent through the father he never met. He attended Dilworth School and Pakuranga College, and gained a commercial pilot's licence.
- **Local government:**
  - Elected to Manukau City Council at 18 (2004).
  - Elected to the new Auckland Council in 2010, co-leading Citizens & Ratepayers.
- **Personal:** formerly married to Lucy Schwaner; two children.

## Political career

- **2011:** won the Botany by-election (5 March 2011) after Pansy Wong resigned, becoming "Baby of the House". He held the seat in 2011, 2014 and 2017.
- **Whip and fundraiser:** a party whip and fundraiser with close ties to Botany's Chinese community. He was chief whip in 2017.
- **2018:**
  - #8 in Simon Bridges' shadow cabinet (transport and infrastructure).
  - An inquiry named him as the leaker of Bridges' expenses.
  - He accused Bridges of electoral-law breaches over a NZ$100,000 donation and released a secretly recorded conversation.
  - Expelled from caucus.
- **2020:** formed Advance NZ, which merged with Billy Te Kahika's conspiracy-oriented NZ Public Party. He withdrew from Botany to stand list-only, and was not elected. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/422062/jami-lee-ross-launches-advance-new-zealand-party))

## Record & notable actions

- **Windscreen washers:** his member's bill cracking down on roadside windscreen washers was folded into a government bill in 2017.
- **Conscience votes:**
  - Voted for marriage equality (2013).
  - As an independent, voted for the End of Life Choice (2019) and abortion (2020) bills.

## Controversies

- **Bullying and harassment (2018–2020):** four women accused him of harassment and bullying in 2018. A 2020 Parliamentary Service investigation substantiated complaints of a "toxic environment" in his office. He denied wrongdoing. ([RNZ](https://www.radionz.co.nz/news/political/368904/four-women-accuse-jami-lee-ross-of-harassment-bullying))
- **Privileges breach (2020):** the Privileges Committee unanimously found Advance NZ had misused edited parliamentary video in a COVID-vaccine misinformation ad. He called the committee a "kangaroo court". ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/424965/parliament-s-privileges-committee-unanimous-on-agreement-mp-jami-lee-ross-broke-rules))
- **Electoral fraud charges (2020–22):** charged and then acquitted (see Current status).
- **Business ventures:** Newsroom reported on his "Praesidium Life" anti-EMF product and his escort agency.
