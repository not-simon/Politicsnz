## Current status

Backbench MP for Mount Roskill since 2023. He is #33 on National's 2026 list and is re-contesting the seat.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born Chun Ho Carlos Cheung in Hong Kong. He moved to New Zealand as a teenager and attended Auckland Grammar School.
- **Research career:** PhD in biological science from the University of Auckland (2013 thesis on diabetes-induced cardiac disease). He worked as a medical researcher but left research because of the difficulty of finding funding.
- **Business:** set up Lifestyle Property Management (2014) and Enfield Property Management. ([1News](https://www.1news.co.nz/2023/10/22/new-national-mp-on-switching-from-science-to-property-management/))
- **Personal:** married to Fiona Lai, a Puketāpapa Local Board member (Communities and Residents).

## Political career

- **2023:** list #48. He beat Labour cabinet minister Michael Wood, who had a 14,000 majority in 2020, by 1,564 votes. This was described as one of the upsets of the election. He was the second Chinese-born electorate MP, after Pansy Wong. ([Electoral Commission](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-25.html))

## Record & notable actions

- Campaigned on the cost of living and law and order, and on reducing costs for landlords.
- He was not in Parliament for the 2013–2022 conscience votes.
