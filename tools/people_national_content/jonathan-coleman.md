## Current status

Former MP. He resigned from Parliament in April 2018, which triggered the Northcote by-election won by Dan Bidois. In an October 2026 NZ Herald op-ed he called National's 2026 campaign pledge to drug-test beneficiaries "punitive and wrong". ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/as-a-doctor-and-former-health-minister-nationals-beneficiary-drug-test-is-punitive-and-wrong-jonathan-coleman/premium/SH32NPWOMNERDBMOFGIAITTOGU/))

## Biography

- Born 23 September 1966 in Auckland; attended Auckland Grammar School.
- Medical degree from the University of Auckland. He practised as a doctor in New Zealand, the UK and Australia, including with the Royal Flying Doctor Service.
- MBA from London Business School (2000).
- Health consultant at PwC and part-time GP in Ōtara.

## Political career

- **Northcote:** won the seat from Labour's Ann Hartley in 2005, the only Auckland seat to change hands that year. He held it until 2018.
- **Ministerial roles:**
  - Immigration and Broadcasting (2008–11).
  - Defence and State Services (2011–14). At Defence he oversaw withdrawals from Afghanistan, Solomon Islands and Timor-Leste.
  - Health and Sport (2014–17), the first doctor in 70 years to be Minister of Health.
- **Leadership:** stood for the leadership in December 2016, then withdrew in favour of Bill English.

## Record & notable actions

- **Health:** oversaw tight health budgets. In 2015 a public meeting in Central Otago backed a vote of no confidence in him over Southern DHB funding.
- **Conscience votes:** voted against marriage equality (2013).

## Controversies

- **Gender reassignment (2015):** called a proposal for funded gender-reassignment surgery a "nutty idea". ([Stuff](http://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/68670002/sex-change-surgery-policy-nutty))
- **Tobacco company box (2006):** as associate health spokesperson he admitted it was a "mistake" to sit in British American Tobacco's corporate box at a U2 concert.
- **Māori ancestry (2005):** claimed "a bit of tangata whenua" in his bloodline without evidence of whakapapa.
