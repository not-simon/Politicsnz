## Current status

Deceased. Nikki Kaye died of breast cancer on 23 November 2024, aged 44. She had left Parliament at the 2020 election. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/534834/former-national-party-minister-nikki-kaye-dies-aged-44))

## Biography

- **Early life:** born Nicola Laura Kaye on 11 February 1980 in Auckland, and grew up in Epsom and Kohimarama. She was head prefect at Corran School.
- **Education:** genetics degree from the University of Otago and a law degree completed in Wellington.
- **Athlete:** Auckland women's 3,000m champion (1997). She completed the Coast to Coast multisport race twice, the second time (2013) as a Cabinet minister.
- **Early career:**
  - Policy researcher for Bill English (2002).
  - Worked in London for the boroughs of Enfield and Bromley, Transport for London and HBOS.
  - Founded the volunteer-matching company networkme.com.

## Political career

- **2008:** beat Labour's Judith Tizard to become the first National MP for Auckland Central, ending a century of left-wing control of the seat.
- **2011 and 2014:** beat Jacinda Ardern for the seat. Her 2011 majority was 717.
- **Ministerial career:**
  - 2013: entered Cabinet with Food Safety, Civil Defence and Youth Affairs.
  - 2014: became ACC Minister.
  - September 2016: took leave for breast-cancer treatment.
  - Early 2017: returned to full duties.
  - May–October 2017: Minister of Education.
- **2020:**
  - 22 May: became deputy leader as Todd Muller's running mate.
  - 14 July: briefly interim leader when Muller resigned, then replaced as deputy by Gerry Brownlee.
  - Announced her retirement two days later. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/421312/live-updates-nikki-kaye-quitting-politics-amy-adams-follows))

## Record & notable actions

- **Social liberal:**
  - Spoke and voted for marriage equality at every stage (2012–13).
  - Voted for End of Life Choice and abortion reform.
  - Led National's successful internal campaign to keep the alcohol purchase age at 18 (2012).
  - Co-sponsored an adoption and surrogacy reform bill with Green MP Kevin Hague.
- **Environment:** in 2010 she broke with her party's plan to allow mining on Great Barrier Island conservation land.
- **Education Committee:** as chair, led an inquiry into 21st-century learning environments and digital literacy.

## Controversies

- **Diversity remark (2020):** as deputy leader, she incorrectly described Paul Goldsmith as Māori while defending the diversity of Muller's shadow cabinet.
