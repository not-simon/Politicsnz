## Current status

Former MP and broadcaster. According to Wikipedia she reports for Radio Waatea from the Parliamentary Press Gallery, and she judged for the NZ International Film Festival in 2022.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born on 8 May 1961 in Auckland and raised in Mount Roskill. She is of Ngāti Porou, Te Whānau-ā-Apanui, Ngāpuhi, Ngāti Kuta and Ngāti Ruanui descent.
- **Education:** BA from Canberra College of Advanced Education.
- **Broadcasting:**
  - Founded the production company Front of the Box in 1993.
  - Executive producer and presenter of *Eye to Eye with Willie Jackson* (2004–09).
  - Produced the award-winning documentary *Children of the Revolution* (2008).
- **Personal:** civil union with Nadine Mau (2007); three children.

## Political career

- **2010:** stood for the Albert-Eden Local Board for Citizens & Ratepayers; not elected.
- **2011:** lost Māngere to William Sio.
- **List MP (May 2013):** replaced Aaron Gilmore as a list MP on 28 May 2013.
- **2014:** withdrew from the election in July, although already selected for Kelston. Chris Penk replaced her as the candidate.

## Record & notable actions

No significant legislative record in the sources consulted. She was not an MP for the 2013 marriage-equality vote.

## Controversies

- **Charge card (March 2014):** handed back her parliamentary charge card after using it for a Christmas trip to Australia.
- **Employing her wife (April 2014):** breached parliamentary rules by employing her wife in her office. She said she was unaware of the rule and ended the employment.
- **Spending after retiring:** reported to have spent about NZ$23,000 in expenses after announcing she would stand down.
