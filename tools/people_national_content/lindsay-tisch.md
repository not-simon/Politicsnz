## Current status

Former MP. He retired at the 2017 election after 18 years in Parliament, and was made a Companion of the Queen's Service Order (QSO) in 2018.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born on 9 October 1947 in Auckland and raised in Matamata.
- **Education:** agriculture diploma from Lincoln College.
- **Career:** farmer, rural valuer and management consultant; Landcorp director (1991–97).
- **Party roles:** joined National in 1966. He was briefly party president in 1994 and campaign manager in 1996.

## Political career

- **1999:** succeeded John Luxton in Karapiro.
- **Electorates:** the seat became Piako in 2002. He successfully lobbied for it to be renamed Waikato, which he won in 2008 with a 12,850 majority over Jacinda Ardern.
- **Whip:** junior whip (2002–05), then senior whip (2005–06).
- **Presiding officer:** Deputy Speaker (2008–11), then Assistant Speaker (2011–17). He was never a minister.

## Record & notable actions

- **Same-sex relationships:** a social conservative. He voted against the Civil Union Act 2004, for the 2005 bill to define marriage as between a man and a woman, and against marriage equality in 2012–13.

## Controversies

- **Accommodation allowance (2009):** reported to be claiming NZ$410 a week, paid to his own property company, Heritage 653 Ltd. He was not alone among MPs in such arrangements.
