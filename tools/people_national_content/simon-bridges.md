## Current status

Former MP. He left Parliament on 6 May 2022, which triggered the Tauranga by-election won by Sam Uffindell. He said the decision was driven by family reasons, including his son's serious injury. After leaving he:
- became CEO of the Auckland Business Chamber (2022);
- launched the podcast *Generally Famous* with Stuff (2022);
- was appointed chair of the NZ Transport Agency Waka Kotahi board in 2024 (per Wikipedia).

## Biography

- **Early life:**
  - Born 12 October 1976 in Auckland, the youngest of six children. His father was a Baptist minister of Māori (Ngāti Maniapoto) and Pākehā descent; his mother was a teacher.
  - Grew up in Te Atatū and was head boy of Rutherford College.
- **Education:**
  - BA and LLB (Hons) from the University of Auckland.
  - Postgraduate study at the LSE and Oxford (St Catherine's College).
- **Legal career:** litigator at Kensington Swan, then a Crown prosecutor in Tauranga (2001–08).
- **Author:** wrote *National Identity: Confessions of an Outsider* (2021).
- **Personal:** married with three children.

## Political career

- **Tauranga:** won the seat in 2008 by 11,742 votes, beating NZ First leader Winston Peters, and held it until 2022.
- **Minister outside Cabinet (April 2012):** Consumer Affairs.
- **Cabinet (from 2013):**
  - Labour, and Energy and Resources.
  - Transport (2014–17).
  - Economic Development and Communications (2016–17).
  - Leader of the House (2017).
- **Leader of the National Party (27 February 2018):** the first Māori leader of a major New Zealand party.
- **Ousted (22 May 2020):** Todd Muller replaced him after a Reid Research poll put him at 4.5% as preferred PM.
- **Later roles:**
  - Under Collins: foreign affairs and justice spokesperson.
  - Demoted by Collins on 24 November 2021, which triggered her ouster.
  - Withdrew from the leadership race in favour of Luxon and became finance spokesperson until his resignation.

## Record & notable actions

- **Animal welfare:** his member's bill to increase animal-cruelty penalties was adopted by the government and passed (2010).
- **Northland by-election (2015):** as Transport Minister, pledged to upgrade ten one-lane bridges in Northland during the by-election. The opposition called it "pork-barrelling". ([Scoop](http://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/PA1503/S00096/national-to-replace-10-single-lane-bridges-in-northland.htm))
- **Epidemic Response Committee (2020):** chaired the committee scrutinising the COVID-19 response while Parliament was adjourned.
- **Conscience votes:**
  - Voted against marriage equality, End of Life Choice and abortion reform.
  - Was one of eight MPs to vote against the 2022 conversion-practices ban (see table).

## Controversies

- **Expenses leak and Jami-Lee Ross (2018):**
  - A leak revealed NZ$113,000 spent on limousines and hotels during his "road-show". ([Newshub](https://www.newshub.co.nz/home/politics/2018/08/simon-bridges-road-show-cash-splash-more-than-100-000-taxpayer-money-on-limos-and-hotels.html))
  - The Speaker's inquiry into the leak was called off. Bridges then named Jami-Lee Ross as the leaker. ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/107848792/National-Party-leader-Simon-Bridges-points-finger-at-Jami-Lee-Ross-in-expenses-leak-inquiry))
  - Ross released a recording in which Bridges called MP Maureen Pugh "fucking useless", and alleged that a NZ$100,000 donation from businessman Zhang Yikun had been split to avoid disclosure.
  - The SFO later charged four people over the donation. Bridges was not charged and denied wrongdoing (see Jami-Lee Ross).
- **Lockdown travel (2020):** criticised for driving between Tauranga and Wellington during the lockdown.
- **"Wokester" (2021):** criticised for calling Police Commissioner Andrew Coster a "wokester".
- **Jacqui Dean remark (2021):** in November 2021 Collins demoted him over a crude "old wives' tales" remark he had made to Jacqui Dean in 2016–17. He acknowledged the remark was inappropriate and said he had apologised privately. Caucus saw the move as a smear and removed Collins the next day. ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/300462440/simon-bridges-demoted-by-judith-collins-over-alleged-historical-comments-to-a-female-colleague))
