## Current status

Former MP. He retired at the 2023 election; his former electorate staffer Suze Redmayne succeeded him in Rangitīkei. His current activities are not detailed in the sources consulted. He holds the MNZM.

## Biography

- Born 1952 in Palmerston North, from a family that has farmed near the Rangitīkei River since 1850.
- Attended Wanganui Collegiate and holds a Diploma of Agriculture from Massey University.
- Career in farming, motor vehicles, property and insurance (Farmers' Mutual Group).
- National president of the Royal Agricultural Society.
- Mayor of Manawatū (2002–11).
- Chair of Special Olympics NZ (2010–19).

## Political career

- **Rangitīkei:** succeeded Simon Power in 2011 and held the seat until 2023 (majority 2,961 in 2020).
- **Committee roles:** chaired the Primary Production and Governance and Administration committees.
- **Assistant Speaker:** appointed in 2022.

## Record & notable actions

- **Livestock rustling (2018):** his bill made livestock rustling an aggravating factor at sentencing. He withdrew it when the government adopted the change in its own legislation. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/national/programmes/the-house/audio/2018630309/cattle-rustling-bill-passes-first-reading))
- **Dog Control (Category 1 Offences) Amendment Act 2019:** passed into law; it lets low-level dog offences be heard by JPs or community magistrates. ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/118134845/rangitkei-mp-ian-mckelvie-passes-law-to-speed-up-dog-offence-cases))
- **BYO alcohol at race meetings (2022):** his Sale and Supply of Alcohol (Exemption for Race Meetings) Amendment passed 74–43 on a conscience vote.
- **Conscience votes:** voted against marriage equality, End of Life Choice and abortion reform (see table).
