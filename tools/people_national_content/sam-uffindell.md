## Current status

Backbench MP for Tauranga since winning the June 2022 by-election. He is #50 on National's 2026 list and is re-contesting Tauranga.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in Auckland in September 1983. He was asked to leave King's College at 16 after taking part in a violent night-time attack on a 13-year-old student, and finished school at St Paul's Collegiate in Hamilton.
- **Education:** BA/BCom from the University of Otago, and a master's in international law and international relations from UNSW.
- **Banking career:** about ten years in Sydney and Singapore, including financial-crime work at Westpac and a vice-president role at Deutsche Bank. He was later head of financial and economic crime at Rabobank NZ.
- **Business:** partner in his wife Julia's fertiliser business, NZ Humates.
- **Personal:** married to Julia; three children.

## Political career

- **Party roles:** National member since 2008; Papamoa branch chair (2021).
- **2022 Tauranga by-election:** selected after Simon Bridges resigned. He won with about 56% of the vote, beating Labour's Jan Tinetti (11,613 votes to 5,259). ([Electoral Commission](https://electionresults.govt.nz/2022_tauranga_byelection/))
- **2023:** list #57. He held Tauranga by 9,370 votes over Tinetti.

## Record & notable actions

- **Early priorities:** Tauranga roads, gang crime, and a planned member's bill to make taking part in gang convoys a fineable offence.
- **Equal suffrage bill (March 2025):** his member's bill to preserve "equal suffrage" in local and central government was criticised by Tauranga Moana iwi leaders as ignoring Māori under-representation. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/ldr/545689/step-backwards-uffindell-s-equal-voting-rights-bill-challenged))
- He was not in Parliament for the 2013–2022 conscience votes.

## Controversies

- **King's College assault (revealed August 2022):**
  - He confirmed the attack and called himself "a thug" at the time. He had apologised to the victim in 2021. ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/300656643/national-mp-sam-uffindell-asked-to-leave-prestigious-kings-college-after-violent-nighttime-attack-on-younger-boy))
  - A former flatmate then alleged bullying in a 2003 student flat, which he denied.
- **Caucus stand-down (9 August – 19 September 2022):**
  - He was stood down from caucus while Maria Dew KC investigated.
  - National said the review found the flat allegations were "not as reported" and could not be substantiated, and reinstated him.
  - The report was not released, which drew criticism. ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/129927774/what-we-know--and-dont-know--about-the-sam-uffindell-bullying-claims))
- **2023 campaign:** National blocked media interviews with him after a widely mocked remark that he did the family's grocery shopping once a month "to give his wife a break".
