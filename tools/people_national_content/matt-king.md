## Current status

Former MP. He left National in 2022 after joining the anti-mandate protest on Parliament's grounds, and founded **DemocracyNZ**. The party won 0.23% of the party vote in 2023 and was deregistered at its own request in February 2024. He farms beef at Ōkaihau in the Far North.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in 1967 and raised in Northland.
- **Education:** BSc from the University of Auckland.
- **Police:** officer in Auckland, then a detective in Northland, until 2007.
- **Later work:** beef farmer and independent fraud investigator; volunteer firefighter.
- **Personal:** married; three children.

## Political career

- **Northland (2017):** won the seat from NZ First leader Winston Peters.
- **Spokesperson:** regional development (North Island), rural communities, and transport.
- **2020:** lost to Labour's Willow-Jean Prime by 163 votes on final results, after leading on election night by 729.
- **2023:** came fourth in Northland as DemocracyNZ's candidate (3,812 votes).

## Record & notable actions

- **"Coward punch" bill (2020):** his Crimes (Coward Punch Causing Death) Amendment Bill was defeated at first reading.
- **Conscience votes:** voted for End of Life Choice and against abortion reform (see table).

## Controversies

- **Climate change (2019):** posted on Facebook that climate change was "natural", rejecting the scientific consensus, and later called himself a "climate inquirer".
- **COVID-19 (2020–22):**
  - Called COVID-19 social-distancing rules "over the top" (2020).
  - Shared claims that vaccines were dangerous (2021) and promoted debunked treatments.
  - Said in 2022 that he did not believe 90% of people had been vaccinated.
  - National and Judith Collins distanced the party from his comments.
- **Parliament protest (2022):** joined the occupation of Parliament's grounds and was trespassed for two years. The Speaker later withdrew the trespass notice.
