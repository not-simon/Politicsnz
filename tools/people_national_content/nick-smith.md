## Current status

Mayor of Nelson. He was first elected in October 2022, beating Matt Lawrey by 9,302 votes, and re-elected in October 2025. He left Parliament on 10 June 2021. ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/nelson-mail/300708404/new-nelson-mayor-nick-smith-gets-down-to-business), [Nelson City Council](https://www.nelson.govt.nz/1your-council/elected-members-mayor-and-councillors))

## Biography

- Born 24 December 1964 in Rangiora, into a family of construction contractors.
- Studied at the University of Canterbury: BE (Hons) in civil engineering and a PhD on soil strength and landslide stability.
- Worked as an engineer and as a director of the family firm.
- Elected to Rangiora District Council at 21.

## Political career

- **Electorates:**
  - MP for Tasman, 1990–96.
  - MP for Nelson, 1996–2020.
  - Lost Nelson to Rachel Boyack in 2020 and returned as a list MP. ([Electoral Commission](https://www.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2020/electorate-details-27.html))
- **Cabinet, 1996–99:** Conservation (after the Cave Creek disaster), Corrections, then Education.
- **Deputy leadership, 2003:** deputy leader for under three weeks under Don Brash. He lost the post to Gerry Brownlee while on stress leave.
- **Fifth National Government:**
  - Environment, Climate Change and ACC (from 2008).
  - Resigned all portfolios in March 2012 over the ACC letter (see Controversies).
  - Returned in 2013 in Conservation and Housing.
  - Building and Housing and Environment, 2014–17.
- **Father of the House** from 2018 until he left Parliament.

## Record & notable actions

- **Emissions trading:** led National's 2009 amendments to the Emissions Trading Scheme.
- **Environment Canterbury (2010):** replaced the elected regional council with government-appointed commissioners. At the time, his brother's company was facing charges from that council. ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/the-press/3613819/Last-rites-for-ECan))
- **Housing and building:** Minister of Building and Housing during the mid-2010s housing crisis.
- **Electoral law:** in 2021 he sponsored a member's bill to repeal the "waka-jumping" Electoral (Integrity) Amendment Act. It was defeated 65–55.
- **Conscience votes:** voted against marriage equality, End of Life Choice and abortion reform (see table).

## Controversies

- **Contempt of court (2004):** found guilty of contempt over comments he made about a constituent's Family Court case.
- **Defamation (2010):** settled the Osmose defamation case with an apology. Taxpayer funding of his legal costs in other cases drew scrutiny.
- **ACC letter (2012):** resigned from Cabinet after writing on ministerial letterhead in support of a friend's ACC claim (the Bronwyn Pullar case). ([RNZ](http://www.radionz.co.nz/news/political/101387/smith-resigns-portfolios-over-acc-letter))
- **Ejection from the House (2020):** escorted out by the Serjeant-at-Arms, the first MP removed that way since Muldoon, after calling Parliament a "Nazi establishment". ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/419892/national-mp-nick-smith-booted-from-the-house-calls-govt-nazi-establishment))
- **Resignation (2021):** left Parliament while a Parliamentary Service inquiry into a 2020 "verbal altercation" in his office was under way. ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/300322387/nick-smith-resignation-audio-recording-of-verbal-altercation-at-centre-of-dispute))
