## Current status

Backbench MP for Napier since 2023. She is #26 on National's 2026 list and is re-contesting the seat.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born Katrina Margaret Nimon and raised in Hawke's Bay. She attended Iona College.
- **Education:** Bachelor of Design (Hons) and an Executive MBA from Massey University.
- **Family:** two of her great-grandfathers, including William Geddis, sat in the Legislative Council.
- **Career:**
  - Worked in advertising.
  - From 2015, marketing manager and then general manager of the family bus company Nimon & Sons. After 116 years the firm lost a school-bus contract in 2021 and was bought by Tranzit.
  - Transport manager at Hawke's Bay Regional Council (2021–22).
- **Personal:** married to Jeremy Lawson in 2022; they had a son about three years later.

## Political career

- **2020:** second in Napier with 37.0%; list #45.
- **2023:** won Napier from Labour by 8,909 votes. ([Electoral Commission](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-26.html))

## Record & notable actions

- Took part in the Cyclone Gabrielle recovery meetings with the Prime Minister in Hawke's Bay (December 2023).
- Spoke against Labour MP Camilla Belich's Crimes (Theft by Employer) Amendment Bill at its third reading (March 2025). She argued employment law already covered wage theft. The bill passed with Labour, Green, Te Pāti Māori and NZ First support. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/544621/theft-is-theft-bill-making-employers-theft-of-workers-wages-a-crime-passes))
- She was not in Parliament for the 2013–2022 conscience votes.
