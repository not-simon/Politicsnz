## Current status

Standing for National at the 7 November 2026 election in **Christchurch Central**, ranked **#22** on the party list published by the Electoral Commission. Not a current or former MP.

## Biography

Sources: [National Party candidate profile](https://www.national.org.nz/team/dalestephens). The party profile is self-published, so the details below are as the party describes them.

- **Background:** Dale Aotea Stephens was born and raised in Christchurch, and attended St Andrew's College.
- **Career:** former police officer; former chief executive of New Zealand's peak bodies for basketball and touch rugby.
- **Governance:** chair of New Zealand Māori Tourism and of the startup group Ministry of Awesome; trustee of Early Start Project.
- **Education:**
  - BBS from Massey.
  - Master of Public Policy and Graduate Diploma in Te Reo Māori from Victoria.
  - LLM from Canterbury.
- **Personal:** married to Tarina; six adult children.
