## Current status

Chief executive of the Australian and New Zealand Institute of Insurance and Finance (ANZIIF) in Melbourne since January 2024. She was previously chief executive of the Funeral Directors Association (2014–18) and Financial Advice NZ (2018–23).

## Biography

- **Early life:** born on 12 May 1969 in Dannevirke.
- **Education:** BBS from Massey University.
- **Career:** accountant, with roles at Westpac, Newton Investment Management (UK) and Audit New Zealand.
- **Personal:** three children.

## Political career

- **List MP (February 2007):** entered Parliament when Don Brash resigned. She had narrowly missed out at #46 on the list in 2005.
- **Ōhāriu (2008):** ran a party-vote-only campaign that helped Peter Dunne keep the seat.
- **Committee chair:** Social Services Committee (2009–11).
- **Early departure:** retired at the end of 2013 instead of serving until the 2014 election. Jo Hayes replaced her.

## Record & notable actions

- **Conscience votes:** voted against marriage equality (2013).
