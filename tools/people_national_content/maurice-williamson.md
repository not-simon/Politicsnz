## Current status

Auckland Councillor for the Howick ward. He was elected in October 2022 on a ticket with Sharon Stewart and re-elected in 2025. Before that he was Consul-General in Los Angeles (2017–2021).

## Biography

- Born 6 March 1951 in Auckland.
- MP for Pakuranga for 30 years (1987–2017).

## Political career

- **Fourth National Government:** Transport, Communications, Broadcasting, and Research, Science and Technology ministries (1990s).
- **2003:** suspended from caucus for criticising leader Bill English, then reinstated under Don Brash.
- **Fifth National Government:** minister outside Cabinet (2008–14) for Building and Construction (during the leaky-homes crisis), Customs, Statistics and Land Information.
- **Resignation (May 2014):** resigned all portfolios after contacting police about a domestic-violence case against National donor Donghua Liu (see Controversies).
- **Retirement:** did not stand in 2017.

## Record & notable actions

- **"Big gay rainbow" speech (2013):** his humorous third-reading speech supporting marriage equality went viral worldwide. He voted for the bill. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marriage_(Definition_of_Marriage)_Amendment_Act_2013))
- **Prostitution law:** a strong supporter of prostitution-law reform.
- **Roads:** long-time advocate of motorway building in East Auckland.

## Controversies

- **Donghua Liu (2014):** resigned as a minister after calling police about the charges against Liu. John Key called it "a serious error of judgement".
- **Remarks:**
  - A 2007 email asked "how come there were no fat people in the Nazi concentration camps?"
  - Made crude jokes about Muslims and Samoans at a 2011 conference.
  - Apologised in 2015 for remarks described as sexist.
