## Current status

Former MP. He retired at the 2023 election to spend time with his family. In 2024 Science Minister Judith Collins appointed him to the board of Callaghan Innovation. ([NZ Gazette](https://gazette.govt.nz/notice/id/2024-go1520))

## Biography

- Born 28 October 1970 in Hamilton; attended St John's College.
- LLB and BCA from Victoria University of Wellington.
- Former KPMG accountant. Owns dairy farms near Te Awamutu.
- Formerly in a long-distance relationship with Australian Nationals senator Bridget McKenzie. He is now engaged to Nicky Preston, with whom he has a child.

## Political career

- **Hamilton East:** won the seat from Labour's Dianne Yates in 2005 and held it until 2020, when he lost to Jamie Strange by 2,973 votes. He then returned as a list MP until 2023.
- **Committee chair:** chaired the Transport and Finance select committees.
- **Ministerial roles:** Food Safety, Veterans' Affairs and Racing, outside Cabinet, under Bill English (2016–17). He was the first Hamilton MP to become a minister since 1984.

## Record & notable actions

- **Marriage equality:** opposed the bill at first and second readings, then switched to support it at the third reading in 2013. He was the only MP recorded as changing to support.
- **Other conscience votes:**
  - Voted for the abortion bill at every stage.
  - Supported the abortion "safe areas" law and the 2022 conversion-practices ban.
  - Voted against the End of Life Choice Bill (see table).
