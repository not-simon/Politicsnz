## Current status

Backbench MP for Ilam since 2023. He is #47 on National's 2026 list and is re-contesting the seat.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in Christchurch and attended Christchurch Boys' High School, where he was deputy head boy.
- **Education:** BSc in genetics and a PhD on viruses and cancer from the University of Otago.
- **Research career:** medical researcher at the Children's Medical Research Institute and MS Research Australia, and lecturer at the University of Sydney.
- **Business:** runs a flower-delivery business and is involved in his family's apple orchard.
- **Personal:** married with two children; lives in Riccarton.

## Political career

- **2020:** lost Wigram to Labour's Megan Woods.
- **2023:** selected for Ilam after Gerry Brownlee went list-only. He won by 7,830 votes over TOP's Raf Manji, regaining the seat Labour had won in 2020. ([Electoral Commission](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-15.html))

## Record & notable actions

- Named youth offending and the cost of living as his priorities.
- He was not in Parliament for the 2013–2022 conscience votes.

## Controversies

- **"Two by Twos" (April 2025):** 1News reported that he belongs to the Christian group known as the "Two by Twos", which was under police investigation over historical sexual-abuse allegations. There was no suggestion that he was involved in any offending. ([1News](https://www.1news.co.nz/2025/04/08/national-mp-part-of-secretive-religious-sect/))
