## Current status

Former MP. He did not stand in 2020. He later sold real estate in Queenstown with Harcourts, then founded Premium Real Estate Queenstown, which has since closed.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in 1985 and raised in Dunedin, where he attended John McGlashan College.
- **Diabetes:** diagnosed at 14.
- **Personal:** his family has roots in Balclutha. His wife, Penny Tipu, is of Māori descent.

## Political career

- **2014:** contested Dunedin South.
- **Clutha-Southland (2017):** a late replacement for Todd Barclay. He won by 14,354 votes.
- **Opposition roles:**
  - Associate agriculture spokesperson (2018–20).
  - Forestry and land information spokesperson (May–July 2020).
- **Local issues:** campaigned to keep Lumsden maternity services.

## Record & notable actions

- **Conscience votes:** voted against the End of Life Choice and abortion bills.

## Controversies

- **Mataura post (Feb 2020):** admitted a social-media post falsely suggesting he had door-knocked in Mataura.
- **Quarantine statement (July 2020):** issued a press release objecting to quarantine facilities for returnees from India, Pakistan and South Korea. Ministers and Dunedin's mayor criticised it as racist "dog whistling", and Todd Muller reprimanded him.
- **Patient-data leak (July 2020):**
  - On 7 July he admitted leaking COVID-19 patient details to media, information he had received from former party president Michelle Boag. ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/300051710/national-mp-hamish-walker-admits-passing-on-leaked-covid19-patient-info-from-former-party-president-michelle-boag))
  - He was stripped of his portfolios, and Muller asked the party board to expel him. Walker announced he would not stand again. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/420754/covid-19-privacy-leak-mp-hamish-walker-announces-he-won-t-stand-for-re-election))
  - Michael Heron QC's inquiry found his actions were politically motivated and "not justified or reasonable".
