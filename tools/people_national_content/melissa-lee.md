## Current status

Backbench list MP. She was a minister from November 2023, but was removed from Cabinet and the Media portfolio in April 2024 and lost her remaining portfolios (Economic Development and Ethnic Communities) in January 2025. She is #29 on National's 2026 list and is again contesting Mount Albert, which she lost by 18 votes after a recount in 2023.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in South Korea in 1966 and grew up in Malaysia and Australia. Her family moved to New Zealand in 1988.
- **Education:** MA (Hons, first class) in communication studies.
- **Career:** spent 23 years in journalism, including at the *Sunday News*, and produced the TV series *Asia Downunder* through her company Asia Vision.
- **Milestone:** first Korean woman elected to a national legislature outside Korea.

## Political career

- **2008:** entered Parliament as a list MP. Her maiden speech was in English, Māori and Korean.
- **Mount Albert by-election, 2009:** distant second to Labour's David Shearer (3,542 votes to 13,260).
- **Mount Albert general elections:** lost to Shearer (2011, 2014), then to Jacinda Ardern (2017, 2020), then to Helen White in 2023 by 20 votes, cut to 18 on recount.
- **In government, 2011–17:** Parliamentary Private Secretary for Ethnic Affairs, and chair of the Social Services (2014) and Commerce (2014–17) committees.
- **Opposition:** spokesperson for broadcasting and digital media and for ethnic communities. In 2018 she pursued Broadcasting Minister Clare Curran over an undisclosed meeting with an RNZ executive, which ended with Curran's demotion. ([ODT](https://www.odt.co.nz/news/national/carol-hirschfeld-resigns-over-clare-curran-meeting))
- **November 2023:** Minister for Economic Development, Ethnic Communities, and Media and Communications.

## Record & notable actions

- **Gift card bill:** her Fair Trading (Gift Card Expiry) Amendment Bill, later taken over by Dan Bidois, became law in 2024. It sets a three-year minimum validity for most gift cards. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/478471/gift-card-expiries-could-be-extended-if-new-bill-becomes-law))
- **Media minister:** criticised for not producing a plan when Warner Bros. Discovery announced the closure of Newshub and TVNZ cut jobs (March–April 2024). Luxon removed her from Cabinet and the Media portfolio on 24 April 2024. ([Newshub](https://www.newshub.co.nz/home/politics/2024/04/melissa-lee-defends-work-during-time-as-broadcasting-minister-amid-newshub-tvnz-woes.html), [RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/515100/media-minister-melissa-lee-demoted-from-cabinet-penny-simmonds-stripped-of-portfolio))
- **January 2025:** lost Economic Development (replaced by the new Economic Growth portfolio under Nicola Willis) and Ethnic Communities (to Mark Mitchell). ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/539370/as-it-happened-luxon-announces-cabinet-reshuffle-shane-reti-loses-health-portfolio-to-simeon-brown))
- **Conscience votes:** consistently conservative. Voted against marriage equality, End of Life Choice and abortion reform, and was one of eight MPs to vote against the 2022 conversion-practices ban (see table).

## Controversies

- **South Auckland remark (2009):** during the by-election she said the Waterview motorway could divert South Auckland criminals away from Mount Albert. She apologised unreservedly, and John Key called it a "stupid statement".
- **NZ On Air (2009):**
  - She was cleared by NZ On Air over claims that her company used its funding for a National promotional video.
  - Separately, she admitted an "innocent error" in using NZ$100,000 of contingency funding contrary to her NZ On Air contract.
- **Racist abuse (2015):** NZ First's Ron Mark was criticised across parties for telling her in the House to "go back to Korea". ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/73722468/nz-first-mp-ron-mark-denies-racism-over-go-back-to-korea-jibe))
