## Current status

Backbench MP for Takanini since 2023. She is deputy chair of the Māori Affairs Committee and a member of the Justice Committee. She is #44 on National's 2026 list. In April 2026 her member's bill to stop public funding going to organisations with gang ties was drawn from the ballot. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/top/592271/national-mp-wants-to-cut-off-taxpayer-funding-for-gangs))

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in Sydney to parents of Lebanese origin. She studied law at Western Sydney University.
- **Move to New Zealand:** came to New Zealand in 2012 after marrying Roger Nakhle, and was admitted to the bar in 2014.
- **Te Mahia Community Village:** executive manager of this transitional and emergency-housing village at a former camping ground owned by her husband's family. She also set up a trust that ran activities for village residents.

## Political career

- **2020:** chosen over sitting list MP Agnes Loheni for the new Takanini seat. She lost to Labour's Neru Leavasa by over 4,500 votes.
- **2023:** beat Leavasa by 8,775 votes. ([Electoral Commission](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-48.html))

## Record & notable actions

- **Corrections (Victim Protection) Amendment Bill:** would stop prisoners contacting victims and people with protection orders. It passed its first reading unanimously in March 2024. ([Parliament](https://bills.parliament.nz/v/6/c993d911-193f-4204-7b2a-08dc22c1c3a5))
- **Gang-funding bill (2026):** a response to the 2021 Kāinga Ora funding of a Mongrel Mob-linked rehabilitation programme.
- **Positions:**
  - Tough-on-crime.
  - Proposed tougher drink-driving penalties.
  - Said she voted against both the euthanasia and the cannabis referendums in 2020.
- She was not in Parliament for the 2013–2022 conscience votes.
