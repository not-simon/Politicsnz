## Current status

Standing for National at the 7 November 2026 election in **West Coast-Tasman**, ranked **#24** on the party list published by the Electoral Commission. Not a current or former MP.

## Biography

Sources: [National Party candidate profile](https://www.national.org.nz/team/katiemilne) and [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Milne). The party profile is self-published, so the details below are as the party describes them.

- **Farming:** dairy farmer near Lake Brunner. She grew up outside Greymouth and trained as a meat inspector.
- **Federated Farmers:** first woman president (2017–2020), after joining the federation in 1991 and leading its West Coast province.
- **Governance:**
  - Director of Westland Milk Products.
  - Boards including Predator Free 2050 and the World Farmers' Organisation.
  - Member of the National Animal Welfare Advisory Committee.
- **Awards:** Fonterra Dairy Woman of the Year (2015); NZ Women of Influence rural winner (2015).
- **Other:** volunteer firefighter for 26 years.
- **Candidacy:** announced as West Coast-Tasman candidate on 11 April 2026.
