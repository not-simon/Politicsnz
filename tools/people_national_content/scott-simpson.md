## Current status

Minister for ACC since February 2025, when he replaced Andrew Bayly after Bayly's resignation. In the April 2026 reshuffle he became Minister of Statistics and Deputy Leader of the House, and Commerce and Consumer Affairs passed to Cameron Brewer. He is MP for Coromandel (since 2011) and #18 on National's 2026 list. During the August 2026 leadership crisis he publicly backed Luxon.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born on 4 November 1959. His ancestors settled at Kūaotunu on the Coromandel Peninsula. He grew up in Auckland and studied law at the University of Auckland.
- **Career:** managed a safety-equipment company, then was chief executive of Make-A-Wish New Zealand (2008–11).
- **Party roles:** National's Northern regional chair and a board member. He lost the 2009 party presidency race to Peter Goodfellow.
- **Personal:** two children with former wife Desley Simpson, now an Auckland councillor and deputy mayor.

## Political career

- **2011:** won Coromandel by 12,740 votes.
- **Re-elections:** majorities of 15,801 (2014), 14,326 (2017), 3,505 (2020) and 17,349 (2023).
- **Committee chair:** Justice and Electoral (2013–14), then Local Government and Environment.
- **2017:** Minister of Statistics and Associate Minister of Immigration and Environment (May–October).
- **Opposition:** spokesperson for the environment, workplace relations and climate change.
- **2023:** not made a minister. Instead he became Senior Whip and Chief Government Whip, and chaired the Environment Committee from May 2024, replacing David MacLeod.
- **February 2025:** minister again after Bayly's resignation. ([The Post](https://www.thepost.co.nz/politics/360593679/who-new-minister-scott-simpson), [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2025/feb/24/new-zealand-minister-quits-after-having-placed-hand-on-staffers-arm))

## Record & notable actions

- **Grocery conflict of interest:** declared a conflict in grocery-sector decisions as Commerce Minister, so Nicola Willis took them. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/543796/new-consumer-affairs-minister-scott-simpson-hands-off-supermarket-decisions-over-conflict))
- **Automated welfare decisions (May 2026):** took through, under urgency, a Social Security Act amendment letting MSD use automated or AI decision-making. The opposition opposed it. ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/politics/360985822/government-uses-budget-urgency-pass-bill-allowing-automation-welfare-decisions), [RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/596738/proposed-law-could-see-government-use-ai-to-make-decisions-about-people-s-benefits))
- **BlueGreens:** led National's environmental advisory group.
- **Conscience votes:** a liberal record. Voted for marriage equality, End of Life Choice and abortion reform (see table).
