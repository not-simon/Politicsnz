## Current status

Former MP. He retired at the 2014 election, and Simeon Brown replaced him as National's Manurewa candidate. He was a Commonwealth election observer in Sri Lanka in 2015. In 2021 he wrote a NZ Herald op-ed criticising the new NZ history curriculum.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born in 1952 and raised in New Plymouth.
- **Medicine:** trained as a dentist at Otago, then retrained in medicine at Cambridge. He worked as an emergency doctor in England and New Zealand, and later as clinical research director for an equipment company.
- **Other interests:** president of the French New Zealand Business Council and a pétanque promoter; he represented New Zealand at the 1995 world championships.
- **Personal:** married to Jenny; two children.

## Political career

- **Joined National:** 2003.
- **2008:** contested Manurewa (#58 on the list) and missed out when the final count cut National's seats by one.
- **List MP (June 2009):** entered Parliament when Richard Worth resigned, and was re-elected on the list in 2011.
- **Committees:** chaired the Education and Science Committee (2013–14); deputy chair of the Justice and Electoral Committee.

## Record & notable actions

- **Laser pointers:** his member's bill restricting high-powered laser pointers in public places became law in July 2014.
- **Conscience votes:** voted for marriage equality (2013) and supported raising the alcohol purchase age to 20.
