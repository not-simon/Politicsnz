## Current status

Former MP and minister. He retired at the 2014 election, expecting a low list placing. Since then:
- **Housing New Zealand:** appointed to the board in 2015.
- **Auckland's Independent Māori Statutory Board:** appointed in 2016; deputy chair from 2019.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born on 29 September 1960 in Ōtara; Ngāpuhi.
- **Family:** great-grandson of MP Taurekareka Hēnare and grand-nephew of Sir James Henare.
- **Early work:** wool classer, union organiser for the Northern Clerical Workers' Union, and community worker.
- **Media:** later a Newstalk ZB talkback host.
- **Personal:** married Ngaire Brown in Parliament in 2012; Chris Auchinvole officiated.

## Political career

- **New Zealand First (1993–98):**
  - Won Northern Māori from Labour in 1993 and became NZ First deputy leader.
  - One of the "Tight Five" who won all five Māori seats in 1996.
  - Minister of Māori Affairs and Racing in the National–NZ First coalition.
- **Mauri Pacific (1998–99):** left NZ First in 1998 after an unsuccessful challenge to Winston Peters and led the new party. It won 0.08% in 1999.
- **National:**
  - Joined in 2002. He contested Te Atatū in 2002, 2005, 2008 and 2011, entering Parliament on the list in 2005.
  - Chaired the Māori Affairs Committee (2008–14).
  - Dropped a 2012 bid for the Speakership.

## Record & notable actions

- **Secret ballots for strikes:** his Employment Relations (Workers' Secret Ballot for Strikes) Amendment Bill became law in 2012.
- **Auckland Māori seats (2009):** lobbied colleagues to be allowed to vote against the Auckland governance bill's lack of Māori seats.
- **Conscience votes:** voted for marriage equality (2013).

## Controversies

- **Mallard altercation (2007):** involved in a scuffle with Labour MP Trevor Mallard outside the chamber. Mallard was convicted of fighting in a public place.
- **Suppression charge (December 2014):** charged with breaching a suppression order by allegedly naming the defendant in a suppressed case on social media.
