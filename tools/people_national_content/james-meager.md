## Current status

Minister for Youth, Hunting and Fishing, and the South Island, and Associate Minister of Transport, outside Cabinet, since the January 2025 reshuffle. He was the first National MP from the 2023 intake to become a minister. He is MP for Rangitata and #17 on National's 2026 list.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born and raised in Timaru, of Ngāi Tahu descent. He grew up in a state house with a single parent on a benefit. Head boy and dux of Timaru Boys' High School.
- **Education:** LLB and BA from the University of Otago. He has described his student self as "loud-mouthed, obnoxious and opinionated".
- **Career:**
  - Ran Michael Woodhouse's 2014 Dunedin campaign.
  - Press secretary to Paula Bennett, and adviser to Bill English and Simon Bridges.
  - Worked in Chris Bishop's office.
  - Senior solicitor at Simpson Grierson, and ran a consultancy.

## Political career

- **2022:** selected for Rangitata, which Labour had won in 2020.
- **2023:** won with 22,792 votes, a majority of 10,846 over Labour incumbent Jo Luxton. ([Electoral Commission](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-40.html))
- **Maiden speech:** gave National's first Address-in-Reply speech of the 54th Parliament, about being a "part Māori boy raised in a state house by a single parent on the benefit". It drew a standing ovation from both sides of the House. ([Stuff](https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/301022638/new-national-mp-gets-standing-ovation-following-maiden-speech))
- **January 2025:** promoted to minister. ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/politics/cabinet-reshuffle-live-updates-christopher-luxon-to-unveil-changes-health-minister-shane-reti-expected-to-lose-portfolio/LNFYGT46YFG2ZC7NBMRYKJ7PUE/))

## Record & notable actions

- Made a second Ashburton bridge his first local priority as an MP.
- Minister for the new South Island portfolio.
- He was not in Parliament for the 2013–2022 conscience votes.
