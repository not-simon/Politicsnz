## Current status

Standing for National at the 7 November 2026 election in **Nelson**, ranked **#40** on the party list published by the Electoral Commission. Not a current or former MP.

## Biography

Sources: [National Party candidate profile](https://www.national.org.nz/team/blaircameron) and [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blair_Cameron). The party profile is self-published, so the details below are as the party describes them.

- **Background:** raised near Methven in rural Canterbury.
- **Education:** won scholarships to Li Po Chun United World College in Hong Kong and to Brown University (BA, International Relations).
- **Career:** worked at Princeton, the IMF, the World Bank (including on anti-corruption research) and the Asian Development Bank. He returned to Nelson in 2020.
- **2023 election:**
  - Led Nelson by 54 votes on election night.
  - Labour's Rachel Boyack won by 29 on the official count.
  - A judicial recount confirmed her win by 26 votes.
- **2026:** re-contesting Nelson.
