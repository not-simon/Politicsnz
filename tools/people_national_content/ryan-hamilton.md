## Current status

Backbench MP for Hamilton East since 2023. He sits on the Finance and Expenditure Committee and is deputy chair of the Regulations Review Committee. He is #35 on National's 2026 list (shown as #36 in policy.nz's earlier list).

## Biography

- **Local politics:**
  - Stood unsuccessfully for Hamilton City Council (2004, 2010, 2016), the WEL Energy Trust and the Waikato DHB.
  - Won a council by-election in February 2018 and was re-elected in 2019 and 2022, when he topped the East Ward poll.
  - Resigned from the council in October 2023 after being elected to Parliament.
- **Family:** his mother, Mavora Hamilton, was also a Hamilton city councillor.
- **Personal:** married with three children.

## Political career

- **2023:** selected in February 2023 to succeed David Bennett; list #66.
- **Result:** beat Labour's Georgie Dansey by 5,060 votes. ([Electoral Commission](https://archive.electionresults.govt.nz/electionresults_2023/electorate-details-12.html))

## Record & notable actions

- As a councillor, he was the only member to vote against requiring My Vaccine Pass for council facilities.
- He was not in Parliament for the 2013–2022 conscience votes.

## Controversies

- **Social-media history (2023):** during the 2023 campaign RNZ reported posts linking him to anti-fluoridation and COVID-19 conspiracy content, including calling vaccine mandates illegal. He did not give interviews, but National issued a statement in his name apologising for the past comments. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/in-depth/498204/ryan-hamilton-national-s-anti-fluoride-anti-mandate-mp-in-waiting))
