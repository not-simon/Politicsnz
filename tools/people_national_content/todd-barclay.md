## Current status

Former MP. He announced in June 2017 that he would not stand again, and left Parliament at the 2017 election. He later moved to London, then Sydney and Saudi Arabia, and works as a consultant for KPMG.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born on 8 June 1990 in Dipton, where his parents ran the Four Square. He attended Gore High School.
- **Education:** BCom in commercial law from Victoria University.
- **Career:** worked for Bill English, Gerry Brownlee and Hekia Parata, then for Philip Morris.

## Political career

- **Clutha-Southland (2014):** succeeded Bill English, winning nearly 64% of the electorate vote. At 24 he was the youngest MP and the first born in the 1990s.
- **Committees:** deputy chair of the Law and Order Committee (2015–17).
- **2016:** won re-selection over challenger Simon Flood.

## Record & notable actions

No significant legislative record in the sources consulted. He was not in Parliament for the tracked conscience votes.

## Controversies

- **Secret-recording scandal (2016–17):**
  - Several staff resigned in 2016, including long-time English electorate agent Glenys Dickson.
  - In June 2017 it was revealed that he had secretly recorded staff he suspected of criticising him. Recording a conversation you are not a party to is a crime in New Zealand.
  - He announced on 21 June 2017 that he would not seek re-election.
- **Campbell Live (2015):** deleted a tweet criticising supporters of the threatened *Campbell Live* show.
