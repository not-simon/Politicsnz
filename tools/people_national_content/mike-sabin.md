## Current status

Former MP. He resigned on 30 January 2015, which triggered the 2015 Northland by-election; Winston Peters won it. In 2015 he was reported to be working at Peppers Carrington Resort in Karikari. His current activities are not verified.

## Biography

- **Early life:** born on 24 September 1968 and raised in Whangārei.
- **Navy and dairy:** seaman officer in the RNZ Navy in the 1980s, then worked in the dairy industry.
- **Police and drug education:**
  - Police officer from the 1990s.
  - Founded the drug-education company MethCon (2006; sold 2010).
  - Helped bring about the Alcohol and Other Drug Treatment Court.
  - Received a Sir Peter Blake Emerging Leader Award (2008).
- **Personal:** father of journalist Brook Sabin. He wrote *The Long Way Home*, about his son Darryl's rugby brain injury.

## Political career

- **Northland:** succeeded John Carter, winning by 11,362 votes in 2011 and 9,300 in 2014.
- **Ministerial prospects:** John Key later said he had considered making Sabin a minister after the 2014 election.

## Record & notable actions

- **Conscience votes:** voted against marriage equality (2013).

## Controversies

- **Resignation (January 2015):** in December 2014 media reported he was under police investigation over an assault complaint. Police, Key and Sabin did not confirm the reports at the time. He resigned with immediate effect, citing personal issues "best dealt with outside Parliament".
