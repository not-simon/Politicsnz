**Most significant corrections**

1. **NZ First coalition stance:** removed the claim that Peters said at the TVNZ multi-party debate he could work with "the parties in government now". No debate coverage found (1News, The Spinoff live blog) records it. The verified "No, I can't" answer about sitting at a Cabinet table with Qiulae Wong (Herald debate, 21 Oct) is kept.
2. **National seat count:** the card said 48 "of 122" while every other card said "of 123". Now 48 of 123, with the note explaining that Papakura is vacant (Judith Collins resigned 14 May 2026; no by-election within six months of an election), so 122 members sat at dissolution.
3. **NZ Herald NZ First costing article** is dated 22 October 2026, not 23 October (card and REPORT corrected). The $24b upper estimate for buying BNZ is now attributed as the Herald gives it: "other estimates", source unnamed.
4. **Opportunity's rule-out of Winston Peters as Foreign Minister** was cited to a 21 Oct 1News article that does not contain it. It comes from August 2026 (1News, 13 Aug); re-dated and re-sourced, with the party's stated openness to other NZ First roles added.
5. **GDP per person** (+0.1%) is an annual figure, not a June-quarter one; fixed. **Treasury PREFU** fact re-sourced to the RNZ PREFU report, with the 43.9% net-debt peak added.
6. **Supermarket market share (82%)** was sourced to Wikipedia's election article, which does not contain the figure. Re-sourced to 1News/The Conversation (22 Sep 2026) and the Commerce Commission's 2022 finding, and dated.
7. **Women's Rights Party headline policies** were given in the party's own slogans without saying so ("Fair play in women's sport", "Clear, plain language about women"); now described concretely from its policy documents, with the slogans quoted and attributed, and linked to policy ids.
8. **Currency:** Labour's Tāmaki Makaurau candidate Kerrin Leoni resigned as a candidate on 23 October 2026; added as a dated note on the Labour card and in REPORT §2.2 (the electorate page and her profile already record it).
9. **Loaded wording** replaced across both files: "tougher" (×4), "supermarket giants" (×2), "clean up politics", "more teeth", "treat everyone the same regardless of race" (ACT framing adopted as description), "mass immigration" and "race-based" without attribution, "the bill is huge", "many New Zealanders are leaving for Australia", "generous support".
