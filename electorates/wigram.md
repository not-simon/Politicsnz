## About this electorate

Wigram covers the south-western suburbs of Christchurch, covering suburbs such as Wigram, Riccarton, Hornby and Sockburn. The seat is currently held by Labour’s Megan Woods, who was first elected in 2011. Woods won in 2023 with 15,590 votes. National won the party vote with 35%, followed by Labour with 29%. The seat is a traditional left-wing stronghold. First formed in 1969, it has only ever been won by a Labour candidate outside the years from 1996 to 2008 that was held by Alliance and Progressive party leader Jim Anderton.
