## About this electorate

Takanini covers some of Auckland’s southernmost suburbs, including Conifer Grove, Randwick Park, and Totara Heights. The electorate is held by National’s Rima Nakhle, who first won election in 2023 with a margin of 21,936. National also won a majority of the party vote with 53%, followed by Labour with 28%. The electorate is in one of New Zealand’s fastest growing areas population-wise: it was first created as an entirely new electorate in 2020, and was 17.7% over population quota in the 2026 redistributions.
