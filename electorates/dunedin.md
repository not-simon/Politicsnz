## About this electorate

Dunedin covers all but the southern most suburbs of Dunedin city. The electorate is held by Labour’s Rachel Brooking, initially a list MP who was elected in 2023 with a margin of 17,111 votes. Labour also won the party vote with 31%, followed by the Greens on 27%. The electorate skews heavily left; in the two elections with this iteration of the electorate, National has only ever come third to Labour and the Greens in the party vote.

_Description quoted from [policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/dunedin-electorate)._
