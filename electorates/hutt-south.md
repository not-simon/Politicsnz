## About this electorate

Hutt South covers much of the Lower Hutt region of Wellington, including the suburbs of Gracefield, Waterloo, Eastbourne, and Newlands. It is currently held by National’s Chris Bishop, who won election in 2023 with a margin of 14,772 votes, and who was also the electorate MP from 2017-2020, and being a list MP from 2014. In 2023, the party vote was close, with National winning 34% followed by Labour with 32%. Bishop is only the third-ever MP for Hutt South; his 2017 predecessor Trevor Mallard held it for 21 years before him.
