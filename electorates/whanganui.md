## About this electorate

Whanganui covers its namesake town and the surrounding smaller towns of Hawera and Stratford while also stretching into the Rupaehu district to include Waiouru and Tongariro National Park. The seat is currently held by National’s Carl Bates, who was first elected in 2023 with a margin of 18,484. National won the party vote in 2023 with 39% of the vote, followed by Labour with 28%. The electorate has the highest proportion of the populace employed in manufacturing jobs in the country.
