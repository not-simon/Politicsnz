## About this electorate

Whangaparāoa covers Auckland’s northern edges, including the communities of Dairy Flat, Hibiscus Coast, and Ōrewa. The electorate is held by National’s Mark Mitchell, who won re-election in 2023 with a margin of 30,742. National won the party vote in 2023 with 53% of the vote, followed by Labour with 15%. Mitchell beat his Labour contender by 23,376 votes in 2023: the largest winning margin of any electorate in 2023.
