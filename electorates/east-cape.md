# East Cape electorate (general)

**Region:** Gisborne / Bay of Plenty  
**Election day:** Saturday 7 November 2026  
**Boundary status:** renamed — predecessor seat(s): East Coast

> Renamed from East Coast after public submissions; boundary changes north of Gisborne proposed in the draft did not proceed.

## About this electorate

East Cape covers the central North Island’s east coast centres, including towns such as Whakātane, Ōpotiki, and Gisborne. The electorate has been established for this election, however its predecessor East Coast electorate was won by National’s Dana Kirkpatrick by a margin of 17,007 votes. Kirkpatrick will stand for East Cape in 2026. The electorate previously existed between 1978 and 1990.

_Description quoted from [policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/east-cape-electorate)._

**Fact-check notes on the description above** (checked against official 2023 results):

- "margin of 17,007" (that figure is the winner’s vote total) — the official 2023 majority was 3,199 votes over Tāmati Coffey.
