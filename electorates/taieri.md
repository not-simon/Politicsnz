## About this electorate

Taieri covers some of Dunedin’s southern suburbs including Anderson’s Bay, Saint Kilda, and Concord, but also stretches south into the Clutha region, incorporating towns such as Mosgiel, Milton, and Balclutha. The electorate is held by Labour’s Ingrid Leary, who was first elected in 2020 and was re-elected in 2023 with a margin of 1,443. Labour narrowly won the party vote in 2023, with 34%, followed by National with 33%. The electorate previously existed between 1866 and 1908, where it was represented by both Donald Reid Sr and Jr, before being re-created over a century later.
