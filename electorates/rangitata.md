## About this electorate

Rangitata covers much of the South Canterbury region south of the Rakaia river, including towns such as Ashburton, Timaru, and Methven. The seat is currently held by National’s James Meager, who was first elected with a margin of 22,792 votes in 2023. National won the party vote in 2023 with 44.9%, followed by Labour with 26%. The electorate works long hours, with 15.2% of the electorate’s population working between 50 and 59 hours a week - the highest percentage in New Zealand.
