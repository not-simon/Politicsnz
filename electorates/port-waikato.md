## About this electorate

Port Waikato covers much of southern Auckland and north Waikato, including the major settlements of Pukekohe, Waiuku and Te Kauwhata. The electorate has been held by National’s Andrew Bayly since its creation in 2020, who is retiring this election. In 2023, the death of ACT party candidate Neil Christensen caused the candidate election to be cancelled, and in a subsequent by-election Bayly was elected with 76% of the vote (though New Zealand First was the only major party to contest the by-election). National won the party vote in 2023, with 50% of the vote, followed by Labour with 19%.
