# Wellington North electorate (general)

**Region:** Wellington

**Boundary status:** renamed — predecessor seat(s): Wellington Central, Ōhāriu

> Most of Wellington Central plus Ngaio, Khandallah, Broadmeadows, Kaiwharawhara, Crofton Downs and Wadestown from Ōhāriu; renamed Wellington North after submissions.

## About this electorate

Wellington North covers the central business district of Wellington City and both its western and northern suburbs such as Northland, Karori, Wilton, Ngaio and Khandallah. The electorate first existed from 1905 to 1946, and was recreated out of parts of the former Wellington Central and Ōhāriu electorates when electorate lines were redrawn in 2025. Green Party MP Tamatha Paul won the former Wellington Central electorate with a margin of 6,066 votes in 2023. She is competing against Labour’s health spokesperson Ayesha Verrall.
