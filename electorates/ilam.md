## About this electorate

Ilam covers Christchurch’s Northeast suburbs, including Riccarton, Avonhead, and Bryndwr. National’s Hamish Campbell, who was first elected in 2023 with a margin of 18,693. National also won the party vote with 43%, followed by Labour with 22%. In 2017, independent Raf Manji finished second, one of the best finishes for an independent not already in parliament in the MMP era.

_Description quoted from [policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/ilam-electorate)._
