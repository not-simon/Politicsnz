## About this electorate

Southland covers much of the area between Otago and Southland, including the twins of Queenstown, Gore, and Alexandra. The electorate is held by National’s Joseph Mooney, who has held the electorate since its creation in 2020. He was re-elected in 2023 with a margin of 23,231 votes. National won the party vote with 48% of the vote, followed by Labour with 16%. The electorate is the most employed electorate in the country, with the most people in full time work of anywhere in the country.
