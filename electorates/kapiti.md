**Region:** Wellington

**Boundary status:** new — predecessor seat(s): Ōtaki, Mana

> Recreated seat (previously 1972–96) covering the Kāpiti Coast and northern Porirua after Ōtaki and Mana were abolished. 2023 result shown is Ōtaki.

## About this electorate

Kapiti covers the northernmost suburbs of Porirua and the Kapiti Coast, including Paraparaumu, Ōtaki, and Plimmerton. The electorate has been recreated for the 2026 election, and is expected to be competitive as the two predecessor electorates, Mana, and Ōtaki, were safe seats for Labour and National. The predecessor Kapiti electorate, which existed between 1972 and and 1993 was similarly competitive, being held by two MPs for Labour and two MPs for National.
