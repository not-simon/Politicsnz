# Kenepuru electorate (general)

**Region:** Wellington  
**Election day:** Saturday 7 November 2026  
**Boundary status:** new — predecessor seat(s): Mana, Ōhāriu

> New seat covering southern Porirua and northern Wellington (Tawa, Johnsonville, Ohariu) after Mana and Ōhāriu were abolished. 2023 result shown is Mana.

## About this electorate

Kenepuru covers the northernmost suburbs of Wellington City and the southern suburbs of Porirua, including Tawa, Porirua city centre, and Titahi Bay. The electorate was created for the first time in 2026, but is expected to be a safe seat for Labour, with both its predecessor Ōhariu and Mana being held by Labour.
