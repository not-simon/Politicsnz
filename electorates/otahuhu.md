## About this electorate

Ōtāhuhu covers several southern Auckland suburbs including Ōtāhuhu, Ōtara, and Middlemore. The electorate will be contested for the first time since 1981 this election, though its predecessor Panmure-Ōtāhuhu was held by Labour’s Jenny Salesa, who is contesting the new Ōtāhuhu seat. Labour also won the party vote in Panmure-Ōtāhuhu in 2023, with 52% of the vote. National was second with 27%. This will be Ōtāhuhu’s third time as parliamentary electorate, having previously existed between 1938-1963, and 1972-1981.
