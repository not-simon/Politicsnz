## About this electorate

Surrounded entirely by the Northland electorate, Whangārei forms an alcove around the city of Whangārei and its harbour, and while also stretching south to Waipū and Langs Beach and north to Whangaruru harbour. The seat is held by National’s Shane Reti, who won the electorate back from Labour in 2023 after previously representing it between 2014 and 2020. He is retiring in 2026, after winning election in 2023 with a margin of 20,843. National won the party vote in 2023 with 38%, while Labour came second with 22%. The electorate has the highest proportion of health care and social assistance workers in the country.

_Description quoted from [policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/whangarei-electorate)._
