## About this electorate

The Ikaroa-Rāwhiti electorate covers the eastern North Island, including Gisborne, Hawke’s Bay, Waiarapa and  parts of the Hutt Valley in Greater Wellington. The seat is currently held by the Labour Party’s Cushla Tangaere-Manuel, who was the only Labour Party candidate to win a Māori seat in 2023. Tangaere-Manuel won 13,747 votes and 54.79% of the party vote. Since its establishment in 1999, the seat has been a stronghold for the Labour Party. The only time Labour has not controlled the seat was when Labour cabinet minister Meka Whaitiri defected to Te Pāti Māori before being defeated in 2023.
