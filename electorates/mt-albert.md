## About this electorate

Mount Albert covers several western Auckland suburbs, including Waterview, Sandringham, and Kingsland. It is held by Labour’s Helen White, initially a list MP who won the electorate vote in 2023 with an 18-vote victory over National’s Melissa Lee, and a total margin of 13,238. National won the party vote with 32%, followed by Labour on 26%, and the Greens on 25%. The electorate has a reputation for producing Labour leaders—Helen Clark, David Shearer, and Jacinda Ardern all held the seat.

## What to watch

- One of the tightest seats in the country: Labour’s Helen White held it by 18 votes in 2023 while National won the party vote. In 2026 it is a four-way contest with National’s Melissa Lee, the Greens’ Ricardo Menéndez March and Opportunity leader Qiulae Wong; a Taxpayers’ Union–Curia poll reported by Stuff in October had White just five points ahead of Lee.
