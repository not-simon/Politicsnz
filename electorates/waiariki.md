## About this electorate

Waiariki is the Māori electorate of the Bay of Plenty, covering population centres such as Tauranga, Whakatāne, and Rotorua. The seat is held by current Te Pāti Māori co-leader Rawiri Waititi who won in 2023 with 21,500 votes and 36.7% of the party vote. Since its establishment, the seat has been a stronghold for Te Pāti Māori. A Labour candidate has only won once since 2005.
