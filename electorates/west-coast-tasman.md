## About this electorate

West Coast-Tasman stretches down almost the entirety of the South Island’s West Coast, starting from the Tasman region at Motueka, going past Westport, Greymouth, and Hokitika, before finishing at Haast. The seat is held by National’s Maureen Pugh, a list MP who became an electorate MP in the 2023 election, but is retiring in 2026. Pugh was elected with a margin of 13,317 in 2023. National won the party vote in 2023 with 33% of the vote, followed by Labour with 24%. Geographically, it is the largest general 
electorate in the country.

_Description quoted from [policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/west-coast-tasman-electorate)._

**Fact-check notes on the description above** (checked against official 2023 results):

- "margin of 13,317" (that figure is the winner’s vote total) — the official 2023 majority was 1,017 votes over Damien O'Connor.
