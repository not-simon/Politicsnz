## About this electorate

Northcote covers many of Auckland’s northern suburbs, including Birkenhead, Glenfield, and Hillcrest. The seat is currently held by National’s Dan Bidois, who was first elected in 2023 (though he also held the seat between 2018 and 2020 after a by-election before losing it in 2020). Bidois won with a margin of 20,807. National also won the party vote with a margin of 43. The electorate has some of the highest proportion of residents aged between 30-45 in the country

_Description quoted from [policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/northcote-electorate)._

**Fact-check notes on the description above** (checked against official 2023 results):

- "margin of 20,807" (that figure is the winner’s vote total) — the official 2023 majority was 9,270 votes over Shanan Halbert.
- "margin of 43" in the party vote should be read as 43% (National’s party-vote share).
