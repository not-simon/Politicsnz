## About this electorate

Napier covers much of the land surrounding the Ngaruroro River and Wairoa district, including the towns of Napier, Wairoa, and Mahia. The electorate is held by National’s Katie Nimon, who was first elected in 2023 with a margin of 21,344 votes. National also won the party vote with 41% of the vote, followed by Labour with 26%. The electorate’s very first MP, Henry Powning Stark, holds the record for the shortest time in parliament: he was elected on 19 February 1861 and resigned on 27 April 1861, lasting 67 days.
