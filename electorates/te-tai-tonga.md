## About this electorate

Te Tai Tonga is the electorate of the South Island. It covers South Island, Stewart Island, the Chatham Islands, and parts of both Wellington City and the Hutt Valley. It is currently held by Tākuta Ferris who won in 2023 as a member of Te Pāti Māori. Ferris did so with 12,828 votes, and 22.69% of the party vote. Following a falling out with Te Pāti Māori leadership, Ferris is running as an independent candidate. The seat had traditionally been a strong seat for the Labour Party, with Rino Tirikatene winning from 2011 through until 2023. Tirikatene has recently joined New Zealand First, whose candidate Tutekawa Wyllie won the seat in 1996 and 1999.
