## About this electorate

Wairarapa covers the south-eastern corner of the North Island, stretching from Waipawa and Waipukurau in the North to Masterton, Martinborough, and Lake Ōnonke in the south. The seat is held by National’s Mike Butterick, who was first elected in 2023 with a margin of 20,313. National won the party vote in 2023 with 39%, followed by Labour with 27%. Between 1999 and 2005, Georgina Beyer, New Zealand’s first transgender MP, held the seat.
