## About this electorate

Mount Roskill covers many of Auckland’s western suburbs, including Hillsborough, Lynfield, and Blockhouse Bay. The seat is held by National’s Carlos Cheung, who was first elected in 2023 with a margin of 15,659 votes. National also won the party vote with 43% of the vote, while Labour was second with 30%. Amusingly, the eponymous suburb of Mount Roskill is actually split between the Mount Albert and Mount Roskill electorates.

_Description quoted from [policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/mt-roskill-electorate)._

**Fact-check notes on the description above** (checked against official 2023 results):

- "margin of 15,659" (that figure is the winner’s vote total) — the official 2023 majority was 1,564 votes over Michael Wood.
