## About this electorate

Banks Peninsula covers the area to the east and south of Christchurch, including Akaroa, Lyttleton, and southern Christchurch suburbs such as Oaklands, Woolston, and Kennedys Bush. It is held by National’s Vanessa Weenink, who narrowly won the electorate for the first time in 2023 with a margin of 17,860. National also won the party vote with 32% of the vote, followed by Labour at 27%. The electorate had the highest turnout of any electorate at the 2023 election.
