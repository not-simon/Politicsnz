**Boundary status:** reconfigured — predecessor seat(s): Tauranga, Bay of Plenty

> Substantially reconfigured: the seat moved westwards and lost Mount Maunganui to the new Mt Maunganui electorate; an area in the lower Kaimai Range stays in Tauranga.

## About this electorate

Tauranga covers the majority of urban Tauranga while also stretching west to cover communities such as Minden and Te Puna, and also south into the Kaimai Ranges. The seat is held by National’s Sam Uffindell, who was first elected in a 2022 by-election, and was re-elected in 2023 with a margin of 18,980. National won the party vote in 2023 with 46%, followed by Labour on 18%. Between 1984 and 2002, the seat was held by current New Zealand First leader Winston Peters, first as a National MP before founding New Zealand First in 1993.
