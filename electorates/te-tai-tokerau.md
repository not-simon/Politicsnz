## About this electorate

Te Tai Tokerau is the northernmost Māori electorate. It covers the Northland region, Auckland’s North Shore and cuts through West Auckland suburbs. The seat is held by Mariameno Kapa-Kingi, who was first elected in 2023 as a member of Te Pāti Māori. Kapa-Kingi won with 10,428 votes and 26% of the party vote and is now running as the leader of Te Tai Tokerau Party, having fallen out with Te Pāti Māori leadership.

## Sitting MP

**[Mariameno Kapa-Kingi](../parties/te-tai-tokerau-party/people/mariameno-kapa-kingi/)** (Te Tai Tokerau Party)  
Standing again here in 2026: **yes**

Elected for Te Pāti Māori in 2023; expelled from the party on 10 November 2025, readmitted after a court ruling, then left in May 2026 to launch the Te Tai Tokerau Party, which she leads.
