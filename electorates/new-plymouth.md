## About this electorate

New Plymouth covers much of the area around Mount Taranaki, including New Plymouth, Ōpunake, and Egmont Village. The electorate is held by National’s David MacLeod, who was first elected in 2023 with a margin of 22,483 votes. National also won the party vote with 42% of the vote, followed by Labour with 24%. The electorate has the highest number of employed miners in the country, with 2.6% of the population in the mining industry compared to 0.2% nationally.
