## About this electorate

Taupō covers the eponymous lake and the towns surrounding it, while also stretching northward to also include towns such as Tokoroa, Putāruru, and Cambridge. The seat is held by National’s Louise Upston and who was re-elected in 2023 with a margin of 24,858. National won the party vote with 45% of the vote, followed by Labour with 22%. The electorate is the only general electorate to have never returned a list MP, though that looks set to change this election, with Labour’s Chris Flatt 20th on his party’s list.
