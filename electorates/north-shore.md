## About this electorate

North Shore covers many of Auckland's northern suburbs, including Devonport, Takapuna, and Mairangi Bay.   
The electorate is held by National’s Simon Watts, who was first elected in 2020 and won re-election in 2023 with a margin of 24,892. National won the party vote with 51% in 2023, followed by Labour on 18%. The very first MP for the electorate between 1946 and 1949 was from Labour—National have won it in every election since.
