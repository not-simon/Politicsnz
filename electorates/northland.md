## About this electorate

Northland covers the northernmost section of Aotearoa, including the population centres of Kaitaia, Kerikeri, and Dargaville. The seat is currently held by National’s Grant McCallum, who was first elected in 2023 with a margin of 16,272. National won the party vote with 35%, followed by Labour with 22%. The electorate has the highest percentage of those engaged in voluntary work in the country, with 22% of the electorate volunteering in some form compared to 15.2% nationally.
