## About this electorate

Hamilton West covers Hamilton’s western half, up until the Waikato River which dives it from Hamilton East. It covers suburbs such as Nawtown, Fitzroy, and Melville. Tama Potaka has been the MP since winning the seat in a 2022 by-election and won re-election with a margin of 17,595. National also won the party vote in 2023 with 41% of the vote, followed by Labour on 26%. The seat is regarded as a bellwether seat, as with the exception of 1993, the seat has always been won by the party which won the plurality of the vote.
