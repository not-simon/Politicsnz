## About this electorate

Waikato covers much of the area between Tauranga and Hamilton, including communities such as Morrinsville, Matamata, and Huntly. The seat is held by National’s Tim van de Molen, who was first elected in 2017 and was re-elected in 2023 with a margin of 27,335. National won the party vote in 2023 with 50%, followed by Labour on 18%. The seat boasted the odd distinction of being the birthplace of both 2020 Labour and National leaders Jacinda Ardern and Judith Collins, though neither ran in the electorate.
