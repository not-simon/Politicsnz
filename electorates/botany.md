## About this electorate

Botany covers much of East Auckland, including the suburbs of East Tamaki, Flat Bush, Somerville, and Botany. Since 2020, it has been held by Prime Minister Christopher Luxon, who easily won re-election with a margin of 24,769 votes. National also won a majority of the party vote at 58%, more than double second-placed Labour’s 22%. The electorate is diverse, boasting a higher proportional Pasifika and Asian population than the rest of New Zealand.
