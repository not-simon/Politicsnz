## About this electorate

Kaikōura covers the Northeast section of the South Island, including the towns of Blenheim, Hanmer Springs, and Goose Bay. The electorate is currently held by National’s Stuart Smith, who first won the seat in 2014 and was re-elected with a margin of 20,741 votes. National also won the party vote with 41%, while Labour was in second with 21%. This electorate has only ever been won by National.

_Description quoted from [policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/kaikoura-electorate)._
