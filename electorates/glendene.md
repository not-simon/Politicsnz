# Glendene electorate (general)

**Region:** Auckland  
**Election day:** Saturday 7 November 2026  
**Boundary status:** new — predecessor seat(s): Kelston, Te Atatū, New Lynn

> New seat created from about half of the former Te Atatū and Kelston electorates plus a small area around Avondale Racecourse from New Lynn (West Auckland reconfiguration that abolished New Lynn, Kelston and Te Atatū). 2023 result shown is Kelston, the seat of the MP now standing here.

## About this electorate

Glendene covers west Auckland suburbs, including Avondale, Kelston, and the Te Atatū peninsula. The electorate was founded for the first time in 2026, though its predecessor electorates of Te Atatū and Kelston were only ever held by Labour in the MMP era. In 2023, the now disestablished seat of Kelston was won by Labour's Carmel Sepuloni, who is running in the Glendene seat in 2026.
