# Mt Maunganui electorate (general)

**Region:** Bay of Plenty  
**Election day:** Saturday 7 November 2026  
**Boundary status:** renamed — predecessor seat(s): Bay of Plenty, Tauranga

> Bay of Plenty was renamed Mt Maunganui after gaining the suburb of Mount Maunganui from Tauranga; Tauranga and Bay of Plenty were substantially reconfigured.

## About this electorate

Mount Maunganui covers the eastern side of Tauranga and its satellite towns, including Pāpāmoa, Welcome Bay, and Matapihi. The electorate is being contested for the first time in 2026, though its predecessor electorate Bay of Plenty was won by National’s Tom Rutherford in 2023 with a margin of 23,303. Rutherford is standing again in Mount Maunganui. National won the Bay of Plenty party vote with 47% of the vote, followed by Labour on 17%.

_Description quoted from [policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/mt-maunganui-electorate)._

**Fact-check notes on the description above** (checked against official 2023 results):

- "margin of 23,303" (that figure is the winner’s vote total) — the official 2023 majority was 15,405 votes over Pare Taikato.
