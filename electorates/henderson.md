# Henderson electorate (general)

**Region:** Auckland  
**Election day:** Saturday 7 November 2026  
**Boundary status:** new — predecessor seat(s): Te Atatū, Upper Harbour, Kaipara ki Mahurangi

> Recreated seat (last used 1993–96) taking Swanson, Rānui, Western Heights, Henderson and Lincoln from Te Atatū, Massey from Upper Harbour and Birdwood Heights from Kaipara ki Mahurangi. Proposed name was Rānui. 2023 result shown is Te Atatū.

## About this electorate

Henderson covers Auckland’s western suburbs, including the areas of Rānui, Lincoln, and Swanson. The electorate has been re-formed for the 2026 election, having not existed since 1993. However, both the historical Henderson electorate and its predecessor Te Atatū and Kelston electorates were almost always held by Labour.
