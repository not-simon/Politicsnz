## About this electorate

East Coast Bays covers the north east areas of Auckland city, covering suburbs such as Albany, Pinehill, and Lucas Creek. It has been held by National’s Erica Stanford since 2017, who won re-election in 2023 with a margin of 27,687 votes. National won a majority of the party vote in 2023 with 58%, while Labour was a distant second at 14%. The electorate was one of the few electorates to be won by a third party in the pre-MPP era, with Social Credit holding the seat between 1980 and 1984.
