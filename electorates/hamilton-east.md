## About this electorate

Hamilton East covers Hamilton’s urban eastern half, up to the Waikato River and covers suburbs such as Claudelands, Flagstaff, and Enderly. National’s Ryan Hamilton is the current MP and was first elected in 2023 with a margin of 17,950. National won the party vote in 2023 with 43% of the vote, followed by Labour with 25%. RyanHamilton is the first MP in New Zealand history to have the same last name as his electorate.

_Description quoted from [policy.nz](https://policy.nz/2026/hamilton-east-electorate)._

**Fact-check notes on the description above** (checked against official 2023 results):

- "margin of 17,950" (that figure is the winner’s vote total) — the official 2023 majority was 5,060 votes over Georgie Dansey.
