# Enrol or update online
To vote in a general election you need to be enrolled by midnight, 13 days before Election Day.
For the 2026 General Election this means you can't enrol or update your details after midnight, 25 October 2026.
## I want to check or update
Choose this if you...
- want to check or update your details online, or
- have a New Zealand driver licence, New Zealand passport or RealMe verified identity
## I want to enrol to vote
- want to enrol online
## I want to upload a form
Got a paper enrolment form to return?
Take a photo or scan it and upload it here.
Do not use this form to upload your overseas voting papers.
