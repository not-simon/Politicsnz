# Election protocols
We’re supported by agencies across the public sector to deliver the 2026 General Election.
Protocols are developed before each general election setting out the roles and responsibilities of different government agencies.
2026 General Election Protocols [2026 General Election Protocols -> https://elections.nz/guidance-and-rules/election-protocols/2026-election-protocols]
In addition, this year a new inter-agency group has been established to strengthen coordination and preparedness across government and support public confidence in the election.
Inter-Agency Coordination Group for the 2026 General Election [Inter-Agency Coordination Group for the  -> https://elections.nz/guidance-and-rules/election-protocols/inter-agency-coordination-group]
Government agencies have also prepared privacy and security advice for candidates standing in the 2026 General Election.
Security advice for candidates [Security advice for candidates -> https://elections.nz/guidance-and-rules/candidate-hub/resources/security-advice-for-2023-election-candidates]
