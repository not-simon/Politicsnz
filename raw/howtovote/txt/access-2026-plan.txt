# Access 2026: accessibility action plan for the 2026 General Election
Access 2026 is the Electoral Commission’s accessibility action plan for the 2026 General Election.
It lists the accessible services available to support Deaf and disabled people, tāngata Turi, tāngata whaikaha Māori, and their whānau, carers and communities to take part in the 2026 General Election.
Access 2026 includes 20 actions, which are accessible services available to support people to work at the election, get election information, enrol, vote and stand as a parliamentary candidate.
Access 2026 is available below as a PDF, an accessible Word document and accessible summaries.
