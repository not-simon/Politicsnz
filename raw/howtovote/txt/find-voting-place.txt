# Find a voting place
Find voting places near you. You can vote at any voting place in New Zealand.
The days and opening hours for each voting place may vary.
Not all voting places are open every day. Some voting places are only open before election day, and some voting places are only open on election day.
Please check the opening hours of your preferred voting place before you go to vote.
On election day, Saturday 7 November, all voting places will be open from 9am to 7pm.
### Find a voting place on a map
You can search for voting places near you on an interactive map.
Search for your address then click on the map pins near you to find the details of your local voting places.
You can filter the map by date or by accessibility.
