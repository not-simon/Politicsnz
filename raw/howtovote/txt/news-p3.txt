# Media & news Te hunga pāpāho me ngā rongo kōrero
### 31 Aug – 20 Sep emails and texts [31 Aug – 20 Sep emails and texts -> https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/31-aug-20-sep-emails-and-texts]
Emails and text messages are being sent to voters from 31 August to 20 September reminding them to check their enrolment details are up to date for the 2026 General Election.
### 24 August notice of texts [24 August notice of texts -> https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/24-august-notice-of-texts]
The Electoral Commission is sending text messages to voters whose enrolment details are out of date on the electoral roll.
### Radio ad referral to police [Radio ad referral to police -> https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/radio-ad-referral-to-police-2]
On 20 August 2026 the Electoral Commission referred MediaWorks and NZME to the Police over an advertisement broadcast outside the election period.
### 17,000 Māori voters change rolls [17,000 Māori voters change rolls -> https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/new-media-and-news-page-2]
Thousands of Māori voters have taken the opportunity to change electoral rolls or update their details before this year’s general election.
### Enrol to vote by 25 October [Enrol to vote by 25 October -> https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/enrol-to-vote-by-25-october]
3.6 million enrolment packs will be posted out to enrolled voters from the start of next week so they can check their details are up to date for the 2026 General Election on 7 November.
### Start of the regulated period [Start of the regulated period -> https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/start-of-the-regulated-period]
Spending limits apply for parties, candidates and third party promoters who publish election advertisements during the regulated period.
### Variation to 2026 Broadcasting Allocation Decision Released [Variation to 2026 Broadcasting Allocatio -> https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/variation-to-2026-broadcasting-allocation-decision-released]
Under section 80 of the Broadcasting Act, the Electoral Commission determined to vary the amount of money allocated to parties in its Broadcasting Allocation Decision of 7 May 2026.
### Decisions on party registrations and logos [Decisions on party registrations and log -> https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/decisions-on-party-registrations-and-logos]
Four new parties have been added to the list of registered political parties in New Zealand.
### Time running out for Māori to change rolls [Time running out for Māori to change rol -> https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/time-running-out-for-maori-to-change-rolls]
Voters of Māori descent who would like to change electoral rolls before the election have one more week to make their choice.
