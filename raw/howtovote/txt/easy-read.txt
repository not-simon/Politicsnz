## Access 2026
Access 2026 is the Electoral Commission’s accessibility action plan for the 2026 General Election.
Read a summary of the action plan in Easy Read format. You can also find two quick Easy Read-type guides about enrolling and voting for the 2026 General Election.
Visit elections.nz/access2026 to view the full action plan. [elections.nz/access2026 -> https://elections.nz/access2026]
