# Find an overseas voting place
If you're overseas, you may be able to vote in person at an overseas voting place.
Use the lookup below to find locations and opening hours for overseas voting places near you.
Overseas voting services are available from Wednesday 21 October.
It's important to check the opening hours of your preferred voting place before you go to vote.
The opening hours and closing dates for each overseas voting place are different. Some overseas voting places are closed on specific days, and some are available by appointment only. Overseas voting places are typically closed on weekends.
In addition, some overseas voting places offer postal voting services only. This means in-person voting is not available at these locations.
All overseas voting places are closed on election day, Saturday 7 November.
If you're not able to get to an overseas voting place, the best way to vote from overseas is to download your voting papers from our website, complete them, then upload them.
