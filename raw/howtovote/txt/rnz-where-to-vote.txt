# Where you can vote in advance and on election day
There will be more than 2300 voting locations open on election day - 7 November.
The Electoral Commission says of these, 67 will be bilingual - voters can go through the voting process in te reo Māori or English - and six will have sign language interpreters.
There will be 1679 fully accessible polling locations, and three locations with quiet times.
"Our voting places are in locations people go in their daily lives including community halls, schools, shopping malls, places of worship, tertiary education providers and marae," chief electoral officer Karl Le Quesne said.
Though votes would be taken at a total of 2638 locations during the voting period, only 2315 will be open on election day on 7 November.
Advance voting will begin on Monday 26 October, the day after enrolments close.
### Where to vote
A list of voting places is available online and at local supermarkets. [available online -> https://vote.nz/2026-general-election/voting-in-the-election/find-a-voting-place]
Le Quesne said the days and times voting places will be open during advance voting varies, so people are asked to check before they go to vote.
"During advance voting, look out for a list of your nearest voting places at your local supermarket as this is a really easy way to find out where to vote in your community. People can also call us for free on 0800 36 76 56 and our team will be ready to help you find your nearest voting place.'
"Anyone who cannot make it to a voting place should also give us a call. We have alternative voting options available."
There is information for people who need help to vote here . [here -> https://vote.nz/2026-general-election/voting-in-the-election/need-help-to-vote]
