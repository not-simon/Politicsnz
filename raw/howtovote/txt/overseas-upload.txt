# Uploading your voting papers
If you’re overseas you can upload your voting papers for the 2026 General Election.
Overseas voting for the 2026 General Election opened on Wednesday 21 October 2026 (NZ time).
Please don't use this service to upload enrolment forms. Enrolment forms uploaded here will not be processed before the election. If you have a completed enrolment form to return, you can upload it here:
Upload an enrolment form [Upload an enrolment form -> https://vote.nz/upload]
If you're returning your voting papers by uploading them, they must be uploaded by 7pm on election day, Saturday 7 November (NZ time). Late voting papers cannot be accepted.
Upload your voting papers [Upload your voting papers -> https://uvp.elections.govt.nz/]
Your upload could be a scan or photograph of your printed and completed voting papers, or the saved file of your voting papers if you have completed them electronically.
IMPORTANT: Before returning your voting papers, make sure both you and your witness have correctly completed and signed your special vote declaration, or your vote may not be counted. We cannot accept typed signatures, including any signature using a handwriting font .
If you're uploading a scan or photograph of printed voting papers, please make sure that no part of your voting papers have been cropped or cut off.
You can find detailed instructions about how to complete your voting papers here . [here -> https://vote.nz/2026-general-election/overseas/how-to-complete-your-voting-papers]
You can only upload your voting papers if you’re overseas. You can only upload your own voting papers.
Your voting papers consist of:
- Your completed declaration form, and
- Your ballot paper.
You must return your declaration with your ballot paper in order for your vote to count.
You can only upload your own voting papers.
Check your voting papers before you submit them. Submitting your voting papers more than once may mean that your vote will not count.
If you’ve completed your voting papers electronically, you either need to draw your signature (e.g. using your device’s touchscreen) or insert an image of your signature.
We cannot accept typed signatures or digital certificates.
The easiest way to get your voting papers is to download them.
Downloading your voting papers [Downloading your voting papers -> https://vote.nz/2026-general-election/overseas/downloading-your-voting-papers]
You can scan or photograph your completed declaration form and ballot paper, or if you completed your voting papers electronically, you just need to select the image file or PDF file that you saved of your completed voting papers.
How to complete your voting papers [How to complete your voting papers -> https://vote.nz/2026-general-election/overseas/how-to-complete-your-voting-papers]
Make sure your images are clear enough to read and show the whole page clearly before you upload them - if they aren’t clear, your vote may not count.
We need to be able to read all the contents of the declaration form, especially:
- the seven-digit number near the top right-hand corner of the page
- your details and signature
- your witness’ details and signature; and
Your declaration form must show your normal signature. We cannot accept typed signatures or digital signatures.
On the ballot paper, we need to be able to read:
- your voting choice.
If you received your voting papers by post, you’ll need to remove the black sticker that will be on the ballot paper before you scan or photograph them so we can see the seven-digit number near the top of the right-hand corner of the page.
Your images will need to be saved as a JPG, PNG, GIF, PDF or TIFF. If you upload the wrong type of file, you’ll see the message 'This file type is not supported’. You’ll need to remove that file from the list to continue.
Each file must be at least 10 KB and no larger than 10 MB.
We’ve designed this upload service to work with the latest versions of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, and Safari.
Uploading your voting papers will automatically encrypt them. The only people who can decrypt your voting papers are electoral officials who work in a secure area and have signed secrecy agreements.
However, we can’t guarantee the security of the computer, scanner, or other device or network you may use to scan, photograph or upload your voting papers.
To make sure your vote stays secret, we recommend you delete any copies of voting papers from your devices and securely throw away the paper copies.
Some organisations may monitor the information you scanned or upload to the internet on their networks.
We handle your information in line with the Privacy Act 2020.
For more information on how we handle your information visit:
Privacy & security [Privacy & security -> https://vote.nz/privacy-and-security]
You can post or hand deliver your voting papers to your nearest overseas voting place.
If you’re still having trouble, you can email overseas@vote.nz for advice. [overseas@vote.nz -> mailto:overseas@vote.nz]
We don’t accept voting papers by email.
Once you’ve uploaded your papers, you’ll receive a message saying you’ve either succeeded or failed to upload. You will get a reference number in either case. You can also request an email receipt.
If your upload wasn’t successful, try again.  If it still isn’t working, you can email overseas@vote.nz with your reference number for further assistance. [overseas@vote.nz -> mailto:overseas@vote.nz]
