It’s easy to vote from overseas and have your say in the 2026 General Election.
Overseas voting for the 2026 General Election starts on Wednesday 21 October. You won't be able to vote or to get your voting papers before this date.
Overseas voting using the download service closes at 7pm on election day, Saturday 7 November (NZ time).
## Vote before you go
Voting within New Zealand starts on Monday 26 October, and ends at 7pm on election day, Saturday 7 November. If you're in New Zealand during this time you can vote at any voting place in your electorate.
## Vote from overseas
The best way to vote from overseas is to download your voting papers from our website, complete them, then upload them. A link to download your voting papers will be available on this website from Wednesday 21 October.
To download your voting papers, you must be enrolled.
The last day to enrol to vote in the 2026 General Election is Sunday 25 October (NZ time).
Downloading your voting papers [Downloading your voting papers -> https://vote.nz/2026-general-election/overseas/downloading-your-voting-papers]
Uploading your voting papers [Uploading your voting papers -> https://vote.nz/2026-general-election/overseas/uploading-your-voting-papers]
You may also be able to vote in person at an overseas voting place.
Find an overseas voting place [Find an overseas voting place -> https://vote.nz/2026-general-election/overseas/find-an-overseas-voting-place]
You can enrol and vote in the 2026 General Election from overseas if all the following statements are true:
- you are 18 years or older
- you’ve lived in New Zealand for more than 12 months continuously at some time in your life
- you are one of the following: a New Zealand citizen who has been in New Zealand within the last 3 years a New Zealand resident for electoral purposes who has been in New Zealand within the last 12 months.
- a New Zealand citizen who has been in New Zealand within the last 3 years
- a New Zealand resident for electoral purposes who has been in New Zealand within the last 12 months.
Enrolling from overseas is not compulsory — it’s your choice whether you enrol.
### Are you a resident for electoral purposes?
You're a resident for electoral purposes if you're eligible to live in New Zealand lawfully and you're not required to leave within a specified time. This includes someone on a resident visa.
### Are you Niuean, Tokelauan, or a Cook Islander?
If you’re Niuean, Tokelauan, or a Cook Islander, you can vote if you’re enrolled, you’ve lived in New Zealand continuously for 12 months or more, and you’ve visited New Zealand in the last 3 years.
### Are you Australian?
If you’re Australian you can vote if you’re enrolled, you’ve lived in New Zealand continuously for 12 months or more, and you’ve visited New Zealand in the last 12 months.
### Are you a public servant or member of the New Zealand Defence Force
You’re eligible to enrol and vote no matter how long you’ve been outside New Zealand if you’re in one of the following groups:
- Public servants working overseas and their families
- New Zealand diplomats or trade representatives and their families
- Members of the New Zealand Defence Force and their families.
Depending on when you last visited New Zealand, you may not be able to enrol or update your details online. If you find you're not able to update your details online, please contact us at overseas@vote.nz . [overseas@vote.nz -> mailto:overseas@vote.nz]
### Contact us if you’re no longer eligible
If you don’t meet the requirements above, please contact us to tell us you’re no longer eligible.
Email: overseas@vote.nz [overseas@vote.nz -> mailto:overseas@vote.nz]
Before you can vote, you must be enrolled to vote. If you’re already enrolled, make sure your details are up to date.
It’s easy to enrol or update your details online using your New Zealand driver licence, New Zealand passport or RealMe verified identity.
Enrol or update your details online [Enrol or update your details online -> https://vote.nz/enrolling/enrol-or-update/enrol-or-update-online]
To vote from overseas in the 2026 General Election, you must enrol (or update your enrolment) by midnight, Sunday 25 October (NZ time). You won't be able to enrol or update after this date.
If you're a member of the New Zealand Defence Force who is stationed overseas, you're not required to enrol in order to vote - but if you're not enrolled, you won't be able to download your voting papers.
Your voting papers are not automatically sent to you overseas.
The easiest and quickest way to vote from overseas is to download your voting papers. Before you download your voting papers you need to be correctly enrolled.
If you're on the unpublished roll you will not be able to download your voting papers. Please contact us on overseas@vote.nz for further assistance. [overseas@vote.nz -> mailto:overseas@vote.nz]
You may also be able to vote at an overseas voting place.
Tick the circle next to the party you choose. You can only vote for one party.
Tick the circle next to the name of the electorate candidate you choose. You can only vote for one candidate.
Overseas voters and their witnesses can complete their voting papers using an electronic device or by inserting a digital signature, as long as it shows their normal signature – i.e. written in their own hand.
You may also choose to print your voting papers and complete them by hand, then scan or photograph and upload them.
How to complete your voting papers [How to complete your voting papers -> https://vote.nz/2026-general-election/overseas/how-to-complete-your-voting-papers]
When you’ve marked your voting papers, return them to us in one of the following ways:
- Upload them online
- Post or hand deliver them to your nearest overseas voting place.
Please contact us on overseas@vote.nz if you are not able to do so. [overseas@vote.nz -> mailto:overseas@vote.nz]
You cannot return your voting papers by email or fax, or by posting them to the Electoral Commission in New Zealand.
If you're uploading your voting papers, you must return them by 7pm (NZ time) on election day.
Your voting papers will include full information on how to return them.

### Telephone dictation voting
If you’re enrolled to vote and you’re blind, partially blind or have another physical disability that prevents you from marking your voting paper without assistance, you’re eligible to vote by telephone dictation.
You will need to register to use the telephone dictation service, even if you've used it at previous elections.
Telephone dictation voting [Telephone dictation voting -> https://vote.nz/2026-general-election/voting-in-the-election/cant-get-to-a-voting-place/telephone-dictation-voting]

### Voting in local elections
You can’t download or return voting papers electronically for local elections. Local elections are run by local councils - for any questions about voting in local elections, you should contact your council's electoral officer.
