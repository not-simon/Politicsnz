# Enrolment updates
Enrolment for the 2026 General Election closes at midnight, Sunday 25 October. It will take time to process all the enrolments and updates received by the deadline, including forms that arrive in the post on Tuesday 27 October.
These are current 'snapshot' enrolment figures and are intended to provide an indication of enrolment progress during the election period.
They should not be regarded as final General Election 2026 enrolment results.
As processing of enrolment forms and data quality assurance activities continue following 25 October 2026, these figures will differ to the  final enrolment statistics published in official results.
The enrolment rate is also subject to change as updates are made to both the electoral roll and the estimated eligible population used to calculate the rate. As a result, movements in the rate may reflect changes to either or both components.

### Definitions
Total electors - the total number of people enrolled on the main general and Māori roll as at the reporting date.
Enrolment rate - the percentage of the estimated eligible voting population that is enrolled to vote.
18-24 enrolment rate - the percentage of eligible 18 to 24-year olds who are enrolled to vote.
Number of Māori electors - the total number of people enrolled to vote who have identified themselves as being of Māori descent. This includes people on both the Māori and general roll.
Number of electors on the Māori roll - the total number of people who are enrolled on the Māori roll.
