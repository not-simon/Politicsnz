# Contact our Enrolment team
Our Enrolment Processing teams look after the electoral roll for each electorate.
Printed copies of electoral rolls are available at selected public libraries (contact your local library for more details) or at Electoral Commission offices.
Find a library (Public Libraries of New Zealand) [Find a library -> https://publiclibraries.org.nz/find-a-library]
## Resources
To order enrolment resources such as enrolment forms, brochures or posters, please complete our order form. Many of our resources are also available as digital downloads.
Get brochures, posters and forms [Get brochures, posters and forms -> https://elections.nz/getting-involved/order-resources]
