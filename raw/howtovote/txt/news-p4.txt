### Take a moment before you share information [Take a moment before you share informati -> https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/take-a-moment-before-you-share-information]
The Electoral Commission is advising people to slow down and take a moment to check information about enrolling and voting before sharing it this election.
### 21 July notice of text messages [21 July notice of text messages -> https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/21-july-notice-of-text-messages]
If you’re Māori, you may receive a text message from the Electoral Commission between Tuesday 21 and Friday 24 July about the deadline to change electoral rolls before the election.
### Join the 2026 General Election team [Join the 2026 General Election team -> https://elections.nz/media-and-news/new-media-and-news]
Tens of thousands of New Zealanders are expected to apply to work in the 2026 General Election as recruitment gets underway today.
### Change to New Zealand First Logo [Change to New Zealand First Logo -> https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/change-to-new-zealand-first-logo]
The Electoral Commission has approved an application to register a substitute party logo for the New Zealand First Party (NZ First).
### 6 July notice of emails and texts [6 July notice of emails and texts -> https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/6-july-notice-of-emails-and-texts]
Emails and text messages are being sent to voters of Māori descent between 6 and 19 July about the deadline to change electoral rolls before the 2026 General Election.
### Email and text reminders - Māori Electoral Option [Email and text reminders - Māori Elector -> https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/email-and-text-reminders-maori-electoral-option]
Voters of Māori descent are being sent email and text message reminders about the 6 August deadline to change electoral rolls before this year’s General Election.
### Application to change party name and logo [Application to change party name and log -> https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/application-to-change-party-name-and-logo]
The Electoral Commission is considering an application from the New Zealand Outdoors & Freedom Party to change the party name and logo.
### Ngā īmera w’akama’ara me ngā pātu’i w’akama’ara - Kōw’iringa Rārangi Pōti Māori [Ngā īmera w’akama’ara me ngā pātu’i w’ak -> https://elections.nz/media-and-news/new-media-and-news-page-2]
E tukuna ana ki te ‘unga kaipōti Māori ‘e pātu’i, ‘e īmera w'akama'ara mō te 6 o Āku’ata, ko te rangi w’akamutunga ‘ei w’akaw’iti rārangi pōti i mua i te Pōtitanga ā-Motu o tēnei tau.
### Billboard referral to police [Billboard referral to police -> https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/billboard-referral-to-police]
The Electoral Commission has referred an individual to the Police over the publication of two election billboard advertisements that did not contain promoter statements.
