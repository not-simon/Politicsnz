# Party lists
These are the registered political parties which you can vote for with your party vote.
Each party's share of the seats in Parliament is determined by its share of the party vote.
If a party's share of the party vote entitles it to more seats in Parliament than the number of seats won by that party's electorate candidates, the shortfall is made up from candidates from the party's list.
Click on the name of each party to see that party's list candidates.
