The 
New Nation Party
 was a political party in 
New Zealand
. It was first led by Michael Jacomb and later, Guy Slocum. The party opposed 
vaccine mandates
, 
Three Waters
, and the 
United Nations
.

History

The party stood former Tauranga City councillor Andrew Hollis as a candidate at the 
2022 Tauranga by-election
.
 He received 260 votes.

The party applied for registration on 27 March 2023.
 It was registered on 12 April 2023.

The party was previously a component party of 
Freedoms New Zealand
.
 On 21 July 2023, the party notified the 
Electoral Commission
 that it was no longer a component party of Freedoms New Zealand.

Polling conducted for 
1News
, between the party's founding and mid-September 2023, indicated the party's support at between 0.1% and 0.5%.

In the 
2023 general election
, New Nation Party received 0.05% of the party vote – 1,530 votes, the lowest result of any party – and did not win any electorate seats, meaning it did not enter parliament.

On 29 January 2025, the Electoral Commission deregistered the party at its own request.
