Overview
Background Information

The Ipsos New Zealand Issues Monitor tracks which issues New Zealanders are most concerned about,
who is worried about what and which political parties they believe are best able to manage these issues.
During 8-14 July 2026 we conducted fieldwork for our 33rd Ipsos NZ Issues Monitor report with a total of
1,002 New Zealanders aged 18 and older*.


This study did not have any external sponsors or partners. It was initiated and run by Ipsos because we
think it is important for businesses and organisations to understand the challenges that New
Zealanders face in the context of their everyday lives.


At Ipsos we strive to deliver reliable information for a true understanding of society, markets and
people, to help our clients make better decisions.


Please direct all queries to Carin Hercock – 021 394 508 | carin.hercock@ipsos.com or
Amanda Dudding – 021 612 264 | amanda.dudding@ipsos.com


The precision of Ipsos online survey is calculated with a credibility interval with a sample of 1,004 accurate to + / - 3.5 percentage points.
Significantly higher / lower figures are reported at 95% confidence.
Where results do not add up to 100, this may be due to computer rounding, multiple responses or the exclusion of ‘don't know’ or ‘not stated’ responses.

Similar to last wave, Labour is perceived as the party best equipped to manage all of New Zealanders' top 5 concerns of inflation / the cost of living,
healthcare, the economy, housing, and unemployment.

Since the last wave, Labour has overtaken National as the party seen as best able to manage the economy.

security-related concerns such as the economy, taxation, immigration, crime and fuel prices.
While the specific issues vary by life stage, this broader pattern remains largely consistent.
