People of Māori descent were oversampled, as were those 18-31 in age. The data in the frequency tables linked below has been weighted by Māori/General electorates, age, gender (from the rolls), highest educational qualification, and party vote and turnout (corrected from the marked rolls), in order to be as representative as possible of those on the rolls. The full weight is vwgt . Across those freshly sampled for 2023, on a conservative basis, not removing any of the original sample for non-availability, the response rate (weighted to take account of the oversampling) was 22 per cent. Vote variables in the additional takes account of the effects of effects of validation, on which the data was weighted.
