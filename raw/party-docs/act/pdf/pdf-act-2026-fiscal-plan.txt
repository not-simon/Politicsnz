Consequential reduction in financing costs
Lower Financing Costs
Reducing borrowing also reduces the cost of servicing
government debt. These consequential savings grow as
the Plan takes effect, reaching $452 million in 2030/31.
Lower interest bills make future budgets easier to
balance without raising taxes.
