NZFirst Announces Candidates For 2026

Sunday, 19 July 2026, 2:42 pm

Shane Jones – Northland

Shane is the New Zealand First Deputy Leader. He is known for his focus on regional economic development, resources, and infrastructure investment. Proficient in both English and Te Reo, Shane has more than 20 years of experience in public life.

Vince Cocurullo - Whangārei

Vince is a former Mayor of Whangārei. Beyond local government, Vince has led major civic and regional initiatives, including digital safety education, rural connectivity improvements, and innovative waste-to-fuel and carbon removal projects developed with national and international partners.

Jenny Marcroft – Kaipara ki Mahurangi

Jenny is a current New Zealand First MP. She previously had a career spanning over 30 years in the broadcasting industry.

Vaughan Taylor – East Coast Bays

Vaughan was brought up on a farm north of Auckland but spent 21 years in South Korea teaching English. He returned to New Zealand in 2022 and has since worked for Corrections at Paremoremo Prison in Auckland.

Bernadette Soares – Upper Harbour

Bernadette immigrated to New Zealand at the age of 21 with her husband. She then went on to gain a Bachelor of Economics and Commerce and later completed a master’s degree in business Innovation and Entrepreneurship in New Zealand. Bernadette founded an intellectual property and management company specialising in beauty products.

Daryl Habraken - Northcote

Daryl is a proud New Zealander whose values were shaped by a strong rural upbringing steeped in hard work, resilience, personal responsibility, and community spirit. He works within the creative industry, directing the famous ‘Not our first rodeo’ video last election.

David Wilson – Henderson

David is a current New Zealand First MP. He was the Chief Executive Officer of the Northland Regional Council's economic development agency Northland Inc from 2013 to 2017. Simultaneously, he was the Chairman of the Economic Development Association of New Zealand.

Alfred Ngaro – Glendene

Alfred is a former New Zealand Cabinet Minister, community leader, pastor, and governance specialist with more than 35 years' experience.

Sheldon Eden-Whaitiri – Mt Albert

Sheldon is an experienced sports administrator, rugby referee, business leader and community volunteer with a career spanning high-performance sport, commercial leadership and public service.

Martin Gummer – Epsom

Martin is a fifth-generation New Zealander, with a degree in law and economics. He has spent most of his career in energy, manufacturing, and land transport.

John Ryan - Tamaki

John has extensive international experience but also decades of service and support for large New Zealand hospitals.

Keegan Langeveld - Maungakiekie

Keegan is committed to practical, common-sense policies that strengthen communities, support regional New Zealand, and create opportunities for young Kiwis. He has represented the party as its Dunedin candidate in 2023 and, as Chairman of Young New Zealand First since 2021.

Catherine Sommerville-Ryan – Pakuranga

Catherine is an experienced educator, with nearly 20 years' experience teaching in East Auckland secondary schools. She is a passionate believer in the role of education in improving life outcomes.

Vicky Hau - Māngere

Vicky was the Māngere Town Centre Manager, leading one of Auckland’s most diverse community and business hubs. Prior to this role, Vicky worked at the Ministry of Social Development, supporting areas including hardship and financial assistance, housing support, and employment services.

Elliot Ikilei - Takanini

Elliot has been working as a spokesperson for Hobson’s Pledge, where he advocates for equal citizenship, democratic representation, and ‘one law for all’. He is also Youth Worker.

Esther Tofilau-Tevaga - Manurewa

Esther has served as a Northern Regional Trust Board Member for Hato Hone St John.

She is also the Chief Executive Officer of the ASA Foundation Trust, where she leads programmes focused on youth development, education, health awareness, and community resilience throughout South Auckland.

George Ngatai - Papakura

George lives and works in Papakura, and leads teams serving communities from Kaitaia to Dunedin. Married to his wife Raewyn for 27 years, George is a dedicated community leader who has spent decades serving families, supporting local businesses, and advocating for stronger communities.

Vincent Anderson - Coromandel

Vincent is a Waikato businessman and, served as a councillor on the Matamata-Paiko District Council. He has more than two decades of leadership across the technology, procurement, and business sectors.

Casey Costello - Port Waikato

Casey is a current New Zealand First MP, and comes from a career with the police, based in South Auckland, and then Parliament's manager of security and operations. In 2016 she became a founding trustee of Hobson’s Pledge.

Robbie Cookson - Waikato

Robbie was born in Te Aroha and has lived in Springdale his entire life. He is a proud father of two daughters, a fourth-generation dairy farmer, and a Waikato Regional Councillor.

Kevin Stone - Hamilton West

Originally from Auckland, Kevin has a background in hospitality and is now a business owner based out of Hamilton. Kevin has been a loyal member of the New Zealand First team since 1993.

Nicola Laboyrie - Hamilton East

Nicola’s career has included tertiary teaching in business studies at Wintec, running a fishing charter business, and currently owns and operates a 295-hectare beef farm near Te Uku, giving her firsthand insight into the challenges facing rural communities, small businesses, and primary producers.

Tracey Coxhead - Mt Maunganui

Tracey currently serves as a Western Bay of Plenty District Councillor, first elected in 2022 and re-elected in 2025. She brings more than 40 years of leadership experience across banking, financial services, business ownership and local government.

Billy Brown - Rotorua

Billy is a respected infrastructure leader and business executive with more than 25 years' experience delivering major civil construction and infrastructure projects across New Zealand and the Pacific.

Lee Smith - Taranaki-King Country

Lee is an active advocate for rural communities and local democracy. Most recently, she has been involved as a local spokesperson for Groundswell New Zealand, a grassroots movement that advocates for practical policy solutions affecting farmers, rural communities, and regional New Zealand.

Te Kira Lawrence - East Cape

Te Kira is a sitting Napier City Councillor, dedicated community leader, and experienced public servant who has built her career around serving people, strengthening communities, and delivering practical results.

Taine Randall - Tukituki

Born and raised in Hawke's Bay, Taine holds a conjoint degree in Law and Commerce (Economics) from the University of Otago. After graduating, he pursued a professional rugby career, reaching the pinnacle of the sport as captain of the All Blacks. Today, he is involved in the family's investment fund, focused on primary industry and commercial property.

Stuart Nash - Napier

Stuart first entered parliament in 2008 before winning the Napier electorate seat in 2014. He went on to become Minister of Police, Revenue, Small Business and Fisheries as well as Economic and Regional Development, Forestry and Tourism while retaining Small Business.

Harete Hipango-Brownlie - Whanganui

Harete is a former National Party Member of Parliament, lawyer, and dedicated public servant with more than 30 years' experience serving New Zealand communities across law, health, governance and public policy.

Steve Gibson - Rangitikei

Steve served for twenty-four years in the New Zealand and Northern Territory Police before spending ten years as a small business owner. He has also served as both a Manawatu District Councillor and a Hastings District Councillor, building a reputation for speaking plainly and standing up for local communities.

Helma Vermeulen - Palmerston North

Helma Vermeulen was born in the Netherlands and immigrated to New Zealand at 20. Helma is qualified in horticulture, floral art, and small business management & currently runs a property development business with her husband. For the last 6 years, Helma has been actively involved at committee level, on the board and members council.

Penny Gaylor - Wellington

Penny has been a local councillor in the greater Wellington Regional Council since 2016. She brings more than 17 years' experience providing leadership on regional and local issues driving outcomes for economic development, public transport and environmental projects.

Taylor Arneil - Wellington

Taylor spent his childhood in the United Kingdom before moving to Australia and then moved to New Zealand in 2016. He has deep knowledge in the public service, specialising in the policy development space and the Official Information Act from his time as a research assistant for New Zealand First and a contractor across multiple government departments.

Gerald Warner - Wellington

Gerald has over two decades of experience spanning 14 government agencies and over 20 programmes and projects. He specialises in building organisation capability.

Jon Blackshaw - Kenepuru

Jon has been a social worker based in Porirua for the last 25 years, with additional experience in education and disability support.

Andy Foster - Remutaka

Andy is a current New Zealand First MP. He was previously Mayor of Welington from 2019 to 2022 and a Wellington City Councillor for nine terms from 1992 until 2019.

Alistair Boyce - Hutt South

Alistair is a proud Wellingtonian playing an instrumental role in the opening of the Backbencher pub in 1990, before going on to become a co-owner in 1997. Having a long career in hospitality employing and managing over 4500 staff.

Jamie Cleine - West Coast-Tasman

With ten years of local government experience, including six years as Mayor of Buller District, Jamie has also led complex work across emergency management and recovery, community and regional development, health services, and civil infrastructure, to deliver outcomes for people, industry and communities.

Jamie Arbuckle - Kaikōura

Jamie is a New Zealand First Member of Parliament and party whip, who represents the Kaikōura electorate. Jamie previously served as a Marlborough District councillor. Jamie focuses on issues affecting regional communities, including local infrastructure, horticulture, and economic development.

Jackie Farrelly - Waimakariri

Jackie is a tourism business owner with a wide range of experience in voluntary involvement in the community.

Jackie is passionate about tourism and conservation and has a strong belief in regional New Zealand is what drives Jackie's belief in New Zealand First.

Shane Wiremu - Christchurch East

Shane has over 30 years of leadership experience across New Zealand, Australia and China. He brings a strong combination of operational management and public communications skills and political awareness.

Mark Arneil - Christchurch Central

Mark is a Digital IT Business Partner, with a career that spans family-owned businesses, venture capital environments, large multinational organisations, and government sectors. This experience has given him deep insight into the challenges organisations face in a rapidly changing world.

Nathan Walton - Ilam

Nathan is a final-year law student at the University of Canterbury and an emerging conservative leader in Christchurch. He serves as Chair of Young New Zealand First Canterbury, is a member of the Young New Zealand First Executive, and is Secretary of the New Zealand First Canterbury Hub.

Lindsay Kerslake - Banks Peninsula

Lindsay has over 30 years of international leadership experience across bio-energy, digital technologies, tourism ventures, agriculture, and commercial development.

Bryce Park - Wigram

Currently Director of Strategy and Investment at Oakhampton Holdings, Bryce leads complex infrastructure and resource projects across New Zealand. His work includes securing investment partnerships, navigating regulatory processes, engaging with iwi and environmental stakeholders, and overseeing strategic planning for nationally significant developments.

Elizabeth Mundt - Selwyn

Elizabeth is a two-term councillor and has an international background in the equine industry. She was an army wife for almost two decades and has raised three children and fostered nine children concurrently.

Isaac Nicholson - Rangitata

Isaac grew up on a dairy farm on the West Coast, South Island. At the age of 18, he started a plumbing apprenticeship, and has spent 27 years working his way up in the construction industry, worked my way up through the trade to become a Certified Plumber, Gasfitter and Drainlayer.

Michael Laws - Waitaki

Michael served as a Member of Parliament from 1990-1996. He then established a successful career in broadcasting, publishing and journalism. Michael was elected Mayor of Whanganui in 2004 and re-elected in 2007, while also serving three terms on the district health board. Since relocating to Central Otago in 2013, Michael has remained active in public service.

Kym McDonald-King - Dunedin

Kym began her professional career as a registered nurse, spending over a decade in hospital and rural health services. In her current role with the New Zealand First Leader’s Office, she works closely with MPs, community organisations and stakeholders throughout the South Island.

Mark Patterson – Taieri

Mark is a current New Zealand First MP. He has been a farmer his whole life later purchasing a farm in Lawrence Otago with his wife in 2008 and previously served as the Otago Federated Farmers Chair.

Jason Herrick - Southland

Jason is a Southland dairy farmer, business owner, and proven community leader, and most recently Southland Federated Farmers President.
