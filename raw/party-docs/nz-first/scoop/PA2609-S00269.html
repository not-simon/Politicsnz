New Plymouth Deputy Mayor Murray Chong To Stand For NZFirst

Friday, 25 September 2026, 12:47 pm
Press Release: New Zealand First Party

New Zealand First is today announcing that Deputy Mayor of New Plymouth Murray Chong will be standing for the party in the upcoming election.

He is a dedicated local advocate, Deputy Mayor of New Plymouth District Council, previous business owner, and lifelong resident of the New Plymouth District. He comes from several generations of community leaders and advocates.

He has had a previous career as a mortgage broker having been self-employed for 25 years and has served 13 years consecutively on council before being chosen as Deputy Mayor in 2025. He has been the highest polling councilor over the past two elections.

He is popular for his strong beliefs in honest public service, bureaucratic transparency, and prudent use of public money.

He has been a long-standing advocate of the principles held by the New Zealand First Party having previously stood as a candidate in 2017. His campaign is anchored on growing our local economy through a return to oil and gas, protecting national sovereignty and restoring democracy by embracing “one country, one people, one law, one flag”.

As a father and a son, he is also passionate about law and order and dignified aged care for our seniors.

He is proud to stand for New Zealand First – a party committed to putting the interests of everyday New Zealanders first.

A dedicated Deputy Mayor, he will be seeking the Party Vote for our nation in a time of critical need of common-sense policies.
