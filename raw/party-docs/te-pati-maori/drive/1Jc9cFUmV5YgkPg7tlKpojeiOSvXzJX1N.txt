Toi Māori
Toi Māori is the creative expression
of knowledge systems, governance,
communication, spirituality, and survival.
Poverty is the result of deliberate policy choices.
In Te Ao Māori, art is both a tool and source of
authority and holistic wellbeing.
Te Pāti Māori will restore Māori authority over Toi
Māori as a total wellbeing strategy, protecting our
indigenous voice and ensuring our people determine
how it is carried into the future.

Māori creativity has always been inseparable
from oranga Māori. Our carving, weaving,
performance, language, design, and
storytelling are not optional cultural
expressions but systems of survival.
The Crown has relegated Toi Māori to
the margins, treating it as an “arts sector”
under ministries that fragment its value and
underfund its impact. This has constrained
Māori ability to design, protect, and project
our own cultural intellectual property.
Funding inequities remain stark. Te Matatini
reaches tens of thousands in person and
millions through broadcast, yet receives a
fraction of the funding allocated to Western
institutions with smaller audiences.
This imbalance reflects a failure to recognise
that Toi Māori as central to Māori wellbeing.
Toi Māori is a total wellbeing strategy for
Māori.
It sustains identity, strengthens language,
restores connection to whenua, supports
physical and mental health, creates economic
opportunity, and carries mātauranga across
generations.
Māori must be the sole determinants of how
our cultural intellectual property is protected
and projected, at home and internationally.

Te Pāti Māori will...
1. Establish Māori Control of Toi Māori
Establish an independent Toi Māori authority,
Māori-led and Māori-controlled.
• Operate the authority on a commissioning
model dedicated to all forms of Toi Māori.
• Ensure decision-making power sits with
Māori.
2. • Secure Statutory Status and Funding
Parity
• Make the Toi Māori authority a Statutory
Body.
• Place it on the Lottery Grants Board.
• Fund it at parity with Creative NZ.
3. Invest in Evidence for Oranga Māori
• Establish a $10m Toi Māori research fund.
• Build a robust evidence base demonstrating
Toi Māori’s contribution to:
• Oranga Māori
• Education
• Health
• Social cohesion
• Economic outcomes.
• Use this evidence to secure equitable
funding across all sectors.
4. • Strengthen Kapa Haka and Community
Toi
• Ensure $19m baseline funding to Te
Matatini.
• Allocate $10m to community, hapū, and iwi
kapa haka and associated art forms.
• Recognise kapa haka as a central
transmission pathway for language,
identity, and wellbeing.
5. Protect and Project Indigenous Voice
• Ensure Māori control how Toi Māori is
presented locally and internationally.
• Protect Māori cultural intellectual property
from exploitation.
• Reject Crown ownership or mediation of
Māori creativity.
6. Protect our Ringa Toi
• Guaranteed income scheme for artists.
• Ensure the state pays a living wage to
employed and contracted artists, and
encourages organisations employing artists
to develop policies to pay them a living
wage.
• Protect the integrity of Māori Art through
WAI262.
Our Commitment
When Māori control our creativity, we control
our narrative.
When we control our narrative, we protect
our future.
When Toi Māori is upheld, oranga Māori is
strengthened for generations to come.
