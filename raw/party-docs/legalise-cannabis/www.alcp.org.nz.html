2026 Elections 7th November

  • We're a passionate team. ALCP exists to legalise, regulate, and normalise cannabis for medicinal, recreational, and industrial use, while reducing harm caused by prohibition.

  • Economic & Social Vision

    ALCP promotes cannabis and hemp as tools for:

    • Job creation and new tax revenue through regulated cannabis commerce.

    • Environmental benefits, including sustainable hemp for fuel, fibre, and reducing deforestation.

    • Community wellbeing, with a focus on fairness, justice, and ending discriminatory laws.

  • The Aotearoa Legalise Cannabis Party will work with any party that’s wanting change for the people to: end cannabis prohibition, promoting justice, reducing harm, and unlocking economic and ecological benefits through a regulated cannabis and hemp industry. Our Kaupapa center’s on Truth, Justice, and Freedom, and we argue that legalisation is a practical, compassionate, and evidence‑based step for Aotearoa

ALCP create solutions, not promises

Our Approach

Our approach to cannabis law reform is grounded in evidence, compassion, and community leadership. We believe drug policy should prioritise health over punishment, reduce harm, and address the injustices caused by prohibition — particularly for Māori and other communities disproportionately affected. By combining international best practice with Aotearoa’s unique social and cultural context, we advocate for practical, regulated solutions that protect people, strengthen communities, and uphold dignity and choice.

Why Vote Aotearoa Legalise Cannabis Party?

·         Comprehensive Cannabis Reform: We are committed to overhauling the Misuse of Drugs Act and creating sensible, compassionate cannabis laws that reflect modern values and science.

·         Personal Responsibility & Choice: We believe adults are capable of making informed decisions about their health, and we respect your right to use cannabis responsibly.

·         Health-Focused Solutions: Our policies put public safety and wellbeing first—regulating cannabis as a food-grade product, deglamorising its use, and protecting our communities.

·         Empowering Patients & Growers: We champion the right to homegrown medication so patients can access what they need, when they need it, and support a thriving hemp industry.

Unifying Voices for Real Change: We bring together New Zealanders from all walks of life, working across party lines to deliver lasting, positive change and social equity

Truth Justice and Freedom PRESS RELEASE Are we the last ones off the Rank to get our Human Rights?” 33rd Annual Jday Protest demands Cannabis Regulation in Aotearoa. Christchurch returns to Latimer Square on Saturday, May 3rd, from 12:00 PM to 4:20 PM, bringing the call for cannabis regulation to the streets of Ōtautahi. This year’s theme echoes the powerful words of advocate Irinka Britnell: “Are we the last cab off the rank to get our human rights?” With a spotlight on medicinal cannabis education, the event explores how access and awareness are developing in Aotearoa — and how much further we need to go. Expect an energizing afternoon of live music, guest speakers, open kōrero, and educational resources in a safe and supportive space. R18 | Free Entry | Food Stalls | Glass-Free Zone J Day Christchurch 2025 Saturday, May 3rd 12:00 PM 4:20 PM Latimer Square, Ōtautahi. Let’s stand together and push for progress. Media & stallholder contact: jdayotautahi@gmail.com
