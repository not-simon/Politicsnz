About this site: this site is built with AI from real, publicly available sources, which are quoted and linked. Daily updates pass automated accuracy and bias checks first — sources are re-verified, contested claims are attributed to who said them, and both sides are represented — before anything publishes. Even so, AI can make mistakes and sources can be wrong — if you spot something, please send a correction or suggestion and we'll fix it fast.
