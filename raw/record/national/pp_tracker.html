How we assess promises

Each promise is tracked against its original wording and stated deadline. Kept means delivered as promised; watered-down means delivered in significantly reduced form; broken means abandoned or reversed. Assessments cite public reporting and official documents. Parliament Pulse is non-partisan — we track promises from all parties in government and, as the campaign develops, commitments made by opposition parties.
